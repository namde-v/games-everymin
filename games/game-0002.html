CRATE KEEPER

Can you tidy the warehouse?

How to play: Move the blue keeper with arrow keys, WASD, the on-screen pad, or swipe on the board. Push crates — you cannot pull them, so plan ahead and avoid corners.

Green pads are targets. Get every crate onto a pad to finish a level. Press U to undo a move and R to restart a level.

5 levels. No timer, no pressure — just clean logic.

* Undo stack is shown in the HUD and is unlimited in practice; the counter counts undos used.
