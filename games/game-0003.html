EMBER BASTION

A micro tower-defense battle. Enemies march along the sandy road from the left to the right edge. If they escape, you lose lives. Build towers on the grass, earn gold for every kill, and upgrade your defences.

  • Build: Choose a tower below, then click / tap a grass tile. You cannot build on the road.
  • Upgrade: Click a tower you already own, then press Upgrade. Upgrades raise damage and range.
  • Waves: Press Start Wave when ready. Enemies get faster and tougher each wave.
  • Win: Survive all 12 waves. Lose: Your lives reach zero.

Keyboard: 1 / 2 / 3 select tower · U upgrade · Space start wave · R restart
