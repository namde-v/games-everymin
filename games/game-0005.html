MIRROR MAZE

How to play:
Click or tap a mirror to rotate it between / and \.
Use Arrow Keys to move the yellow cursor, Space to rotate the selected mirror.
Press Fire Laser (or F) to shoot the beam from the green emitter.
The beam bounces off mirrors, passes through targets, and stops at walls.
Light every target in as few rotations as possible to beat par.

No downloads. No network. Keyboard, mouse and touch supported.

Controls: Mouse / Touch — click a mirror to rotate it  |  Arrow Keys — move cursor  |  Space — rotate selected  |  F — fire  |  R — restart
Mirror rules: A / mirror sends East→North, North→East, West→South, South→West. A \ mirror sends East→South, South→East, West→North, North→West.
Tip: Targets do not block the beam. Walls do. Plan the path backwards from the last target.
