How to Play:
1. Drag back from the slingshot with your mouse or finger to aim. Watch the dotted flight path.
2. Release to launch. Gravity pulls the stone down, wind pushes it sideways.
3. Hit a target hard enough to knock it off its platform. A target that falls to the ground counts.
4. Clear all targets to climb to the next summit. No shots left with targets standing means Game Over.
Keyboard: Arrow keys adjust aim, Space launches, R restarts, Enter starts.
