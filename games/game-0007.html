Dune Dash

How to play: Run as far as you can across the dunes. Jump over cacti, rocks and sand gaps. Slide under swooping vultures. Grab coins for bonus points. The desert gets faster — and darker — the farther you go.

Jump: Space / Up / W / Tap  |  Slide: Down / S / Swipe Down  |  Restart: Enter

Tip: time your jump for gaps — landing in a gap ends the run.
