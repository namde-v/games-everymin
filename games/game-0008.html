How to play: Click or tap a gem, then click an adjacent gem to swap them. Make a row or column of 3 or more matching gems to clear them. Cleared gems fall, new gems drop in, and cascades score extra. Keyboard: Arrow keys move the glowing cursor. Press Enter or Space to select a gem, then an Arrow key to swap in that direction, or Enter on an adjacent gem. Reach the target score to win the level. If moves or time run out first, it is game over.

💎 Gem Cascade

A sliding match puzzle.
Swap adjacent gems, match 3 or more, chain cascades, and beat the target before your moves or time run out.

Mouse / touch: tap two adjacent gems.
Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Enter selects, arrows swap.

Level 1 target: 1000 points • 25 moves • 120 seconds
