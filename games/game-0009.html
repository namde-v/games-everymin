🏠 Haven Colony

Simulation / Resource Allocator
Lead a tiny settlement through spring, summer, autumn and a hard winter. Every day you decide where your people work — and every day they must eat and stay warm.

How to Play

1. Use + / − (or F, E, R keys) to assign workers to Food, Energy and Research.
2. Press Next Day. Production is added, then food and energy are consumed.
3. Grow by keeping a surplus, spend Research on upgrades, and react to random events.
4. Win by surviving 30 days or reaching 50 people. Lose if the population collapses or power fails for 2 days in a row.

Controls: Mouse, touch taps, and keyboard. No internet needed.
