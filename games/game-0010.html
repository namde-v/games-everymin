SKYLINE LEAP

Run across the rooftops, grab every star, dodge the spikes, and reach the flag before you fall.

  • Move: Arrow Keys / A D, or the on-screen Left and Right buttons
  • Jump: Space / W / Up Arrow, or the Jump button — jump again in mid-air for a double-jump
  • Forgiving jumps: Coyote time lets you jump briefly after leaving a ledge
  • Goal: Reach the flag on Level 3 to win. Spikes and falls cost a life. You have 3 lives.

Tip: Hold a direction while jumping to clear wide gaps. Land on moving rooftops in Level 3.
