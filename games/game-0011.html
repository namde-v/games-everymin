★ COMET COURIER ★

The galaxy's parcels won't deliver themselves. Pilot your courier ship through a drifting asteroid field, grab glowing parcels, and drop each one at the beacon of the same colour. Wrong beacon? No delivery. Crash into an asteroid? Lose a life.

Pink parcel → pink beacon Blue parcel → blue beacon Gold parcel → gold beacon Purple parcel → purple beacon

How to Play: Arrow keys or WASD to fly. Mouse: move to steer, click to boost. Touch: drag to steer. Press Space to boost. Grab a parcel, then fly into its matching beacon before the timer runs out. Complete every level's deliveries to win. You have 3 lives.

Level quotas: 4, then 5, then 6 deliveries — 15 total. Deliver fast for a speed bonus. Sound starts only after you press Start.
