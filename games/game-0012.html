⬢ HEX VAULT ⬢

How to Play: A power surge scrambled the vault wiring. Every hex tile carries pipes on some of its six sides. Click or tap a hex to rotate it 60°. Use the arrow keys to move the glowing selection ring and press Enter or Space to rotate the selected tile.

Power flows only where two neighbouring pipes meet edge to edge. Light up all 5 vault locks (the padlock hexes) to win. Each rotation costs 1 move — you have 40 rotations. Fewer moves used means a higher score.

Tip: start at the glowing power core and trace one branch at a time. Locked tiles with a single pipe must point exactly at their neighbour.
