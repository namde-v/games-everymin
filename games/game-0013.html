⚓ HARBOR MASTER

How to Play: Ships queue offshore with a cargo type and a patience timer. Tap or click a ship to select it, then tap its matching dock below (or press keys 1-4) before its patience runs out. A correct dock unloads cargo for points. A wrong dock or a ship that gives up costs 1 reputation.

Cargo: 📦 Containers (Dock 1)   🚚 Oil (Dock 2)   🌾 Grain (Dock 3)   🐟 Fish (Dock 4)
Survive all 5 waves and unload 40 cargo to WIN. Lose all 5 reputation and the harbor closes.
Controls: Mouse click, Touch tap, Keys 1-4 to assign, Arrow keys / A-D to change selection, Enter to start.

GAME OVER

Select a ship, then its matching dock!
Tip: work the ship with the shortest patience bar first. Later waves bring more ships, faster.
