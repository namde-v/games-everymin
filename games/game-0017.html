🌾 Meadow Market

Run a little farm stall and reach 500 gold before the 20-day season ends!

How to Play:
• Pick a crop below (Wheat, Carrot, Pumpkin), then tap / click an empty soil plot to plant it. Planting costs seeds.
• Crops grow a little each day. Tap a fully grown crop to harvest it into your basket.
• Market prices change every day! Sell when prices are high. Tap a crop in your basket or use Sell buttons.
• Press Next Day to grow crops and get new prices. Win at 500 gold. Run out of days and you lose.
• Keys: 1 / 2 / 3 choose crop  •  N next day  •  S sell all  •  Enter start / restart

Tip: Pumpkins cost more and take longer, but sell for much more on a good day. Wheat is fast and safe.
