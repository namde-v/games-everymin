Pendulum Perch

Ride the swing. Time the hop. Reach the golden perch!

How to Play: A bird sits on a swinging branch. A glowing target zone sweeps back and forth over the next perch. Press Space, click, or tap exactly when the swinging marker is inside the green zone to hop. A perfect hop (dead centre) earns bonus points. A mistimed hop costs a life. Chain hops to build your score.

Reach perch 12 to win. You have 3 lives. The swing gets faster and the target zone gets smaller as you climb!

Controls: Space / ArrowUp / Click / Tap to hop  •  R to restart after game end
