STORM CHASER

You are a storm chaser in a research van. Drive through the storm and recover 12 weather sensors before the storm takes you out!

How to Play:
🚐 Move: Arrow keys / WASD, mouse, or touch-drag to steer the van.
🧊 Dodge: falling hail, tumbling debris, and lightning strike zones (watch for the warning glow!).
📡 Collect: glowing weather sensors for +100 points each.
❤️ Survive: you have 3 lives. A hit costs 1 life and briefly shields you.
🏆 Win: collect all 12 sensors. Lose: lose all 3 lives.

Sound starts after you press Start. Best on keyboard, mouse, or touch.

🏆

YOU WIN!

Tip: lightning zones flash before they strike — get out of the glowing circle fast! Hail gets faster as you collect more sensors.
