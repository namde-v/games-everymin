⚙️ Clockwork City

How to Play: The crank on the left spins with power. Place gears on the grid so power flows through meshing gears to all 5 city buildings on the right and top.

  • Meshing matters: adjacent gears spin in opposite directions. A powered gear only passes power to a neighbour it meshes with.
  • Size matters: small gears connect only to orthogonal neighbours (up / down / left / right). Large gears also connect diagonally — great for shortcuts and corners, but they cost 2 budget instead of 1.
  • Budget: you have 20 gear-units. Click or tap an empty cell to place the selected gear. Click a placed gear to remove it and get its cost back.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys move the cursor, Enter or Space places a gear, Backspace removes it, 1 / 2 choose gear size.
  • Touch: tap a cell to place or remove a gear.
  • Win: power all 5 buildings. Game Over: your budget hits 0 before all buildings are powered.

Sound is procedural Web Audio and starts only after you press Start.

You Win!

Tip: build a spine along the middle row, then branch to the top, bottom and far-right buildings. Large gears help at diagonal corners.
