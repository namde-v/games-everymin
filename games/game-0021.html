ORBIT FLIP

You are a little orb runner racing through a space tunnel. There is no jump button — your only move is to flip gravity.

How to Play
• Press Space / Up Arrow / W, click the tunnel, or tap the FLIP button to flip gravity.
• Stick to the floor or the ceiling and dodge the red blocks and spikes.
• Collect golden stars for +50 points. Avoiding an obstacle is +10 points.
• You have 3 lives. Reach 3000 m to win. Press P to pause.

Tip: flip early — you travel in an arc, so time your flip before the obstacle arrives.
