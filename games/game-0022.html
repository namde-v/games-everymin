WORD WEAVER

How to Play — read this before you start:

  • Tap or click letters to select them. Each new letter must touch the previous one — up, down, left, right, or diagonally.
  • Build a real word of 3 or more letters, then press Submit. Valid words score points and refill with fresh letters.
  • Every submitted word (right or wrong) uses 1 move. Reach the word quota before moves run out to clear the level.
  • Clear Level 1 (6 words), Level 2 (8 words), and Level 3 (10 words) to WIN. Run out of moves and the game is over.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys move the glowing cursor, Enter / Space selects a letter, Backspace undoes the last letter, S submits, C clears.
  • Longer words score much more: 3 letters = 30, 4 = 50, 5 = 80, 6 = 120, 7+ = 180 points.
