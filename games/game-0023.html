SKYPORT CONTROL

How to Play: Planes fly in from the edges, each coloured to match one of the four runways. Select a plane, then send it to its matching runway before it leaves the sky or hits another plane.

  • Mouse / Touch: tap a plane to select it, then tap a runway to route it.
  • Keyboard: Left / Right arrows cycle planes, Up / Down cycle runways, Enter or Space confirms the route, keys 1–4 send the selected plane straight to that runway.
  • Scoring: +100 for a matching landing, +25 bonus for a fast landing. Wrong runway or a mid-air collision costs a life. A plane that exits unlanded costs a life.
  • Win: land 20 planes. Game Over: lose all 3 lives.

Sound starts only after you press Start.
