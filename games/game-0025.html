Prism Breaker

Color-matching breakout!

How to Play: Bounce the ball to smash the prisms. A prism only breaks when the ball's color MATCHES the prism's color. A mismatched hit just bounces off, so switch colors at the right moment!

Ruby   Amber   Sapphire   Jade

Move: Left / Right arrows or A / D, mouse, or touch drag  |  Switch ball color: Space or tap the SWITCH button  |  Launch ball: Space / click / tap

Clear every prism to win. You have 3 lives. Each level adds new layouts and a faster ball.
