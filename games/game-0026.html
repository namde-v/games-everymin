🪨 Boulder Balance

Stack oddly-shaped boulders into a tall, stable tower. Balance matters more than speed.

Boulder Balance

How to Play

  • Boulders fall from the sky. Move each one over the tower before it lands.
  • Rotate odd shapes so a flat-ish side lands down. A badly balanced boulder tilts and rolls off.
  • Land near the centre of the boulder below. Big overhangs are risky.
  • Stack 12 boulders to win. Lose a boulder off the side and you lose a fall.
  • 3 falls and the tower crumbles — game over.

Controls: Left / Right move  |  Up / Q / E rotate  |  Space / Down fast drop  |  Mouse move + click to drop  |  Touch drag + tap to drop

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads, fully offline.

Tip: wide boulders are stable, tall narrow ones are risky. Aim for the middle of the stack, not the edge.
