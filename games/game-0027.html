Lighthouse Keeper

Five nights. One lamp. A sea full of ships heading for the rocks.

How to Play

  • Aim the beam: move the mouse, drag on touch, or use Left / Right arrows. Ships caught in the beam turn away from the rocks.
  • Flash a warning: press Space, click, or tap Flash. A flash warns every ship in the beam at once.
  • Fuel: the lamp burns fuel every second. Press F (or Fuel) to refuel for 15 supplies.
  • Repair: storms wear the lamp down. Press R (or Repair) to restore it for 20 supplies.
  • Rescue: after a wreck, shine the beam on floating sailors to rescue them for bonus supplies.
  • Win: survive all 5 nights. Game Over: fuel hits 0, the lamp fails, or 5 ships wreck.

Keyboard: Left / Right aim  |  Space flash  |  F fuel  |  R repair

Night Complete
