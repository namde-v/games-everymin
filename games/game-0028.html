Orbit Golf

Gravity wells bend every putt. Use them — don't fight them.

How to Play:
• Aim with Arrow keys (Left / Right rotate, Up / Down fine-tune) or drag with mouse / touch from the ball.
• Hold Space to charge power, release to shoot. Or drag back and release.
• Purple wells pull the ball — a curved path can go around walls and into the hole.
• Press R to restart the current hole (adds a stroke penalty of 1).
• Complete all 5 holes in 28 strokes or fewer to win. Exceed 28 and it's Game Over.
• The ball must stop or drop in the hole before your next shot.

Sound starts only after you press Start.
