✦ RUNE DUEL ✦

Outwit the rune mage. Play smart, spend mana wisely, survive the duel.

How to Play

You and the enemy mage each have 30 HP. On your turn you gain mana and draw cards. Play rune cards to attack, shield, heal, or trick your foe. Spend mana carefully — unused mana does not carry over. End your turn and the enemy strikes back. Win a round by reducing the enemy to 0 HP. Win 2 rounds to win the match! If your HP reaches 0, the duel is lost.

Controls: Keys 1–5 select a card, Enter / Space plays it, E ends your turn. Or click / tap a card to select it, then click Play, or tap a card twice to play it.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
