How to Play: The lock hides a secret 4-symbol code. Symbols may repeat. You have 10 guesses to crack it.

  • Pick a symbol below, then tap a slot — or just tap the picker and it fills the next empty slot.
  • Press Submit Guess when all 4 slots are filled.
  • ● Black peg = right symbol in the right place. ○ White peg = right symbol, wrong place.
  • Use the pegs to narrow it down. Solve it in fewer guesses for a bigger score!

Controls: Mouse / touch — tap picker and slots. Keyboard — keys 1–6 pick a symbol, ← → move slot, Enter submit, Backspace clear last, R restart.
