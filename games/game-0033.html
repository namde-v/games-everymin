RAIL BARON

Strategy / Train Routing

  • How to play: Trains roll in from the left, each painted to match one coloured station on the right.
  • Switch junctions J1, J2 and J3 before a train reaches them: tap / click a diamond, or press keys 1, 2, 3.
  • J1 up goes to Red, J1 straight then J2 up goes to Green, J3 down goes to Blue, and all straight goes to Yellow.
  • Correct delivery: +100 points. Wrong station or a train collision: lose a life.
  • Win: deliver 12 trains correctly. Game Over: 3 mistakes.
  • Faster trains can catch slower ones — route them apart to avoid a crash!

Keyboard: 1 / 2 / 3 switch junctions  •  Mouse: click a junction  •  Touch: tap a junction
