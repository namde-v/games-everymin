🎵 TEMPO TOWER

Blocks swing above your tower on the beat.
Lock each one at exactly the right moment!

How to Play
1. Each falling block shows a key: ◀ ▶ ▲ or ● TAP.
2. Wait for the beat ring to glow green — that is the lock window.
3. Press the correct key (or tap its on-screen button) inside the window.
4. A centred lock scores more. A block locked far off-centre topples!
5. If a beat passes with no lock, the block drops. 3 misses = Game Over.
6. Stack 15 floors to WIN. The swing gets faster as you climb!

Keyboard: ArrowLeft / ArrowRight / ArrowUp / Space  •  Mouse & touch: tap the buttons below

You Win!

Tip: do not spam keys. A correct key outside the green window is Early / Late and does not lock. A wrong key inside the window drops the block.
Sound starts only after you press START.
