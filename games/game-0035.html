METEOR MINER

Pilot. Drill. Get rich. Don't blow up.

● Gold asteroids are valuable ore. ● Red pulsing asteroids are explosive — never mine them, and don't ram them.

How to Play
Rotate: ← → or A / D  •  Thrust: ↑ or W
Mining Beam: Hold SPACE, hold mouse button, or hold touch
Aim the beam at a gold asteroid and hold it until it breaks apart.
WIN: Collect 500 credits  •  LOSE: Hull hits 0% or the 120s timer runs out.

Tip: tap an asteroid's edge with the beam, then back off — explosive blasts hurt up close.

MISSION COMPLETE

Keyboard: Arrows / WASD + Space  •  Mouse: move to aim, hold click to mine  •  Touch: drag to aim & thrust, hold to mine. Works fully offline — no downloads, no ads.
