Tile Turner

A Lights-Out style puzzle with a twist: you must match a target pattern, not just clear the board.

How to Play
  • Tap / click a tile: it flips, and so do its up, down, left and right neighbours.
  • Match the big board exactly to the small Target pattern to clear the level.
  • Each level has a move limit. Use as few moves as possible for bonus points.
  • Clear all 5 levels to WIN. Exceed a level's move limit and it's Game Over.
  • Keyboard: ← ↑ ↓ → to move the cursor, Space / Enter to flip, R to restart the level, H for a hint flash.
