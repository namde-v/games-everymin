🍞 Bakery Rush

Run your own bakery! Customers queue up hungry for breads and pastries. Bake fast, serve the right order, and earn 300 coins to win. But beware: lose 3 customers and the shop closes!

  • How to play: Click a recipe below (or press keys 1-4) to start baking in a free oven.
  • When an item is baked it lands on your fresh tray.
  • Click a customer (or press Q / W / E / R) to serve them what they ordered.
  • Each customer has a patience bar. Serve them before it empties!
  • Faster, pricier items (like Cake) take longer to bake but pay more.

Mouse, touch, and keyboard supported. Sound starts after you press Start.
