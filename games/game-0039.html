🌿 VINE VAULT 🌿

Arcade / Grapple Swing — Swing across the jungle chasm!

How to Play: Hold SPACE, CLICK, or TOUCH & HOLD to grab the nearest vine and swing. Release at the top of your swing to fly forward with momentum. Use LEFT / RIGHT arrows (or A / D) while swinging to pump for extra speed. Grab fruits for bonus points. Reach the far platform at 1000m to WIN. Falling into the chasm costs a life — 3 falls and it is Game Over!

Controls: SPACE / Mouse Hold / Touch Hold = Grab & Release  |  ← → / A D = Pump  |  R = Restart
