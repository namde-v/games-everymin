WAR COUNCIL

Game #0040 — Strategy / Turn-Based Tactics
Command your war council across the battlefield. Outmanoeuvre the enemy general, capture their banner, or rout every foe.

  • How to play: Click / tap a green-bordered unit, then a green tile to move, then a red tile to attack.
  • Each unit moves and attacks once per turn. Press End Turn when done — the enemy then moves.
  • Win: Capture the enemy banner (top row, gold dashed tile) OR defeat all enemy units.
  • Lose: All of your units are defeated, or the enemy captures your banner (bottom row).
  • Keyboard: Arrows move cursor, Enter / Space selects, E ends turn, R restarts.
  • Units: ⚔️ Knight (strong, short range) · 🏹 Archer (long range) · 🔮 Mage (splash) · 🛡️ Guardian (tanky) · ✚ Cleric (heals allies).

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads.
