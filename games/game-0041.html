☀️ Solar Sailor

Ride the wind. Dodge the debris. Charge the sun.

How to Play:

  • Move: Arrow keys / WASD, mouse drag, or touch-drag to steer your sail ship.
  • Ride currents: Glowing wind lanes push you forward and build your gust streak. Stay in a lane to charge a speed boost.
  • Collect: Grab golden solar cells (+50). Collect all 40 cells to WIN.
  • Dodge: Grey debris costs 1 life. Red asteroids cost 1 life and break your streak. You have 3 lives.
  • Gust streak: Every 5 seconds riding a current without a hit adds +1 streak and a speed boost. A hit resets it.
  • Pause: Press P or Space to pause. M mutes sound.

Tip: wind lanes drift sideways — follow the flowing arrows. Blue cells are worth double during a gust boost. The starfield scrolls faster as your speed rises, so watch the edges: debris spawns from the right and drifts left. Survive, collect, and ride the storm to victory!
