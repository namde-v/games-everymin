Ink Trail

Draw one continuous ink path from the green start, through every glowing checkpoint, to the golden goal.

How to Play
• Drag with mouse or finger across neighbouring squares, or use Arrow Keys / WASD.
• You may only step up, down, left or right — never diagonally.
• Never cross your own trail, revisit a square, or pass through a dark wall.
• Collect every checkpoint before the goal opens. Get stuck? Press Undo or Restart Level.
• Stuck with no moves left costs a life. Lose all 3 lives and the game ends.

6 hand-built levels — grids grow from 6×6 to 10×10.
