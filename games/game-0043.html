🌴 Oasis Outpost

How to Play
Lead a desert outpost from 12 settlers to a thriving town of 60 people.
Each day your crews produce resources, then your people eat and drink. If water or food runs out, people leave or worse — if population falls to 0, the outpost is lost.
Build wells, farms and shelters. Watch for sandstorms: they damage shelter and drain supplies, but a well-stocked, well-sheltered outpost weathers them.
Newcomers arrive when morale is high and there is spare shelter.

Win by reaching 60 population. Survive, balance, and grow.

Mouse, touch and keyboard supported. Sound starts only after you press Start.
