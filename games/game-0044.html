⚒ BEAT SMITH

Forge 8 quality items before your 90-second shift ends!

How to Play:

  • A hammer swings and a beat marker travels toward the gold zone on the anvil. Strike exactly on the beat.
  • PERFECT (centre of the gold zone): +100 points, +heat, and great progress on the current item.
  • GOOD (edge of the gold zone): +50 points and steady progress.
  • MISS (outside the zone, or letting a beat pass): breaks your combo and cools the forge. Three misses in a row costs a life.
  • Combos build Heat. At 100% heat you enter MOLTEN MODE for 10 seconds: every hit scores double.
  • Each item needs 3 good strikes (or 2 perfect strikes) to finish. Forge 8 items to WIN. Lose all 3 lives, or run out of time, and the shift is over.
  • The tempo speeds up as you forge more items — stay on the beat!

Controls: Space / Enter / J, mouse click, or touch.
