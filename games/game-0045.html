🥌 Curling Clash

Slide your stones down the ice and park them closest to the button. Out-score the CPU over 4 ends to win the match.

Tip: aim carefully — too much power sails through the house, too little stops short.

How to Play

  • Aim: move the mouse / finger left-right, or press ← → arrow keys. The dotted arrow shows the path.
  • Power: hold SPACE, hold the mouse button, or hold your finger to charge power, then release to throw. The bar shows power.
  • Curl: pick Curl Left / Curl Right (or Q / E) before throwing — the stone bends that way as it slows.
  • Sweep: while a stone slides, hold S, the Sweep button, or touch the ice to make it travel farther and straighter.
  • Scoring: after all 8 stones in an end, each of your stones closer to the button than the CPU's closest stone scores 1 point.
  • Win: most points after 4 ends wins. Stones knocked out of the house score nothing.
  • Keyboard: ← → aim, ↑ ↓ power nudge, SPACE hold/release throw, S sweep, Q/E curl, R restart.

Mouse, touch and keyboard all work. Sound starts after you press Start.
