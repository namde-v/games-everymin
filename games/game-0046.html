🤖 DROID DEPOT

Your factory, your line. Droids need parts, and the parts will not route themselves. Lay conveyors, drop in assemblers to turn Raw scrap into Gears, and use sorters to split mixed lines to the correct bins.

How to Play

  • Goal: Meet every bin quota before time runs out. Wrong delivery costs a life. You have 3 lives.
  • Build: Pick a tool, then tap a grid cell. Conveyors carry parts in their arrow direction. Assemblers convert Raw → Gear and send it East. Sorters send Gears East and everything else South.
  • Sources & bins are fixed — you cannot build on green sources, gold bins, or grey blocked machines.
  • Levels get harder: Level 1 is one straight line. Level 2 adds a second line and obstacles. Level 3 mixes Raw and Circuit parts — sort them or lose lives.
  • Tip: Parts wait patiently if the path is incomplete, so build first, then watch them flow. Clear tiles with the Eraser to re-route.

Tap Start to enable sound. Sound is procedural only — no downloads, fully offline.
