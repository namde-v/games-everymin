🫧 Bubble Bounty

Aim the cannon, fire coloured bubbles, and pop groups of 3 or more. Clear every bubble before the board descends past the red danger line!

How to Play
• Mouse / Touch: move to aim, click or tap to shoot.
• Keyboard: ← → to aim, ↑ or Space to shoot, R to restart.
• Match 3+ bubbles of the same colour to pop them.
• Bubbles cut off from the top fall for bonus points — chain them for combos!
• Every 6 shots the board creeps down. Reach the red line and you lose a life. Lose 3 lives and it is Game Over.

Tip: bounce shots off the side walls for tricky angles!

Game Over

Press R or tap Play Again to start a fresh board immediately.

Keyboard: ← → aim  |  ↑ / Space shoot  |  R restart  |  M mute  •  Mouse and touch fully supported. Sound starts only after you press Start.
