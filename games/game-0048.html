🔢 Number Nexus

A slide-and-merge number puzzle. Forge target values, chain merges, and beat all three levels.

  • Slide the whole grid with arrow keys, WASD, swipe, mouse drag, or the on-screen pad.
  • When two equal tiles collide they merge into double (2+2=4, 4+4=8, and so on).
  • Each level gives you a target tile to forge: 32, then 64, then 128.
  • Every slide costs 1 move. Run out of moves without the target and you lose a life.
  • Chain merges: merging 2+ pairs in one slide pays a combo bonus (×2, ×3...).
  • Reach the 128 tile to WIN. Lose all 3 lives and it is game over. One Undo per level.

Fully offline. No downloads, no accounts. Sound starts only after you press Start.
