⚓ TIDE TRADER

Game 0049 — Simulation / Sea Trading

Sail between five island ports, buy low and sell high, and grow 500 gold into 5000 gold before the 90-day trading season ends.

  • Prices shift with seasons, storms and harbour events.
  • Every voyage costs days — long trips can miss a price boom.
  • Watch the news ticker: a storm may sink prices, a festival may spike them.

How to Play: Click / tap a port on the map to sail there, or steer with Arrow keys / WASD. When you arrive, the market opens. Buy goods cheap, carry them to a port where they cost more, and sell. Press 1–5 to pick a port, B to buy the first good, S to sell, W to wait a day. Touch: tap ports and buttons; drag on the sea to steer your ship.

Win: Reach 5000 gold. Lose: Day 90 arrives without reaching the goal, or you go broke with no cargo to sell.

Tips: Silk is dear in the north, fish is cheap in the south. Never fill your hold with one good — diversify, and sell before a storm warning ends.
