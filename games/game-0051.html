EMBER ESCAPE

Climb 1000 metres — or collect 25 embers — to reach the summit before the rising lava swallows you.

How to Play
⌨️ Arrow keys / A & D: move & steer in mid-air  •  Space / W / Up: jump
🖱️ Mouse: move to steer, click to jump
👆 Touch: drag to steer, tap to jump
🪨 Platforms sink while you stand on them — keep moving! Lava rocks crumble, embers are worth 100 points, height adds score too.

Tip: jump from the edge for extra distance, bounce on springy orange rocks, and never wait on a sinking platform. Falling embers are a bonus — grab them mid-jump!
