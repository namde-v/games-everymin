RUNE RINGS

How to Play: Four concentric rings carry ancient runes. Every ring is scrambled. Rotate rings step by step until every spoke — a straight line from the centre outward — shows the SAME rune on all four rings. A golden line marks a solved spoke.

Select a ring by tapping / clicking it, or with keys 1–4. Rotate it with the on-screen buttons, ← / → arrow keys, A / D, or by dragging around the centre. Each rotation step costs 1 move. Solve all 8 lines in 40 moves to Win.

Runes: red ✦   blue ◆   green ●   gold ▲ — match by colour and shape.

Keyboard, mouse and touch all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.
