🐜 ANTLION EMPIRE

Build tunnels. Feed the colony. Defend the Queen.

How to Play
  • Build: pick a tool below, then click / tap a glowing cell next to your colony. Tunnels are free, Pantries cost 20 food and earn food over time, Barracks cost 35 food and train soldiers.
  • Gather: worker ants automatically walk your tunnels to green food piles and carry food back to the Queen.
  • Defend: press Start Wave when ready. Soldiers hunt intruders; any intruder that reaches the Queen deals 10 damage.
  • Win: survive all 8 waves with the Queen alive. Lose: the Queen falls.
  • Keys: 1 = Tunnel, 2 = Pantry, 3 = Barracks, Space / W = Start Wave, P = Pause, R = Restart.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Mouse, touch and keyboard all work.

Victory!

Choose a tool, then build beside your colony. Press Start Wave when ready.

Tip: reach the green food piles with tunnels first — food is how you afford soldiers before the later, harder waves.
