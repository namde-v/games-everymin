✈ Paper Plane Pilot

Precision / Wind Glider

How to Play:

  • Fly through 20 floating rings to win. Each ring is worth 100 points, plus a bonus for a clean, centred pass.
  • Watch the wind! Gusts push your plane sideways and change every section. The HUD arrow shows direction and strength.
  • Avoid the clouds, birds and storm cells. A crash costs one life. Crash 3 times and the flight is over.
  • Steer gently — the paper plane glides and drifts. Small corrections beat big jerks.

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to thrust and tilt • Mouse: move to steer, hold click for thrust • Touch: drag to steer, your finger gives lift.
