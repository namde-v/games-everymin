🌊 Deep Dive Dash

Swim through the flooded cave, dodge rocks and stalactites, grab air bubbles and pearls, and reach the glowing exit at 1200m!

How to Play
Move the diver up and down to steer through gaps. Collect 🫧 air bubbles to refill oxygen and 🦪 pearls for 50 points each.
Hitting a rock or stalactite costs 1 life. 3 collisions, or oxygen reaching 0%, ends the dive. Reach 1200m to win!

⌨️ Keyboard: ↑ / ↓ or W / S to swim  •  🖱️ Mouse: move to steer, click to dash  •  👆 Touch: drag to swim

Tip: stay near the middle of gaps, grab every bubble you see, and use a short dash (click / Space) to escape tight spots. Oxygen drains faster the deeper you go — bubbles are life!
