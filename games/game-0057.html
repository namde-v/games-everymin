Shadow Shapes

Fill the shadow. No piece may stick out.

  • Pick a piece: click or tap a piece in the tray at the bottom.
  • Place it: click or tap a square on the board. The piece's top-left corner lands there.
  • Rotate / Flip: press R / F, or use the buttons above the board.
  • Take a piece back: click or tap a piece already on the board.
  • Keys 1-5: select a tray piece. Esc: deselect.
  • A placement that overlaps or sticks outside the shadow counts as a strike. 3 strikes, or running out of time, ends the game.
