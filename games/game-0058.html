🏙️ Skyline Tycoon

Can you build a city people love?

How to Play: Pick a building below, then tap / click a grid square to place it. Each day lasts 20 seconds — or press Next Day early. Earn income from shops and houses, keep pollution down with parks and services, and keep citizens happy.

🏠 House $100 (+8 people)   🏪 Shop $150 (+12 jobs, income)   🌳 Park $80 (+happiness, −pollution)   🏥 Service $200 (+happiness, −pollution)

WIN by Day 30: Population 150+ AND Happiness 60%+.
LOSE if: you go bankrupt (budget below $0 at day end) or happiness collapses to 0%.

Keys: 1–4 select building, 5 bulldoze, Arrows move cursor, Enter/Space place, N next day.
