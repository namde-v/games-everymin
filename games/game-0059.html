🌊 SYNTH SURFER

How to Play — Ride a living synth wave from start to finish. Peaks and troughs scroll toward the glowing hit zone. Strike each one exactly on time to keep your energy charged and your combo alive.

PEAK (wave top): press UP / W, click the TOP half, or tap the top half
TROUGH (wave bottom): press DOWN / S, click the BOTTOM half, or tap the bottom half
Move your surfer: move the mouse or drag a finger to steer along the wave
Miss a peak or trough: lose 14 energy  |  Perfect hit: +10 energy, big combo bonus

Survive the whole track with energy left to win. Energy at zero ends the ride.
