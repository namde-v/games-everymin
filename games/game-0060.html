Boomerang Bay

How to Play: You stand on the beach at the bottom-left. Hit every target in number order before you run out of throws. Clear all 5 levels to win!

Drag to throw: Press on (or near) the boomerang and drag in a curve. A straight drag flies straight; a bent, arcing drag adds curve, so the boomerang bends around obstacles and swings back to you. Longer drags fly farther. Watch the wind — it pushes the boomerang sideways.

Mouse: drag & release to throw  |  Touch: drag & release  |  Keyboard: Arrow keys aim, A / D adjust curve, Space throws, R restarts.
Wrong-order targets flash but do not count. Catch the boomerang when it returns!
