FROSTBITE FRONTIER

You are the rescue ranger. Stranded climbers are waiting on a frozen lake. The ice is so slick you cannot stop on your own — you slide until you hit a snowbank.

How to Play
• Arrow keys / WASD — slide up, down, left or right
• Mouse — click a tile in the direction you want to slide
• Touch — swipe, or use the on-screen pad
• Slide into a climber 🧗 to rescue them
• Avoid crevasses ⬛ and thin ice: thin ice cracks once, then breaks!
• Rescue everyone, then slide into the glowing igloo ⛺ to finish the level
• You have 3 lives. Complete all 3 levels to win.

Tip: Plan every slide. A snowbank is your brake — a crevasse is not.
