How to Play
Every rune appears exactly twice in the grove. Draw a path from one rune to its twin. Paths travel up, down, left or right through empty stones — never diagonally, never through another rune, and never across another path.

Connect every pair to clear the grove and grow to the next level. Crossing a path or joining the wrong rune costs a life. You have 3 lives. Clear all 10 groves to win.

Mouse: drag from a rune  •  Touch: drag  •  Keyboard: arrows + Space
