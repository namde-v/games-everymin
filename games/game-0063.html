RAILWAY RENDEZVOUS

How to Play: Trains roll in from the left, each carrying a coloured cargo lamp. Route each train to the station of the SAME colour. Click a junction (the big circles) to flip its switch before the train reaches it. Junction 1 picks the upper or lower half of the yard; Junctions 2 and 3 pick the exact station. Click a moving train to hold it at a red signal, click again to release it — use this to stop a fast train rear-ending a slow one. Every train has a timetable bar: deliver before it runs out.

Keys: 1 flips Junction 1, 2 flips Junction 2, 3 flips Junction 3, Space pauses. Mouse and touch work the same way. Deliver 15 trains to win. A crash, a wrong station, or a missed timetable costs a life.
