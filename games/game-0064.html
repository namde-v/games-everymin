🌻 Beat Botanist 🌻

Garden Groove: Seeds, water drops and suns fall down four garden beds on the beat. Catch each one exactly when it lands on the glowing harvest line to grow your garden.

How to Play
🌰 Seed = plant   💧 Water = grow   ☀️ Sun = ripen   🌾 Harvest = score big
Keys: D   F   J   K for beds 1–4
Or tap / click a bed at the bottom. Perfect hits build combos.
Every 4 perfect-or-good hits grows one crop. Harvest 16 crops before the song ends to win!
Misses cost a life. Lose all 5 lives and the garden wilts.

Perfect = within a blink of the beat • Good = close • Miss = too early, too late, or untouched.
