🧲 Magnet Minefield

You are a roaming magnet in a field of charged orbs and live mines. Flip your polarity to pull in the orbs you need — but the wrong flip pulls the mines straight toward you!

How to Play
Move: WASD / Arrow keys, mouse move, or touch-drag
Flip polarity: Space / X, mouse click, or the Flip button
Red orbs = POSITIVE   Blue orbs = NEGATIVE
Matching polarity orbs are ATTRACTED to you — collect them.
Opposite polarity orbs are REPELLED — they push away.
Mines are pulled in when their polarity matches yours. Flip away to push mines back, but then your target orbs push away too!

Collect the quota on all 3 levels to WIN. Touching a mine costs a life. You have 3 lives.
