CLOCKWORK COURIER

How to Play

The courier waits at the green depot on the left. The brass delivery door stands on the right. Between them sits a machine full of interlocking gears, each carrying a channel. Click, tap, or use the arrow keys plus Space to select a gear and rotate it a quarter turn. When every gear along a route meshes so its channel lines up with its neighbours, the mechanism clicks into place and the courier rolls through to make the delivery.

Each level gives you a limited number of rotations. Waste them all and you lose a gear-life and the level resets. Lose all three lives and the machine jams for good. Complete all five levels to win.

Mouse, touch, or keyboard: Arrows to move, Space or R to rotate.
