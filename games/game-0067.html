☀️ Desert Drone Dash

Canyon Racer — Pilot your solar drone through desert canyons!

Thread the glowing gates for bonus points, dodge rock spires, and keep your battery alive. Sun patches recharge you — shade and boosting drain you. Reach 20 gates or 3000 m to WIN!

HOW TO PLAY
⬅️ ➡️ ⬆️ ⬇️ / WASD — Steer drone  |  SPACE / Hold — Boost (fast, drains battery)
🖱️ Mouse — Move to steer  |  👆 Touch & drag — Steer  |  Tap boost button on screen for boost
☀️ Fly through sun patches to recharge  |  🚩 Thread gates for +150  |  🪨 Hitting a spire costs a life

Battery drains over time and faster in shade. Don't let it hit 0%!
