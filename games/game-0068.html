ORBIT ORCHARD

Farm tiny orchards on drifting asteroids!

How to Play:
• Six asteroids orbit the sun. Your station can only reach an asteroid when it passes inside the green harvest ring around the station.
• Click / tap an asteroid (or use ← → keys) to select it, then Plant a seed while it is in range.
• Trees need water and sunlight. Asteroids closer to the sun grow faster but dry out quicker. Water thirsty trees before they wither!
• When a tree turns golden and ripe, wait until its asteroid is back in range, then Harvest for fruit and credits.
• A withered tree costs 1 life. Harvest 120 fruit in 10 days to win. Spend credits on extra seeds.
• Rain showers sometimes refill an asteroid’s water for free — watch for the blue splash.

Keyboard: ← / → select asteroid  |  P plant  |  W water  |  H harvest  |  B buy seed  |  Space start / restart
Mouse: click an asteroid or a button  |  Touch: tap an asteroid or a button
