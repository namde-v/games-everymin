How to Play

  • Watch & Listen: the chamber plays a sequence of glowing tiles, each with its own tone.
  • Repeat it: tap the tiles in the exact same order. Every correct tile scores points.
  • Grow the echo: each round adds one more tone. Sequences get longer and faster.
  • Survive: a wrong tile costs a life. You have 3 lives. Reach Round 12 to win the chamber.
  • Combo bonus: finish a round without a mistake for a +50 round bonus.
