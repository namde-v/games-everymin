Lantern Labyrinth

Guide the lantern beam through the dark labyrinth to the glowing exit crystal.

How to Play
  • Place a mirror: tap / click an empty floor tile. You have only a few mirrors per level.
  • Rotate a mirror: tap / click a placed mirror to flip it between / and \. Press R to rotate the selected tile.
  • Remove a mirror: use Remove Mode, or double-tap / double-click a placed mirror.
  • Fire the beam: press Light the Beam or Space. If the beam reaches the crystal, you win the level.
  • Watch your fuel: the lantern burns down every second. If fuel hits zero, or you lose all 3 lives, it is Game Over.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys move the cursor, Enter places / rotates, Space fires, R rotates, C clears the last mirror, M toggles remove mode.
  • Touch: tap tiles to place and rotate, use the buttons below to fire, remove, and restart.

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads, no network — fully offline.
