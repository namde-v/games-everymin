ARCADE / STORM FISHING

⚓ TEMPEST TRAWLER

Captain, a wild storm is rolling in — and the fish are biting anyway. Steer your trawler through towering waves and lightning, scoop up leaping fish and floating supply crates, and haul in 500 points before the sea claims your boat.

  • Move: Arrow keys / WASD, mouse move, or drag on touch. Your trawler follows you.
  • Collect: Fish (+10, golden fish +25) and crates (+30, restores a bit of hull). Catch fish with your net zone at the stern.
  • Avoid: Rogue waves (big rolling swells) and lightning strikes — red warning zones flash first, then strike!
  • Lives: You have 3 hull hearts. A wave or bolt costs 1 heart. Survive, reach 500, and you win.
  • Tip: The storm gets fiercer as your score climbs. Hug calmer water between waves and grab crates when hurt.

How to Play

Press Start, then steer. No buttons needed to fish — just sail over fish and crates. Watch the lightning warnings and keep your hull intact.

Steer with keyboard, mouse, or touch. Collect fish and crates, dodge waves and lightning, reach 500 points to win. Press R anytime to restart.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Best played with sound on.
