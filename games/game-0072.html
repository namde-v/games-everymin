How to Play (quick)

1. Move the glowing cursor with Arrow Keys or tap / click a cell.
2. Press Enter / Space or tap the highlighted cell again to place.
3. Fill a whole row to clear it as a pattern row.
4. Clear 10 rows to win and complete the mosaic.
5. If a tile cannot fit anywhere, discard it — but discarding costs a life. Lose all 3 lives and the game ends.

Target Pattern Hint

Rows made from a single colour are Perfect Mosaics. Try to save matching colours for the same row.

Mosaic Mason

Welcome, Mason! The great hall floor is bare. Lay coloured mosaic tiles on the 8 by 8 grid and complete pattern rows to restore it.

How to Play

  • Goal: Complete 10 pattern rows to win. A pattern row is a row completely filled with tiles.
  • Place: Use Arrow Keys to move the cursor, then Enter or Space to place. On mouse or touch, tap a cell to move the cursor there, tap again to place.
  • Rotate: Press R or tap Rotate to turn your tile 90 degrees before placing.
  • Score: +10 per tile cell placed, +100 per cleared row, +250 extra for a Perfect single-colour row.
  • Lives: You have 3 lives. Discarding a tile costs 1 life. If a tile has no possible placement, you must discard it.
  • Game Over: Lose all 3 lives and the mosaic is left unfinished.
  • Controls: Keyboard: Arrows + Enter / Space + R + D. Mouse: click. Touch: tap. All three work.

Tip: Keep rows tidy. Fill the bottom rows first and group the same colour together for Perfect bonuses.
