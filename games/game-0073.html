⚓ PORTSIDE PLANNER

Strategy / Dock Scheduler
Ships are arriving at the harbour. You are the harbour master. Assign each ship to a dock that can hold it, unload its cargo before the deadline, and complete 12 shipments to win the day.

  • How to play: Tap / click a waiting ship to select it (it glows gold), then tap / click a free dock to berth it. Unloading starts automatically.
  • Capacity matters: Dock A holds size 1–2, Dock B holds size 1–3, Dock C holds size 1 only but unloads fastest. A ship too big for a dock cannot berth there.
  • Deadlines matter: A waiting ship with an expiring timer leaves angry — you lose a life. Lose 3 lives and the day ends.
  • Scoring: Earn cargo points plus a speed bonus for fast unloading. Bigger ships pay more, but take longer.
  • Keyboard: Keys 1–5 select a waiting ship, A / S / D berth it at Dock A / B / C, Enter starts or restarts.
  • Mouse & touch: Click or tap a ship, then a dock. That is the whole job — and it gets busy fast.
Fully offline. Sound is procedural and starts only after you press Start. No network needed.
