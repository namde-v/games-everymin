CINDER SPRINT

Sprint across cooling lava crust before it crumbles beneath you.

Reach 1500 metres to reach the safe shore and win. You have 3 lives. Hitting a rock or falling into a lava gap costs a life.

How to Play
← / → or A / D — switch lanes
↑ / Space / W — jump over rocks and lava gaps
Mouse: click left third = left, middle = jump, right third = right
Touch: swipe left / right to switch lanes, swipe up (or tap) to jump
Collect ⭐ embers for +50 points. Distance also scores points.

Tip: jump a beat early for wide gaps. Embers often mark the safe lane.
