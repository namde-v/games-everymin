Knot Garden

How to Play: The garden vines have grown into a terrible tangle. Drag a glowing knot with your mouse, finger, or stylus to a clear patch of soil. A rope is tangled wherever two vines cross. Untangle every crossing before your moves run out.

Keyboard: Arrow keys move the selected knot • Tab / Q / E cycle knots • Enter confirms a move • R restarts • M mutes sound.
Mouse: drag any knot. Touch: drag any knot. A move counts only when you drop a knot in a new spot.
Tip: start with knots on the outside, work the worst crossings first, and leave room around busy knots.

Level 1: 6 knots • Level 2: 8 knots • Level 3: 10 knots • then endless tangled gardens.

No timers, no rush — just careful untangling. Sound starts only after you press Start.
