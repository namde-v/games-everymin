🏭 JUNKYARD TYCOON

Turn other people's trash into your fortune. No two days — and no two scrap piles — are the same.

📖 How to Play

  • Buy a scrap lot ($25) — 6 mystery items roll onto the conveyor belt.
  • Sort before it falls off! Tap the right bin, or press 1-4 / M-P-G-E. Correct sort = refined goods + points. Wrong sort or missed item = lose reputation.
  • Sell refined goods when prices are high. Prices change every day — E-Waste pays best, Glass pays least.
  • Grow: Buy the Crusher ($80) for +50% sale value, then a second Truck upgrade ($120) for cheaper lots and bonus items.
  • Each day costs $15 rent when you press End Day. Keep sorting and selling to stay alive.

🏆 Win / 💥 Lose

WIN: Reach $1,000 cash at any time. LOSE: Lose all 3 reputation hearts, go broke with nothing to sell, or reach Day 15 without $1,000.

Tip: Space sorts the current item correctly only if you have studied it — actually, Space is a hint: it flashes the correct bin for 1 second, but costs 5 points. Use it when stuck!
