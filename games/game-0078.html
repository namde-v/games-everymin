How to Play

  • Goal: Paddle 1500 m down the rapids and cross the finish line. Pass through the yellow gates for bonus points.
  • Beat paddling: A beat pulses about every 0.8 seconds. Press SPACE, click, or tap inside the green beat window for a Perfect Stroke — big speed boost and +25 points.
  • Off-beat strokes still move you, but slowly. Spamming without rhythm wastes energy and slows the kayak.
  • Steering: ← → or A D to steer, move the mouse, or drag a finger across the river.
  • Danger: Hitting a rock costs 1 life. Lose all 3 lives and the run is over. Missing a gate costs no life, but costs bonus points.
  • Combos: Chain perfect strokes and clean gates to build a combo multiplier up to ×5.

Keyboard, mouse, and touch all work. Sound is generated on your device only after you press Start.
