STARLIGHT SNIPER

Precision / Constellation Aim. Falling stars carry the sky's lost patterns. Shoot them in the right order before they fade past the horizon.

How to Play
  • Goal: Complete all 3 constellations in order: Little Dipper, Lyra, and Orion's Path.
  • Trace in order: Only the bright pulsing star counts. It is connected by a dotted guide line. Shooting other stars wastes a shot.
  • Wind drift: A gust pushes every falling star sideways. Watch the wind arrow and lead your shot — stars curve as they fall.
  • Limits: You have 42 shots and 3 lives. A target star that hits the ground costs 1 life.
  • Scoring: +100 per correct star, +25 speed bonus if caught high in the sky, +500 per finished constellation.
  • Controls: Mouse move to aim, click to shoot. Touch: drag to aim, tap to shoot. Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD to aim, Space or Enter to shoot, R to restart.

Tip: do not rush the first shot. Let the star drift into open sky, then lead it slightly into the wind.

You Win

Press R for an instant restart at any time.

Wind: calm — watch the arrow at the top of the sky.
