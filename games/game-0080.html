❄️ Glacier Gardener 🌱

Strategy / Ice Farm Sim
Can you grow food where winter never ends?

🌟 How to Play

  • Goal — Win: Harvest crops to reach 200 points before you run out of lives.
  • 1. Thaw: Frozen blue plots cannot grow anything. Use Thaw (costs 12 Heat) to turn one into dark soil.
  • 2. Plant: Pick Frostberry, Ice Lettuce, or Snow Potato, then tap a soil plot. Faster crops are worth less; slower crops are worth more.
  • 3. Water: Water a growing crop (costs 10 Water) to give it a big growth boost. Heat and Water slowly refill on their own.
  • 4. Harvest: When a plot turns golden and says READY, harvest it for points. Do not leave it out in a frost wave.
  • 5. Survive frost waves: A wave hits every 30 seconds. Turn on the Heat Shield (30 Heat) before it hits to protect everything, or risk losing crops. Losing 2 or more crops in one wave costs 1 life.
  • Game Over: Lose all 3 lives and the glacier wins. Win or lose, Restart is instant.

🎮 Controls

Mouse: click tools, crops, and plots. Touch: tap the same things. Keyboard: 1–4 tools, Q/W/E crops, Arrow keys to move the yellow selection, Space or Enter to use the selected tool, H for shield. Sound is procedural and starts only after you press Start.
