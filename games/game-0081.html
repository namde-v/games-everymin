ASHEN AVIATOR

Smoke-Ring Flight over a burning valley

How to Play: You are a lone glider pilot threading rising smoke rings above a wildfire valley. Your glider constantly sinks — hold thrust to climb, release to glide down. Thread the centre of each glowing smoke ring for points. Clip a ring edge, miss a ring entirely, or fly into a drifting smoke plume and you lose a life. Clear all 24 rings to win before your lives run out.

  • Climb / thrust: Hold Up Arrow or W, or hold the mouse button, or hold a finger on the screen.
  • Descend / glide: Release all thrust — gravity pulls you down gently.
  • Steer: Left / Right arrows or A / D nudge you forward and back; the glider otherwise drifts steadily onward.
  • Score: Perfect centre = 100 pts, clean pass = 60 pts, near miss through the edge = 20 pts. Combo bonus grows with consecutive clean rings.
  • Danger: Smoke plumes drift across the sky — avoid them. Missing a ring costs a life just like hitting smoke does.
  • Pause: Press P or Space to pause / resume.

Goal: clear 24 rings with 3 lives. Good luck, aviator.

Keyboard: Up / W thrust  •  Mouse: hold to climb  •  Touch: hold screen to climb
