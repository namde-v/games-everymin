Sundial Sleuth

The old garden hides nine sundials. Each dial casts a shadow toward one of eight numbered markers around its rim. The clue tablet tells you the true number each shadow must point to.

  • Goal: Rotate every dial so its shadow points at its clue number before you run out of rotations.
  • Rotate: Click or tap a dial to turn it 45° clockwise. Each turn costs one rotation.
  • Clues: The brass number under each dial is its target. A green ring means that dial is solved.
  • Deduction tip: Count how many turns a dial needs — a dial pointing at 2 that must reach 6 needs exactly 4 turns. Spend rotations only where they count.
  • Keyboard: ← ↑ ↓ → to select a dial, Enter or Space to rotate it.
  • Mouse / Touch: Click or tap any dial to rotate it.

You have 45 rotations and 3 attempts. A wrong full check is not needed — every wasted turn matters.
