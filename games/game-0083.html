Welcome, Keeper! Press Start when you are ready to route your bees.

How to Play

  • Goal: Collect 240 honey before the end of Day 12. Winter arrives on Day 13 — if you are short, the colony loses.
  • Route bees: Click / tap a flower patch, then click / tap a bee (or press 1–5 to send that many bees to the selected patch). Click the hive to recall bees.
  • Think in trade-offs: Clover is close and safe but low-yield. Sunflowers pay well but take longer. Wild lavender is rich but far — and a patch can be depleted or hit by a storm.
  • Advance the day: Press Space / Enter or the Advance Day button. Assigned bees fly out, gather, and return honey at day's end. Unassigned bees rest and gather a little from the garden.
  • Weather & events: Each morning brings a forecast — rain slows far patches, a bloom doubles one patch, a cold snap cuts yields. Read the morning report before you assign!
  • Win: Reach 240 honey. Game Over: Winter arrives while you are still short. Restart instantly any time.

Controls: Mouse click · Touch tap · Keys 1–5 assign bees · Space/Enter advance day · R restart · H help.
