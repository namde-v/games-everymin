CYMBAL STORM

Rhythm / Percussion Timing — Feel the beat. Strike the crash.

How to Play

  • Cymbal crashes fall down two lanes in sync with a procedural beat.
  • Strike each cymbal exactly as it crosses the glowing hit line near the bottom.
  • Perfect (dead centre) = 300 pts · Good = 150 pts · Early / Late = 50 pts.
  • Consecutive hits build your combo — every 10 combo adds a bonus. A miss breaks the combo and costs a life.
  • Win: survive the whole song and reach the final crash. Game Over: lose all 5 lives.
  • Controls: D or J = left lane, F or K = right lane. Or click / tap the left or right lane. Space also restarts after a game ends.
Audio starts only after you press Start. Headphones recommended. No downloads needed.
