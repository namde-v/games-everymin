⚓ ANCHOR AIM

Toss your anchor across the harbour and drop it inside the moving target rings. Chain length and wind bend every throw — judge both, or splash out!

How to Play:
• Mouse / Touch: press and drag anywhere to aim the arc, release to throw.
• Arrow Keys: Left / Right change angle, Up / Down change power.
• Space: throw the anchor. R: restart instantly.
• Land in the centre ring for 100 points, middle ring for 50, outer ring for 25.
• Wind pushes the anchor sideways mid-flight. Reach 300 points in 12 throws to win!

Best played with sound on — audio starts only after you press Start.
