🦀 Tidepool Tumble

Steer your crab along the shore and catch the pearls tumbling down from the waves!

How to Play:
• Move: Arrow Keys or A / D to scuttle left and right.
• Mouse / Touch: Drag (or move the pointer) to steer the crab.
• White pearls = 10 points. Pink pearls = 25 points.
• Golden pearls = 50 points + a bonus life (max 5).
• Jellyfish sting! Lose a life if one lands on you.
• Catch a starfish for a brief slow-motion tide that calms the fall.
• Reach 300 points to win. Lose all lives and the game is over.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
