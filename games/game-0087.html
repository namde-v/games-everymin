Span Solver

Connect every island with bridges. The number on an island is exactly how many bridges must touch it. Build one single connected network — no crossings, no loose ends — to win.

  • How to play: Click or tap an island, then click or tap a second island in the same row or column to add a bridge. Click the same pair again to cycle 1 bridge → 2 bridges → no bridge. Drag from one island to another works too.
  • Rules: At most 2 bridges between a pair. A bridge may not pass through another island and may not cross another bridge. Green = island complete, red = too many bridges, white = still needs bridges.
  • Controls: Mouse / touch click or drag between islands. ↑ ↓ ← → select an island, Enter / Space adds a bridge in the last arrow direction, U undo, R restart, G give up.
  • Win: Every island shows its exact number and all islands are joined into one network. Game Over: Press Give Up (or run out of ideas) and the puzzle ends — then restart instantly.

One hand-crafted puzzle. No timers, no luck — pure logic.

You Win!
