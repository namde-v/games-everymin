🌬️ Windmill Warden

Build a wind farm, chase the shifting wind, and keep the lights on for a full week. Place turbines, rotate them into the wind, repair storm damage, and meet each day's grid demand before stability collapses.

  • Goal: Survive 7 days meeting grid demand. If grid stability hits 0%, the grid collapses and the game ends.
  • Build: Pick a turbine with keys 1 2 3, then click / tap an empty green plot to place it. Click a placed turbine to rotate it 45° toward the wind.
  • Wind matters: A turbine facing into the wind makes full power. Facing away makes almost nothing. Watch the wind arrow and compass — the wind shifts every few seconds.
  • Repair: Storms damage turbines (red, smoking). Press R for Repair mode, then click a damaged turbine (costs 30). Press R again to return to Build mode.
  • Days: Each day lasts 45 seconds. Press N or Next Day to end a day early — but only if you are meeting demand, or stability will fall.
  • Turbines: 1 = Breeze (cost 60, 18 MW) · 2 = Gale (cost 120, 38 MW) · 3 = Tempest (cost 220, 70 MW). Bigger turbines need better alignment to pay off.
  • Keyboard: 1 2 3 select turbine · R repair · N next day · Enter start / restart.
  • Mouse / Touch: Click or tap plots to place, rotate, or repair. Works on phone, tablet, and desktop.

Tip: spread turbines out, keep one spare budget for repairs, and rotate fast when the wind swings!

Welcome, Warden! Press Start, then place your first turbine. The town needs 60 MW on Day 1.

How to Play

Controls — Mouse / Touch: click or tap a plot to place the selected turbine, click a turbine to rotate it, and in Repair mode click a damaged turbine to fix it. Keyboard: 1–3 select turbine, R repair mode, N next day, Enter start / restart.

Power: output = turbine size × wind speed × alignment. Perfect alignment (facing into the wind) gives 100%; sideways gives about 50%; facing backwards gives about 10%. Demand rises each day: 60, 80, 105, 130, 160, 195, 235 MW. Meeting demand earns score and slowly restores stability; falling short drains stability, and overbuilding wastes budget you need for storm repairs.

Storms: from Day 2 onward, storms can strike, damaging 1–3 turbines and shifting the wind violently. Damaged turbines produce zero power until repaired. Win by finishing Day 7 with stability above 0%. Game Over if stability reaches 0% at any time.
