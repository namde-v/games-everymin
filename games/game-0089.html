How to Play

  • Listen: The frost flute plays a short melody. Watch the pads light up and listen to each note.
  • Replay: When it says “Your turn!”, tap the pads in the exact same order.
  • Grow: Every round adds one more note. Round 1 has 3 notes; later rounds get longer and faster.
  • Score: Earn 10 points per correct note, plus a round bonus of 25 × round number.
  • Lives: A wrong note costs one frost heart. Lose all 3 lives and the melody freezes — game over.
  • Win: Complete all 10 rounds to melt the winter spell and win!

Controls: Number keys 1–4 or letter keys A / S / D / F play the four notes. You can also click a pad with the mouse or tap a pad on touch screens. Press Enter to start or restart, R to replay the current melody (costs 5 points, once per round), and M to mute sound.

Tip: hum the melody or count the flashes. Rhythm helps memory!
