🏰 Castle Cartographer

Map a kingdom, one tile at a time.

How to Play:

  • You are given a deck of 28 kingdom tiles. One tile is drawn at a time and shown in the Current Tile panel.
  • Place each tile on an empty square of the 6 by 6 map grid. Every placed tile uses one card from the deck.
  • Score points for matching terrain adjacencies: Forest next to River, Castle next to Road, Castle next to Field, and same-terrain neighbours. See the scoring list beside the map.
  • Rotate a tile before placing it: its facing arrow matters. If it faces a matching neighbour, you earn a +2 facing bonus.
  • Reach 60 points before the deck runs out to WIN. If the deck ends and you are short of 60, the game is OVER.
  • Plan ahead: early tiles in the centre give you more neighbours later. Corners give fewer scoring chances.

Controls: Mouse / Touch — click or tap a grid cell to place the current tile, tap the Current Tile panel to rotate it. Keyboard — Arrow Keys move the selected cell, Enter or Space places the tile, R rotates the tile.

Sound is procedural Web Audio and starts only after you press Start.
