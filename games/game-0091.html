QUARRY QUAKE

Rocks are tumbling down the quarry walls! Dodge the boulders, collect sparkling gems, and reach 500 points to win.

How to Play
← → or A / D — move left and right
Mouse / finger — drag to move the miner
◆ Gem = +25 points  |  ⬢ Rock hit = lose 1 life
You have 3 lives. The quake speeds up as you score!
