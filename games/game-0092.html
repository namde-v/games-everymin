How to Play

Click or tap a mirror to rotate it. Use Arrow Keys to move the yellow cursor and Space or Enter to rotate the selected mirror. The red emitter fires a beam. Mirrors bend it: / and \ reflect in different directions. Guide the beam into the green target before you run out of moves.

Every rotation costs one move. Finish a level to earn points plus a bonus for moves left. Clear all 5 levels to win.

Keyboard: Arrows move, Space rotates, R restarts level. Mouse and touch work too. Fully offline.
