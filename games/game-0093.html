⚓ QUAY COMMANDER

Ships are arriving at your quay! Assign each ship to a dock big enough for its cargo before its deadline runs out. Loaded ships pay coins. Miss too many, and the harbour closes.

How to Play
• Mouse / Touch: Tap a ship in the sea, then tap an empty dock to assign it.
• Keyboard: Press 1–5 to select a ship, A / S / D to send it to Dock 1 / 2 / 3, U to upgrade the selected dock.
• Tap Upgrade under a dock (50 coins) to raise its capacity by 2.
• A dock cannot take cargo larger than its capacity. Bigger ships pay more!
• WIN: Earn 1000 coins within 5 days. LOSE: 5 ships depart unserved, or Day 5 ends under 1000 coins.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Works fully offline.

Game Over

Press R or Enter to restart instantly.
