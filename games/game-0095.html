Gravity Gauntlet

Slingshot a probe around gravity wells and strike the golden target. Bend your path, beat all 5 levels, and do not waste a probe.

How to Play:
• Drag from the launch pad to aim — pull back like a slingshot, release to fire.
• Gravity wells bend your flight. Use them to curve around obstacles.
• Hit the gold target to clear the level. Miss, fly off-screen, or stall and you lose a probe.
• Keyboard: Arrow keys adjust aim, Space fires, R restarts. Mouse and touch dragging also work.
