Ride the wind! Catch sky lanterns, surf the updrafts, and dodge storm clouds and birds.

How to Play

  • Move: Arrow keys / WASD, or drag with mouse / touch. The kite follows your pointer.
  • Collect: Golden sky lanterns give 10 points. Collect 40 lanterns to win.
  • Ride: Pale blue updraft columns lift you gently and give bonus points while inside.
  • Dodge: Storm clouds and birds cost 1 life. 3 crashes = Game Over.
  • Wind: Gusts push you sideways — fight them, or let them carry you to a lantern.

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads needed — fully offline.

You Win!

Press R or tap Restart to fly again.
