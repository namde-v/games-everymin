A seal of four sigils blocks the vault.

The vault keeper hid a secret sequence of 4 sigils, chosen from the six ancient marks below. Sigils may repeat. You have 10 guesses to deduce it.

How to Play

  • Tap a sigil in the palette to place it in the next empty slot, or tap a filled slot to clear it.
  • Press Submit Guess when all four slots are filled.
  • After each guess, gold dots mean a sigil is correct and in the right place. Silver dots mean a sigil is in the code but in the wrong place.
  • Use the clues to narrow the code down. Solve all four sigils to win before your guesses run out.
  • Keyboard: keys 1–6 place sigils, Enter submits, Backspace clears, R restarts.

Mouse, touch, and keyboard all work. Sound begins only after you press Start.
