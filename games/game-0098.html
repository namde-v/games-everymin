🍇 Vineyard Vizier

Goal: Earn 1500 coins in 4 seasons. If vineyard health falls to 0, the vineyard collapses.

How to Play

  • Plant: pick Merlot, Chardonnay, Syrah or Riesling, then tap an empty brown plot. Planting costs coins.
  • Water: watered vines grow faster. Each watering costs 5 water from your seasonal budget.
  • Prune: pruning improves wine quality and restores a little health. Do it while vines grow.
  • Harvest: when a vine is purple and ripe, harvest it into wine stock. Unpicked vines wither at season end.
  • Sell Wine: turn wine stock into coins. Sell often so you can afford more vines.
  • Seasons: each season lasts 45 seconds or ends early with End Season. Water refills each season.
  • Danger: too many thirsty vines drain health. Water, prune, and harvest on time.
