Welcome, Piper!

The magical pipes sing a melody. Watch the pipes glow and listen to their notes, then repeat the exact sequence by tapping the pipes. Each round adds more notes. Complete 8 rounds to win. Three wrong notes and the melody fades.

Tip: Play steadily — fast, correct replies earn rhythm bonus points.

How to Play

  1. Press Start Game. The pipes play a short melody.
  2. When the prompt says Your turn, tap the pipes in the same order.
  3. Use the mouse, touch, or keys 1–5 / A S D F G.
  4. Correct notes score points. A wrong note costs one life.
  5. Finish round 8 to win. Lose 3 lives and it is game over.
1 / A = Pipe 12 / S = Pipe 23 / D = Pipe 34 / F = Pipe 45 / G = Pipe 5
