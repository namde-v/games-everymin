Climb 100 heights to the golden summit. Grab stars, ride moving ledges, and do not fall!

How to Play
Move: Arrow keys / A D, or drag with mouse / finger.
Jump: Space, Arrow Up, W, click, or tap.
Land on platforms to keep climbing. Each platform is higher than the last.
Collect stars for bonus points. Reach Height 100 to win.
Falling below the screen costs a life. You have 3 lives.

Tip: steer in mid-air. Moving gold platforms drift sideways - time your landing. The summit platform is wide and golden with a flag.
