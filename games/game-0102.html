How to Play

You are the locksmith. Each ledger is sealed by a secret 4-digit combination. Use the written clues to work out the digits — no guessing wildly, or the lock will seize up.

  • Select a dial: click or tap it, or use ← / → arrow keys.
  • Change a digit: click the dial’s arrows, tap the digit, or press ↑ / ↓ or + / -.
  • Test your guess: press the Test Combination button or Enter.
  • After each test you learn how many digits are correct and in place and how many are correct but misplaced.
  • Each lock gives you 8 guesses. Solve all 3 locks to win. Running out of guesses on any lock ends the game.

Tip: Write the clues down mentally — a digit that is “in place” in one guess can help you eliminate whole families of combinations.
