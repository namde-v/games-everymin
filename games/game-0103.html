🔦 BEACON BUILDER

How to Play

  • Goal: Cover every dark rock on the shipping lanes with lighthouse light. Survive 3 waves to win.
  • Place: Tap / click a sandy land cell to build a lighthouse. It costs 35 gold and lights a wide circle. Click a lighthouse to remove it and refund 20 gold.
  • Lanes: Blue routes cross the sea. Dark rocks sit on them. A ship that reaches an unlit rock crashes — you lose a life.
  • Waves: When ready, press Start Wave. Ships sail every lane. Safe ships earn score and gold. Finish Wave 3 to win.
  • Tips: Overlap wastes gold — spread lights to cover more rocks. Rocks already lit glow gold. Watch coverage % in the HUD.
  • Controls: Mouse click / touch tap to place. W Start Wave, R Restart, 1 place mode, Esc back to title.

100% offline — no downloads, no accounts. Sound starts only after you press Start.

🏆 YOU WIN!
