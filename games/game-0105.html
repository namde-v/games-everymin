⚓ ANCHOR AIM

Precision / Pendulum Drop

Time the swing. Drop the anchor. Sink it dead-centre.

How to play: A pendulum swings above the sea. Press Space, click, or tap to release the anchor.

Score: The closer to the target centre, the more points — up to 100 per drop. Targets drift and shrink every round.

Watch the wind: Wind in the HUD slightly speeds up or slows down the swing.

Win: Hit all 10 targets. Lose: 3 misses and the game is over.

Space / Click / Tap = Release  •  R = Restart after game over
