Spore Sprinter

Run 1000 metres through the enchanted forest!

Collect glowing spores for points, leap over thorn bushes, and glide across the gaps. Every step gets faster — stay sharp!

How to Play:
Jump: Arrow Up / W / Space, mouse click, or screen tap.
Double-Jump: Press jump again while in the air.
Glide: HOLD the key, mouse button, or finger while falling to float down on a leaf.
Goal: Reach 1000 m to WIN. Thorn bushes and falls cost a life. 3 hits = Game Over.

Tip: glide early over wide gaps, and grab spore trails for bonus points.
