CIRCUIT COURIER

Deliver the signal. Do not short the circuit.

How to Play: A glowing signal must travel from the green START chip to the gold TARGET chip. Click or tap a cell next to the wire tip to extend the wire. Click any earlier wire cell to rewind back to it. Wires may never cross themselves and may never pass through grey blocked cells.

Each level has a maximum wire length. Reach the target before you run out of wire. Complete all 5 circuits to win. Running out of wire costs one retry.

Mouse / Touch: tap a neighbouring cell  |  Keyboard: ↑↓←→ move cursor, Space place wire, U undo, R retry level
