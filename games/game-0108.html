Oasis Overseer

How to Play: You are the Overseer of a desert oasis village. Over 10 days you must gather 60 food to meet the village quota.

Each day you receive a limited supply of water (usually 12 units). Share it between the Date Palms (need 4, yield 3), the Vegetable Plots (need 5, yield 5), and the Goat Herd (need 3, yield 2). A sector watered below its need loses health and yields less; a fully watered sector thrives.

Beware random heatwaves (needs rise) and sandstorms (damage a sector). If village Morale falls to zero, or the quota is missed on Day 10, it is Game Over. Meet the quota and you Win!

Click or tap the + / − buttons, or use keys 1–4. Press Start when you are ready.

Mouse, touch, and keyboard all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.
