Starlight Sonata

A gentle rhythm game under a midnight sky. Golden notes fall in five lanes toward the glowing keyboard line. Strike each note exactly as it lands to keep the sonata alive.

How to Play
Keyboard: 1 2 3 4 5 or A S D F G
Mouse: click a piano key below the canvas.
Touch: tap a piano key or tap its lane on the canvas.
Hit notes near the line for Perfect or Good points. Misses drain energy.
Finish all 60 notes with energy left to win!

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads, no connection needed.
