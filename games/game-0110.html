Canyon Catapult

Your mission: catapult stones across the canyon and hit every moving target platform in all 3 rounds. You have a limited number of stones each round. Run out of stones before clearing the targets and the game is over.

How to Play

🎯 Angle dials: Up / Down arrows, or the Angle slider, or drag on the sky — dragging up raises the angle, dragging right raises the power.

💪 Power dials: Left / Right arrows, or the Power slider.

🚀 Launch: Press Space, Enter, or tap the LAUNCH button.

🌬️ Wind: Watch the HUD. A right wind pushes the stone right; a left wind pulls it back. Aim into the wind on strong days.

🖱️ Mouse / touch: Drag anywhere on the canvas to tune angle and power, then release to keep the setting. Click LAUNCH to fire — this is a dial-aim game, not a pull-back slingshot.

🏆 Scoring: Bullseye centre = 150, platform edge = 100, plus a round-clear bonus. Misses cost one stone.
