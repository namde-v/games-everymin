KELP KEEPER

Swim the reef, gather the pearls, and keep your air tank full. The deep gets hungrier the longer you stay down!

How to Play
• Move: Arrow keys / WASD, mouse (diver follows pointer), or drag on touch screens.
• Collect: Pearls (+10 points, +1 pearl). Bubbles refill your air.
• Avoid: Jellyfish sting you (lose a life) and sharks are deadly (lose a life and clear nearby threats).
• Win: Collect 100 pearls. Lose: Run out of lives or air.
• Air drains slowly and faster at depth; grab bubbles and stay shallow to breathe easier.

Space or Enter also starts. P pauses.
