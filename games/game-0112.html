Mirror Maze

Guide the laser from the glowing emitter to the star target by rotating mirrors on the grid. Every rotation costs a move — plan before you spin!

How to Play
• Tap / click a mirror to rotate it 90 degrees. It flips between / and \.
• Arrow keys move the yellow cursor, Space / Enter rotates the selected mirror.
• Press Fire Laser (or F) to test your path. A red beam that misses costs nothing — but running out of moves does.
• Hit the target to win the level and earn 100 points + 25 per move left.
• Walls block the beam. The beam stops at the board edge, a wall, or the target.
• R restarts the current level. Finish all 8 levels to win the game!

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads, fully offline.
