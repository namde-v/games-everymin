Vineyard Vintner

You inherited six vineyard plots and $500. Grow grapes across 8 seasons, turn them into wine, and earn $3,000 in total wine income before the final season ends. Spend too much and go bankrupt, or let the soil collapse, and the vineyard is lost.

How to Play

  • Tap / click a plot to select it (or press 1–6).
  • Plant ($20): choose a grape in an empty plot. Different grapes ripen at different speeds and prices.
  • Water ($5): speeds growth and restores a little soil. Prune ($8): raises quality a lot, slows growth slightly.
  • Harvest: only when a vine is ripe. Ripe grapes rot if left for another season — harvest promptly!
  • Sell Wine: converts all stored grapes to money at today's market price, boosted by quality and season.
  • Next Season: advances time. Crops grow, soil recovers or degrades, weather and prices change. Autumn harvests pay best; winter kills unharvested vines.
  • Watch soil: planting and harvesting wear soil out. Resting a plot for a season restores it. Soil below 30% halves your yield.
  • Win: $3,000 total wine income. Lose: money below $0 with nothing to sell, or 8 seasons pass without reaching the target.

Controls: Mouse, touch, and keyboard all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.
