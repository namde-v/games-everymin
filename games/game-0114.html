Beat Bazaar

Customers are lining up at your market stall, and each one wants a dish served exactly on the beat. Tap the right lane as each order note hits the serving line!

How to Play:
🍎 D / F / J / K or tap a lane — catch notes in the gold zone.
🥁 Notes fall in 4 lanes: Fruit, Spice, Bread, Sweets.
⭐ Perfect = 100 pts, Good = 50, Miss = lose a customer.
🔥 Chain hits to build your combo multiplier.
❤️ Lose 5 customers and your stall closes. Serve all 60 orders to WIN!

Sound starts only after you press Start. Headphones recommended!
