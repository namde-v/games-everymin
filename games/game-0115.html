☄️ COMET CATCHER

Orbit the planet. Time the catch. Do not let the comets slip past.

How to Play
• Your catcher automatically orbits the planet on the glowing ring.
• Comets streak across the sky. Each one crosses the ring at one flashing point.
• Press SPACE, click CATCH, or tap the screen at the exact moment the comet reaches the ring.
• Perfect timing (very close) = 100 pts. Good timing = 50 pts. A near graze = 20 pts.
• Miss a comet, or catch too early / too late, and you lose a life. Catch 15 comets to win. 3 misses ends the game.
• Watch the comet's trail and the pulsing target marker — the shrinking timing ring shows how close you are.

Keyboard: SPACE / ENTER to catch  •  Mouse: click  •  Touch: tap anywhere

You Win!

Tip: catch just as the comet's head touches the orbit ring — not before, not after.
