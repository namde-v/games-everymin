STORM SURFER

Ride a procedurally generated storm wave! Jump the ramps, dodge lightning strikes and floating debris, and collect 25 storm crystals to win.

How to Play
• Move: ← → or A / D to steer, or drag / tap sides on touch
• Jump / Trick: ↑, W, Space, mouse click, or tap the board — jump ramps for bonus points
• Duck / Dive: ↓ or S to duck under lightning arcs
• Collect: glowing cyan crystals (+100 pts). Ramp tricks: +150 pts. Near-miss lightning: +50 pts.
• Avoid: debris, lightning bolts, and whirlpools. You have 3 lives. Hitting an obstacle costs 1 life and brief invincibility.
• Win: collect 25 crystals. Lose: lose all 3 lives.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Works fully offline.
