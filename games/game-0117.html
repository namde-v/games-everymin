Read the runes. Remember the riddle. Carve the answer.

How to Play

  • Watch: the ancient stones light up in a secret order. Memorize it!
  • Repeat: tap the runes in the same order to solve the riddle.
  • Logic twists: from level 3, riddles add rules — sometimes you must play the sequence backwards, sometimes only the even positions, sometimes the odd positions. The riddle banner tells you the rule.
  • Lives: a wrong rune costs 1 life. You have 3 lives per run.
  • Score: +10 per correct rune, +50 level bonus, +25 speed bonus if you finish a level without a mistake. Complete all 10 levels to win!
  • Controls: click / tap a rune, or press keys 1–8 to pick a rune, R to restart, Enter to start.
