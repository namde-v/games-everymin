How to Play

Ships arrive carrying Grain, Electronics, Oil, Cars or Medicine. Each has a fee, a cargo size (loading time), and a deadline. Dock a ship before its deadline, let the crane load it, and collect the fee. A ship that waits too long — in queue or at a dock — leaves angry and costs 15 reputation. Use a crane boost to load a docked ship twice as fast. Reach $6000 profit before the 180-second shift ends to win. If reputation hits 0, the port closes and you lose.

Mouse / Touch: tap a ship card, then tap a dock. Keyboard: Up/Down or 1–5 to pick a ship, A/B/C to pick a dock, Enter to assign, Space to boost the selected dock, R to restart after the game ends.

⚓ HARBOR MASTER

Strategy / Port Logistics

You are the harbor master for one busy shift. Ships are lining up, deadlines are ticking, and only three docks stand between order and chaos.

How to Play

  • Select an incoming ship, then assign it to a free dock.
  • Watch its loading bar and its deadline countdown.
  • Collect the fee when loading finishes. Serve urgent ships first.
  • Spend a crane boost to load twice as fast — you only have 3.
  • Win at $6000 profit before 180 seconds. Lose if reputation hits 0.

Controls: Mouse, touch, or keyboard (1–5 select ship, A/B/C select dock, Enter assign, Space boost).

No downloads, no network — the whole port runs in this page.
