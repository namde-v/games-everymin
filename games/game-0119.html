Welcome to the Concert!

The ensemble plays a melody phrase. Watch the lights, listen to the notes, then repeat the exact same order by tapping the instruments.

How to Play
  • Watch & listen: the ensemble plays a phrase, one instrument at a time.
  • Repeat it: tap / click the instruments in the same order, or press keys 1–4.
  • Phrases grow: each round adds one more note. Finish all 6 songs on the setlist to win.
  • Wrong note: costs one life. You have 3 lives. A perfect round earns bonus points.
  • Controls: Mouse click, touch tap, or keyboard 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 (and Q / W / E / R).

Tip: hum the phrase as it plays — rhythm helps memory more than speed.
