Ricochet Range

One ball. Four walls. A room full of targets. Bend every shot off the walls and clear the range before your shots run out.

How to Play
• Drag from the launcher (mouse or finger) to aim — longer drag = more power.
• Release to fire. The ball ricochets off walls and obstacles.
• Hit every target to clear the range. Fewer shots = bonus points.
• Keyboard: Arrow keys aim, hold Space to charge, release Space to fire. R restarts.
• Bonus points for every wall bounce before a hit. Clear all 5 ranges to win!
