🌊 TIDE TURNER

Storm water is rising at the source spring. Twist every tide-gate so the flow reaches the harbor before the tide timer runs out.

How to Play:
• Click / tap a tile to rotate it 90° clockwise.
• Arrow keys move the glowing selector, Space rotates the selected tile.
• Blue spring (left) and golden harbor (right) never rotate.
• Connect a continuous pipe path from spring to harbor to clear the level.
• Beat the timer to bank bonus points. You have 3 lives across 5 levels.

Keyboard, mouse and touch supported. Sound starts after you press Start.
