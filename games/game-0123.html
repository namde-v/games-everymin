Starship Steward

How to Play: You command a starship with four crew members and four stations. Each turn a crisis strikes one station. Assign enough crew to that station before you resolve the event.

  • Tap a crew member, then tap a station — or select a crew member and press keys 1–4.
  • A specialist on their home station counts double. Unassigned crew cannot help.
  • Meet the event demand to gain points. Fall short and the hull takes damage.
  • Survive all 10 events to win. If hull reaches 0%, the ship is lost.
  • Press N (or Resolve Event) when your assignments are ready. Press R to recall everyone.

Crew: Ada loves Engines, Boris loves Shields, Cleo loves Science, Dex loves Medbay.
