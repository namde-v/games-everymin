🧲 MAGNET MINER

Swing the crane. Flip the magnet. Fill the cart!

How to Play: The magnet crane swings back and forth above the mine floor. Ore chunks sit below, each marked + or −. Your magnet only attracts opposite polarity — flip it until it is opposite to the ore you want, then swing over that ore to grab it.

While carrying ore, flip polarity again to repel and drop it. Time the drop so the ore falls into the moving cart. Drop 12 ore into the cart to win. 3 missed drops end the run.

Space / Click / Tap — Flip polarity (also drops carried ore)
← → / A D — Nudge the swing  •  R — Restart
On-screen buttons work with mouse and touch too.

Red ore = +, blue ore = −  •  Gold bonus ore is worth 50 points  •  Cart moves — watch its position!

GAME OVER

Tip: grab ore near the centre of its swing arc, then wait until the magnet is directly above the cart before flipping to drop.
