Glyph Grader

How to Play

  • Glyphs fall from the top of the board, one at a time.
  • Each round shows a NEW logic rule: sort by shape, by colour, by number of sides, by dots inside, or by a mixed rule.
  • Pick the matching bin below: click or tap it, or use Left / Right arrows to highlight a bin and press Enter (or keys 1–4) to sort.
  • Correct sort: +100 points. Wrong sort: lose a life and the glyph is lost.
  • Sort all 8 glyphs in a round to advance. Clear all 5 rounds to win. Lose all 3 lives and the game ends.
  • Bonus: sorting 3 in a row without a mistake earns +50 streak bonus.

Keyboard: ← → to select, Enter to sort, 1–4 for bins, R to restart.
