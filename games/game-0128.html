Bakery Baron

How to Play: You run a bakery for 7 days. Start with 120 coins and 50 reputation. Buy ingredient packs, bake bread, cake and cookies, set a fair price, and serve customers before they storm off.

  • Each served customer pays your price (cakes pay double) and raises reputation.
  • Each customer who leaves unserved costs reputation. Reputation 0 = Game Over.
  • Coins below 0 = bankruptcy = Game Over.
  • Win: finish Day 7 with at least 300 coins and reputation above 0.
  • Rent of 10 coins is charged at the end of each day — plan ahead!

Click / tap to play. Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline.
