Chime Chase

The bells are singing — can you sing them back? In each round, the bells play a secret melody. Listen carefully, watch them glow, then repeat the exact same order.

  • Listen: Bells light up and chime one by one. No tapping yet!
  • Echo: Tap the bells or press keys 1–4 in the same order.
  • Grow: Every round adds a note. Round 1 has 3 notes; Round 8 has 10.
  • Score: +100 per correct note, +250 round bonus. Wrong note = lose a life.
  • Win: Finish all 8 rounds. Lose: Run out of 3 lives.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.

📖 How to Play

Goal: Echo the melody perfectly for 8 rounds.

  • Watch and listen while the bells play. Try humming it or counting the colours.
  • When the status says Your turn!, tap bells in the same order.
  • Gold = 1, Green = 2, Blue = 3, Purple = 4. You can also click or tap directly.
  • Stuck? Use Replay Melody once per round — it replays the tune without penalty.
  • Each wrong note costs a life, but the round restarts so you can try again.
  • Tip: chunk the melody — remember it as two small groups instead of one long string.
