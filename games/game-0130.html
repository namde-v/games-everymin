Pendulum Plunge

A plunger swings from the ceiling. Time your release and drop it dead-center on the moving targets below!

How to Play
• Space, click, or tap to release the plunger.
• Land on a target: center = 25 pts, inner ring = 15, outer = 10.
• Targets move faster and shrink in later rounds.
• Miss a target (or drop off the floor) and you lose a life.
• 3 misses ends the run. Reach 150 points to win!
• Bonus: hit 3 targets in a row for a +10 streak bonus.

Keyboard: Space to drop  |  R to restart  |  P to pause
