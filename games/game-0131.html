AVALANCHE ACE

Carve down the mountain! Thread every slalom gate for points, dodge rocks and pine trees, and reach the finish before you run out of lives.

How to Play:
⬅️ ➡️ Arrow keys or A / D — steer left / right
🖱️ Mouse move — steer toward the cursor
👆 Touch & drag — steer with your finger
Gate passed: +50 points  |  Missed gate: lose 1 life
Crash into rock/tree: lose 1 life
🏆 Win by reaching 1000 points!

Sound starts only after you press Start.
