CIPHER CIRCUIT

Power the grid! Click or tap a tile to rotate it. Keyboard: arrow keys / WASD to move the cursor, Space or R to rotate, Enter to start.

  • Goal: light up EVERY target lamp on the grid before moves or time run out.
  • Wires & corners carry power straight or around bends.
  • AND gates output power only when BOTH inputs are powered.
  • OR gates output power when ANY input is powered.
  • NOT gates flip the signal: an unpowered input gives a powered output.
  • Sources always output power. Targets must receive power to light.
  • Running out of moves or time on a level costs one life. Lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over. Clear all 5 levels to win.
