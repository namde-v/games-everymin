🌳 Orchard Overseer

How to Play: You manage a small orchard across four seasons. Click or tap a plot, then plant a tree. Water it through its growth stages, harvest ripe fruit, and earn coins. Meet each season's harvest quota before the days run out!

🍎 Apple costs 5 coins, sells for 4 each • 🍐 Pear costs 7, sells for 6 • 🍒 Cherry costs 10, sells for 9. Trees yield 2–4 fruit when ripe. Rainy days refill water. Miss a quota and the orchard fails!

Mouse / Touch: click a plot, then use the action buttons. Keyboard: Arrow keys move the cursor, A/P/C plant, W waters, H harvests, N advances a day, R restarts.
