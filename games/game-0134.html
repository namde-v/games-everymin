BEAT BEACON

Catch the light exactly on the beat.

How to Play
Five beacons glow around the ring. Each one flashes white on its beat and sends a pulse outward.
Tap or click the flashing beacon right as it flashes — not early, not late.
Perfect hits build your combo and score. A missed beat breaks your combo and costs a life.
Keyboard: 1–5 (or A S D F G) for beacons left to right, Space to start / restart.
Mouse: click a beacon. Touch: tap a beacon. Complete all 48 beats to win!

Headphones recommended. Sound starts only after you press Start.

You Win!

Tip: watch the expanding ring — the flash lands when the ring touches the beacon.
