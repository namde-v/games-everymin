INK INFILTRATOR

You are a night runner with a spray can. Dash through the city grid, paint every glowing target wall, and vanish before the patrolling guards spot you.

  • How to Play: Move with WASD / Arrow keys, mouse drag, or touch drag.
  • Spray: Stand beside a glowing target wall and press SPACE, click Spray, or tap the Spray button. Each target is worth 100 points.
  • Guards: Yellow cones are their vision. If you are inside a cone and close enough, you are caught and lose a life.
  • Win: Paint all targets in all 3 levels. Lose: Lose all 3 lives.
  • Tip: Walls block vision. Hide behind a building block to break a guard's line of sight.

Sound starts only after you press Start. No internet needed.
