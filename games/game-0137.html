Sigil Solver

An ancient seal hides a secret sequence of four runes. Your task is to deduce it by logic, not luck.

  • How to play: Build a four-rune guess from the palette, then submit it.
  • Clues: Green means the rune is correct and in the correct position. Gold means the rune is in the sigil but in another position.
  • Win: Solve all 3 rounds. Each round grows trickier and gives fewer spare guesses.
  • Lose: Run out of guesses in any round and the seal breaks.
  • Controls: Mouse click, touch, or keyboard keys 1–8, Backspace, and Enter.

Scoring: 100 points per solved round, plus 20 points for every unused guess. Hints cost 25 points but never reveal a position you already know.
