⚓ Dockside Director

You are the harbour master. Ships arrive with cargo that only fits one dock. Assign each ship to its matching berth before its departure timer runs out!

How to Play:
• A ship appears at sea with a coloured cargo badge: Containers (blue), Grain (yellow), Oil (red), Cars (green).
• Click / tap a ship to select it, then click its matching dock at the bottom to berth it.
• Berthed ships unload automatically — cranes and trucks do the work. On-time delivery earns coins!
• If a timer expires, the ship sails away angry and you lose reputation.
• Keyboard: ← → to pick a ship, 1–4 to send it to a dock, Space to start / restart.
• Win: serve 12 ships. Game Over: reputation hits 0.

Tip: serve a ship with lots of time left for a speed bonus. Do not send a ship to the wrong dock!
