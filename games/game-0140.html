CRANE COMMANDER

Precision Crane Stacking

Build a skyscraper, one swinging crate at a time. Time your release perfectly — a wobbly tower is a doomed tower.

  • How to play: The crane hook swings back and forth. Press SPACE, click, or tap to release the crate.
  • Perfect drop: Land near the centre for bonus points and a stability bonus.
  • Danger: A badly misaligned crate slides off and costs a life. Three misses and the site shuts down.
  • Wind: Each level brings a new wind gust that pushes falling crates sideways — aim upwind!
  • Win: Stack 12 crates high to finish the tower.
  • Controls: SPACE / ArrowDown to drop, R to restart, M to mute.

Keyboard, mouse and touch all work. No downloads — everything runs in this one file.
