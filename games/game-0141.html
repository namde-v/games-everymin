⛏️ METEOR MINER

Pilot your mining ship through a drifting asteroid field. Blast rocks with your laser, scoop up the glowing ore they drop, and dodge every collision. Fill the ore quota to clear each level!

How to Play
• Thrust: Arrow keys / WASD, or drag on touch screen
• Fire laser: Space, mouse click, or tap FIRE
• Aim: Mouse moves the ship's nose toward the cursor
• Collect: Fly over glowing ore chunks (+50 pts each)
• Survive: 3 lives. Hitting a big asteroid costs a life.
• Win: Clear all 5 levels by meeting every ore quota.

Tip: small asteroids are worth more ore but drift faster. Keep moving — standing still is a miner's worst enemy.
