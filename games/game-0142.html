🌾 Hex Harvest

How to Play
Swap two adjacent hex tiles to form a cluster of 3 or more matching crops. Every cluster is harvested: bigger clusters and chained cascades score bonus points. Reach the harvest quota of 70 crops before you run out of 25 moves to win!

Mouse / Touch: tap one hex, then tap an adjacent hex to swap.
Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD move the cursor, Space selects a hex, then an arrow key swaps in that direction. R restarts.
Watch for cascades — new crops fall in and can trigger chain harvests worth double points!

Tip: corner clusters are sneaky — a cluster can snake around the hex grid in any direction, not just a straight line.
