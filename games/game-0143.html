🏜️ DESERT DEPOT

How to Play: You run a desert trading post for 20 days. Travel between five oasis towns, buy goods where they are cheap and sell where they are dear. Every journey costs water and food, and prices shift every day. Reach 2000 gold to win! Run out of days, or strand your caravan without supplies and gold, and the desert wins.

🖱️ Click a town on the map or a travel button  |  ⌨️ Arrow keys select a town, Enter travels, B buys, S sells  |  👆 Touch works too.

Goods: Dates (cheap staple), Pottery (steady), Spices (volatile), Silk (rare and pricey). Watch the market table: green-ish cheap prices are bargains, high prices are selling chances. Use "Rest & Resupply" at any town to refill water and food for gold, and "Wait a Day" to let prices drift. Selling needs stock; buying needs gold. A caravan carries at most 40 units total.
