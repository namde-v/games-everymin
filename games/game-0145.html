Lantern Lift

Guide your hot-air balloon to the summit at 3000 metres. Burn to rise, vent to fall, and thread every wind gate at exactly the right altitude.

How to Play:
• HOLD Space / Up Arrow / mouse / touch to fire the burner and rise.
• RELEASE to cool down and descend gently.
• Fly through glowing wind gates (+100 pts) — missing one costs a life.
• Collect lanterns (+50 pts) and avoid storm clouds (−1 life).
• Reach 3000m to WIN. Lose all 3 lives and the flight ends.

Keyboard, mouse and touch all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.

You Win!

Tip: short bursts give fine altitude control. Watch the gate arrow — gates drift sideways with the wind.
