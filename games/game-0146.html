🪸 Coral Courier

You are Pip, the reef's fastest seahorse courier. Grab a parcel at the golden depot, then swim it to the glowing anemone before your air runs out. Jellyfish sting, currents shove you sideways, and bubbles are your best friends.

  • How to play: Pick up a parcel, follow the glowing trail to the pulsing anemone, deliver it, then grab the next one. Make 8 deliveries to win.
  • Air: Each delivery run has its own air timer. Grab bubbles to refill air. Run out of air and you lose a life.
  • Hazards: Jellyfish cost a life on contact. Strong current zones push you — ride them, don't fight them blindly.
  • Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to swim • Mouse: move to steer toward the pointer • Touch: drag to swim • Space: dash (short burst, limited).
  • Scoring: +100 per delivery, +25 per bubble, time bonus for fast deliveries, combo bonus for back-to-back clean runs.

Tip: stay near the middle of the reef, grab bubbles on your route, and use a dash to escape a jellyfish — but dashing costs a little air. Deliver fast for a bigger bonus!
