🎨 Pattern Painter

How to Play

  • A hidden picture is waiting inside a 10 × 10 grid. The numbers beside each row and above each column are your clues.
  • A clue like 3 1 means: a run of 3 painted cells, then at least one empty cell, then 1 painted cell — in that order.
  • Paint cells you are sure belong to the picture. Mark cells you are sure are empty with an ✖.
  • Painting a cell that is not part of the picture costs one life. You have 3 lives.
  • Reveal every painted cell in the picture to win. Use hints sparingly — each one costs 25 points.
  • Three pictures rotate: a Rocket, a Flower, and a Cat. Finish one, then try a new picture for a fresh puzzle.

Controls: mouse click, touch tap, or keyboard arrows + Space / X / E. Sound starts only after you press Start.
