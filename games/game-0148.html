🐝 Apiary Architect

How to Play: You are the Keeper of a young hive. Spring is generous, Summer is rich, Autumn is short, and Winter arrives on Day 61. Stock 150 honey before Winter to win. If hive health reaches 0, the colony collapses.

  • Build comb: Press Build Comb, then click a glowing empty hex cell. Comb stores honey and houses bees.
  • Foragers gather pollen and honey. Guards protect health from mites and wasps. Nurses raise new bees and heal the hive.
  • Balance matters: Too few guards and pests hurt health. Too few nurses and bees decline. Too few foragers and you starve.
  • Seasons change every 15 days: Spring, Summer, Autumn, then Winter. Foraging is best in Summer, worst in Autumn.
  • Click cells to inspect them. Watch pollen — comb and recruits cost pollen or honey.

Keyboard, mouse and touch all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.
