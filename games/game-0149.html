🎺 METRONOME MARCH

Lead the marching band down the parade route!

How to Play: A metronome ticks at 120 BPM. Step exactly on the beat — press an arrow key (↑ ↓ ← →), click a step button, or tap the screen when the beat pulse flashes and the target arrow matches. On-beat steps march the band forward and raise your score. Off-beat or wrong steps break formation — lose all 5 formation members and the parade falls apart!

Reach the finish line at 400 m to win. Watch the beat ring: step while it glows green for a Perfect step.

Beat window: ±140 ms for a Good step, ±70 ms for Perfect (+50 bonus). Combo bonus: every 8 consecutive on-beat steps adds +100. The tempo rises slightly as you near the finish — stay sharp!
