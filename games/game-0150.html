ZIP LINE ZEPHYR

One cable. Eight canyons. Zero second chances per jump.

How to Play:
• You ride a zipline from the left cliff toward the right cliff.
• Watch your shadow on the ground. When it is over the glowing landing platform, RELEASE!
• Release too early or too late and you plunge into the canyon — that costs a life.
• Land dead-centre for a PERFECT bonus. Chain landings to build your score.
• Land on all 8 platforms to reach the Final Peak and WIN. You have 3 lives.
• Controls: SPACE or ↓ to release  •  Mouse click / tap on the canyon to release  •  ENTER to start  •  R to restart.

Tip: the wind pushes you while you fall — check the HUD before you let go.

YOU WIN!
