VOLCANO VOYAGER

The volcano is erupting! Sprint across the scorched ridge, leap the lava pools, dodge falling rocks, and grab every ember gem on the way to the summit.

How to Play:
• Jump: Press Space / Up Arrow / W, click, or tap the screen.
• Double Jump: Press jump again mid-air for a second boost.
• Goal: Survive 1200m to reach the summit and WIN.
• Hazards: Lava pools and falling rocks cost 1 life. You have 3 lives.
• Score: Distance points + 50 per ember gem. Rocks smashed by landing on them? No — avoid them!
• Speed increases the further you run. Good luck, Voyager!
Sound starts after you press Start. Best on keyboard, mouse, or touch.
