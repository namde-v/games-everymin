Knot Keeper

A tangled web of ropes has appeared. Drag the glowing knots until no two ropes cross.

  • Mouse / touch: drag any knot to a new spot. Releasing it counts as one move.
  • Keyboard: press Tab (or [ and ]) to pick a knot, arrow keys to nudge it, Enter to confirm a move.
  • Goal: clear all crossings before the move limit runs out.
  • Levels: 5 levels, each with more knots and fewer spare moves. You have 3 lives.

How to Play: Crossed ropes glow red. Untangled ropes glow green. Plan your drags — every move counts!
