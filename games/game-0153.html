🍺 Brewmaster Bay ⚓

A seaside tavern strategy game — serve the bay before the day shift ends!

Welcome to the bay! Press Start to open the tavern.

How to Play

Goal: Earn 150 coins before the 150-second day shift ends. Lose 3 customers to impatience and the tavern's reputation sinks — game over!

1. Read the order: Each customer at the bar shows a coloured drink bubble above their head. 2. Brew it: Tap a drink button at the bottom (or press 1–4) if you have its ingredient and a free brewing pot. Brewing takes 3 seconds. 3. Serve it: Tap the matching customer (or select with ← → and press Enter / Space) once the drink is ready. Fast service earns a tip!

Drinks: Sea Ale (Barley, 10 coins) · Honey Mead (Honey, 14) · Herbal Tea (Herb, 8) · Apple Cider (Fruit, 12). A supply crate adds +3 of every ingredient every 25 seconds. Watch the patience bars — they drain the longer a customer waits!

Controls: Mouse click, touch tap, or keyboard. R restarts instantly on the end screen.
