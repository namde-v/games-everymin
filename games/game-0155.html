Harpoon Harbor

How to Play: You stand on the dock at the top left. Drag with the mouse or finger to aim the harpoon — the arrow shows angle and power. Release to fire. Harpoons arc and drift with the underwater current, so aim a little upstream of moving fish. Catch the target number of fish to clear the level. Sharks cost you a harpoon and a score penalty, and missing wastes a harpoon. Clear 3 levels to win!
Keyboard: Left/Right or Up/Down adjust angle and power, Space fires, R restarts. Mouse: drag and release to aim and fire. Touch: drag and release on the water.
