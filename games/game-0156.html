Pogo Pioneer

How to Play

Your pogo hero bounces automatically! Steer left and right in mid-air to land on platforms, grab stars, and climb as high as you can.

Watch out: platforms shrink as you climb, spikes end a bounce, and falling into a gap costs a life.

Controls: Arrow keys / A & D to steer, mouse to steer toward the pointer, touch left/right side of the screen. Reach 500m to WIN!

Collect stars for bonus points. A perfect centre landing gives a super bounce.
