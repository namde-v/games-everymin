Railroad Tycoonlet

Can you run a tiny railroad empire?

  • Build: Tap grass squares to lay track. Each piece costs 5 credits. Connect Central Depot to a factory, then on to a town.
  • Dispatch: Read the demand at the top, then press Dispatch Train. The train picks up cargo at the factory and delivers it to the town.
  • Earn: Every delivery scores 60 points. Reach 300 points before time runs out to win.
  • Avoid disaster: Erasing track under a train derails it. Two trains on the same square collide. Each disaster costs 1 life. Lose all 3 lives and the railroad closes.
  • Plan: A missing link means no route. Watch the message line for route hints.

How to Play: Build, Dispatch, Deliver, Repeat. Use mouse, touch, or keyboard.
