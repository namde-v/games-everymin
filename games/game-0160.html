Tightrope Titan

How to Play: Walk from the left cliff to the right platform. Wind gusts push your balance meter off centre. Lean against the wind to stay upright. Collect all five flags for bonus points, and reach the far platform before you run out of lives.

⌨️ Arrow Left / A = lean left  •  Arrow Right / D = lean right  •  Arrow Up / W / Space = step forward
🖱️ Click LEFT / RIGHT halves to lean, centre to step  •  👆 Touch and drag left / right to lean, tap centre to step

Fall off the rope and you lose a life. Stay in the gold zone for steady progress. A strong gust warning means lean hard!

Game Over

Tip: lean early when the gust arrow appears. Small, frequent corrections beat one huge overcorrection.
