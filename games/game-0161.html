GLACIER GLIDER

How to Play

  • Slide on ice: You keep gliding with inertia. Ice is slippery, so plan your stops! Friction is very low.
  • Move: Arrow keys / WASD to push. On touch, swipe in a direction. Click / tap the canvas to glide toward that point.
  • Collect: Grab glowing crystals for 100 points. Every 10 crystals = a new level, faster ice and more snowballs.
  • Avoid: Dark cracks cost a life if you slide over them. Rolling snowballs bounce around and cost a life on hit.
  • Win: Collect 50 crystals to reach the Glacier Summit. Lose all 3 lives and the run freezes over.
  • Tip: Use the arena edges and snow mounds to slow down. Tap opposite direction to brake gently.
Sound starts only after you press Start. Keyboard, mouse and touch all work. Press P to pause.
