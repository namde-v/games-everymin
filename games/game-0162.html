ᚱᚢᚾᛖ REACTOR

Story: An ancient reactor runs on a secret sequence of four runes. Feed it the right sequence and it powers up. Feed it ten wrong sequences and it overloads.

How to Play:

  • Tap runes (or press keys 1–6) to fill the four slots, then press Channel Guess (or Enter).
  • After each guess you get pegs: gold for a rune in the correct position, silver for a correct rune in the wrong position. Pegs are not in slot order — you must reason out which is which.
  • Each rune is used at most once. You have 10 guesses. Win fast for bonus points; the Clue button reveals one secret rune at a cost of 50 points.
  • Work like a codebreaker: a rune that never earns a peg across several guesses is almost certainly absent. Cross-check guesses against each other.

Scoring: 1000 points for a win, plus 100 per unused guess, minus 50 per clue used, minus 1 point per second elapsed. Lose and you keep whatever points you banked from clues-free play: 0.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no network needed.

How to Play

Pick 4 different runes from the six on the pad. Submit to learn how many are exactly right (gold) and how many are right but misplaced (silver). Use logic across guesses to narrow the secret down before your 10 guesses run out. Example: if a guess earns 2 gold and 1 silver, three of its four runes belong in the secret, and two are already in exactly the right slots.

Controls: Mouse click, touch tap, or keyboard (1–6, Backspace, Enter). Press R for a new reactor after a game ends.
