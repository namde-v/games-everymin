Observatory Operator

Your mission: reach 150 Reputation by completing telescope observations — or finish Night 5 with at least 10 completed observations. Lose 3 weather windows and the director ends your shift.

  • Select a request by tapping its card, or pressing 1, 2, 3 or 4.
  • Observe costs budget credits. It only makes progress in Clear weather. Cloudy weather pauses it; Storm weather pauses it and eats into its window.
  • Every request has a window timer. Finish before it runs out, or it counts as a missed window.
  • Urgent requests (red border) pay bonus reputation. Short, cheap targets are safer when clouds are coming.
  • Watch the night clock: at dawn, unfinished work is cleared and the next night begins.
  • Win at 150 Reputation, or by surviving 5 nights with 10+ completions. Game over after 3 missed windows.

No downloads. No network needed. Sound starts only after you press Start.

How to Play

  • Pick a request, press Observe, and finish it before its window expires.
  • Clear weather = progress. Cloudy or Storm = paused. Plan around the forecast.
  • Balance cost, duration and reward. Urgent targets pay more but expire faster.
  • Reach 150 Reputation to win immediately. Three missed windows ends the game.
