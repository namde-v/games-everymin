ORBIT ORDEAL

Slingshot a probe around planets. Bend its path with gravity. Dock with the moving station.

You have 5 launches to dock in all 3 rounds. Crash into a planet or fly into deep space and you lose a launch. Grab stars for bonus points. Precision beats power.

  • Mouse / touch: press near the launch pad, drag backwards like a slingshot, release to launch.
  • Keyboard: ← → adjust angle, ↑ ↓ adjust power, Space to launch, R to restart.
  • Gravity from every planet bends your flight. Use it to curve around obstacles toward the station.
  • Dotted preview shows your predicted path. Watch it change as planets pull.
  • Win by docking in Round 3. Lose if you run out of launches.
