HEX HAVOC

The grid is scrambled. The power is out.

Tap a hex tile to rotate it 60°. Connect a continuous path from the glowing SOURCE hex on the left to every TARGET hex on the right. Powered tiles glow gold. Finish the board in the move limit to advance.

How to Play — Hex Havoc
  • Mouse / Touch: Tap or click any hex to rotate it clockwise. Long-press is not needed — one tap = one rotation = one move.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD move the yellow cursor. Press Space, Enter, or R to rotate the selected tile. Press H for a hint, N for a new shuffled board on the same level (costs 1 life).
  • Goal: Light ALL targets. A connection only counts if both neighbouring tiles point at each other.
  • Moves: Every rotation costs 1 move. Run out of moves and you lose a life and retry the level. Lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over.
  • Scoring: +100 per target lit, +250 per level cleared, plus +10 for every move left over. Chain levels without losing a life for bonus points.
  • Tip: Start at the source and work outward. Straight pieces run opposite sides; corners bend; T-pieces split to two targets.
