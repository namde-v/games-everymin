🌾 Farmstead Frontier

Your frontier farm must thrive for three seasons. Earn 1200 gold and harvest 30 crops before the end of Fall (Day 90) — or the frontier wins.

How to Play

  • Plant: Click a plot, then a crop button (or keys 1–6). Each crop has its own price, growth time, water need, and soil effect.
  • Rotate crops: Planting the same family twice in a row drains soil and cuts yield. A different family earns a rotation bonus (+25% yield) and protects the soil.
  • Water daily: Crops only grow when watered. Two dry days in a row and the crop withers — costing you a life.
  • Soil matters: Corn and Pumpkin are hungry and drain soil; Beans and Carrots restore it. Low soil means small harvests.
  • Watch the market: Prices shift every day. Harvest when your crop's price is high for maximum gold.
  • Seasons: Spring (Days 1–30), Summer (31–60), Fall (61–90). Some crops grow faster in their favourite season.
  • Win by reaching 1200 gold. Lose if you run out of lives or Day 90 ends short of the goal.

Controls: Mouse / touch to tap plots and buttons. Keyboard: arrows move selection, 1–6 plant, W water, H harvest, N next day.
