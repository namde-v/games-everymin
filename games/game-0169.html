Meadow Maestro

🐦 🎵 🐤 🎶 🦉

You are the conductor of a dawn chorus. The birds sing a melodic call — listen, then echo it back note for note, on the beat.

How to Play
  • Watch & listen: the birds sing a sequence, one glowing bird at a time.
  • Echo it: tap the same birds in the same order. Each bird has its own voice and key.
  • Stay on tempo: later rounds get faster and longer. A perfect round builds your combo multiplier.
  • Careful: a wrong note costs a heart. Lose all 3 hearts and the chorus falls silent.
  • Win: conduct all 10 rounds to earn the Golden Feather.

How to Play

  • Phase 1 — Listen: birds light up and sing. Do not tap yet; just memorise the melody.
  • Phase 2 — Echo: repeat the melody in order. The beat ring shows the tempo — tap near the pulse for bonus points.
  • Tempo shifts: Gentle, then Breezy, Lively, Swift, and finally Dawn Rush. Each shift shortens the beat.
  • Combos: every flawless round raises your multiplier, up to x5. A mistake resets it.
  • Keys: 1/A, 2/S, 3/D, 4/F, or tap a bird, or use arrow keys Left/Up/Down/Right in order.
