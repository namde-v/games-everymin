Tip: strong wind pushes arrows sideways in flight. Aim slightly into the wind and a touch high for far targets.

How to Play

  • Goal: Score 1000 points before you run out of arrows. You have 5 rounds with 3 arrows each.
  • Aim: Move the mouse or drag on touch to aim the bow. The dotted line previews the first part of the flight.
  • Shoot: Click, tap, or press Space to loose an arrow. Hold longer for more power? No — power is set by how far you drag back, or by Left / Right arrows.
  • Keyboard: Up / Down change angle, Left / Right change power, Space shoots, R restarts, Enter starts.
  • Wind: Each round brings a new wind. A rightward wind pushes arrows right, a leftward wind pushes them left. The HUD and the drifting snow show its strength.
  • Drop: Arrows fall with distance. Far targets need a higher aim. Moving targets need a lead — shoot slightly ahead of where they are going.
  • Scoring: Closer to the centre means more points. A perfect bullseye also earns a distance bonus on far shots.
  • Win / Lose: Reach 1000 points at any time to win instantly. If all 15 arrows are used below 1000, the game ends.
