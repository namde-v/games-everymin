Locksmith Labyrinth

How to Play: You are the locksmith trapped in a three-chamber maze. Find the red key to open the red door, then the blue key for the blue door, then the green key for the green door guarding the exit. Keys are single-use: each one vanishes when its door opens. Grab gold stars for bonus points and avoid the red spike traps — each trap costs a life. Reach the glowing exit to win!

Controls: Arrow Keys / WASD to move • Click or tap a nearby maze cell to step there • Use the on-screen arrows • Press R to restart.

Tip: do not waste a key — a door only opens when you bump into it while carrying its matching key. Plan a route through each chamber, collect every star you can, and watch out for traps near the doors. You have 3 lives and the clock is ticking for a time bonus.
