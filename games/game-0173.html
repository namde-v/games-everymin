🌴 Oasis Operator

How to Play: You run a desert oasis for 12 days. Reach 120 Prosperity before the season ends — or the oasis dries up and the game ends.

  • Click / tap a plot to select it, then use an action below.
  • 1 Water — spend 2 water to grow palms and crops.
  • 2 Worker — assign a free worker to boost that plot.
  • 3 Harvest — collect a ripe plot for coins and prosperity.
  • 4 Market — turn a ripe harvest into a market stall bonus.
  • Space — advance to the next day. Wells refill water; thirsty days cost prosperity if water runs out.
  • Wells give water each morning. Palms need water and workers to ripen. Stalls earn coins every day they are staffed.

Keyboard: 1–4 select action · Space advance day · Click / tap works everywhere.
