Constellation Connector

Draw the hidden constellation, star by star. Find the right pattern, never cross a line, and never reuse a star.

How to Play:
• Mouse / touch: press on a star and drag through the stars in order, or click them one by one.
• Arrow Keys: move the selection ring. Enter: connect the selected star.
• C: clear your current line. A faint golden glow marks a helpful hint star.
• Wrong star or crossed line? The line shakes and you lose a life. Clear all 8 constellations to win!

Later levels add decoy stars and mirrored patterns — the shape may be flipped! Watch the clue name, count the stars, and trust the shape, not just the position. You have 3 lives per run. Good luck, stargazer.
