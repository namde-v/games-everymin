🚢 How to Play

Goal: Collect 5000 treasure within 8 days. You have 3 divers. Lose a diver when oxygen or battery hits zero underwater.

Choose a wreck: Click a dive card below, or press 1–4. Deeper wrecks pay more but drain oxygen faster and carry more risk.

Underwater: Salvage automatically fills your cargo. Press Space or Surface Now to bank your cargo as treasure. Surfacing early is safe; staying long is rich — and risky.

Between dives: Oxygen and battery recharge at the surface. Watch storms: a storm warning means higher hazard on the next dive.

Controls: Click / tap dive cards and buttons. ← → move selection, 1–4 select dive, Space dive / surface, R restart after game ends.

Tip: Cargo over 70 is heavy — surfacing takes longer. A wreck with low risk is best on Day 1. Never dive twice with a half-empty battery if a storm is forecast.
