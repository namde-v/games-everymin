How to Play:

  • Notes scroll from right to left, one row per choir section.
  • Press 1 / A for Soprano, 2 / S for Alto, 3 / D for Tenor, 4 / F for Bass — or tap / click the coloured section buttons.
  • Cue at the golden beat line: Perfect (+50), Good (+25), Early / Late (+10).
  • A missed note breaks the harmony. Lose all 3 lives (or let harmony hit zero) and the concert ends.
  • Complete all 3 phrases — each faster than the last — to earn a standing ovation and WIN.
  • Mouse clicks on the buttons work too. Sound starts only after you press Start.
