🤠 LASSO ROUNDUP

Runaway animals are stampeding across the prairie! Throw your lasso, time the throw, and round them all up. A bigger loop can catch a whole herd at once — but it flies slower and the animals keep moving.

How to Play
• Mouse / Touch: move to aim, press & hold to grow your loop, release to throw.
• Arrow Keys: move the aim reticle. Space: hold to grow the loop, release to throw.
• Missed throw = lose 1 rope. Catch animals inside the loop when it lands.
• Sheep 10 pts • Pig 15 pts • Cow 20 pts • Golden Horse 50 pts (very fast!)
• WIN by scoring 180 points. GAME OVER if you run out of rope or time.
Tip: wait until two animals bunch together, then throw a big loop over both for a combo bonus!
