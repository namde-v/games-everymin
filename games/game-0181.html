🛶 CANYON CRASHER

Steer your raft down a wild canyon river! Dodge rocks and churning rapids, grab supply crates, and survive 3000 metres to reach calm water.

How to Play:
← → or A / D — steer left and right
↑ / W — paddle faster  •  ↓ / S — slow down
Mouse / touch — drag to steer the raft
Rocks cost 1 life. Rapids slow you and shake the raft.
Supply crates: +100 points and restore a little speed.
You have 3 lives. Reach 3000 m to WIN!

Tip: stay near the centre of the channel when the river narrows. Rapids are wider and paler than rocks — steer around them if you can, but a crate is always worth a small detour. Press P to pause.
