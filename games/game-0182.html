🔮 Prism Pathfinder

Route the light. Wake the crystals.

A beam of light leaves the glowing source. Tap or click a prism to rotate it 90° in effect — mirrors flip between / and \ — and bend the beam through every crystal on the grid before you run out of rotations.

How to Play: Click / tap a prism to rotate it. Use arrow keys to move the glowing cursor and Space to rotate. R restarts the level. Walls block the beam. Crystals glow when lit. Finish all 5 levels to win!

Tip: Follow the beam! A dark crystal past a mirror means that mirror is pointing the wrong way. Plan the whole path before you spend rotations — each level has a tight rotation budget. If you run out, you lose a life and the level resets.
