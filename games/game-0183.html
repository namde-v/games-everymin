Tea House Tycoon

Run a cosy tea house for 3 timed days and earn 300 coins to win!

How to Play

  • Guests arrive and ask for a specific tea. Watch their patience bar!
  • Tap a tea button (or press 1–5) to brew it in the kettle, then tap a guest and press Serve.
  • Serve the matching tea to earn coins plus a patience tip. Wrong or missing tea earns nothing.
  • If a guest storms out, you lose one reputation heart. Lose all 5 hearts and the shop closes.
  • Between days, spend coins in the shop: restock leaves, speed up the kettle, add a seat, or boost tips.
  • Reach 300 total coins earned by the end of Day 3 to win.

Controls: Mouse / touch: tap teas, guests and buttons. Keyboard: 1–5 brew, ← → select guest, Enter serve, R restart.
