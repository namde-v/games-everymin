Boomerang Bounty

How to Play: You are the hunter at the bottom-left. Aim your boomerang, then throw it. It flies out, curves wide, and comes back to you — hit as many moving targets as you can in ONE throw for chain bonuses!

Wind pushes your boomerang sideways, so aim into the wind. Angle and power matter: a soft throw curves tight, a hard throw sweeps wide. You have limited throws per round. Clear all 3 rounds to win!

Mouse: move to aim, drag and release to throw  |  Touch: drag and release  |  Keyboard: Arrow keys aim / adjust power, Space throws, R restarts.

Tip: watch the wind arrow at the top. Chain 3+ hits in one throw for +150 bonus per extra hit. If your boomerang returns to you, you keep throwing — but if you run out of throws with targets left, the round ends in defeat. Press Space to throw!
