💧 WORD WELL

Spell words. Clear letters. Don't let the well fill up!
Letters fall one at a time into an 8-wide well. Steer them left and right, drop them, then tap two neighbouring settled letters to swap them and finish a word.

  • Move: Arrow keys Left / Right, or the on-screen ◀ ▶ buttons. Swipe left/right on touch screens.
  • Fall faster / Drop: Down arrow for faster, Space or Drop button for an instant drop.
  • Swap: Tap / click one settled letter, then tap an adjacent settled letter to swap them. Swapping can complete a word!
  • Clear words: Any horizontal run of 3–5 letters that spells a real word clears, scores points, and lets letters above fall down.
  • Win: Spell 8 words to win. Game Over: If a new letter cannot enter because the well is full to the top, you lose a life. Lose all 3 lives and the well overflows.
  • Scoring: 3-letter word = 30, 4-letter = 50, 5-letter = 80, plus bonuses for longer chains and combos.

Keyboard: ← → ↓ • Space = drop • P = pause • R = restart
