Welcome, Terminal Manager!

Flights are arriving. Assign each flight to a free gate before its passengers lose patience. Staff up to turn planes around faster, and earn revenue from happy departures. Lose 3 flights to anger or missed departures and the airport shuts you down.

How to Play
  • Mouse / Touch: Tap a flight in Arrivals, then tap a free gate to assign it. Tap Hire Staff to speed up turnarounds.
  • Keyboard: ↑ / ↓ select a flight, keys 1–4 assign it to Gate 1–4, S hire staff, Enter start / restart, H toggle these instructions.
  • Goal — WIN: Earn $5,500 revenue and serve at least 10 flights before the shift ends.
  • Game Over: 3 flights leave angry, or the shift timer runs out before you reach the goal.
  • Each gate turnaround takes time. More staff = faster service and happier passengers. Delayed flights pay less; angry flights cost a reputation life ❤️.
  • Tip: assign urgent (red) flights first, and hire staff early — it pays for itself.

Fully offline. Sound is procedural and starts only after you press Start.
