How to Play — Folk Fiddle Fest

  • Goal: Finish the whole folk tune and reach 1,500 points to win. Lose all 3 hearts and the crowd walks away.
  • TAP notes (yellow circles): Press and release quickly when the note reaches the glowing bow zone on the left.
  • HOLD notes (green bars): Press and KEEP HOLDING until the whole bar has passed through the bow zone. Releasing early breaks the note.
  • SLIDE notes (purple bars): Hold like a long note — the pitch slides up as you hold. Keep holding to the end for a big bonus.
  • Controls: Hold SPACE, Down Arrow, or F — or press and hold the mouse on the canvas — or press and hold with touch on mobile. Release to stop the bow.
  • Scoring: Perfect = 100, Good = 60, Hold/Slide completed = +150 bonus. Perfect streaks raise your combo multiplier. Misses cost crowd cheer and, after 3 bad misses in a row, a heart.
  • Tip: Watch the note colour in the bow zone: gold = perfect window, orange = good window. Listen — a held note only sings while you hold it in the zone!

Keyboard: SPACE / ↓ / F to bow  •  Mouse: hold click  •  Touch: hold finger  •  R = restart, H = how to play
