🎯 Dartboard Duel

Your rival scored 220 points. You have 15 darts across 3 rounds. Beat 220 to win the duel!

How to Play
• Move your mouse, finger, or arrow keys to aim the crosshair.
• The board drifts, spins and shrinks — and your hand sways! Time the sway.
• Click, tap, or press Space / Enter to throw a dart.
• Bullseye = 50, Inner Bull = 25, Triple ring = 3× segment, Double ring = 2× segment.
• A glowing gold bonus ring sometimes appears: hit it for +40 bonus!
• Press R anytime to restart. Beat the rival before darts run out to win.

Tip: throw when the board slows near the centre and the sway crosses the bullseye. Later rounds are faster and smaller — aim for triples when the board is big, bullseyes when it is small.
