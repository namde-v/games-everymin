MAGMA MARATHON

You are the Ember Runner, sprinting across a crumbling volcanic ridge. The ground falls away into lava pools and rock gaps — keep moving, keep jumping, and do not stop!

HOW TO PLAY
Arrow Up / Space — Jump (press twice for DOUBLE-JUMP)
Mouse Click — Jump  •  Touch / Tap — Jump
Collect ember gems (+50 points) • Survive to the finish line or reach 2000 points to WIN
Falling in lava or hitting rocks costs a life. Lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over.

Tip: jump late over wide lava pools, use your second jump in mid-air to clear rock gaps, and chain gems for bonus points. Speed rises every 250 metres — stay calm and time your jumps!

GAME OVER

The volcano claims another runner.

Press Start, then jump with Space, click, or tap.
