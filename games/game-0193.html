⚓ Seaport Supervisor

Your goal: Earn 5000 coins in 10 days without letting reputation fall to zero.

How to Play

  • Each day, ships arrive with a cargo need: Containers, Grain, Oil or Cars.
  • Tap / click a ship card, then tap / click a free dock (or press keys 1–4) to berth it.
  • Docked ships load automatically. Finish before the deadline to earn the full fee plus an early bonus.
  • If a deadline expires, the ship sails away and you lose 15 reputation.
  • Spend coins on crews and cranes to load faster on busy days.
  • Press Space to end the day early — unfinished ships will count as missed.

Win: 5000 coins  |  Game Over: reputation 0, or day 10 ends below 5000 coins.
