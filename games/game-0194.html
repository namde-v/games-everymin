TIMPANI THUNDER

Notes tumble down four drum lanes. Hit each drum the instant its note crosses the golden strike line. Chain hits for combos and climb through three faster levels!

How to Play
Keyboard: D F J K for lanes 1-2-3-4
Mouse: click a lane  |  Touch: tap a lane
Perfect = within the centre glow, Good = near the line
Win: finish all 90 notes with 80%+ accuracy
Game Over: 20 misses, or under 80% at the end

Sound starts only after you press Start. Headphones recommended.
