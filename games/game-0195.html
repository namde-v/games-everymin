CROSSBOW CLASH

Five rounds. One crossbow. A sky full of moving targets — and a wind that refuses to sit still.

  • How to play: Move the mouse or your finger to aim. Click / tap to shoot.
  • Keyboard: Arrow Keys aim, Space shoots, R reloads early.
  • Wind drift: Your bolt bends with the wind. Aim slightly upwind on strong gusts.
  • Reload timing: Every shot takes 0.9 seconds to reload. A loaded shot is worth more than a rushed one.
  • Targets: Balloons 50 pts, Ducks 100 pts, Golden Targets 250 pts, Popping Targets 150 pts. Watch for fast movers!
  • Bolts: 8 per round. Score 1500 points across 5 rounds to win.

Tap Start, then shoot after the bowstring is drawn.
