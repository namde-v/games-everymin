Bubble Bouncer

How to Play: Bounce upward on floating bubbles. Bigger bubbles give a bigger bounce. Bubbles pop after a few bounces, so keep moving up!

Collect stars for points. Avoid spiky bubbles — 3 hits ends the game. Do not fall below the screen.

Controls: Arrow Keys / A-D steer in air, Space = bounce boost, mouse move = steer, touch drag = steer.

Win by reaching the top cloud at height 3000!
