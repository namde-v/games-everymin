Mirror Mansion

How to Play: A lantern shines a beam across a haunted grid. Rotate the mirrors so the beam reaches every ghost door.

  • Click / Tap a mirror to rotate it between / and \
  • Arrow Keys move the glowing selection, Space rotates, R restarts the level
  • Splitter (+) splits the beam into two perpendicular beams
  • Colour filter changes the beam colour — red and blue doors only open to matching light
  • Brick blockers stop the beam cold. Each rotation costs 1 move!
  • Solve all 10 levels to win. Run out of moves and the mansion claims you.

Levels get harder: more mirrors, splitters and colour filters.
