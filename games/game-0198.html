Bakery Blitz

How to Play: Customers arrive with an order. Click an oven, then pick an item (keys 1–5) to start baking. When an item is ready, click the matching customer — or select them and press Space — to serve. Serve before patience runs out. Wrong orders cost reputation. Between rushes, spend coins on upgrades: faster ovens, extra patience, or a second oven slot.

🍞 Bread (8s, 30 coins)  |  🥐 Croissant (12s, 50)  |  🧁 Cupcake (10s, 45)  |  🍪 Cookie (6s, 25)  |  🎂 Cake (18s, 90)
Controls: Click / tap orders and ovens. Keys 1–5 select item. Space serves. R restarts. M mutes sound.

Win: Earn 4000 coins in 8 days. Lose: Reputation reaches 0%.

Game Over

Tip: Cakes pay most, but cookies are fastest when patience is low.

Keyboard: 1–5 select item • Space serve • R restart • M mute • Mouse and touch supported.
