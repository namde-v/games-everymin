How to Play

  • Listen: Press Start. The game plays a marimba melody, one glowing bar at a time.
  • Repeat: Tap or click the same bars in the same order, or use keys A S D F G H.
  • Grow: Each round adds notes. Later rounds add syncopation — uneven rhythms and quick pairs — so listen for timing, not just pitch.
  • Hints: From Round 5, sheet-note hints appear above the bars. From Round 6 you can also toggle the hint strip on or off.
  • Score: +10 per correct note, +50 round bonus, +25 for a perfect round with no replay.
  • Win: Complete Round 10. Game Over: 3 wrong melodies.

Sound starts only after you press Start, as required for offline play.
