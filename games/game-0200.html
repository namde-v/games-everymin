🏰 Catapult Castle

How to Play: You are the siege commander. Smash the castle before you run out of rocks!

  • Mouse / Touch: Press near the catapult, drag back to aim, release to launch. Longer drag = more power.
  • Keyboard: Up / Down arrows adjust angle, Left / Right adjust power, Space launches.
  • Watch the wind — it pushes rocks left or right in flight. Gravity pulls every rock down in an arc.
  • Hit castle blocks to collapse them. Destroy the red-banner targets for big bonus points.
  • Clear all targets to advance. Clear Level 8 to WIN. Run out of rocks with targets left and it is Game Over.

Tip: Aim high in strong wind, and knock out the bottom blocks first to bring the whole tower down.
