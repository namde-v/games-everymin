ZEPHYR ZIPLINE

Strap in! You are riding a zipline down a wild mountain.

How to Play: Steer left and right to dodge branches and grab glowing wind tokens. Hold boost to go faster and score more distance. Watch for GAPS in the line: build up boost, then release at the right moment to leap across — if you are not boosting when you hit a gap, you fall and lose a life!

Branches cost 1 life. You have 3 lives. Reach 3000m to reach the valley and WIN.

Arrow Keys / A-D steer • Hold Space to boost • Mouse move steers, hold click to boost • Touch drag steers, hold touch to boost
