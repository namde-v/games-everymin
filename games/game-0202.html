✎ Quill Quest

How to Play

  • Tap or click letters that touch each other — sideways, up, down, or diagonally — to spell a word.
  • Valid words score points and clear their tiles. New letters cascade down from the top.
  • Words of 3 letters score 20, 4 letters score 40, and 5 or more score 60 points.
  • Reach 100 points to win. Beware: every invalid word raises the Ink Fill, and ink slowly rises over time. If Ink Fill hits 100%, the board is full — game over.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move the glowing cursor, Enter selects a letter, Backspace removes the last letter, S submits.

Word list includes: CAT, DOG, SUN, MOON, STAR, FISH, BIRD, BOOK, TREE, FIRE, WIND, RAIN, SNOW, LEAF, ROCK, GOLD, QUILL, QUEST, INK, PEN, PAGE, SONG, KING, QUEEN, RIVER, MOUNT, OCEAN, APPLE, BREAD, LIGHT, DREAM.
