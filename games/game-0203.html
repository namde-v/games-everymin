Lighthouse Keeper

Night is falling and the fleet is coming in. Ships sail in from the dark sea toward the harbor on the right. Rocks lie in wait. Your beam is their only guide.

How to Play:
Aim the beam with your mouse, touch-drag, or ← → arrow keys.
Ships caught in the beam steer safely to harbor. Ships left in the dark drift and may strike rocks.
Press F (or hold mouse / touch) for Focus: a narrower, stronger beam that guides faster but burns fuel faster.
Save 12 ships before the night ends to win. Lose 5 ships or run out of fuel and the night is lost.
Every rescued ship brings a little extra fuel.

Tip: sweep the beam across the whole sea — do not leave one ship in the dark for long.
