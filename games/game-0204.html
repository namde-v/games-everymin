Sitar Serenade

How to Play: Notes glide down four sitar strings. Pluck a string the instant its note crosses the glowing golden target line. A perfect pluck scores 300, a good pluck 100. Chain plucks to build your combo. Finish the song with at least 65% accuracy to win — but 20 misses ends the serenade early.

You can also click or tap a string lane on the canvas.

Tip: watch the note, not the falling speed. Perfect timing is a small window right on the line — early or late still counts as Good if you are close. Keep the combo alive for bonus points on every hit.
