Boomerang Bandit

How to Play: You are the Bandit at the bottom of the clearing. Aim your boomerang, then throw it — it flies out in a wide curve and comes right back to you. Hit every moving target before you run out of throws!

Outbound hits score 100 points. Hits on the return path score 200 and build your combo. A wide, powerful arc can sweep several targets in one throw. Watch out: targets drift, bounce and speed up as you clear them.

Aim with the mouse or your finger. Hold to charge power, release to throw. Keyboard: ←→ aim, ↑↓ power, Space throw, R restart.
