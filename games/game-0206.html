🪸 CORAL COLLECTOR 🐠

Dive deep. Grab treasure. Surface before your oxygen runs out!

How to Play: Dive below the waves and collect coral treasures. Deeper treasures are worth more, but they cost more oxygen. Return to the surface to bank what you carry and refill your oxygen. Reach 500 banked treasure to win.

⚠️ Avoid sharks (they cost a life) and jellyfish (they stun you and drain oxygen). Running out of oxygen underwater ends the game.

🪸 Coral = 10–60 pts (deeper = richer) 🦈 Shark = lose a life 🪼 Jellyfish = stun + oxygen loss 🫧 Air bubble = +20 oxygen

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to swim • Mouse: hold & move to steer • Touch: drag to steer
