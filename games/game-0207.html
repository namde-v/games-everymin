How to Play
  • Goal: Connect the yellow power source (⚡) to every pink device (💡) using wire tiles.
  • Rotate: Tap / click a wire tile to rotate it 90°. Each rotation costs 1 move.
  • Power flows only through matching wire ends. Powered wires glow green; a lit device turns green.
  • DANGER: If power reaches a red hazard tile (☠), you cause a SHORT CIRCUIT and lose immediately. Tip: rotate tiles next to hazards first — power flows live after every rotation!
  • Move limit: Each level has a strict move limit. Run out of moves and it is Game Over.
  • Win: Complete all 3 levels. Tiles: straight (─), corner (⌐), cross (✚), source, device, hazard, empty.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys move the highlighted tile, R or Space rotates it, Enter starts / restarts.
