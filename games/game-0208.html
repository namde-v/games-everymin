    🍇 Vineyard Venture

    How to Play: You run a small vineyard for 2 years (8 seasons). Plant grape varieties in 12 plots, keep them watered and pruned, and harvest at peak ripeness. Wine orders appear each season — harvest the right variety, then Sell Order to earn money. Reach $1200 total revenue or complete 5 orders to Win. Run out of money with no growing grapes, or reach the end of Year 2 without the target, and it is Game Over.

    Controls: Mouse / touch: tap a plot to select it, tap buttons to act. Keyboard: Arrow keys move selection, 1/2/3 plant varieties, W water, P prune, H harvest, S sell, N next season, Enter start / restart.

    Varieties: Pinot Noir is balanced, Merlot is high value but thirsty, Chardonnay grows fast and cheap. Sun and rain vary each season — adapt!
