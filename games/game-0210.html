⚓ Anchor Angler

Cast with precision. Land your hook inside the glowing target rings for the best catch!

How to Play
• Drag on the water (or use ← → for angle, ↑ ↓ for power) to aim. Release to cast.
• Wind pushes your hook sideways — aim upwind. Wind changes every cast.
• Perfect ring = golden fish (50 pts), good ring = 30 pts, edge = 15 pts. Miss = no fish.
• After a hit, tap / press SPACE rapidly to reel in before the fish escapes.
• Avoid the dark snag rocks: landing on one costs a cast and no fish.
• Win by catching a haul of 8 fish before you run out of 12 casts or 120 seconds.

Keyboard: Arrows aim  |  Space cast / reel  |  R restart  |  Mouse & touch supported
