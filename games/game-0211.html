Glacier Glider

How to Play: You are a skater gliding down an endless icy slope. Steer left and right to dodge grey rocks and dark crevasses. Jump over crevasses, collect sparkling snowflakes, and survive as the slope gets faster and faster!

CONTROLS
Keyboard: ← → or A / D to steer • Space / ↑ / W to jump
Mouse: move to steer • click to jump
Touch: drag to steer • tap JUMP button to jump

WIN: Collect 40 snowflakes or glide 2000 m.
LOSE: Hit 3 obstacles and your run ends.

Game Over

Tip: snowflakes are worth 10 points each. A clean near-miss earns a bonus. Watch the speed — it rises every 200 m!
