How to Play: Plant the glyph in your hand on any empty garden plot. When 3 or more matching glyphs line up in a row or column, they bloom, clear, and score points. Bloom every target glyph before you run out of 30 moves to win the garden.

Mouse / touch: tap a plot to plant. Keyboard: Arrow keys move the cursor, Space or Enter plants, R re-rolls your glyph (3 per game), M mutes sound.

Longer blooms and back-to-back blooms build your combo multiplier. Plan ahead — a careless plant can block a big bloom!
