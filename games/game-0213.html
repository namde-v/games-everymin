🔭 Observatory Overseer

Strategy / Telescope Scheduling — You run a mountain observatory for one night. Planets, comets and nebulae rise and set on their own schedules. Assign telescopes wisely and make 12 discoveries before dawn at 05:00.

How to Play
  • Mouse / Touch: tap a target card below the sky, or tap a target in the sky, to assign your first free telescope.
  • Keyboard: press 1, 2, 3 to pick a telescope, ← → to pick a target, Enter / Space to assign it.
  • Each target needs a set observation time and is only visible inside its time window. Miss the window and it sets — lost!
  • Clear weather observes at full speed, Partly Cloudy at 60%, Cloudy at 30%. Rare targets are worth more points.
  • Comets move fast and pay bonus points. Nebulae take longest but are worth the most. Planets are reliable mid-value targets.
  • Win by reaching 12 discoveries before 05:00. Otherwise dawn ends the night.

Single-file, fully offline game. Sound starts only after you press Start. Keyboard, mouse and touch supported.
