🎻 Cello Cadence

A concert in your hands. Notes fall down four cello strings — C, G, D and A. Bow each string at exactly the right moment.

How to Play:
• Keys: D = C string   F = G string   J = D string   K = A string
• Mouse / Touch: click or tap a string lane when its note reaches the golden bow line.
• Perfect (dead centre) = 100 pts, Good = 50 pts, Early / Late = 20 pts.
• Long golden notes: press AND HOLD that string until the tail passes the line for a bonus.
• Chain hits to grow your combo. 10 misses ends the concert. Finish all 52 notes to take a bow and win!

Sound starts only after you press Start. Headphones recommended.

Bravo!

Tip: watch the bow line glow — a note flashing gold is in the Perfect window. Hold long notes all the way through for +150 bonus points.
