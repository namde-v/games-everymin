DART DYNAMO

How to Play
A dartboard slides and sways across the pub wall while the wind pushes your dart off course. Move your mouse, finger, or arrow keys to aim the crosshair. Hold Space (or press and hold on the board) to charge power, then release to throw. Land darts to score points — bullseyes are worth 50!

Reach 300 points in 5 rounds (3 darts per round) to win. Watch the wind arrow: stronger wind means more drift. Perfect power (in the green zone) flies true; too weak or too strong wobbles.

Controls: Mouse / Touch to aim & throw • Arrow keys / WASD to aim • Space to charge & throw • R to restart
