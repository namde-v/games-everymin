Prism Pathfinder

How to Play: Coloured light beams leave the glowing emitters on the left. Place prism mirrors on empty grid squares to bend each beam into the matching coloured receiver. Click (or tap) an empty square to place a / prism, click it again to rotate it to \, and click once more to remove it. Beams of different colours must never cross, and a beam must never hit the wrong receiver.

Keyboard: Arrow keys move the cursor, Space places / rotates, C clears all prisms, G gives up. Mouse and touch work too. Light up every receiver to win the level — finish all 3 levels to win the game!
