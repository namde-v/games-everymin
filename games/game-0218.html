🐝 Apiary Architect

How to Play: You are the keeper of a young beehive. Build wax cells, assign bees to jobs, and keep the colony alive through Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter.

  • Build cells: Click an empty hex cell in the hive (left side) to build it for 5 honey. Built cells house bees.
  • Assign bees: Pick a job below, then click a built cell to assign that bee — Foragers gather honey, Pollinators raise Bloom, Guards fight wasps.
  • Balance: Foragers need Bloom to gather. Winter eats 2 honey per day — stockpile before it comes! Wasps and mites attack; Guards protect the hive.
  • Win: Survive all 80 days and finish with at least 200 honey. Lose: Hive health hits 0, or honey hits 0 in Winter.
  • Controls: Mouse / touch: click cells and buttons. Keyboard: Arrow keys move the selector, 1/2/3 pick a job, Enter or Space builds/assigns, B builds a cell.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
