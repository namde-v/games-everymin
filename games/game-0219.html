How to Play

Notes roll down four highway lanes toward the golden marker line. Each lane is a harmonica hole:

  • Lane 1 — Hole 1 (Blow): press D, click, or tap lane 1
  • Lane 2 — Hole 2 (Draw): press F, click, or tap lane 2
  • Lane 3 — Hole 3 (Blow): press J, click, or tap lane 3
  • Lane 4 — Hole 4 (Draw): press K, click, or tap lane 4

Hit a note exactly as it crosses the marker for Perfect (+100), close for Good (+50). Miss a note and you lose a life. Chain hits to build your combo — every 10 combo adds a bonus. Survive the whole song to Win! Lose all 3 lives and the run ends.

Sound is generated live in your browser with procedural harmonica-style tones. No downloads, no internet needed.
