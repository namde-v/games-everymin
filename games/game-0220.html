LASSO LEGEND

Swing it. Time it. Rope 'em all!

How to Play: Calves wander across the ranch. Hold to swing your lasso — the loop grows, then shrinks, then grows again. Release at just the right moment so the loop is big enough to fit over a calf, but not so big it flops! A perfect-sized loop scores bonus points. Golden calves are worth double. Rope 600 points in 90 seconds to win.

MOVE: ← → or A / D  •  AIM: mouse / touch  •  HOLD + RELEASE: mouse, touch, or SPACE to throw
