ARCADE • STORM RUNNER

Tempest Trek

How to Play: You are a storm-runner sprinting through a raging tempest. Dodge falling lightning bolts, wind gusts and flying debris. Collect glowing storm-orbs to charge your way out.

Win: Survive 90 seconds and collect 25 orbs. If the clock runs out first, keep running in overtime until you have all 25!

Lose: 3 hits and the storm claims you. A hit gives you a brief shield of invulnerability — use it to escape!

Move: Arrow Keys / WASD  •  Steer: Move Mouse  •  Touch: Drag to Move
Speed: The storm gets faster every 15 seconds  •  Bonus: Near misses +10, Orbs +100

Tip: Lightning flashes a warning marker before it strikes — leave that lane fast! Wind gusts push you sideways, so brace against them. Debris tumbles diagonally. Stay near the middle for the best escape routes.
