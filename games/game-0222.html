Build the Mosaic!

A glowing target pattern appears on the right. Flip tiles on your board to match it exactly before your flips run out. Clear all 5 levels to win the game.

How to Play
  • Mouse / Touch: Tap or click a tile to flip it on or off.
  • Keyboard: Arrow Keys move the glowing cursor, Enter or Space flips the selected tile.
  • Goal: Make your board identical to the target pattern.
  • Flips: Each flip uses one flip. If flips hit zero before you match, the level fails.
  • Scoring: Unused flips become bonus points. Perfect matches earn extra points.
  • Levels: 5 levels — grids grow from 3x3 up to 5x5 and patterns get trickier.

Tip: plan your flips — flipping a tile twice wastes two moves.
