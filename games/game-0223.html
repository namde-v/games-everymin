Canyon Cartographer

Strategy / Map Expedition — Lead three scouts into a wild canyon and chart it, region by region, before hunger and fatigue end the expedition.

  • How to play: Pick a scout below (or press 1, 2, 3), then click a foggy canyon region — or move the golden cursor with the Arrow Keys and press Enter or Space to assign.
  • Scouts: Aria maps fast, Brum endures hazards, Cleo finds hidden caches. A scout can only map one region at a time.
  • Watch your numbers: Mapping and daily travel drain Supplies. Low Morale slows mapping. Rest restores Morale but costs Supplies. Finished regions sometimes hide a supply cache or a hazard.
  • Win: Reveal all 24 regions. Game Over: Supplies reach zero first.
  • Controls: Mouse / touch click, Arrow Keys, Enter / Space, 1 / 2 / 3, R to Rest.
