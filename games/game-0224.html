How to Play

  • Notes roll down four lanes toward the golden pick line near the bottom.
  • Pluck a note exactly as it crosses the line: press D, F, J, or K, click a lane, or tap a lane on touch screens.
  • Timing matters: Perfect (+300), Great (+200), Good (+100). Missing a note breaks your combo.
  • Long golden notes are banjo rolls — hit them for a bonus. Hold nothing; one clean pluck each.
  • Finish the whole song with 70% accuracy or better to Win.
  • Fail if your accuracy falls below 40% (after 20 notes judged) or you miss 20 notes.
  • Combos multiply your score: every 10 combo adds a bonus. The crowd cheers as your streak grows!

Tip: watch the beat markers and listen for the pluck. Relax, find the groove, and pick with the rhythm — not just speed.
