Catapult Canyon

Precision Catapult Launch across 8 canyon levels

Knock every target off its canyon ledge before you run out of rocks. Watch the dotted trajectory preview, mind the wind and the gaps, and make every rock count.

  • How to Play: Press Start, then drag from the catapult to aim. The dotted line shows where your rock will fly. Release to launch.
  • Mouse / Touch: Drag anywhere near the catapult to aim, release to fire. Longer drags mean more power.
  • Keyboard: Up / Down arrows adjust angle, Left / Right arrows adjust power, Space launches, R restarts the level.
  • Scoring: 100 points per target knocked off, plus a 250 point bonus for clearing a level with rocks to spare.
  • Win: Clear all 8 levels. Game Over: You run out of rocks on a level without clearing it.

Tip: High arcs clear tall cliffs. Low, fast shots smash targets on low ledges.
