Firefly Frenzy

A night-meadow chase under the stars. Catch the glow, dodge the sting.

How to Play
Move your glowing jar around the meadow and catch 30 fireflies before the 90-second timer runs out.
Move: Arrow Keys or WASD · move the mouse to steer · drag on touch screens.
Catch: Press Space, click, or tap to sweep your net. A firefly inside the net ring is caught.
Watch out: Fireflies blink and dart unpredictably. Touching a wasp costs a life. Three stings ends the game.
Scoring: 100 points per firefly, plus a time bonus when you win. Golden fireflies are worth 250.

Best on keyboard, mouse, or touch. Sound starts only after you press Start.

You Win!

The meadow glows for you.

Every firefly is safely in the jar, blinking happily in the dark.

Game Over

The night fades.

Try again — the fireflies will be back out as soon as dusk falls.
