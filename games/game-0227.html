Sundial Solver

Puzzle / Shadow Logic — 6 puzzles, one sun, many shadows.

How to Play:
• Each round shows a stone sundial with gnomons (circles) and mirrors (diamonds).
• Every piece casts a shadow in the direction it points. A gold diamond marks where that shadow must fall.
• Click / tap a piece to rotate it 45° clockwise. Rotate all pieces until every shadow touches its target at the same time.
• Arrow Keys select a piece, Space rotates the selected piece, R resets the current puzzle.
• Solve the puzzle before you run out of moves. Later puzzles add more pieces, mirrors, and tighter planning.
No downloads, no network — fully offline. Sound starts only after you press Start.
