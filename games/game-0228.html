GLACIER GUILD

You lead a guild of climbers on a frozen mountain. Hire climbers, assign them to routes, spend food and gear wisely, and plant your flag on the summit before winter closes the mountain in 30 days.

HOW TO PLAY
• Click a route to select it, then click climbers or press 1 2 3 to assign them.
• Press Enter / Space or Send Expedition to climb the selected route.
• ↑ ↓ move route selection. Mouse and touch work everywhere.
• Rest restores stamina. Supply Run trades gear for food. Hiring adds a climber.
• WIN at 100% summit progress. LOSE if food hits 0 or winter arrives first.
Choose a route, assign climbers, then send the expedition!

🗺️ Routes — click to select

👥 Your Climbers — click or press 1 / 2 / 3 to assign

Assigned climbers climb together. Higher total skill means a safer, stronger climb. Tired climbers (low stamina) risk storms and injuries. Rest them before a big push. The Summit Push only opens at 70% progress.
