Horseshoe Hero

First to 21 points wins the match!

How to Play: You and your opponent take turns across 5 rounds. Each round you throw 2 horseshoes at the stake. First, the angle needle sweeps left and right — stop it when it points at the stake. Then the power bar sweeps up and down — stop it for a strong, accurate toss. Wind pushes your shoe sideways, so aim into the wind. A Ringer (around the stake) scores 3, a Leaner (touching the stake) scores 2, and a Close shoe scores 1.

Controls: Move the mouse or drag on touch to aim. Click / tap or press Space to stop a meter and throw. Arrow Left / Right adjust angle, Arrow Up / Down adjust power.

You Win!

Final score will be shown above. Play again and chase another perfect match!

Tip: In strong wind, aim a little against it. A gentle, well-aimed toss beats a wild full-power throw.
