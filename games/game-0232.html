🪐 Orbit Oracle

You are the Oracle. Place gravity wells, bend the probe's path through every glowing ring, and land it on the target planet — without touching a sun.

How to Play
  • Place a well: tap / click empty space (up to the level's well limit).
  • Move a well: drag it with mouse or touch. Click a well to select it (gold ring).
  • Arrow Keys: nudge the selected well. Space launch / reset probe. R reset level wells.
  • Double-click a well to toggle its pull between Normal and Strong, or to remove it with a second double-click prompt — use the Remove button instead for safety.
  • Pass rings in order (gold ring is next), then reach the green target planet.
  • Each launch costs 1 fuel. You start with 45 fuel for 12 levels. 3 misses on one level resets that level's wells.
  • Crashing into a sun, flying off the map, or drifting for too long counts as a miss.

Tip: watch the dotted preview path before you launch — it shows where gravity will bend the probe.

You Win!

Place wells, then press Space or Launch.

Keyboard: Arrows move selected well • Space launch / reset • R clear wells • S strong toggle • Delete removes selected well
