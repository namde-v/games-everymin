🌊 Tidepool Tycoon

Goal: After 10 tide cycles, finish with 500+ coins and health above 50. If ecosystem health hits 0, the pool collapses and the game ends.

How to Play

  • Snails 🐌 make food. Without food, your other creatures starve and health falls.
  • Anemones 🌸 are cheap and safe. Crabs 🦀 earn more but eat more. Starfish ⭐ earn the most and clean the water.
  • Too many hungry creatures and too little food hurts health. Starfish and a balanced pool restore water quality.
  • Each tide: creatures eat, earn, and the water shifts. Buy wisely, then press Next Tide.
  • Tip: Start with 2-3 Snails, add Anemones, then Crabs and Starfish. Sell weak tiles with Backspace for a refund.

Mouse, touch, and keyboard supported. Sound starts only after you press Start.
