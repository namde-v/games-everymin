Xylophone Xpress

Xylophone bars tumble down four rainbow lanes. Tap each bar exactly when it lands on the glowing hit zone and ride the beat to the end of the song.

How to Play
D = pink lane   F = yellow lane
J = green lane   K = blue lane
Mouse: click a lane  •  Touch: tap a lane
Perfect = 100 pts  •  Good = 50 pts  •  Miss = lose health
Combo streaks raise your multiplier. Health 0 = Game Over.

Song length: about 60 seconds. No sound plays until you press Start.
