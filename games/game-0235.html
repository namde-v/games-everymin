🧊 Boomerang Bounty

Throw it out, bend it back, and cash in on the return!

Your boomerang curves in flight. Hit moving targets for points and sweep up gems on the way home. Every round gives you limited throws — reach the target score to advance.

How to Play:
Drag from the hunter to aim, then release to throw.
Arrow Left / Right: aim angle  |  Arrow Up / Down: curve strength
Space: throw  |  R: restart  |  P: pause

Targets = 20–40 pts  |  Gems = 15 pts  |  Golden target = 50 pts
Clear all 5 rounds to win. If throws run out below target, the hunt ends.
