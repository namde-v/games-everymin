Coral Cruiser

Pilot your mini-sub through a glowing coral reef. Collect 40 pearls to win before your oxygen runs out or your hull breaks!

How to Play
Arrow Keys / WASD — Move the submarine
Mouse Move — Steer toward the cursor
Touch Drag — Drag to steer on mobile
Space — Dash (fast burst, brief cooldown)

🦪 Pearls = +100 points, count toward victory
🫧 Air Bubbles = +30 oxygen
💣 Mines = -25 hull   🪼 Jellyfish = -15 hull
⛽ Dash drains a little extra oxygen — use it to escape, not to cruise!

Sound starts after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.

You Win!

Press R or tap Restart to dive again.

Tip: Grab bubbles often — oxygen ticks down even when you are careful!
