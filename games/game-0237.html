Light every exit tile by bending lantern beams around the dark maze.

Lantern Labyrinth

How to Play

  • The maze is dark. Place lanterns and mirrors to send light to every golden exit tile.
  • Lantern beams travel straight, stop at walls, and bounce off mirrors.
  • Click an empty tile to place the selected tool. Click a mirror to flip it between / and \. Click a lantern to rotate its beam.
  • Lanterns cannot be placed right next to an exit — use mirrors and clever angles instead.
  • Each placement or rotation costs 1 move. Running out of moves costs a life. Lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over.
  • Solve all 10 levels to win.

Controls: Mouse / touch click tiles  •  Arrow Keys move cursor  •  Space place / rotate  •  R reset level  •  M switch tool

You Win!

Tip: A lit exit glows green-gold. Watch the beam path — if it hits a wall, add a mirror just before it.
