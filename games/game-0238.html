🌬️ Windmill Warden

Can you keep the town lit for 8 days?

How to Play:

  • You own 6 hillside plots. Build windmills, angle their blades to match the wind direction, and store power.
  • Each day the town needs a set amount of power. Meet it to earn coins; miss it and the town counts a failed day.
  • Storms damage windmills. Repair them before they stop producing.
  • Surplus power goes into your battery (max 60) for the next day.
  • Win by meeting demand all 8 days with 300+ coins. Lose if you fail demand twice or go below 0 coins.

Controls: Mouse / touch — click plots and buttons. Arrow Keys — move selection. 1 = Build, 2 = Repair, 3 = Angle, Enter / Space = Confirm, N = Next Day.
