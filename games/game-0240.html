🥌 Curling Champion

How to Play: Slide your red stones down the ice into the house at the far end. Stones closest to the button score. Knock rival blue stones out of the house! Sweep a moving stone to make it travel farther and straighter.

Play 6 ends. Win the match by outscoring the rival. Drag from your stone to aim, then release to slide. Press Space to sweep while a stone moves.
