Kite Kraze

How to Play

  • Steer your kite with Arrow keys / WASD, by moving the mouse, or by dragging on touch.
  • Dodge dark storm gusts and birds. A hit costs 1 life. You have 3 lives.
  • Collect gold stars (+50) and glowing wind boosts (+25 and a speed burst).
  • Your score also grows every second you stay airborne.
  • Win by reaching 1500 points. Lose all lives and the kite comes down.
Press Enter or Space to start. Sound starts only after you press Start.
