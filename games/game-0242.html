HOW TO PLAY

  • Watch the runes glow in order, then tap or click them back in the same order.
  • Each round adds one more rune to the sequence. Complete 8 rounds to win.
  • Round 5+: a red-ringed decoy rune flashes during playback. Ignore it — it is never part of the sequence.
  • Round 7+: a Mirror Round — repeat the sequence in reverse order.
  • You may make up to 3 mistakes. A mistake replays the current round.
  • Keyboard: keys 1–6 (or Q W E A S D) tap runes, Enter starts, R restarts.
Mouse, touch, and keyboard all work. Sound begins after you press Start.

Press R or Enter to restart instantly.
Tip: say the rune names out loud as they glow — it really helps at round 6 and beyond.
