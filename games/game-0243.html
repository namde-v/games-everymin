How to Play

Welcome, Baron! You have 7 days to earn 500 coins in total sales.
1) Buy ingredients — flour, sugar, butter and eggs cost coins.
2) Bake goods — each recipe uses ingredients, takes time in the oven, and stocks your shelf.
3) Set prices with + / − buttons. Higher prices earn more per sale but customers may still buy — patience is the real risk.
4) Serve customers — click / tap a customer (or press their number key) while you have their item in stock, before their patience runs out.
5) If a customer leaves angry you earn nothing. If your coins drop below 0, you are bankrupt — Game Over.
Earn 500 coins total to WIN before Day 7 ends!

Controls: Mouse / Touch: tap buttons and customers. Keyboard: 1–4 bake, Q/W/E/R buy flour/sugar/butter/eggs, A/S/D/F serve customer 1–4, + / − adjust selected price, N end day, Enter start / restart.

Tip: Bread is cheap and fast. Cake is slow but very profitable. Keep the shelf stocked and never let the oven sit idle!
