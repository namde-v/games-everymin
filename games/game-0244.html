Marimba Marathon

Run the marathon of melody! Catch falling marimba notes right on the beat line.

How to Play:
• Notes fall down 4 coloured lanes toward the glowing hit line.
• Press D / F / J / K, or tap / click a lane, exactly when a note reaches the line.
• Perfect (very close) = 100 pts, Good = 50 pts, Miss = lose accuracy and combo.
• Chain hits to raise your combo multiplier up to x5.
• Tempo speeds up each level. Reach 5000 points to win. If accuracy hits 0%, the run ends.
