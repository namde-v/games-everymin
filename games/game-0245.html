DARTS DYNAMO

Step up to the oche! Your aim reticle wobbles, the wind pushes it around, and every throw counts across 10 rounds.

How to Play:
• Watch the red reticle wobble around the dartboard.
• Press SPACE, click, or tap the board to throw when the reticle is over the bullseye.
• Bullseye = 50 pts, inner ring = 40, middle rings = 30 / 20, outer ring = 10, board edge = 5, miss = 0.
• A new wind gust shifts your aim every round — check the HUD wind direction and strength.
• Reach 300 points to win. Fall short after round 10 and it is game over.
• Press R at any time to restart.

Keyboard: SPACE to throw  |  Mouse: click to throw  |  Touch: tap to throw
