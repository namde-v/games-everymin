🚀 ROCKET RODEO

Ride your rocket through a wild asteroid field and wrangle 25 fuel cells to win!

How to Play:
• Move: Arrow keys / WASD, or drag with mouse / touch.
• Tether Lasso: Hold SPACE, mouse button, or touch to swing your glowing tether. Fuel cells caught inside the tether ring are collected!
• Gravity Wells: Purple swirls pull your rocket toward them — steer clear or slingshot around.
• Asteroids: Hitting one costs 1 hull point. You have 3 hull points.
• Win: Collect 25 fuel cells. Lose: Hull reaches 0.
• Tip: Lasso from a distance — you do not need to touch a cell to grab it while the tether is out.
Press Enter or Space to start • Sound starts after you press Start
