How to play
  • Click, tap, or press Space / R on a tile to rotate it 90 degrees. Each rotation uses 1 move.
  • Move the selection with Arrow keys or WASD, then rotate with Space, Enter, or R.
  • Power flows only where two neighbouring tiles both point at each other. Light all lamps to finish the level.
  • Short circuit: if the powered wires form a closed loop, that attempt fails at once.
  • You have limited moves per level and 3 fails per level. Fail a level 3 times and it is Game Over.
  • Complete all 6 levels to win. Larger grids and tighter move limits appear as you advance.

Fully offline single-file game. Keyboard, mouse and touch supported.
