⚓ HARBOR HAVEN

Can you run a busy harbor for 6 shifts?

  • Goal: Earn 800 coins across 6 shifts. Each fully loaded or unloaded ship pays its fee.
  • How to play: Tap an incoming ship to select it, then tap a free dock to berth it. Tap Service (or press Space) repeatedly to load/unload its cargo before its departure timer runs out.
  • Watch your resources: Every service uses fuel and crew. If either is too low, servicing stops — buy fuel or rest your crew, but that costs coins.
  • Lose if: 3 ships depart unserved, or you finish shift 6 with fewer than 800 coins.
  • Tips: Big ships pay more but take longer. Serve the ship with the shortest timer first. Keep fuel and crew above 20.

Keyboard: Enter to start • 1-4 to berth • Space to service • R to restart
