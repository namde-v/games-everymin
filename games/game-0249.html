Accordion Alley

Busk your way down the alley! Notes roll in from the right. Squeeze the accordion exactly when a note lands on the glowing beat line.

How to Play:
• Alternate sides! Notes are marked L or R. Press Left / A for L notes and Right / D for R notes — or tap the on-screen buttons.
• Timing matters: Perfect = 150 pts, Good = 80 pts, Early/Late = 40 pts. Misses break your combo and drain pressure.
• Pressure: Your bellows slowly leak. Good hits refill it. If pressure hits 0, the music stops — Game Over!
• Win: Reach 4000 points before the song ends to win the street crown.
• Tip: alternate L-R-L-R even when notes repeat — the crowd loves the pumping motion!

Keyboard: A / ArrowLeft = Left squeeze  |  D / ArrowRight = Right squeeze  |  Enter = Start / Restart
