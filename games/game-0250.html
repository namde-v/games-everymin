🎳 Bowling Blitz

Knock them down. Chase 120+ points!

How to Play:
1. Move the mouse, drag on touch, or use ← → to aim the ball.
2. Use A / D or the Hook buttons to add curve (hook).
3. Press Throw (Space or tap Throw) to start the power meter, then press again to release.
4. High power + centre entry = strike. Pick up spares on roll 2.
5. Play all 10 frames. Score 120 or more to WIN, otherwise it is Game Over.

Keyboard: ← → aim • A/D hook • Space throw • R restart
Mouse + touch fully supported.
