Corgi Canyon

How to Play: Dash through the canyon, leap over rocks and duck under cacti, and gobble every treat you can find. Reach 2000 points to win!

Jump: Up Arrow / W / Space / Tap upper half  |  Slide: Down Arrow / S / Tap lower half
Double Jump: Press Jump again mid-air  |  Restart: R key after Win / Game Over
Mouse click also jumps. Treats = 50 points. Golden bone = 150 points. Distance adds points too!

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads needed — everything runs offline.
