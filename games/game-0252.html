HEX HIVE

Welcome to the hive! The bee's path to the flower is broken. Every hex tile holds a piece of golden honey-path, but the tiles have been spun out of place.

  • Goal: Rotate tiles so one unbroken honey-path runs from the HIVE (left) to the FLOWER (right).
  • Tap / click a tile to rotate it 60 degrees. Each rotation costs 1 move.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys move the glowing cursor, Space or R rotates, Enter starts / restarts.
  • Touch: Tap a tile to rotate it. No swiping needed.
  • Finish a level before your moves run out. You have 3 attempts for the whole game. Complete all 5 levels to win!
  • Paths light up gold when they connect. Watch the hive glow when the route is complete.

How to Play: click a tile to spin it. A path segment only counts when both neighbouring tiles point at each other. Plan ahead — moves are limited!
