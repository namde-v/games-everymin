🌵 CACTUS KINGDOM 🌵

Rule a desert garden for 7 days. Plant cacti, water them wisely, harvest their blooms and sell them. Reach 600 coins before Day 7 ends to Win!

How to Play

  • Plant: Pick a seed below (keys 1-4), then tap / click an empty brown plot.
  • Water: Press W or tap Water Mode, then tap a plant. Water costs 4 water per plant. Dry plants wither!
  • Harvest & Sell: When a cactus blooms pink, tap it (or Harvest Mode + tap) to sell it for coins.
  • Buy Water: Tap Buy Water: 15 coins buys 20 water. Do not run dry.
  • Next Day: Tap Next Day or press N to advance. Each day is also 40 seconds long.
  • Weather: Watch for Heatwave (plants dry fast), Rain (free water!) and Bloom Festival (double bloom prices).
  • Lose if: You go broke with nothing to sell, 8 plants wither, or Day 7 ends under 600 coins.

Keyboard: 1-4 seeds · W water · H harvest · N next day · B buy water · R restart · Enter start

Tip: Barrel Cacti are cheap and fast. Saguaro pays the most but takes longest. Mix fast and valuable crops!
