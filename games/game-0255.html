🏹 Javelin Journey

Can you reach 300 points in 10 throws?

How to Play: Set your launch angle and power, watch the wind, then throw! A strong tailwind carries the javelin farther; a headwind holds it back. Land close to the golden target zone for bonus points.

Controls: Arrow Up / Down = angle • Arrow Left / Right = power • Space = throw • R = restart • Mouse drag on the field = aim • Touch drag = aim • Tap Throw = throw.

Scoring: every metre earns points, plus +20 for landing within 5 m of the target and +10 within 10 m. Weak throws score little, so commit to your power! After round 10, 300+ points wins the championship.
