MOLE MINER

How to Play: You are a brave little mole under the earth. Dig tunnels through the dirt, collect 30 sparkling gems to win, and watch out — loose rocks fall when the dirt below them is dug away! A falling rock costs a life. You have 3 lives.

Move: Arrow keys or WASD  •  Click / tap a tile to step toward it  •  Swipe to dig in that direction  •  On-screen arrows work on touch screens.
Rocks cannot be dug through — dig around them. Cave-ins rumble every so often: get out from under a shaking rock!
Press Enter or click Start to begin. Press R to restart instantly after a win or game over.

Tip: clear dirt under a rock from the side, then step away quickly. Gems sparkle — follow the sparkle!
