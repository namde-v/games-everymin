BEAT BOXER

🥊 Punch on the beat. Don't drop the rhythm!

How to Play: Beats scroll down two lanes toward the hit line. When a beat lands on the line, punch it at exactly the right moment. Left beats need a left punch, right beats need a right punch. Perfect hits build your combo and score big. Misses and wrong punches drain your stamina.

A / ← Left Punch    L / → Right Punch    Tap pads Touch / Mouse

Perfect = 150 pts  |  Good = 100 pts  |  OK = 50 pts  |  Miss = -15 stamina
Combo bonus: +10 pts per combo level  |  Win at 4000 points  |  Game Over at 0 stamina

Tip: listen for the bass thump on every beat. The glove flashes when you are on time.

🏆 YOU WIN!

Champion of the ring!

You hit the beat, kept your combo alive, and scored 4000 points. The crowd goes wild!

Press R or Enter to restart instantly.

😡 KNOCKED OUT!

Your stamina ran out.

Missed beats and mistimed punches wear you down. Watch the hit line, time your punches, and protect that stamina bar.
