10 rounds. One arrow each. 300 points wins the crown.

How to Play:
• Mouse / Touch: Press and drag anywhere from the bow, pull back like a slingshot, release to loose the arrow.
• Keyboard: Up / Down aim, Left / Right power, Space or Enter shoots, R restarts.
• Wind matters: A right wind pushes arrows right, a left wind pushes them left. Aim into the wind.
• Scoring: Bullseye 50 • Inner gold 40 • Red 30 • Blue 20 • Outer white 10 • Miss 0.
• Targets drift up and down and get faster in later rounds. A perfect round streak earns bonus points.
