TEMPEST RIDER

Ride the storm. Chase the crest. Survive the sea.

Steer your surfer left and right down endless waves. Ride the glowing wave crests for a speed boost and bonus points, grab golden shells, and dodge rocks and spinning whirlpools.

HOW TO PLAY
← / → Arrow keys or A / D — Steer left and right
Mouse — Move the mouse to steer
Touch — Drag your finger to steer
Crest (bright wave band) = +speed and +points  |  Shell = +50 points
Rock or whirlpool hit = lose 1 life  |  3 hits = Game Over
WIN: Reach 2000 points or surf 1500 metres!

3 lives. Speed rises every 10 seconds. Good luck, surfer!
