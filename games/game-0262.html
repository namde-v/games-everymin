PRISM PATH

Bend light. Light every crystal. Win.

How to Play: A beam fires from the glowing emitter on the left. Click or tap any mirror tile to rotate it between two diagonal angles. Arrows move the glowing cursor, Space / Enter rotates the selected tile.

Guide the beam through all 3 crystals before you run out of moves. Each rotation costs 1 move. Wrong mirrors can send the beam into a dark block or off the board.

Keyboard: Arrows + Space · Mouse: Click a mirror · Touch: Tap a mirror · R resets.

Out of Moves

Try a different mirror arrangement — the solution needs all four path mirrors set correctly.

Tip: Start at the emitter and follow the beam. If it stops at a block, rotate the last mirror it hit. The four path mirrors are at (3,3), (3,5), (5,5) and (5,2) in 1-based grid terms — but you must find their correct angles yourself.
