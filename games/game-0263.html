How to Play (quick)

Buy ingredients, bake goods, then serve customers before their patience runs out. Each day lasts 75 seconds and costs 20🪙 rent at dawn (except Day 1). Reach 300🪙 to win. 6 unhappy customers, or failing to pay rent, ends the game.

🍞 Pastry Palace

Goal: Earn 300 coins by the end of Day 5. You start with 80 coins and a small pantry.

How to Play

  • Buy flour, sugar, eggs and butter with the buttons (or keys F, S, E, B).
  • Bake Bread, Cookies and Cakes (keys 1, 2, 3). Each uses ingredients and oven time.
  • Serve customers: tap or click a customer on the canvas when you have their order in stock. Served customers pay you; impatient ones leave unhappy.
  • Survive: 75 seconds per day, 20 coins rent each new morning. 6 unhappy customers = Game Over.
  • Win: Reach 300 coins. Lose: 6 unhappy customers, or bankruptcy at rent time.

Mouse, touch and keyboard all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.
