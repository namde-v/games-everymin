Tightrope Tango

Step onto the high wire and dance your way across a 60-metre canyon! The wind gusts and your balance sways — correct it in time, step forward on the beat, and reach the far platform without falling.

How to Play:
← / → arrow keys or the LEAN buttons (or tap left / right side) to lean against the sway.
Press SPACE, STEP button, or tap the centre to step forward — best on the beat!
Keep the balance meter in the green zone. Falling off costs a life. 3 falls = game over.
Reach 60 m to win. Gusts get stronger as you cross!
