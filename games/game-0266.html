Glowbug Garden

Catch the blinking fireflies before the night ends. Dodge the wasps and chase the rare golden ones!

  • How to Play: Move your glowing jar with arrow keys / WASD, mouse, or touch.
  • ● Firefly = 10 points   ★ Golden firefly = 50 points
  • ● Wasp = lose 1 life   Catch 3 wasps and the game ends.
  • Reach 250 points in 90 seconds to win. Fireflies blink in patterns — catch them while they glow bright!

Tip: Golden fireflies blink fast and dart away. Corner them near the garden edges!
