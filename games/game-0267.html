GLYPH QUEST

How to Play: An ancient stone tablet flashes a secret sequence of runes. Memorize it! Then rebuild the exact sequence by tapping runes from the palette below the tablet, before the timer runs out.

Each level the sequence grows longer. Finish Level 5 to break the seal and win. A wrong sequence or an empty timer costs a life. You have 3 lives. Use a Clue to reveal the next rune (2 per level, costs 25 points).

Keyboard: press 1-8 to pick a palette rune, Backspace to undo, C for a clue. Mouse and touch work too.

You Win!

Tip: watch the tablet closely — the runes vanish fast on higher levels. Sound starts only after you press Start.
