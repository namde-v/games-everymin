⚓ PORT CAPTAIN

Ships are arriving with cargo. Dock them, unload them, and send trucks to the right warehouse before deadlines expire.

  • How to Play: Click / tap a waiting ship to select it, then click an empty dock to berth it.
  • Watch the crane unload it. When the bar is full, click the docked ship again to dispatch its truck and earn its fee.
  • Match cargo to its warehouse color: Containers = blue, Oil = red, Grain = yellow, Autos = green.
  • Ships waiting too long leave angry: you lose $150 and gain a Miss. Four misses ends the day.
  • If more than 3 ships are waiting, congestion costs $5 every second. Keep the harbor moving.
  • Win: Reach $2500 revenue before the 150-second day ends.
  • Keyboard: 1–4 select a waiting ship, A / S / D berth it at Dock 1 / 2 / 3, Space dispatches a finished ship, R restarts, Enter starts.
  • Mouse / Touch: Tap ships and docks on the harbor map. Everything is clickable.

Sound starts only after you press Start.

Day Complete

Select a ship, then an empty dock.

Tip: High-fee ships have shorter deadlines. Prioritize red flashing ships — they are about to leave. Trucks earn the fee only when cargo goes to the matching warehouse, which the game does automatically on dispatch.
