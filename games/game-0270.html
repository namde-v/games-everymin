🥌 CURLING CLASH

How to Play: You and the rival each throw 4 stones per end, for 4 ends. Aim from the hack, set power, and release. While your stone slides, sweep furiously to make it glide farther and curl toward the button.

After all stones are thrown, only the team with the stone closest to the button scores — one point for every one of its stones closer than the rival's best stone. Knock rival stones out of the house to steal points!

Mouse: move to aim, hold to charge power, release to throw. Touch: drag to aim, release to throw, tap rapidly to sweep. Keyboard: ← → aim, ↑ ↓ power, SPACE hold/release to throw, tap SPACE rapidly to sweep. Sweep keys also include S and tapping the canvas.

Sweeping matters: it reduces friction and bends the stone toward the house. Guard stones can block the button — use a takeout to blast them away.
