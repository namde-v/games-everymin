Penguin Plunge

How to Play: Your penguin is sliding down an icy slope! Steer left and right to dodge grey ice rocks and dark cracks, and collect fish for points.

Fish = +10 points  •  Golden fish = +25 points
Rock or crack = lose 1 life (you flash briefly and stay safe)
Reach 500 points to slide into the cozy igloo and WIN!

Keyboard: ← → or A / D to steer, Space to start / restart
Mouse: move or click left / right side  •  Touch: drag to steer
