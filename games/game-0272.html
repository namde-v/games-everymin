🎨 Mosaic Maker

Puzzle / Tile Pattern Matching

How to Play:

  • A target mosaic is shown on the right. Your job is to copy it exactly on the 6 × 6 board.
  • Tap a colour in the palette, then tap a board cell to place it. Tap a filled cell with another colour to replace it.
  • Correct tiles score points. A wrong colour, or a tile on a cell that should stay empty, counts as a mistake. 5 mistakes = Game Over.
  • Complete the pattern with at least 80% accuracy to win. Faster wins earn a time bonus.
  • Use New Pattern to shuffle a fresh design, Undo to take back a move, and Clear Board to start the board again.
  • Keyboard: Arrow Keys move the cursor, 1–6 pick a colour, Space places a tile, U undoes, R restarts.
  • Each win raises the Level and makes the next pattern denser and trickier.

Mouse, touch, and keyboard all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.
