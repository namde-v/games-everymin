Lighthouse Keeper

How to Play: Ships sail in from the right at night. Aim your golden beam at a ship and it will steer toward the green harbour at the top right. Unlit ships drift and can strike the grey rocks — each wreck costs a life.

Controls: Mouse move = aim beam  |  Drag on touch = aim
Arrow Keys / A-D = rotate beam  |  Space = boost beam
F = refill oil (30 coins)  |  H = foghorn (slows ships)
R = restart  |  Earn 20 coins per safe ship. Win by guiding 12 ships home.

Watch your oil: boosting drains it faster, and fog shortens your beam. Use the foghorn when a storm of ships arrives.

You Win!

Tip: keep the beam on the ship closest to a rock. A lit ship turns toward safety; a dark ship keeps drifting.
