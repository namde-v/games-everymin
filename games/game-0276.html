🌋 VOLCANO VAULT

Reach the summit. Claim the vault.

Leap between rising rock platforms above a sea of lava. Collect gems, dodge lava bubbles, and climb 300 metres to the summit.

How to Play
← → or A / D — Move  •  ↑ / Space / W — Jump
Mouse — click left / right half to move, on-screen JUMP button to jump
Touch — ◀ ▶ buttons, JUMP button, or swipe up to jump
R — Restart  •  Falling in lava costs 1 life. You have 3 lives.

Gems are worth 50 points. Every metre climbed is 1 point. Bubbles pop — don't touch them!

YOU WIN!

Keyboard: Arrows / WASD + Space  •  R restarts  •  Click the canvas halves to steer on mouse
