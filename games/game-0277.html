The castle wizard sealed the gate with a secret 4-rune code, chosen from 6 runes. You have 10 guesses to crack it.

How to Play

  • Build a 4-rune guess from the rune palette, then submit it.
  • Gold clue = right rune, right place. Silver clue = right rune, wrong place.
  • Runes may repeat. Clue order never tells you the slot — use logic to narrow it down.
  • Win by cracking the code. Score grows with guesses remaining and speed.
  • Keyboard: ← → move slot, ↑ ↓ cycle rune, 1–6 pick a rune, Enter submit, Backspace clear, R restart.
  • Mouse / Touch: tap a slot, tap a rune, tap Submit Guess.

Tip: a rune that earns no new clue on a second try is probably absent — cross it off mentally and move on.
