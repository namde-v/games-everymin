Zoo Zookeeper

Strategy / Animal Habitat Management. Your animals get hungry, dirty, and bored. Happy animals attract visitors and earn coins — unhappy ones cost you stars!

How to Play

  • Watch the bars: Hunger, Dirt, and Boredom rise over time. Lower them before happiness falls to zero.
  • Act fast: Select an enclosure, then Feed, Clean, or Play. Each action costs coins.
  • Earn coins: Happy animals attract visitors. Visitors pay you coins every few seconds.
  • Survive 5 days: Each day lasts 45 seconds and adds a new animal. Lose all 3 stars and it is Game Over.
  • Controls: Arrow Keys + 1/2/3, mouse clicks, or touch taps. Press R anytime to restart.
