Banjo Bonanza

Notes roll down four banjo strings toward the golden strum line. Strum each string at just the right moment!

How to Play
  • Keyboard: Press A (G string), S (D string), D (A string), F (E string). Press Space to start or restart.
  • Mouse / Touch: Tap the string pads below the stage, or click a string pad, when its note reaches the strum line.
  • Scoring: Perfect = 100 pts, Good = 50 pts. Chord notes (two strings at once) are worth double — hit both together for a bonus!
  • Win: Finish all 50 notes in the setlist. Game Over: Miss 12 notes and the song falls apart.

Tip: watch the glow — the strum line flashes when a note is in the Perfect zone.
