Deepsea Dispatch

You are the reef's fastest diver-courier! Grab glowing parcels, then swim them to the golden checkpoint before the eels and sharks catch you.

How to Play:
• Arrow Keys / WASD — swim up, down, left, right
• Mouse move — steer toward the mouse pointer
• Touch drag — swim toward your finger
• Collect a parcel (📦), then touch the glowing checkpoint (⭐) to deliver it
• Avoid eels and sharks — each hit costs 1 life. You have 3 lives.
• Score 100 points per delivery, plus a speed bonus. Win at 12 deliveries!

Tip: Grab parcels near the reef edge first, then cut across open water only when the checkpoint is clear.
