How to Play

Goal: Match every cell of the target pattern exactly.
Mouse / touch: Click or tap a cell to toggle it between thread colours.
Arrow Keys: Move the selection cursor around the loom.
Space: Toggle the selected cell.
U: Undo your last toggle (refunds the move).
H: Hint — flashes one cell that still differs (costs 2 moves).
Scoring: +100 per completed pattern, plus 5 bonus points per move left. Finish fast for a bigger bonus!
Lose: If moves reach zero before the pattern matches, the loom unravels.
