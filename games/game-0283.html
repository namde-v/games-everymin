🌳 Grove Guardian

How to Play: You run an orchard for one day. Plant trees on empty plots, water them to grow faster, prune them for a bigger harvest, and harvest ripe fruit. Fill customer orders before they expire to earn money and reputation. Reach $120 before the day timer ends to win!

🍎 Apple sells for $12   🍐 Pear sells for $16   🍒 Cherry sells for $22
Planting costs $5. Watering is free. Pruning adds +1 fruit to the next harvest.

Controls: Mouse / touch click plots and buttons • Arrow Keys select a plot • 1 Plant • 2 Water • 3 Harvest • P Prune • Enter confirm • R restart
