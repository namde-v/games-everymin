Cajon Cadence

How to Play: Notes fall down four drum lanes. Hit the matching drum exactly when a note crosses the glowing hit line.

  • Perfect (near the line): +100 points · Good: +50 points · Miss: lose 1 life and your combo
  • Keyboard: D, F, J, K for the four lanes · Space to start or restart
  • Mouse: click a drum pad · Touch: tap a drum pad
  • Win by finishing the 60-second song with 2,000+ points. Game over if you lose all 5 lives.

Every sound is made live in your browser — no downloads, no internet needed.
