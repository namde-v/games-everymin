Boomerang Bounce

How to Play: You stand at the lower-left. Aim your boomerang, throw it, and let its curve sweep back toward you. Hit floating targets on the outbound and return path for bonus points. Avoid the rocky obstacles — smacking one ends that throw early. Reach 150 points in 12 throws to win!

Gold targets = 30, Pink = 20, Blue = 10. Small fast targets are worth more. A return-path hit earns a +10 Curve Bonus.
