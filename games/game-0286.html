🏙️ Skyline Swing

Grapple. Swing. Land. Survive the skyline!

Run across the rooftops, hold to grapple an anchor above the gaps, build momentum, and release at just the right moment to fly to the next roof. Collect stars, avoid falling, and reach the final golden rooftop to win.

How to Play:
• Space / click / tap & HOLD — grapple the nearest anchor and swing
• Release — let go and fly forward
• Arrow Keys — Left / Right steer in the air, Up adds a little pump while swinging
• Touch — touch & hold to grapple, lift your finger to release
• Land on a rooftop to save your progress. Fall 3 times and it's game over.

Tip: release near the top of your swing for the longest flight!

You Win!
