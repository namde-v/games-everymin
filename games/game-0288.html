🚇 Subway Scheduler

Goal: Serve 20 passengers before the 180-second rush hour ends. Two trains run the metro loop. Passengers wait at stations for a ride to their destination station.

  • How to play: Trains stop briefly at each station and pick up waiting passengers, then drop them at their destination. Watch the waiting timers — a passenger waiting over 40 seconds is missed.
  • Spacing: If the trailing train catches the train ahead, they collide! Hold a train back to keep distance.
  • Switches: Click a gold switch diamond (or press S) to toggle the selected train between the Local loop and the Express shortcut, which skips two stations.
  • Hold / release: Hold Space to hold the selected train, release to let it go.

Controls: Mouse / touch — click trains and switches  •  Arrow Keys — select train / switch  •  Space — hold / release train  •  R — restart

Tip: hold the faster train when it closes in, and use Express only when no one is waiting at the skipped stations.
