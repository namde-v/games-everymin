Mirror Meadow

Morning sun is trapped in the meadow! Rotate the mirrors and place new ones so one bright beam bounces through the grass and wakes every flower before you run out of moves.

Keyboard: arrows to move, Space / Enter to rotate or place, R to restart. Mouse and touch work too.

How to Play

  • Goal: Light every flower (🌸) with the sunbeam. A flower glows golden when lit.
  • Rotate: Click or tap a mirror to flip it between / and \. Each rotation costs 1 move.
  • Place: If you have mirrors left, click an empty grass square to place a new / mirror. Each placement costs 1 move.
  • Mirror rules: A / mirror turns a right-moving beam up and a down-moving beam left. A \ mirror turns a right-moving beam down and a left-moving beam up. Walls (#) block the beam.
  • Win: All flowers lit. Lose a life: Moves reach zero before all flowers are lit. Lose all 3 lives and the game ends — but you can restart instantly.
  • Controls: Mouse click, touch tap, or arrow keys + Space / Enter. R restarts the level.
🌞 = sun source  •  / \ = mirrors  •  🌸 = flower target  •  ▓ = stone wall  •  Golden beam = lit path
