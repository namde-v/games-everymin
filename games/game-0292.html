☀ PHOTON PILGRIM

Solar Wind Riding

Ride the solar wind across the void! Pilot your light-sail craft, collect glowing photons to charge your journey, and dodge tumbling asteroids and debris.

How to Play: Steer with Arrow Keys / WASD, move with the mouse, or drag with touch. Wind gusts push you sideways — surf them, don't fight them! Grab photons for points, reach 1500 points to WIN. You have 3 lives — one asteroid hit costs a life.

⌨ Arrow Keys / WASD  |  🖱 Mouse move  |  👆 Touch drag  |  P = Pause  |  R = Restart
