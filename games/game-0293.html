Cinderfield Cultivator

The volcano left the fields grey, but the soil still remembers how to grow. Plant crops, water them, and harvest before the ash storms bury them.

How to Play
Choose a crop (1 / 2 / 3), click a plot to plant, water it, then harvest when it glows golden. Ember Wheat is fast and cheap, Ashroot is balanced, Cinder Melon is slow but very valuable. Ash storms arrive every 25 seconds and wither unharvested crops — harvesting just before a storm is smart farming. Buy extra water at the well with harvested value. Reach 300 harvest value to win. Lose all 3 hearts to storms, or run out of seeds, water and growing crops, and the farm is lost.

Keyboard: Arrows move, P plant, W water, H harvest, 1-3 select crop. Mouse and touch work too.

Tip: watered crops grow twice as fast. A storm withers every unharvested crop, so never leave a golden field standing when the sky turns grey. Melons survive one storm if fully watered.
