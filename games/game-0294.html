🎵 Metronome Maze

How to Play

  • Move on the beat: the golden dot flashes and the metronome ticks. Step only while it glows.
  • Off-beat move = lost life. Watch the beat bar — move near the flash, not between beats.
  • Collect ♪ notes for +100 points. Perfect on-beat steps earn +10.
  • Reach the golden ★ exit before the timer runs out to clear the level.
  • Tempo rises every level: 90 → 110 → 130 BPM. Faster beats, tighter timing, bigger bonuses.
  • Controls: Arrow keys / WASD, on-screen pad, swipe, or tap a pad button. Click the pad with a mouse too.

3 lives. 3 levels. One rhythm. Fully offline — sound starts only after you press Start.
