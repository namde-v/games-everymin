Paper Plane Patrol

Glide your paper plane across the sky, thread every floating ring, ride the updrafts and dodge the downdrafts!

Goal: Fly through all 12 golden rings to win. You have 3 crashes.
Controls: Arrow Keys / WASD to climb and dive · Mouse: move to steer · Touch: drag to steer.
Physics: Diving builds speed, pulling up trades speed for height. Green zones push you up, red zones drag you down.
Crashes: Hitting a storm cloud, the ground, or stalling out costs a life. Fly through a ring to bank it as a checkpoint.
