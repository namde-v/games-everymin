ᚱ RUNE RELAY ᚱ

Watch the runes. Remember the relay. Repeat it.

An ancient circle of nine runes glows in sequence. Memorize the pattern, then tap the runes back in the same order to relay the magic onward.

  • Watch carefully: runes light up one at a time. That is your sequence.
  • Tap, click, or press keys 1–9 to repeat the sequence in order.
  • Decoy runes glow faintly to mislead you — only the bright flash counts.
  • Each round adds one more rune and the timer gets shorter. Beat all 8 rounds to win.
  • Wrong rune or timeout costs a life. You have 3 lives. Score grows with round and speed.
  • Keyboard: keys 1–9 match the grid left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Mouse and touch work too.
