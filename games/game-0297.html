QUARRY QUEST

You are a brave miner with a flickering lantern. Dig tunnels, collect 12 gems, and dodge falling rocks before the light runs out.

How to Play
• Move: Arrow keys / WASD, on-screen arrows, swipe, or tap a nearby tile.
• Dig: walk into soft rock to tunnel through it. Solid dark rock cannot be dug.
• Collect gems for 100 points. A rock on your head costs a life.
• Lantern oil: grab oil cans to add 15 seconds. Reach 12 gems to win.
• Watch out: loose rocks fall when the ground below them is dug away.

Tip: dig sideways under a loose rock, then step away quickly. Gravity is not your friend.
