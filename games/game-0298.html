STARLIGHT STITCH

Ancient constellations have unravelled. Re-stitch them, star by star, before the night fades.

How to Play
• Goal: Complete all 5 constellations to win.
• Order matters: Start on the pulsing gold star, then follow the pattern in order.
• No crossing: Your thread must never cross an earlier thread.
• No repeats: Each star can be used only once per constellation.
• Lives: A wrong star, a repeat, or a crossing costs 1 life. Lose all 5 and the night ends.
• Controls: Mouse click, touch tap, or Arrow keys + Enter / Space. Press H for help.

Score 100 per stitch • 250 bonus per constellation completed
