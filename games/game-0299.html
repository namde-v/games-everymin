LIGHTHOUSE LEDGER

You are the keeper of Gull Rock Light. One long night, one lamp, and a busy shipping lane full of rocks. Guide the fleet home and keep an honest ledger.

How to Play
  • Aim the beam: move the mouse, drag on touch, or press Left / Right arrows. The beam must touch a ship while it is in the golden warning zone.
  • Warn ships: a warned ship turns away from the rocks and is saved. An unwarned ship that reaches the rocks crashes and costs a life.
  • Fuel: the lamp burns fuel. Press F or the Refuel button before it runs dry. Refuelling has a short cooldown.
  • Log ships: every saved ship joins the ledger queue. Click its correct type (Cargo, Fishing, or Passenger) to log it for bonus points. Keys 1, 2, 3 work too.
  • Win: survive the whole night with at least one life left and warn at least 10 ships. Lose all lives, or let the lamp stay dark too long, and the night is lost.

Mouse / touch to aim • Arrow keys to rotate • F to refuel • 1 / 2 / 3 to log
