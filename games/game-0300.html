🌙 LUNAR LEAP

How to Play: You are a moon explorer hopping between craters in low gravity. Charge a jump, release at the right moment, and land squarely on each glowing landing pad in order.

Land gently and near the centre for bonus points. Land too hard, fall into a crater, or fly off the top of the sky and you lose a life. Visit all 6 pads to win!

Controls: ← → or A / D to move • Hold SPACE / W / JUMP to charge, release to jump • Mouse click also starts • Touch buttons below work on phones

Tip: short hops for close pads, full power for wide craters. Watch the red danger line at the top — cross it and you drift into space! Green pads are done, gold is your next target.
