🐠 Coral Courier

ARCADE • UNDERWATER DELIVERY

How to Play: You are a courier fish in a coral reef. Swim to a glowing parcel, pick it up, then deliver it to the sparkling checkpoint shell. Complete 5 deliveries to win!

Watch out: jellyfish drift and sharks patrol. You have 3 lives — a hit costs one life and drops your parcel. Dash through tight spots, but dashing has a short cooldown.

Controls: Arrow Keys / WASD to swim • Mouse move to steer • Touch drag to steer • Space to dash • R to restart

Tip: parcels glow gold, checkpoints glow green. Stay near coral for cover, but never stop moving when a shark is near!
