Hex Harvest

Rotate your harvest into place on a honeycomb board. Swap neighbouring hex tiles to line up 3 or more of the same crop in a connected cluster, clear them for points, and reach the target before you run out of moves.

How to Play
• Click / tap a hex, then click / tap a neighbour to swap — or drag from one hex to a neighbour.
• Arrow Keys move the glowing cursor. Space / Enter selects or swaps. R restarts instantly.
• A swap costs 1 move. Matched crops are worth 50 points each; clusters of 5+ earn a bonus.
• Chain reactions from refilled tiles score extra points without costing a move.
• Reach 1200 points in 25 moves to win. Run out of moves first and the harvest is over.
