❄️ GLACIER GOVERNOR

How to Play: You govern a frozen colony for 8 days. Assign workers to shelters (warmth), fishing huts (food) and research posts (science). Each day the colony eats food and burns warmth — storms make it worse.

  • Mouse / Touch: Tap a building to assign a worker to it. Use the panel buttons to remove workers or add heat.
  • Arrow Keys: Move the glowing cursor between buildings. Space / Enter: Assign a worker.
  • N: Advance to the next day. R: Restart at any time.
  • Win: Reach Day 8 with population 18+, food above 0, warmth above 25 and science 20+.
  • Lose: Food or warmth hits 0, or population falls below 10.

Tip: Shelters first in a storm, fishing when food is low, research when you are stable. Balance is everything.
