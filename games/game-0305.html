COMET CATCHER

Catch 20 comets before you miss 3!

How to Play: Your catcher rides the glowing orbit ring around the star. Comets streak across the sky on curved paths. Steer the catcher onto a comet's path, then press Space (or click / tap) to fire a catch pulse just as the comet passes. A well-timed pulse catches from farther away. Catch in a row to build a streak bonus. Let a comet fly off screen and you lose a life — 3 misses ends the run.

Move: Arrow Keys / WASD  |  Aim: Mouse Move / Touch Drag
Catch Pulse: Space / Click / Quick Tap  |  Restart: R
