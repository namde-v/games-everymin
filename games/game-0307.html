🏺 Bazaar Baron 🏺

Buy low, sell high, and become a legend of the market!

💰 Gold: 120
📅 Day: 1 / 12
🎯 Target: 600 gold
💎 Fortune: 120

Bazaar Baron

How to Play:
• You start with 120 gold and 12 market days to reach a fortune of 600.
• Four stalls sell Spice, Silk, Pottery and Gems. Prices move every day with supply, demand and surprise events.
• Buy when a price is low, hold it, then sell when it spikes. Your fortune = gold + value of goods you hold.
• Click a stall or use Arrow Keys to select it. Buy and Sell buttons trade one unit. Press N to open the next market day.
• Reach 600 before day 12 ends to win. Run out of days below target and the bazaar closes on you!

Mouse / Touch: click stalls & buttons  |  Arrows: select  |  Space: Buy  |  Enter: Sell  |  N: Next Day  |  R: Restart
