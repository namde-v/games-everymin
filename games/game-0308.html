ECHO EXPLORER

The cave is pitch black. Your sonar is your only light.

How to Play: Move through the dark cave, ping to briefly reveal walls, crystals, creatures and the exit. Collect all 6 glowing crystals to unlock the golden exit, then reach it. Touching a cave creature costs a life. Pings recharge quickly — use them often!

Arrow Keys / WASD to move • Space, mouse click or touch tap to ping • R to restart

Tip: ping toward a new direction before you walk into it. Creatures patrol back and forth — watch their revealed glow and time your moves.
