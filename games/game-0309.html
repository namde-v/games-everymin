ORBIT ORGANIZER

Can you untangle the orbits?

How to Play:
• 18 satellites drift on 3 orbits, in 3 colours.
• Click / tap one satellite, then another, to swap them — even across orbits.
• Sort each orbit so all of its satellites share one colour.
• You have 150 seconds. Sorting an orbit earns 200 points; every swap earns 10.
• Arrow Keys move the selection, Space / Enter selects, R restarts.

Tip: start with the orbit that already has the most of one colour.
