STORM SHEPHERD

Precision / Wind Herding — guide the storm home before it hits town!

How to Play:
• Storm clouds drift in from the west, heading for the town on the right.
• Steer your wind source (the glowing fan) with Arrow Keys / WASD.
• Mouse move / drag or touch drag to aim and blow gusts that push clouds.
• Hold Space (or hold the mouse / finger down) for a strong gust blast.
• Herd each cloud down into a rain barrel along the bottom to fill it.
• A cloud that reaches the town costs 1 town health. Fill 5 barrels to win; 3 town strikes ends the game.
• Press R at any time to restart.

Tip: push clouds from behind and slightly above — gusts shove them away from your fan. Gentle nudges beat wild blasts.

You Win!

Press R or tap Restart to play again.
