EMBER ESCAPE

How to Play: You are a brave runner fleeing a erupting volcano. Dodge falling embers and jump over lava gaps. Catch blue water drops for +25 points. Surviving embers earns points over time. Win at 500 points — lose all 3 lives and the volcano wins.

⌨️ Arrow keys / WASD to move  •  SPACE to dash  •  🖱️ Move mouse to steer  •  👆 Drag on touch screen  •  R to restart

Dash gives a brief burst of speed and brief invulnerability. Lava gaps on the ground hurt — jump or dash over them!

GAME OVER

Press R or click Play Again for an instant restart.
