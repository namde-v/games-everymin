MIRROR MAZE

Guide a single laser beam through every glowing target by rotating mirrors on the grid. One wrong turn can send the beam off the board.

How to Play
• Click / tap a mirror to rotate it 90° (it flips between / and \). Each rotation costs 1 move.
• Arrow keys / WASD move the yellow selection box. Space / Enter rotates the selected mirror.
• Light all targets on a level to advance. Targets glow green when the beam touches them.
• Run out of rotations and you lose a life and retry the level. Lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over.
• Complete all 8 levels to win. Walls block the beam. The beam stops at the board edge.

Tip: trace the path backwards from a target to find the mirror that must feed it.
