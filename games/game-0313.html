Welcome, Sovereign!

Your desert kingdom awaits. Assign workers, balance food, water and stone, weather the sandstorms, and raise monuments to glory.

  • How to Play: Use the + / − buttons (or tap them) to assign workers to Farms, Wells and Quarries. Idle workers do nothing.
  • Press Build Monument when you can afford it (25 Stone, 15 Food, 10 Water). Build 3 monuments before Day 30 to win.
  • Press Next Day (N) to advance. Each day your people eat and drink; farms, wells and quarries produce.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys move the cursor, Space / Enter confirms, N advances a day, R restarts after the game ends.
  • Lose if: Food or Water reaches zero, Stability runs out, or Day 30 passes with fewer than 3 monuments.
