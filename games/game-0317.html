🔦 Lighthouse Keeper

Guide 15 ships safely past the rocks before 3 wreck. Ships sail in from the right at night. Your beam is their only guide: a ship caught in the light steers toward the safe channel and away from rocks. A ship left in the dark drifts — and drifts into trouble.

Drag on the sea (mouse or touch) to aim the beam  •  ← ↑ ↓ → fine-aim
Space / Enter: lamp on / off  •  W: sound storm warning  •  E: repair beam
N: end the night early (+40 oil, harder seas)  •  R: restart after Win / Game Over

Manage your lamp oil — the lamp burns oil while lit. Storms damage your beam if ships are not warned in time; press E to repair. Oil refills between nights. No ship is safe in the dark for long.

How to play: keep the beam sweeping the incoming ships, watch the storm banner, and never let oil hit zero mid-crossing.

Game Over

Or press R to restart instantly.

Press Start, then sweep your beam across the incoming ships.

Tip: a guided ship glows gold. A dark ship with a red wake is drifting toward danger.
