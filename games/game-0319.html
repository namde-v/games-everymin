Pendulum Pilot🪢 Swing. Release. Land.

Chain pendulum swings across floating islands and reach the golden goal platform on all 10 stages. One mistimed release and you are falling into the clouds!

  • Grab / Release: SPACE, mouse click, or screen tap. Press once to leap, grab a rope automatically when close, press again to let go.
  • Pump & steer: ← → arrow keys while swinging or flying. Drag left / right on touch screens to pump.
  • Land: drop onto a platform top to stand safely. Land on the gold platform to clear the stage.
  • Lives: falling off the bottom costs a life. You have 3 lives for the whole run.
  • Tip: release near the top of the forward swing for maximum distance. Higher, faster swings fly further.

Controls: SPACE / Click / Tap = grab & release  |  ← → = pump & steer  |  R = restart  |  P = pause
