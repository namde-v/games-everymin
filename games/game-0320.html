🦴 FOSSIL FINDER

Dig carefully. The bones are fragile.

Each dig site hides 3 fossils buried in an 8 × 8 grid. Use sonar numbers to work out where the bones are — then brush them out safely.

How to Play
⛏️ Shovel: digs an empty cell and reveals a sonar number = how many hidden bone cells touch it (including diagonals). Digging a bone with a shovel BREAKS it!
🖌️ Brush (B): uncovers a cell safely — perfect for suspected bones. Brushes are limited per site.
🖱️ / 👆 Click or tap a cell to use the current tool  •  ← ↑ ↓ → move cursor  •  Space / Enter dig  •  B switch tool  •  R restart
💥 Break 3 bones in total and the expedition ends. Clear all 5 sites to win!

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads.

You Win!

Press R to restart instantly.

Choose Shovel or Brush, then dig a cell. Numbers are your clues!
Sonar numbers count hidden bones in the 8 neighbouring cells. A 0 means no bone touches that cell — safe ground. Broken bones still count as found, but cost a break. Finish a site for a +500 bonus.
