SOLAR SWOOPER

Dive toward the sun. Grab its fire. Dodge the rocks.

How to Play
Fly your ship and collect glowing solar orbs (+10 points) as they stream up from the sun.
Avoid asteroids — each hit costs 1 life.
Reach 200 points to win. Lose all 3 lives and the run is over.
Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to fly • Mouse to steer • Touch & drag to fly.
Near the sun? Orbs are worth more there — but asteroids fly faster!

Sound starts only after you press Start. Best on keyboard, mouse, or touch.
