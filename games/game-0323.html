🌽 Vineyard Baron

Become a legendary wine baron before the end of Year 4!

How to Play:

  • Plant vines on empty plots (15 gold each). Vines grow over seasons.
  • Water and Prune growing vines to raise grape quality (up to ★★★).
  • Harvest ripe vines in Autumn for grapes, then Sell Wine — each grape earns 10 gold + quality bonus and reputation.
  • You have 6 worker actions per season. Press Next Season when done.
  • Each season brings weather: drought, rain, frost or pests can help or hurt your vines. Winter upkeep costs 20 gold.
  • WIN by reaching 600 gold and 40 reputation before Year 4 ends.
  • GAME OVER if you go bankrupt (gold below 0) or time runs out.
