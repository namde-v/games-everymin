🌋 Geyser Glider

How to Play: You are a winged glider soaring over a volcanic valley. Hold SPACE / ↑ / mouse / touch to flap and climb, release to dive. Fly through the glowing geyser updrafts — they shoot you skyward for free! Collect golden rings (+100) and glowing feathers (+25) for points.

Watch out for jagged rock spires — a crash costs one life. You have 3 lives.

🏆 WIN by flying 3,500 m  •  💥 GAME OVER at 0 lives

SPACE / ↑ = flap  •  Mouse hold = flap  •  Touch & hold = flap  •  P = pause

Game Over

Tip: ride a geyser column all the way up, then dive through a line of rings for a big combo.
