CIPHER CANYON

Ancient travellers carved messages into the canyon wall — but every letter was replaced by a petroglyph symbol. Crack the code, word by word, and reach the end of the trail.

  1. How to play: Tap a symbol on the wall (or in Step 1), then tap a letter (or press A–Z on your keyboard).
  2. A correct guess reveals that letter everywhere. A wrong guess costs 1 attempt.
  3. Use the clue and the frequency chart. Blue tiles are starting clues, given free.
  4. Solve all 5 messages to win. If your attempts run out, the trail ends.

Controls: mouse, touch, and keyboard — arrow keys move between symbols, Enter starts or restarts.
