AURORA OUTPOST

Strategy / Simulation — Arctic Research Station

Lead a tiny crew through 30 days of polar night. Finish 100% research before winter ends — without letting food, heat or morale collapse.

  1. Assign your 12 crew among Research, Food, Power and Morale.
  2. End the day and face the weather: storms, blizzards, auroras, breakdowns and rare supply drops.
  3. Win at 100% research. Lose if food or morale hits 0, the station freezes, or day 30 ends unfinished.
  4. Watch the sky: aurora nights are precious — rest your crew and let the lab shine.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Works with keyboard, mouse and touch.
