🎵 Tempo Tailor 🎵

Notes slide down the seam. Stitch each one exactly on the beat as it crosses the golden needle line!

How to Play:
• Press D F J K for the four lanes, or click / tap a lane.
• PERFECT (right on the line) = 100 pts  •  GOOD = 50 pts
• A mistimed stitch or a missed note snaps the thread — lose a life.
• Keep your combo alive for bonus points. Finish all 60 stitches with 2000+ points to WIN. Lose all 3 lives and it's Game Over.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Headphones recommended for the beat!
