🌋 Cinder Climber

How to Play

Jump from ledge to ledge up the volcano. Collect glowing gems for bonus points. Avoid falling embers and lava bursts — each hit costs a life.

⌨️ Arrow keys / A & D to move, Space / W / ↑ to jump
🖱️ Click left or right side to move, click the climber to jump
📱 Touch: tap left/right to move, tap the climber to jump

Reach the glowing summit flag to WIN. Falling off-screen or an ember hit loses a life. You have 3 lives.
