🏮 Lantern Logic

How to Play

  • Goal: Light every empty tile. A lantern shines up, down, left and right until it hits a dark wall.
  • Rule 1 — No glare: No two lanterns may shine on each other in the same row or column without a wall between them.
  • Rule 2 — Numbered walls: A number tells you exactly how many lanterns touch that wall (up / down / left / right). Plain walls have no clue.
  • Controls: Click or tap a tile to cycle Empty → 🏮 Lantern → ✕ Mark → Empty. Keyboard: Arrow keys move, Space or Enter cycles, H for hint, C to check.
  • Five nights, growing grids: Solve 5 × 5, 6 × 6, 7 × 7, 8 × 8 and 9 × 9. You have 3 lives and 5 hints for the whole game.
  • Fail state: Run out of moves on a puzzle and you lose a life and must retry it. Lose all 3 lives and the game is over. Solve all 5 nights to win.

Tip: Start with 0 clues (no lantern touches them) and 4 clues, then fill the darkest corners.
