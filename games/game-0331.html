QUARRY QUAKE

A quake has loosened the cliffs above the old gem quarry. Boulders are raining down — and so are the gems!

How to Play
Move with Arrow keys or A / D, or drag with mouse / finger.
Collect gems (+10) and rare golden gems (+25).
Dodge boulders — each hit costs 1 life.
Reach 300 points to WIN before your 3 lives run out.
Boulders fall faster every level. A shield pickup blocks one extra hit!

Gem = 10 points | Golden gem = 25 | Shield = blocks 1 hit

GAME OVER

Keyboard: Arrow keys / A-D to move, Space or P to pause. Mouse: move to steer, click to start. Touch: drag to move.
