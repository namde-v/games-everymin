Hexfield Harvest

Swap neighbouring hex crops, grow clusters of 3 or more, and fill the harvest baskets before you run out of moves!

How to Play
• Tap / click one hex, then tap / click a neighbouring hex to swap them.
• Drag or swipe is not needed: two taps swap a pair.
• Matching 3+ connected crops of the same kind clears them and scores points.
• Bigger clusters score bonus points and can trigger cascades.
• Harvest 15 Wheat, 15 Carrot and 15 Berry to win.
• A swap with no match costs 1 life. You have 3 lives and 30 moves.
• Keyboard: Arrow keys / WASD move the cursor, Enter or Space selects and swaps.

Tip: look for corners where one swap joins two groups at once. Cascades after a refill are free — they cost no extra move.
