Beacon Keeper

Seven nights. One lighthouse. Every ship counts.

How to Play

  • Aim the beam: move the mouse, drag on touch, or use A / D and arrow keys. The beam sweeps from the lighthouse tower.
  • Warn ships: shine the beam on a ship while it is inside the danger zone (the red-tinted water near the rocks) and it will turn away, safe.
  • Manage oil: the lamp burns oil while it is ON. Press Space, click the lamp button, or tap the tower to toggle it. Running dry in the dark is dangerous.
  • Protect the lens: the beam slowly wears the lens. A cracked lens makes a narrower beam. Repair it between nights with points earned from saved ships.
  • Between nights: spend points on oil and repairs, then begin the next night. Survive all 7 nights to win. Three wrecks and the coast is lost.
  • Storms: later nights bring faster ships and rough seas. Watch the warning bells: a ship flashing red is about to strike.

Controls: Mouse / Touch drag to aim  |  A / D or Arrow keys  |  Space = lamp  |  P = pause
