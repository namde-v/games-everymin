Sky Shepherd

You are a shepherd bird on a windy morning. Fluffy clouds are drifting away — herd them into the golden pen before the storm clouds catch you!

How to Play
• Move: Arrow keys / WASD, or drag with mouse or touch.
• Herd: Fly into a white cloud to nudge it along. Push it into the golden pen (top right) to herd it.
• Wind Gust: Press Space, click, or tap the Gust button to blast nearby clouds away from you.
• Avoid: Dark storm clouds. Touching one costs a life and scatters nearby clouds.
• Win: Herd 12 clouds into the pen. Lose: Lose all 3 lives.

Tip: herd clouds one at a time and use gusts when a storm cloud closes in.
