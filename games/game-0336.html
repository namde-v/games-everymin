✦ RUNE RELAY ✦

A memory-sequence puzzle. The ancient stones light up in a secret order. Watch carefully, then tap the runes back in exactly the same order.

How to Play

  • Watch: When a round starts, runes glow one by one. Memorise the order and each rune's sound.
  • Repeat: Tap / click the runes, or press keys 1-9, in the same order.
  • Grow: Each round adds one more rune. Round 1 has 3 runes; later rounds get longer and faster.
  • Score: Earn 10 points per correct rune, plus a round bonus. A mistake costs one life.
  • Win: Complete all 8 rounds to light the final gate and win. Lose all 3 lives and the relay ends.
  • Tip: Use Replay Pattern once per round if you forget — it costs 5 points but saves a life.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
