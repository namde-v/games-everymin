🔥 FOUNDRY FOREMAN

You are the foreman of a busy foundry. Nine workers, three stations, five impatient customers.

How to Play

  • Assign workers with the + / - buttons, or keys 1-3 + arrow keys. You have 9 workers total.
  • Keep the chain balanced: Furnaces make molten metal, Presses turn it into parts, Packers turn parts into crates. A bottleneck starves the next station.
  • Fill orders before their deadlines. Each completed order scores points. A missed deadline costs a life.
  • Watch the heat! Too many furnace workers overheat the line. Vent heat, or move workers off the furnaces. At 100% heat you lose a life and the line stalls.
  • Win by filling all 5 orders. Game Over if you lose all 3 lives.
  • Tip: Rush Shift doubles output for 10 seconds but adds a lot of heat. Use it only when an order is close.

Sound is procedural and starts only after you press Start.
