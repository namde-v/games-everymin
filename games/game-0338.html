METRONOME MINES

The pickaxe never stops swinging. Your job is timing, not speed. Wait for the perfect moment — then strike!

How to Play

  • Strike: Press SPACE, Click the mine, or Tap the screen when the pickaxe tip is inside the glowing green strike zone.
  • PERFECT: Tip dead-centre — mines 2 ore and builds your Perfect Chain for bonus points.
  • GOOD: Tip inside the wider zone — mines 1 ore, but resets your Perfect Chain.
  • MISS: Tip outside the zone — you lose 1 life and your chain. Lose all 3 lives and the mine collapses.
  • WIN: Mine 24 ore to fill the quota and strike it rich. Golden veins appear every few rocks — a Perfect hit there pays double.
  • Tempo rises as you mine more ore, so the swing gets faster. Watch the beat bar below the mine too.

Keyboard: SPACE = Strike, ENTER = Start / Restart  |  Mouse: Click = Strike  |  Touch: Tap = Strike

Waiting for your first swing…
Tip: Do not mash! One well-timed strike beats five wild swings. Listen for the tick — the centre beat is your cue.
