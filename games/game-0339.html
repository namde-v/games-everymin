INK INFILTRATOR

You are a thief of lost stories. Sneak through the ink-dark rooms, gather the five glowing scrolls, and reach the exit — without ever stepping into a guard's lantern light.

How to Play
Move: Arrow keys / WASD — or tap / click a direction pad below, or tap the maze to walk toward that spot.
Guards patrol fixed routes. Their yellow vision cones are danger: if a cone touches you, you lose a life and restart from the entrance.
Collect all 5 scrolls to unlock the green exit door, then reach it to win. You have 3 lives. Stay in the shadows!
