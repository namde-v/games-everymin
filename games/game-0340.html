STAR CARTOGRAPHER

You are the keeper of lost sky-maps. Ancient constellations have faded — only their stars remain. Re-draw each one in the correct order, without letting your lines cross.

How to Play

  • Goal: Complete all 5 constellations to win the game.
  • Connect: Click, tap, or use arrow keys + Enter/Space to select stars in order.
  • Clues: Read the clue panel below the sky. It shows the shape and a hint for the starting star and direction.
  • Rule: A line that crosses an earlier line is forbidden — it costs a life and clears the current drawing.
  • Wrong star: Picking the wrong next star costs a life.
  • Lives: You have 3 lives. Lose them all and the sky fades — Game Over.
  • Scoring: +100 per correct star, +250 bonus for a flawless constellation.

Keyboard: Arrows move the cursor • Enter / Space select • R restart • H hint
