🏔️ AVALANCHE AVIATOR 🏔️

Fly the supply run through the avalanche peaks!

How to Play: Steer your little red plane through the gaps between the snowy peaks. Dodge the swirling snow gusts — they shove you off course and can push you straight into a mountain. Grab floating supply crates to score.

Collect 20 supply crates to WIN the run. Crash into a peak 3 times and the run is over.

Score: +100 per crate, +1 per metre flown. Gusts slow you and cost distance.

Controls:
Keyboard: Arrow Keys or W / A / S / D to fly  •  P pause  •  R restart
Mouse: move the mouse over the sky to steer, hold click for a speed boost
Touch: drag your finger to steer, lift to glide
