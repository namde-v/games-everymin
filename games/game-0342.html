How to Play

  • Watch: the keys light up in order. Memorize the whole sequence.
  • Repeat: tap or press the keys in exactly the same order.
  • Grow: every round adds one more step. Finish Round 12 to WIN.
  • WILD TWIST: every 4th round (4, 8, 12), one key glows as WILD during playback — you must SKIP it and repeat only the other steps. Tapping the wild key costs a life.
  • You have 3 lives. A wrong key costs a life and replays the round.
  • Keyboard: 1–8 or Q W E R A S D F. Mouse: click. Touch: tap. Enter / Space: start or restart.
