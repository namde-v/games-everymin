Timber Tycoon

You inherited a small forest plot. Grow it, harvest it, and replant it — sustainably.

  • Goal: Earn 500 coins before the 240-second timer runs out.
  • Plant a sapling for 5 coins (select Plant, then tap a dirt plot).
  • Harvest a mature tree (tall, dark green) for 25 coins — but replant after!
  • Watch out: storms snap trees and wildfires can spread to neighbours.
  • Lose if: time runs out below 500 coins, or your living trees fall below 3.
  • Seasons change every 60 seconds: trees grow fastest in Spring and Summer.

Mouse / touch: tap plots and buttons. Keyboard: arrows move cursor, P plant, H harvest, Space act, R restart.
