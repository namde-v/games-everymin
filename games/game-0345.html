Tidepool Trawler

Catch 500 points of fish to win!

How to Play: Steer your boat left and right along the surface. Press Space, click, or tap DROP to lower the hook. The hook sinks, grabs the first thing it touches, then reels back up. Fish are worth points — golden fish are rare and valuable. Junk is worthless, and a jellyfish stings and cuts your line! Lose 3 lines and the trip is over.

Keyboard: ← → or A / D to move, Space to drop, R to restart. Mouse: move to steer, click to drop. Touch: drag to steer, tap DROP.

Game Over

Or press R to restart instantly.

Fish values: Blue 10 · Orange 20 · Purple 30 · Golden 80 · Junk 0 · Jellyfish cuts a line! Watch the depth meter — deeper fish are shyer and faster.
