⚓ DOCKSIDE DYNAMO

How to Play: Cargo ships dock at the top of the harbor, each loaded with colored containers. You command 3 cranes. Click (or tap) a ship to send a free crane to it. The crane unloads one container at a time and stacks it in the matching color warehouse bay at the bottom — each correct delivery scores points. Clear a ship before its departure timer runs out!

Strategy: A ship with many containers takes longer, so start big ships early. A ship that departs uncleared costs you a ship (life). Clear 12 ships to win. Fast clears earn a speed bonus; every missed departure loses points too. Click a crane to recall it to a different ship.

Controls: Mouse click / touch tap a ship to assign a crane · Keys 1–6 select a docked ship, Enter assigns a crane · R restarts anytime · P pauses.
