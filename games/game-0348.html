🪩 SAMBA STEPS

Dance on the beat. Do not miss the groove!

How to Play: Coloured arrows scroll down four lanes toward the glowing target zone. When an arrow lands in the zone, hit its matching key or tap its pad at exactly the right moment. Perfect hits score big and build your combo. Misses break your combo and drain health.

KEYS: ← ↓ ↑ → or A S W D  •  MOUSE: click a pad  •  TOUCH: tap a pad
Perfect = 300  •  Good = 100  •  Miss = health -8
Survive the whole song to win. Health reaching zero ends the dance.

Song length: about 64 seconds • 4 lanes • 96 dance steps
