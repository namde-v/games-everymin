CANYON COURIER

How to Play: You are the canyon courier. Ride your hover-bike, grab glowing parcels, and drop them at the matching outpost before the parcel timer runs out. Deliver 8 parcels to win!

⚠️ Dodge rock spires — a crash costs a life. Sandstorm gusts push you sideways; fight them or ride with them. Expired parcels vanish and cost points.

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to steer  •  Mouse: move pointer to steer toward it  •  Touch: drag to steer  •  Space = boost (limited)  •  P = pause  •  R = restart

📦 Pick up parcels🏠 Deliver to outposts⏱️ Beat the timer💨 Beware gusts

Tip: delivering fast earns a time bonus. Chain deliveries without crashing for a combo bonus.
