🌿 Botanical Blueprint 🌻

Design the perfect garden! Each garden is a 6 × 6 blueprint of sunny, watery, shady and soil beds. You are given a tray of plants. Every plant will only thrive in the right spot.

  • 🌻 Sunflower — needs a sunny bed, or a bed next to a sunny bed.
  • 🌿 Fern — needs a water bed, or a bed next to a water bed.
  • 🌵 Cactus — needs soil or sun, and must NOT touch water.
  • 🍄 Mushroom — needs a shady bed, or a bed next to a shady bed.
  • 🌹 Rose — needs rich soil, or a bed next to rich soil.

Tap a plant in the tray, then tap a bed. A wrong placement costs one attempt. You have 3 attempts per whole game. Satisfy every plant in all 5 gardens to win. Rocks can never hold a plant. Plan before you plant!

How to play: Mouse, touch, or keyboard — arrows move the glowing cursor, number keys pick plants, Enter or Space plants, R restarts.
