Glacier Glider

How to Play: You are a glider on slippery ice. Pick a direction and you keep sliding until you hit a rock wall or a snow block. Plan each slide like a puzzle.

Collect all the glowing crystals, then slide onto the golden goal flag to finish the level. Sliding over a cracked tile breaks the ice and costs a life. Finish all 8 levels to win!

⌨️ Arrow Keys / WASD to slide  •  👆 Swipe or tap the D-pad  •  🖱️ Click a D-pad button  •  R = Restart

Tip: You can only change direction when you stop. Use walls to steer. Crystals are worth 100 points, and finishing a level fast earns a bonus.
