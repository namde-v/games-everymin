⬡ HEX HAVEN ⬡

Welcome to the Haven! Rotate the incoming hex tile and place it on the honeycomb board.

  • Match 3+ hexes of the same color touching each other to clear a cluster and score.
  • Every placement spawns new tiles. Clear 12 clusters to win.
  • If the board fills up or you run out of lives, the game ends.
  • Placing a tile that clears nothing costs nothing — but a full board costs a life.

Controls: Mouse / touch — click a glowing cell to place, click the tile tray or press Q / E to rotate. Keys 1–6 pick a color. R restarts. Space rotates.

Tip: build clusters at the edges first, then chain clears in the middle. Chained clears in one move give bonus points. Bigger clusters are worth much more than the minimum three!
