Bazaar Baron

The market day is about to begin. Turn your starting gold into a fortune before sunset!

How to Play:
• Goal: Reach 600 gold net worth (gold + inventory value) before the 180-second day ends.
• Buy low, sell high: Prices drift every second and events shake the market — a drought, a caravan, a royal feast.
• Watch demand: A glowing stall means customers want that good — sell for a bonus!
• Controls: Click a stall card, then Buy / Sell. Keys 1–5 select a good, B buy, S sell, R restart, Enter start.
• Mouse & touch work on the canvas stalls and all buttons. Don't end the day below target — or broke with no stock!
📰 Market news: The bazaar opens. Traders are setting up their stalls…
Tip: Spices are volatile — big risks, big rewards. Grain is steady but slow.

Keyboard: 1–5 select • B buy • S sell • R restart • Enter start  |  Mouse / touch: tap stalls and buttons  |  Score grows with profitable trades.
