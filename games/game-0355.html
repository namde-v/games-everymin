🌋 Volcano Voyager

Hop across rock platforms over boiling lava!

  • Move: Left / Right arrows or A / D — also drag with mouse or touch.
  • Jump: Up arrow, W, Space, mouse click, or tap the screen. Double-jump is ON!
  • Collect: Glowing embers (+50 points). Golden ember (+150) appears near the top.
  • Avoid: Lava bursts that shoot up, falling ash chunks, and the lava below.
  • Goal: Climb 900m to the summit flag to WIN. Falling in lava costs a life.
  • Tips: Moving platforms carry you — ride them! Bursts flash red before erupting.

How to Play: platforms rise and fall. Time your jumps, grab embers, and never touch lava or ash. You have 3 lives. Press R anytime to restart.

Sound starts after you press Start. Best on keyboard, mouse, or touch.
