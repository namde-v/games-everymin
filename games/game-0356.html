Welcome, Codebreaker

Six sealed chambers guard the Citadel. Each chamber hides a word, scrambled on rotating letter wheels. Use the clue, turn every wheel to the right letter, then submit your answer.

How to Play
  • Tap a wheel, or use ← → to select one. Turn it with ↑ ↓ or the on-screen arrows.
  • Press Enter or tap Submit to test the word. A wrong word costs one life.
  • Press H or tap Hint to lock one correct letter. You have only 3 hints for the whole citadel.
  • Solve all 6 chambers to win. Lose all 3 lives and the citadel seals shut.

Keyboard, mouse and touch are all supported. Sound starts only after you press Start.
