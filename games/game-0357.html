🚂 Railway Regent

Rule the rails! Two trains run clockwise around the island loop, carrying passengers between five coloured stations. You are the signal master: flip the junctions to route trains, keep them apart, and deliver everyone on time.

  • Click / tap a junction diamond (or press 1 / 2) to switch it between LOOP and SHORTCUT. A shortcut is faster but skips stations.
  • Deliver: a train automatically picks up waiting passengers and drops them at their matching coloured station. Each delivery earns fares plus a time bonus.
  • Avoid collisions: if two trains get too close on the same track, you lose a life and both trains are reset to the depot.
  • Missed deliveries cost lives: if a passenger's timer runs out, they storm off and you lose a life.
  • Win by delivering 25 passengers. Game Over if you run out of lives.
  • Keys: 1 / 2 junctions   P pause   R restart   Space slow a train briefly (brake).

Mouse, touch and keyboard all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.
