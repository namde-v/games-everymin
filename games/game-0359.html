How to Play: You are the Orchard Outlaw, racing a cart under the trees! Catch the WANTED fruit shown in the HUD to fill your harvest quota of 25 before the 90-second timer runs out.

Catching the wanted fruit scores big and builds your combo. Other fresh fruit is worth a few points. Rotten fruit and falling branches cost a life! You have 3 lives.

Controls: ← → or A / D to move  |  Mouse move  |  Touch and drag
Press Start, or press Enter. Press R anytime to restart.

You Win!

Press R or tap Restart to play again.

Tip: chain wanted fruit in a row to grow your combo multiplier!
