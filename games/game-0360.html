Labyrinth Ledger

How to Play: You are the cartographer. Draw a single continuous path from the start tile through the maze. Pick up every numbered key in order (1, then 2, then 3), then reach the green exit gate.

Rules of the ledger: you may never step on a tile twice. A key taken out of order is refused. A wrong turn is not fatal — undo it step by step. But if you paint yourself into a corner with no free tile left, the route is ruined and you lose a life.

Move with Arrow keys / WASD, click or tap a neighbouring tile to extend the path, click any tile already on your path to rewind to it. Backspace / Z undoes one step. R restarts the level.

Sound is generated live in your browser after you press Start. No downloads, no network.

Victory

Gold circles are keys — take them in numbered order. The green gate is the exit. The amber ribbon is your plotted path. A dead end costs one life; lose all three and the expedition ends.
