🏹 Aurora Archer

How to Play: Aim your bow at the glowing aurora targets drifting across the night sky. Drag from the bow with your mouse or finger to set angle and power, then release to shoot. You can also use the Arrow Keys to adjust aim and Space to fire.

🎯 Bullseye = 50 points  •  Outer ring = 20 points  •  3 misses lose a life  •  Reach 500 points to win!

Watch the wind! A new gust arrives each round and bends your arrows in flight.

The aurora shines for a true master archer!
