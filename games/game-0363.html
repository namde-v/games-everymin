⚓ Harbor Harbormaster

You are the harbormaster for a busy 120-second shift. Ships arrive with cargo, deadlines, and very little patience.

  • How to Play: Click / tap a waiting ship to select it (gold outline), then click its matching dock. Or press Enter to auto-berth the selected ship at a matching free dock.
  • Matching matters: Containers → Dock 1, Oil → Dock 2, Grain → Dock 3, Passengers → Dock 4. A wrong dock will not accept the ship.
  • Patience: The bar under each waiting ship drains. If it empties, the ship leaves angry and counts as lost. Lose 5 ships and your shift ends in disgrace.
  • Unloading: Berthed ships unload over time, then depart and pay you. Faster berthing = bigger bonus. If the waiting bay is full, new arrivals turn away.
  • Keys: 1–5 select a waiting ship, Q / W / E / R choose Dock 1–4, Enter auto-berth, M mute.

Tip: Big ships pay more but take longer to unload. Keep one dock free for the next urgent arrival.
