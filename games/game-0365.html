TEMPEST TAMER

Pilot your storm-chaser through the tempest!

How to Play: Steer with arrow keys / WASD, your mouse, or by dragging on touch screens. Dodge yellow lightning bolts and white hailstones. Collect glowing storm cells for points. Beware — sudden wind gusts will push you sideways!

⌨️ Arrows / WASD to fly  •  🖱️ Move mouse to steer  •  👆 Drag to fly
⚡ Lightning = lose a life  •  🧊 Hail = lose a life  •  🔮 Storm cell = +25 points
❤️ 3 hits ends the run  •  🏆 Win by surviving 90 seconds OR collecting 20 cells

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads needed — fully offline.
