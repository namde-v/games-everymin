Rune Reservoir

How to Play:
A magic spring (💧) must reach the reservoir (🏺). Click or tap a rune tile to rotate it 90°. Connect a continuous path of glowing channels from spring to reservoir before time runs out and before you run out of rotations. Every level has fewer spare rotations and less time. Complete all 5 levels to win!
Mouse: click a tile  •  Touch: tap a tile  •  Keyboard: Arrow keys to select, Space / R / Enter to rotate
Tip: Corners turn the stream. Straights carry it on. Watch the Flow status — it counts how far the water travels from the spring. If a tile glows blue, water already reaches it!
