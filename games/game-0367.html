ASSEMBLY ARCHON

Customers want gadgets, and they want them NOW. Read each order, grab parts from the bins, and build the gadget in the exact sequence shown before the order timer runs out.

How to Play:
• The active order (top, highlighted) shows the part sequence it needs.
• Click / tap a part bin, or drag a part onto the assembly line, to add it.
• Keys 1–6 add parts, B = backspace (remove last part), R = restock, Enter = ship / start.
• A wrong part buzzes and costs points. Stock is limited — restock when a bin runs low (it takes a few seconds).
• Complete a sequence to ship the order and earn points + a time bonus. Expired orders count as missed.
• WIN: complete 8 orders. LOSE: miss 3 orders.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads.
