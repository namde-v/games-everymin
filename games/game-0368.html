PULSE PAINTER

Paint the canvas to 70% before the 60-second track ends.

How to Play: A beat pulses 120 times a minute. Colored paint blobs pop up in rhythm on the canvas. Pick the matching color with keys 1-4 or by tapping a palette color, then tap or click the blob right on the beat to splat it. A perfectly timed splat paints a big clean patch and builds your combo. A mistimed tap smudges gray paint, breaks your combo, and costs 25 points. Missing a blob costs 10 points. Each splat paints part of the canvas — reach 70% coverage to win.

Colors: 1 = Coral, 2 = Sun, 3 = Mint, 4 = Sky. You can also click the palette below, or press Space to pause.
