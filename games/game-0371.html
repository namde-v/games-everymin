🏮 Lantern Lifter

Escort the flame to the tower top!

How to Play: Your lantern drifts upward on its own. Steer it away from sideways wind gusts and falling rain drops, and scoop up glowing fireflies for points.

Move: ← → ↑ ↓ or WASD • Mouse move • Touch drag

Wind Shield: press Space or tap / click to raise a shield for a short burst. It blocks one gust or drop, then needs a few seconds to recharge.

You have 3 lives. Reach 300m altitude to WIN. A gust or raindrop without a shield costs a life. Collect 12 fireflies for a bonus life (max 5).

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
