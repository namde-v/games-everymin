Chart the night sky. Connect the stars to trace each lost constellation — without ever letting your lines cross.

How to Play
  • Mouse / touch: tap a star, then tap a second star to draw a line. Or drag from one star to another.
  • Keyboard: use Arrow keys to move the glowing cursor between stars, press Enter or Space to select a star, then select a second star to connect them.
  • Faint dotted lines are your star-chart guide. A correct link scores 100 points. A wrong link costs a move.
  • Never cross a line you already drew — a crossing costs a life. You have 3 lives and 45 moves for all 5 constellations.
  • Finish a constellation for a 250-point bonus, then chart the next one. Complete all 5 to win.
