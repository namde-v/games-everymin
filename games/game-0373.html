🍄 Mycelium Mayor

Become mayor of a living mushroom city. Plant hubs, feed the network, and hold back the blight.

How to Play
• Goal: Reach 120 population before Day 12 ends.
• Place: Tap a grid tile, or press keys 1–4, then tap a tile.
• 1 Spore Hub — grows population (20 nutrients, 10 water).
• 2 Nutrient Farm — makes nutrients each day (15 nutrients).
• 3 Water Spire — makes water each day (15 water).
• 4 Light Canopy — makes light each day (20 light).
• Blight: Purple tiles spread each day. Tap one to cleanse it for 10 nutrients.
• Balance: Hubs consume resources daily. Starved hubs stop growing and blight grows faster.
• Controls: Mouse click, touch tap, keys 1–4, R to restart.

Tip: Build farms and spires first, then hubs. Cleanse blight early.
