🧗 Cadence Climber

Climb the cliff on the beat — or lose your grip!

Golden holds light up in rhythm. Tap, click, or press a key exactly when the beat ring closes on the hold to grab it and climb higher. Perfect hits build your combo. Early, late, or missed taps cost grip.

CONTROLS: Arrow Keys / Space = Grab  •  Mouse Click = Grab  •  Touch Tap = Grab

Reach the summit flag at 100% altitude to win. You have 3 grip hearts. A missed beat or a badly timed grab loses one heart. Chain Perfect grabs for bonus points. Sound is generated live in your browser and starts only after you press Start.
