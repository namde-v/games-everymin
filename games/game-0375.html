FROST FERRY

Pilot your ice ferry through a frozen harbour. Pick up shivering passengers, slide them to their destination dock, and dodge drifting ice floes and hungry whirlpools.

How to Play
🧭 Move: Arrow keys or WASD for thrust • Mouse: steer toward cursor • Touch: drag to steer
🧍 Pick up: Sail slowly into a glowing dock with a waiting passenger.
🏁 Deliver: Carry them to the gold destination dock to score.
⚠️ Danger: Ice floes and whirlpools cost a life. You have 3 lives.
🏆 Win: Deliver all 6 passengers before you run out of lives.
🧊 Ice floe = danger🌀 Whirlpool = danger⭐ Gold dock = destination
