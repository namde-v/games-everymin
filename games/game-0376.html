Someone stole the Golden Saffron!

The market's rarest saffron vanished at dusk. Five traders were nearby. Only one is the thief — but every suspect has a story, and some stories are lies.

How to Play

1. Click (or tap) a market clue to examine it. Each clue clears or implicates suspects.
2. Click a suspect card to select them. Use Eliminate to cross out suspects the clues clear.
3. When you are confident, press Accuse Selected. You have only 3 accusations.
4. Correct accusation = Win. Three wrong accusations = Game Over.
Controls: Mouse click to select clues and suspects · Keyboard arrows to move selection, Enter to examine the selected clue or suspect, E to eliminate, A to accuse · Touch tap.

Scoring: +100 per clue found, +50 per correct elimination, +500 for catching the thief. Wrong accusations cost a life, not points.

Sound starts only after you press Start. No internet needed.

The Case: The Golden Saffron was taken from Stall 7 between 6pm and 7pm. The thief left saffron dust, loves cardamom, and was seen near the well — or so the clues say. Read carefully: not every clue points the same way.
