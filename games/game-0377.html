FOUNDRY FLUX

Route molten metal through your factory before it cools.

How to Play: Tap or click a grid cell to place the selected piece. Click a placed piece to rotate it. Press R to rotate the piece you are about to place. Press 1 Straight pipe, 2 Corner pipe, 3 Sorter. The Sorter sends Copper straight ahead and all other alloys turning right. Straight pipes run both ways along their axis; Corner pipes bend at a right angle. Fill every mold order to win. You have 5 lives.
