Echo Engineer

How to Play:

  • Press Start. The console plays a pattern of tones and flashing pads.
  • Repeat the pattern in the same order by tapping the pads, clicking them, or pressing keys 1-4 or the arrow keys.
  • Timing matters: repeat each tone close to the steady beat (about 600 ms apart) for bonus points. Rushed or dragging presses score less.
  • Each round the pattern grows longer. You have 3 lives — a wrong pad loses one life.
  • Finish Round 8 to win the game. Lose all lives and it is Game Over.

Controls: Keyboard 1-4 / arrows, mouse click, or touch tap on a pad.
