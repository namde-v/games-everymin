Canopy Courier

Zipline through the jungle canopy, grab every parcel, and drop it at a glowing delivery platform!

How to Play:
• Space / Click / Tap — launch onto a zipline, then press again to release mid-swing.
• Arrow keys / on-screen steering — steer left and right in the air; Up glides.
• Arrow keys on ground — walk along a platform.
• Touch a brown parcel to pick it up (one at a time), then land on a gold drop platform to deliver it.
• Deliver all 5 parcels to win. Fall out of the canopy and you lose a life (3 lives).
