🪸 Reef Ranger

How to Play: Six coral beds need your help. Choose an action with keys 1, 2 or 3 (or tap the buttons), then click or tap a coral bed to apply it. Plant coral to raise health, clean the dark pollution patches before they spread, and protect beds before storms and bleaching hit.

Resources regenerate over time. Reach 85% average reef health before Season 8 ends to win. If three beds die, or health collapses, the reef is lost. Storms and heat waves strike each season — protected beds take no damage.
