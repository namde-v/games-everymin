Juniper Juggler

Catch the falling juniper berries before they hit the forest floor!

How to Play:
← → Arrow keys or A / D to slide the juggler.
Mouse move or touch-drag to position the juggler.
Catch berries in the basket for points. Chain catches for combo multipliers.
Golden berries are worth 50! Rotten berries cost a life if caught.
Drop 3 good berries and the show is over.
Reach 500 points to win the Juggler's Crown!

Press Enter or tap Start to begin.

Game Over

or press R to restart instantly
