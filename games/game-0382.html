Obsidian Oracle

The oracle carves a hidden rune sequence in obsidian. Colours and shapes each follow their own repeating cycle — but the cycle lengths are secret. Study the stones, deduce the pattern, and place the runes that must come next.

How to Play
  • Look at the first 4 runes on the stone tablet. Each colour repeats on its own cycle, each shape on its own cycle.
  • Predict the next rune: click or tap one of the 6 candidate stones, or use ← → to highlight and Enter / Space to place it. Keys 1–6 pick a candidate directly.
  • Correct prediction: +100 points. Complete all 3 predictions in a sequence: +50 bonus and the next sequence begins.
  • Wrong prediction: lose 1 life. Lose all 3 lives and the oracle falls silent.
  • Win: solve 5 sequences. Game Over: run out of lives. Press R or Restart at any time.
Mouse: move + click  •  Touch: tap / drag  •  Keyboard: ← →, Enter, 1–6, R to restart
