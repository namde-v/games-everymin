🌿 Willow Warden

How to Play: Blight creatures march along the winding dirt path toward the grove on the right. Click an empty grass square to plant the selected guardian. Guardians attack automatically. Earn sap by defeating enemies and clearing waves.

🌵 Thorn fires sharp seeds. 🌳 Root slows and crushes nearby foes. 🍃 Breeze knocks enemies backward. Survive all 8 waves to win. If grove health reaches 0, the forest falls.

Mouse: click to place · Hover to preview · Keys: 1 / 2 / 3 choose guardian, Space next wave, R restart · Touch: tap to place, drag to preview

Victory!

Tip: Root guardians near corners slow whole groups. Breeze guardians are best just before the grove. Thorn guardians cover long straight paths. Press Space when you are ready for the next wave.
