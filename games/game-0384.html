🎣 TEMPO TRAWLER

How to Play:

  • Fish swim left to right along 4 lanes: Shallow, Reef, Deep, Abyss.
  • A glowing catch zone sits near the right side. When a fish is inside it on a beat, cast in that lane!
  • Keys: D / F / J / K for lanes 1-4. Or click / tap the lane pads below the water.
  • Perfect on-beat catches score more and build your combo. Off-beat or wrong-lane casts count as a miss.
  • WIN: catch 25 fish before the song ends. LOSE: 10 misses, or the song ends first.
  • Restart instantly with the Restart button or the R key.

Tip: watch the pulsing beat ring and the song bar — cast on the flash, not before it!
