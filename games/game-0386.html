🌳 Moss Merchant

A travelling market-trading game

How to Play:

  • You start with 100 coins and a cart full of hope.
  • Three villages sell four moss goods at different prices, and prices drift every day.
  • Buy goods cheap in one village, travel to another, and sell them for more.
  • You have 10 market days. Win by reaching 200 coins (double your stake). If you end day 10 below 200, or go broke with nothing to sell, the road ends.
  • Watch the trend arrows and the price bars on the scene below — green means a price rose, red means it fell.
  • Travel is free, but every day that passes is a day of prices moving without you.

Controls: mouse click, touch tap, or keyboard — 1/2/3 to travel, arrow keys to pick a good, B to buy, S to sell, N for next day, R to restart.
