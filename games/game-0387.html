☀ SOLAR SORTER

Beams of coloured light arrive in mixed order. Rotate the central mirror, aim the beam, and divert it into the prism of the SAME colour.

  • How to Play: A beam waits at the emitter (top left). Turn the mirror, then FIRE it.
  • Controls: Left / Right arrows or A / D to rotate the mirror. Mouse move to aim, mouse click to fire. Touch drag to aim, tap FIRE to shoot. Space or Enter fires. Keys 1–6 jump-aim at a prism.
  • Swap trick: Press S (or Swap Prisms) to swap two neighbouring prisms when the layout gets tricky — the colours move with them, so re-aim!
  • Win: Sort 30 beams with fewer than 5 errors. Game Over: 5 wrong prisms, and the spectrum collapses.
  • Every 10 sorted beams the prisms shuffle to a new order. Watch the labels!

Headphones optional: all sound is generated in your browser, nothing is downloaded.
