HOLLOW HARVESTER

Pilot your little harvester through a glowing cavern. Gather luminous fungi, dodge falling rocks and cave-in zones, and keep your lamp burning.

How to Play
Collect 40 glowing fungi to win.
🪨 Dodge falling rocks and stay out of marked cave-in zones before they collapse.
💡 Your lamp fuel drains over time — grab glowing fuel cells to refill it.
❤ You have 3 hull points. A rock hit or a cave-in costs 1 hull.
Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to fly • Mouse: move to steer • Touch: drag to steer • P pauses • R restarts.

Sound starts after you press Start. Best played with keyboard, mouse, or touch.

You Win!

You harvested 40 fungi!

Press R or click Restart to play again instantly.
