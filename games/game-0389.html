🌬️ Zephyr Zookeeper 🦜

Strategy / Wind Aviary Management — keep the flock happy for 6 days!

Day 1 / 6 Score: 0 Flock Happiness: 100% Escapes: 0 / 5 Status: Title Selected: none
Welcome, Keeper! Press Start to open the aviary.
How to Play: Wind-borne creatures arrive at the holding perch (bottom). Each creature loves one wind: Breeze, Gale, Thermal or Storm. Drag a creature — or click it, then click an aviary — into its matching wind to raise happiness. A wrong wind, hunger, or tiredness drains happiness; at zero, the creature escapes! Feed hungry creatures and rest tired ones. Survive 6 days with fewer than 5 escapes to win. Keys: 1–4 assign selected creature to an aviary, F feed, E rest, ← → change selection, Enter start, R restart. Mouse and touch dragging both work.
