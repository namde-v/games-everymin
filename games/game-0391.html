🎯 Polaris Bowmaster ✨

Under the northern lights, only a steady hand wins. Shoot the drifting aurora targets before they fade!

How to Play

  • Aim: Drag with mouse or finger anywhere on the sky, or use ↑ ↓ to tilt the bow and ← → to adjust power.
  • Shoot: Release the drag, or press Space / Enter.
  • Wind gusts: Watch the wind indicator — gusts bend arrows mid-flight, so aim into the wind.
  • Scoring: Bullseye 50, inner ring 30, outer ring 15. Consecutive hits build a combo multiplier.
  • Win: Reach 500 points. Game Over: 3 missed arrows end the run.

Tip: golden targets are worth double points but move faster!
