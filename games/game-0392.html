BRINE BRIGADIER

Smugglers are slipping through the strait under cover of fog. Place your patrol ships, read the fog, and cut off their routes before they reach the safe harbor on the far shore.

How to Play
1. Deploy: Tap or click 4 sea squares to place your patrol ships, then press Begin Patrol.
2. Move: Each turn you get 3 moves. Select a patrol ship, then tap an adjacent square to move it. Keyboard: arrow keys move the cursor, Enter selects, E ends the turn.
3. Intercept: A smuggler is caught when it moves onto, or next to, a patrol ship. Fog hides smugglers far from your patrols — keep your net spread wide.
4. Win: Intercept 8 smugglers before 3 slip through to the harbor on the right edge.

Mouse, touch, and keyboard supported. Sound starts only after you press Start.

Victory!

Patrol ship Smuggler (when spotted) Safe harbor (right edge) Selected ship glows white — fog hides distant waters.
