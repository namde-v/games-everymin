COBALT COURIER

You are the cobalt van driver for the whole downtown grid. Six parcels are waiting — deliver them all before your step budget runs out!

How to Play

  • Move with Arrow keys / WASD, click an adjacent street square, tap it on touch screens, or use the on-screen arrows.
  • Deliver by driving onto a parcel square (letters A–F). Each delivery scores points.
  • One-way streets (blue arrows) only let you exit in the arrow direction. Driving against one costs a life — 3 violations and your licence is suspended!
  • Traffic blocks (red barriers) cannot be driven through at all. Plan around them.
  • Win by delivering all 6 parcels within 58 steps. Lose by running out of steps or lives.
  • Press H or the Hint button to highlight the nearest undelivered parcel.
