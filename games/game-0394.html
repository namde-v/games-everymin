Three tribes. One desert. Five treaties stand between peace and war.

Welcome, Diplomat

You are the envoy of the Oasis Council, sent to unite three rival desert tribes before the dry season turns to war. Each round a tribe brings you a demand. How you answer shapes their trust — and the fate of the dunes.

How to Play

  • Read the demand card each round: a tribe asks for water, spice, safe passage, or respect.
  • Choose one response: Concede (costs resources, builds trust), Bluff (free, but risky — it can backfire), or Trade (spend one resource to gain another and please everyone a little).
  • Watch the trust meters. Every tribe starts at 50. If any tribe's trust hits 0, negotiations collapse and you lose.
  • Seal treaties by raising a tribe's trust to 80 or higher. Seal 5 treaties to win the game.
  • Manage resources: Water, Spice, and Gold. Running low makes concessions harder — bluff and trade wisely.
  • Controls: Click or tap the choice cards, or press 1 / 2 / 3 to pick a response. Press Enter to start or restart.

Tip: Bluffs work best when a tribe already trusts you. Desperate tribes see through bluffs. A well-timed Trade can rescue a tribe on the edge without spending much.
