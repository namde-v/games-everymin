Ember Acrobat

The big top is on fire — literally! Leap from platform to platform above the flames, landing exactly on the beat. Perfect timing builds your combo; sloppy jumps wobble your balance.

How to Play:
• Press SPACE, click LEAP!, or tap the screen to jump.
• Jump when the beat pulse hits the gold zone for a PERFECT leap (+100, combo up).
• Early or late jumps still land, but cost balance and break your combo.
• A badly mistimed jump makes you fall — 3 falls ends the run.
• Reach the golden Finale Platform (12) to win the show!
Sound starts after you press Start. Headphones recommended.
