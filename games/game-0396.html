Fjord Forager

Wander the misty fjord shore and fill your basket with safe mushrooms and berries. Beware the poisonous look-alikes!

How to Play
• Move with WASD / Arrow keys, or drag / tap on touch screens. Click a spot to walk toward it.
• Walk up to a mushroom or berry. A clue card appears showing its cap shape, colour and spots.
• Press SPACE or E, or click / tap the item itself, to harvest it — only when you are close.
• SAFE: round cap with solid colour; deep-blue round berry clusters.
• POISON: pointed cap, OR white spots, OR bright-red single berries. Never pick those!
• Safe mushroom = +20, safe berries = +10, poison = lose 1 life.
• Reach 300 points to win. Lose all 3 lives and the foray ends.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.

Field Guide — Identify Before You Pick: Safe mushroom: round cap, solid warm colour, no spots. Poison mushroom: pointed cap OR white spots. Safe berries: deep blue, round, in a cluster. Poison berries: bright red, single berries. When in doubt, leave it and find another!
