Granite Gardener

A quiet Sokoban-style garden puzzle. Shove heavy granite boulders onto the mossy plots — but boulders can only be pushed, never pulled, and every level has a push limit.

How to Play
  • Move: Arrow keys or WASD, the on-screen arrows, swipe, or tap a neighbouring tile.
  • Push: Walk into a boulder to push it one square. It stops at walls and other boulders.
  • Goal: Get every boulder onto a mossy plot to finish the garden.
  • Push limit: Running out of pushes costs a life and restarts the level.
  • Stuck? Press R or the Reset button. A boulder wedged in a corner can never move again.
  • Complete all 5 garden layouts to win. You have 3 lives.
