Harbor Harpist

At dusk the harbor sings. A ghostly harp plays a short melody on its six strings — listen carefully, then play it back in the same order.

How to Play
1. Press Start Performance and listen — glowing strings play the melody.
2. When it says Your turn!, tap the strings in the same order (or press keys 1–6).
3. Sequences grow longer each round. A wrong note costs a life.
4. Survive all 8 rounds to win. Lose all 3 lives and the performance ends.
5. Tap Replay Melody anytime during your turn to hear it once more.

Mouse, touch, or keyboard (1–6) all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.

Performance Complete!

Keys 1–6 play strings left to right • R replays the melody • Click or tap a string to play it.
