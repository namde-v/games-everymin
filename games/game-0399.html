Ivory Inventor

You are the workshop inventor. Power cells, gears and brass wires lie scattered on the bench. Rotate the tiles so power flows to every machine — but never overload a fragile junction node!

How to Play
  • Rotate: Click, tap, or select a tile with arrow keys and press Space. Straight wires and gears flip; elbows turn 90°.
  • Test: Press Energize (or Enter) to send power through the circuit.
  • Win a contraption: every machine glows and no junction node has more than 2 live connections.
  • Danger: a junction node with 3 or 4 live connections short-circuits and costs 1 life. You have 3 lives.
  • Goal: complete all 6 contraptions to win the game.

Keyboard: Arrows move, Space rotates, Enter energizes, R restarts. Mouse and touch work too. Fully offline.

You Win!

Tip: tiles that can rotate have a small curved arrow. Junction nodes are diamonds — keep them to two connections only.
