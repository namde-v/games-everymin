Jubilee Jester

The jester has released a storm of carnival balloons! Pop balloons of the target color shown in the HUD before they float away.

How to Play:
🎈 Click / tap a target-color balloon to pop it (+10 × combo).
⬛ Never pop a black balloon — it costs a life!
⭐ Popping other colors breaks your combo (but costs no life).
🎯 Chain target pops without a miss to raise your combo up to ×5.
⌛ A fresh target color is announced every 20 seconds.
🏆 Reach 400 points to win. Lose all 3 lives and the show ends.

Keyboard: arrow keys move the jester's wand, Space / Enter pops at the wand.
