🌿 Mossy Mechanic

How to Play: The workshop has gone wild. Gears and pipes grew moss and spun out of alignment, cutting power to the machines.

Tap or click any tile to rotate it 90°. Use Arrow Keys to move the glowing selector and Enter / Space to rotate the selected tile. Line up every pipe and gear so power flows from the glowing power spring (top-left) to every mossy machine.

Win before you use 70 moves. Each rotation costs 1 move. Fewer moves = higher score. Sound starts only after you press Start.

You Win!

Tip: a machine glows green when it is powered. Follow the pipes!
