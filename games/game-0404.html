How to Play:

  • Each turn, choose 1 of 3 cards from your hand. Every card costs stamina.
  • Sword Slash deals damage. Aegis Shield blocks the foe's next attack. Healing Hymn restores HP. Special cards like Fireball and Venom Quill deal extra or lingering damage.
  • After you play, the Shadow Scribe plays a card. Stamina refills by +2 each turn (max 10).
  • Win by reducing the foe to 0 HP. Lose if your HP hits 0. Faster wins score more!
  • Controls: tap/click a card, or press 1, 2, 3 on the keyboard. Touch works too.
