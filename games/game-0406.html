Rustic Ranger

How to Play: You are the forest ranger with a trusty slingshot. Drag back from the slingshot (bottom-left) to set angle and power, then release to launch a pinecone. Moving forest targets drift across the hills — acorns, mushrooms and leaf badges are worth different points. Watch the wind: it pushes every shot sideways!

Mouse / Touch: drag from the slingshot and release. Keyboard: ←→ angle, ↑↓ power, Space to shoot, R to restart.

Score 120 points in 14 shots across 3 rounds to Win. Run out of shots below 120 and the forest patrol ends.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.

Game Over

Tip: Strong wind? Aim upwind. Far targets need more power and a higher arc. Golden targets are worth 30!
