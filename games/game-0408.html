🌊 Tidal Tinkerer

How to Play

  • The sea is at the top, your village at the bottom. Between them lies open shoreline where you build defenses.
  • Build phase: pick a tool, then click or tap a shoreline tile to place it. Click a placed structure to sell it back for half price.
  • Sand Barrier (2): absorbs 2 surging water units before washing away.
  • Tide Gate (5): a sturdy gate that absorbs 5 water units.
  • Pump (6): sucks up to 3 water units that pass beside it during each wave.
  • Release the wave when ready. Water rushes down each column; anything reaching the bottom row damages the village.
  • Earn resources after every survived wave. Survive all 8 waves to win. If village HP hits 0, the village is lost.
  • Keys: 1/2/3 select tools, arrows move the cursor, Enter places, Space releases a wave, R restarts.
