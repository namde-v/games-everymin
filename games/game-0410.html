Velvet Voyager

How to Play: Drag anywhere on the starfield to aim your launch arrow, then release to fire the velvet spacecraft. Gravity from each planet bends your path — swing around them to slingshot toward the glowing goal star. Do not touch a planet or drift off the edge of space.

You have 3 attempts per level and limited fuel. Each launch costs fuel; slingshot flybys and near-misses earn bonus score. Reach the goal star on all 5 levels to win.

Controls: Mouse / touch drag to aim, release to launch  |  R = restart level  |  Space = launch with last aim  |  Arrow keys = nudge aim  |  M = sound on/off
