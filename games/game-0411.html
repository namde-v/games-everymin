Amber Alchemist

The old alchemist left you eight recipes — and only glowing vials to remember them by. Watch each sequence, then pour it back in exactly the same order.

Goal: Complete 8 brews. Sequences grow longer. You have 3 lives — each wrong pour costs one.

  • Watch: Vials glow one at a time, with a unique tone each. Do not tap while they glow.
  • Repeat: Tap or click the vials in the same order, or press keys 1, 2, 3, or 4.
  • Score: 100 points per correct pour, plus a brew bonus of 50 × brew number.
  • Lives: A wrong pour loses one life and replays the same recipe. Lose all 3 lives and the run ends.
  • Win: Finish all 8 brews to become a Master Alchemist.
  • Tip: Say the colours aloud, or group them in pairs: red-green, blue-gold.

Tip: turn your sound on — every ingredient has its own musical note.
