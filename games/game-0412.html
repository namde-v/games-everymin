Bramble Bandit

Swing through the thorny valley, grab every berry, and reach the far treehouse!

How to Play
• HOLD Space, mouse button, or touch the screen to grab the nearest vine.
• While swinging, press Left / Right (or A / D) to pump your swing.
• RELEASE at the top of the arc to fly to the next vine or platform.
• On a platform, use Left / Right to walk and Up / W to jump.
• Collect berries for 100 points each. Falling into thorns costs a life.
• Reach the treehouse on the far right to WIN. You have 3 lives.

Tip: release when you are swinging forward and high — that gives the longest leap!

Keyboard: Space = grab / hold  |  Arrows / A D = move & pump  |  Mouse: hold to grab  |  Touch: hold screen to grab. Press Enter to start or restart.
