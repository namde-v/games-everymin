🪸 Coral Cartographer

How to Play: You are a survey boat on a 12 × 12 ocean grid. Hidden coral reefs are scattered under the fog. Move to a tile to chart it. Charting a reef scores points; charting open water still costs fuel.

Press Sonar Ping (or Space) to briefly reveal reefs in a 3 × 3 area around your boat. You have only 8 pings. Bump into a storm tile and you lose one hull heart.

Win: Map 90% of all reefs before fuel reaches zero. Lose: Fuel runs out or all hull hearts are lost.

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to move · Click or tap an adjacent tile to move · Space or the Ping button for sonar · R to restart.

Tip: Pings do not cost fuel, but moving does. Use a ping when you are surrounded by fog, then sail straight toward the pink reef glows. Storms flash dark grey — steer around them after a ping reveals them.
