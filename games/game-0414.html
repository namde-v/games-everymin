Dune Drifter

Ride your sandboard down an endless desert dune. Dodge rocks and cacti, leap the sand ridges, and scoop up shells on your way to 2000 metres!

How to Play
Steer: Left / Right arrow keys, A / D, mouse move, or touch drag
Jump: Space, mouse click, screen tap, or the JUMP button
Dodge rocks and cacti. Jumping clears ridges and low obstacles.
Collect shells for +50 points. A crash costs 1 life. 3 crashes = Game Over.
Reach 2000m to win!

Tip: jumping at the last moment over a ridge gives a style bonus.
