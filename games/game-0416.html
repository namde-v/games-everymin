🦅 Frost Falconer

You are a swift falcon hunting over a snowy valley. Catch 12 prey before the storms wear you down!

Goal: Dive onto rabbits and mice on the ground. Each clean dive-catch scores 1.

Danger: Icy storm clouds drift across the sky. 3 storm hits = Game Over.

Tip: Dive only when you are right above prey. A missed dive bounces you back up.

Move / Glide: Arrow keys or WASD, or drag with mouse / touch. Your falcon follows the pointer.

Dive: Hold Space, hold mouse button, or hold touch. Release to pull up and glide again.

Catch: Touch prey while diving. Touching prey while just gliding will scare it away — no point!

Storms: Grey swirling clouds with snow and lightning. Fly around them or above / below them.

Win: 12 catches. Lose: 3 storm hits. After a hit you flash and are briefly protected.
