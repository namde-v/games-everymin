Granite Gardener

Welcome, gardener. Each level shows a target harmony pattern. Recreate it on your grid using rocks, raked sand, and moss, then check your work. Complete all five gardens to win.

How to Play
  • 1 — Rock: tap a square to place a granite stone.
  • 2 — Rake: tap a square to draw flowing raked sand.
  • 3 — Moss: tap a square to plant soft green moss.
  • 4 — Clear Cell: tap a square to return it to plain sand.
  • Press Check Garden (or key C) when you are ready. Score 80% or better to pass; otherwise you lose a life.
  • You have 3 lives across all 5 gardens. Plan calmly — there is no timer.
