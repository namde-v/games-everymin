⚑ Harbor Harbinger

Decode semaphore signals before the ship sails past the harbor!

Welcome, Signal Watcher!

How to Play: A signalman on the pier waves two flags. Each flag pattern stands for one letter. Look at his flags, find the matching letter in the Signal Chart, then tap that letter button or press that key on your keyboard. Decode every letter in the message before the ship reaches the harbor entrance.

Decode all 6 messages to win. You have 3 lives — each wrong decode costs one life. A correct letter scores 20 points; finishing a message earns a 50-point bonus. If the ship sails past before you finish a message, you lose a life and move to the next message.

Controls: Mouse click, tap, or keyboard letters A–Z. Press Enter to start.
