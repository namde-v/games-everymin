Jade Juggler

How to Play: Jade balls arc from hand to hand. Each ball glows gold at its perfect catch moment as it passes over a catch zone. Press the matching key — D, F, J, or K — or tap / click the matching zone exactly then. Perfect catches score more and build your combo. Early or late catches still count, but a missed ball costs a life.

D — LeftF — Centre-LeftJ — Centre-RightK — Right

Win by making 50 catches. You have 3 lives. Balls get faster and more frequent as your streak grows. Sound starts only after you press Start.

Keyboard, mouse, and touch all work. Press Space to start or restart.
