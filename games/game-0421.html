KELP CAPTAIN

Run a coastal kelp farm! Plant fronds, water them, prune rot, and harvest before the storms roll in.

How to Play:

  • Goal: Harvest 25 kelp to WIN the season.
  • Plant costs 5 coins, Water speeds growth, Prune removes rot before it spreads.
  • Harvest mature (golden) kelp for +8 coins each. Rotten kelp is worthless — prune it fast!
  • Storms strike as the bar fills: unharvested mature kelp is torn away and farm health drops.
  • Game Over if your budget hits 0 with nothing growing, or farm health hits 0.

Controls: Click / tap a plot, or move with arrow keys + Space. Keys 1–4 switch tools. M = mute.

Click a plot to use the selected tool. Watered plots grow twice as fast. Rot appears on neglected plots — prune it before the storm!
