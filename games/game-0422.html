Lightning Locksmith

How to Play

  • Tap, click, or use arrow keys + Space to select and rotate a circuit tile.
  • Each rotation costs 1 move. Source and lock tiles never rotate.
  • Connect a glowing path from the yellow lightning source to every lock on the board.
  • Finish the level before moves run out. Clear all 5 levels to WIN.
  • Straight, elbow, T and cross tiles carry current only where their wires point.

Keyboard: Arrows move the cursor, Space / Enter rotates, R restarts.
Mouse and touch work on every tile.
