Arcade / Asteroid Mining Dash

Pilot your mining ship through a tumbling asteroid field. Drill ore-rich rocks, dodge the rest, and haul 100 ore back to win before your hull gives out!

How to Play:
  • Fly: Arrow keys / WASD, or drag on the screen to thrust toward your finger or mouse.
  • Drill: Hold SPACE, hold the mouse button, or hold DRILL on touch while touching an asteroid. Drilling cracks it and spills ore chunks.
  • Collect: Fly over glowing ore chunks to scoop them up. Golden asteroids are worth triple ore!
  • Survive: Smashing into an asteroid at speed damages your hull. Slow down near big rocks. Hull at 0% = Game Over.
  • Win: Reach 100 ore. Watch for the quota bar under the HUD.

Tip: tap thrust in short bursts for fine control. Drilling a rock to pieces also clears your path, but every hit shakes loose ore you can grab. Good luck, Miner!
