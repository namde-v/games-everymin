Night Navigator

How to Play: The night sky hides four constellations. Connect the stars of the current constellation in the correct order to chart its route.

  • The next correct star pulses gold only after you find the first star — use shape, spacing and trial-and-error to discover each route.
  • Click or tap a star to link it. A wrong link costs an error and breaks the current route.
  • Finish all 4 constellations before the timer ends. 6 errors ends the voyage.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys / WASD move the sky cursor, Enter or Space selects, R restarts.

Score: +100 per correct link, +250 per completed constellation, +50 time bonus per second left on a win.

Tip: completed constellations stay lit in green. The faint nebula stars are decoys — linking one is an error, so watch the pulse and the route shape. Press Start when you are ready; sound begins only after you press Start.
