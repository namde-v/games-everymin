💎 Opal Outpost

Goal: Turn 100 gold into 500 gold before Day 30 ends. Each outpost sells one good cheap and craves another — find the profitable routes!

  • Tap / click an outpost on the map, or use arrow keys + Enter to travel there (1 day).
  • Buy the local bargain, sell where it is scarce. Prices shift every day.
  • Upgrade your wagon to carry more cargo. Watch for market news!
  • Win at 500 gold. Game Over if you go bankrupt or the season ends short.

Keyboard: ← → cycle outposts, Enter travel, B buy 1, S sell 1, A sell all, U upgrade. Mouse and touch work on the map and every button. Travelling costs 1 day and a small road toll. Selling is free. Prices drift daily and a random market event may spike one good — read the news line before you buy!
