PRISM PIPER

Listen. Remember. Play it back — in rhythm.

How to Play

The Prism Piper plays a short melody on five glowing pipes. Listen carefully, then repeat the exact sequence by tapping the pipes.

  • Each round adds one more note to the melody.
  • Complete 8 rounds to win.
  • You have 3 lives — a wrong pipe costs one life.
  • Fast, confident echoes earn bonus points. Slow echoes still count, so stay calm.
  • Keys 1–5 play the pipes left to right. Mouse and touch work too.

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads, no network — everything plays offline.
