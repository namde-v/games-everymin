QUARRY QUESTER

You are a prospector trapped in a collapsing quarry. Dig through the earth, collect at least 8 gems, then reach the glowing exit shaft before time runs out!

How to Play
• Move / Dig: Arrow keys or WASD — walk into soft earth to dig through it.
• Gravity: You fall through empty space. Dig carefully below boulders!
• Boulders: Grey rocks fall when unsupported. If one lands on you, you lose a life.
• Cave-ins: Watch for shaking tiles and falling rubble after loud rumbles.
• Gems: Each gem is 100 points. The exit opens only with 8+ gems.
• Win: Reach the golden exit with 8 gems. Lose: Crushed 3 times or timer hits zero.
• Touch / Mouse: Use the on-screen arrows, or tap / click the canvas to dig toward that spot.

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads, fully offline.
