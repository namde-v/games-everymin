Press Start, then run! Collect runes in order. Dodge stone blocks.
How to Play: You run automatically. Move between the three lanes with ← → or A / D, jump with ↑ / Space / W. On touch, swipe left / right to change lanes and swipe up (or tap) to jump. Mouse users can click the ◀ ▶ lane buttons or click the canvas to jump. Each floating rune carries a letter. Take only the next letter of the target word: grabbing the wrong letter, or hitting a stone block, costs a life. Spell all four words — RUNE, GOLD, TEMPLE, MYSTIC — to win. Lose 3 lives and the run ends. Correct runes are worth 100 points, word bonuses are worth 250.
