☀️ Solar Shepherd

Herd every wandering solar sheep into the glowing sun pens before nightfall. Your shepherd dog guides them — sheep shy away from the dog, so drive them from behind!

How to Play
• Move the dog: WASD / Arrow keys, or click / tap and drag on the field.
• Press Space or the Bark button: a short bark nudges nearby sheep away from the dog.
• Push sheep into a golden pen. A penned sheep glows and stays put.
• Beware creeping shadow blobs from the dark edges — a sheep touched by shadow is lost.
• Win: pen all 8 sheep. Lose: 3 sheep lost, or night falls first.

Keyboard, mouse and touch all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.

You Win!

Tip: do not chase sheep head-on — get behind them and steer them toward the nearest golden pen.
