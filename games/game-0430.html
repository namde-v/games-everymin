Timber Trapper

The critters are marching down the forest trail toward your cabin. Lay snares, spikes and log traps in the grass beside the trail — then send the wave!

How to Play:
• Pick a trap below (or press 1 2 3), then tap / click the grass near the trail to place it.
• Press Space or Send Wave to start the next wave. Traps fire automatically when critters wander into range.
• Snare slows critters, Spike deals sharp damage, Log Drop smashes a wide area.
• Earn gold for each critter stopped. Survive all 8 waves to WIN. If 10 critters reach the cabin, it is Game Over.
• R restarts instantly. P pauses.
