☂️ Umbra Umbrella

Storm clouds are gathering over Umbra! Move the umbrella to shield your friend from the rain and catch golden stars for bonus points.

How to Play:
← → or A / D keys to move  •  Mouse move  •  Touch drag
Deflect blue raindrops with the umbrella canopy.
Catch ★ golden stars (+25 pts)  •  A drop hitting the ground costs 1 life.
Lose 3 lives = Game Over  •  Reach 500 points = You Win!
Sound starts only after you press Start. Drops get faster the longer you survive!
