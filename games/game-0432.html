Velvet Voyager

Guide a velvet probe through the gravity of strange planets. Slingshot around them, collect every data orb, then glide into the glowing target zone.

How to Play
• Click / touch and drag from the probe to aim — a violet arrow shows your launch.
• Release to launch. Planets pull your probe with gravity; use them to curve your path.
• Collect ALL data orbs, then reach the golden target ring.
• Crashing into a planet or drifting off course costs one launch.
• Press R to retry the current level. Mouse, keyboard and touch all work.
Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
