🌿 Willow Warden 🌿

How to Play: Blight beetles, grubs and thorn-fiends march along the winding dirt path toward the sacred grove on the right. Stop them before 10 slip through!

  • Pick a tower with keys 1 / 2 / 3 or by tapping its button, then click / tap an open grass tile to plant it. Towers cannot sit on the path.
  • Thorn Spitter (50 sun): rapid thorns, medium range. Bramble Mortar (100 sun): splash damage, slow. Moonbloom Sniper (150 sun): very long range, heavy damage.
  • Press Start Wave (or key Space / W) to send the next wave. Each kill earns sunlight and score. Survive all 10 waves to win!
  • Mouse: click to plant. Touch: tap to plant. Keyboard: 1/2/3 select, Space/W starts a wave, R restarts after the game ends.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
