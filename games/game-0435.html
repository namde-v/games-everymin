Yeti Yodeler

How to Play: Climb the mountain and reach the summit flag before the avalanche swallows you!

Move with Arrow keys / A & D, jump with W / ArrowUp. Press Space, click, or tap SHOUT to yodel — your echo shatters ice blocks above you and scares falling icicles.

Collect 💎 echo crystals for points. Dodge falling icicles — 3 hits and the climb ends. If the avalanche touches you, it is Game Over.

Touch: drag left / right to move, tap to shout, swipe up to jump.
