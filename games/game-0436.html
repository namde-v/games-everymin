🌱 Zephyr Zealot

Guide the floating seed to the flower!

How to Play: The seed drifts one tile at a time in the direction of the fan it stands on. Click or tap a fan tile to rotate it 90° clockwise. Use Arrow Keys to move the golden cursor and Space to rotate the fan under it. Enter launches the seed along the airflow. Collect breeze petals for bonus points, and reach the flower before your moves run out.
