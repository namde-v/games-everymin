ATLAS ACROBAT

A grappling swing platformer across the canyon

How to Play:
• Hold SPACE, hold the mouse button, or touch and hold to fire your grappling rope to the nearest anchor above you.
• Release to let go and fly with your momentum. Swing, build speed, and cross every chasm.
• Arrow keys / A D add a little push while swinging or in the air. W / Up shortens the rope.
• Collect gold medals for points. Avoid red spikes and falling into the canyon.
• Reach the golden flag platform at the far right to WIN. You have 3 lives.
