BEACON BUILDER

Light the way home for every ship!

How to Play: Ships are waiting out at sea. Place lighthouses on the golden coastline (row near the bottom) so their light beams reach every ship. Each lighthouse costs 20 budget and shines in a wide circle. Hover or move the cursor over a coastline cell to preview its beam before you build.

Mouse / touch: tap a coastline cell to build. Keyboard: Arrow Keys move the glowing cursor, Enter builds. Cover all 7 ships to WIN. If your budget runs out with ships still in the dark, it is Game Over.
