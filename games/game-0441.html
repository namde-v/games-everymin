Ember Engineer

You run a three-furnace forge. Fuel carts roll in from the depot — route them to the right furnace, keep each fire in its order's sweet spot, and never let a furnace overheat or go cold.

  • Goal: Complete 8 forge orders to win. Lose all 3 lives and the forge shuts down.
  • Dispatch: Click a furnace's STOKE button (or press 1 / 2 / 3 then F) to send a fuel cart rolling to it. A cart arriving adds heat; heat slowly cools on its own.
  • Vent: Click VENT (or V) on a hot furnace to bleed heat fast.
  • Orders: Each order names a furnace and a target heat zone. Hold the furnace inside that zone for the required seconds to fill the order and score points.
  • Danger: Above 95 heat the furnace overheats and you lose a life. Below 8 heat a burning order stalls and its timer resets.
  • Bonus: Filling an order exactly in the middle of its zone earns bonus points. Carts restock over time — don't waste them.
  • Controls: Mouse, touch, or keys — 1/2/3 select a furnace, F stoke, V vent, Space start / restart.
