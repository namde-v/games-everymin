🦅 FROST FALCON

Dive. Snatch. Soar. Don't get clipped by an eagle.

How to Play: Your falcon circles above a frozen lake. Steer left and right, then dive at exactly the right moment to snatch a fish through an ice hole. A clean catch builds your combo — a missed dive breaks it. Eagles patrol the sky and wind gusts shove you sideways, so watch the sky as well as the ice.

🐟 Catch 15 fish to win. You have 3 lives. Hitting an eagle or diving into solid ice costs a life. Bonus points for combos and for catching the rare golden fish.

⌨️ Arrow keys / A D to steer  •  Space to dive  •  🖱️ Move mouse to steer, click to dive  •  👆 Drag to steer, tap to dive
