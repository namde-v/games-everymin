IRON INKMASTER

How to Play: A glowing glyph appears on the parchment. Trace over it as accurately as you can. Drag with your mouse or finger to draw. On a keyboard, move the brush with the Arrow keys and hold Space to lay down ink.

Stay close to the glowing line for high accuracy. Shaky, jerky strokes spill ink and drain your inkwell faster. Finish each glyph before the ink dries. You have 3 lives. Complete all 10 glyphs to win.

Tip: slow, steady strokes beat fast scribbles. Lift and restart a stroke any time by releasing and pressing again. Press R to restart after a game ends.
