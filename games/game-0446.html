Juniper Jumper

Action / Vine Bounce Platformer

Bounce on springy juniper vines to climb the great cliff. Time your bounces to spring higher and reach the sunny summit ledge!

How to Play

Move: Arrow keys / A & D, or drag with mouse / touch

Bounce: land on a vine — press Space, click, or tap just as you land for a SUPER bounce

Avoid falling pinecones! Each hit costs a life. Fall off the bottom and you lose a life too.

Collect golden juniper berries for bonus points. Reach the summit flag to win!
