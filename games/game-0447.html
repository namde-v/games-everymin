LANTERN LURKER

A stealth light maze in a sleeping manor.

You are the Lurker. Slip through the dark manor, snuff out lamps to carve shadows, dodge the guards' sweeping light cones, gather relics, and reach the vault.

How to Play
  • Move with WASD or arrow keys. On touch, drag to steer; tap the canvas to walk toward a point.
  • Douse lamps: stand near a glowing lamp and press Space (or tap DOUSE). Darkness hides you.
  • Stay out of light cones: guards patrol with sweeping cones. Being caught in a cone costs a life.
  • Collect relics (golden diamonds) for points, then reach the vault door to win the level.
  • Your own lantern glows dimly — it reveals the maze near you, but guards see you sooner in open light.

Sound is procedural and starts only after you press Start.
