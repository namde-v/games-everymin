Marble Miner

Roll your marble through twisting underground tunnels. Momentum is everything — brake early, swing wide, and let gravity do the work.

Collect all 12 gems per level, dodge bottomless pits and rolling boulders, then reach the glowing exit shaft.

Steer: Arrow keys / WASD tilt the tunnel — your marble accelerates in that direction and keeps rolling. Tap Space to brake hard.
Mouse: Hold and drag — the marble rolls toward your pointer.
Touch: Drag anywhere to steer; lift your finger to coast.
Rules: Falling in a pit or being hit by a boulder costs 1 life. You have 3 lives. Collect every gem to open the exit. Finish all 3 levels to win. Boulders roll downhill and bounce off walls — use tunnels as shields.

Best played with sound on. Audio starts only after you press Start.
