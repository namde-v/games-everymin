NOVA NOMAD

Strategy / Space Trade Route Planning

Chart your own course across a pirate-haunted star map. Short hops are safe but thirsty; long direct jumps save fuel but invite pirate ambush. Take contracts, haul cargo, and bank 5,000 credits to win.

  • Click (or tap) stations on the star map to add them to your route. Click again to remove the last stop.
  • Press Launch Route (or Space) to fly. Each jump burns fuel based on distance.
  • Accept a contract, pick up its cargo at the origin station, then deliver it to the destination for credits.
  • Long jumps (over 170 units) risk pirate interception: you lose cargo and 1 hull. Hull 0 or fuel 0 ends the run.
  • Refuel at any station for 2 credits per unit. Keyboard: arrows select a station, Enter adds it, Space launches, R restarts.
  • Win by reaching 5,000 credits or completing 5 deliveries.
