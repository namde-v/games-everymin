ONYX ORACLE

A Puzzle / Rune Deduction Logic ritual. Twelve cursed runes hide among thirty-seven true runes on a 7 × 7 prophecy grid. Deduce them all — before your three lives fade.

How to Play

  • Attune (tap / click / Enter): reveal a rune. A true rune scores points and shows how many cursed runes surround it. A cursed rune costs one life.
  • Mark (Mark Mode, right-click, or M): seal a rune you deduce is cursed. Correct seals score points; wrong seals can be tapped again to unseal.
  • Win: reveal all 37 true runes and seal all 12 cursed runes. Or reveal every true rune — the Oracle accepts a complete attunement.
  • Game Over: lose all 3 lives by attuning cursed runes.
  • Clues: the number on a revealed rune counts adjacent cursed runes. The edge tallies list cursed counts per row and column.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys move the golden frame, Enter or Space attunes, M marks or unmarks, R restarts instantly.
  • Touch: tap to attune, or turn Mark Mode on and tap to seal. A long press also toggles a seal.

Scoring

  • True rune: +10  •  Correct seal: +25  •  Cursed attunement: −15 and −1 life  •  Hint: −25  •  Win bonus: + remaining lives × 100 + time bonus

Headphones optional. Sound is procedural and begins only after you press Start.
