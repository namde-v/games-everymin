PRISM PILOT

How to Play: You are the glowing light orb. Fly through the corridor, fire your beam, and bounce it off the silver mirrors to strike the golden targets hiding around corners.

  • Move: Arrow keys / WASD, or drag with mouse / touch
  • Aim & Fire: Move the mouse to aim, click or press Space to fire
  • Rotate mirrors: Click a mirror, or press Q / E to rotate the nearest mirror
  • Score: +100 per target hit. Reach 1500 points to WIN
  • Danger: Touching a moving prism costs 1 life. You have 3 lives — 0 lives is Game Over

Sound is procedural and starts only after you press Start.

🏆 YOU WIN!

You reached 1500 points and mastered every mirror in the corridor. Brilliant piloting, Prism Pilot!

Final Score: 1500

💥 GAME OVER

The prisms overwhelmed your light orb. Your beam fades — but pilots always fly again.

Final Score: 0

Keyboard: WASD / Arrows move  •  Space fire  •  Q / E rotate nearest mirror  •  R rotate all mirrors
Mouse: move to aim, click to fire (click a mirror to rotate it)  •  Touch: drag to move, tap to fire
