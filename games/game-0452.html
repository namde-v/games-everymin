💎 Quartz Quest

How to Play: Click, drag, or use arrow keys to trace a path through ore tiles. Start on a gem, travel through matching-coloured ore, and end on the matching gem of the same colour to complete a vein. Longer chains score more points!

Do not cross your own path or step on a finished vein. Complete 8 veins before you run out of moves or time.

Mouse / touch: drag from a gem. Keyboard: arrows to move, Enter to start / confirm a vein, Backspace to undo a step, R to restart.

Tip: plan a route that uses many tiles of the same colour — each extra tile adds +15 points, and a vein of 8+ tiles earns a bonus. A dead-end costs a move, not a life. You lose a life if you cross your path or hit a blocked tile.
