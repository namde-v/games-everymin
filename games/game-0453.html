🌊 River Ranger

You are the River Ranger. Rain fills the reservoir, the river runs past five farms, then past three towns. Open farm gates to water crops, but do not let the downstream flow flood the towns.

  • How to Play: Click a farm gate (or press keys 1–5) to open / close it. Click the dam (or press D) to HOLD or RELEASE water.
  • Each farm needs its blue supply bar to reach full before the season ends. Supply all 5 farms for 5 seasons to win.
  • Watch the reservoir: HOLD too long and it overflows. RELEASE too much and the river floods a town.
  • Rain storms raise inflow — react fast! A flooded town is lost. Lose 3 towns and the game is over.
  • Controls: Mouse click, touch tap, keys 1–5 for gates, D for dam, P to pause.

Tip: keep 2–3 gates open at a time. Close gates when a farm is full so more water reaches the next farm. Use HOLD during storms, then RELEASE slowly when the sky clears.
