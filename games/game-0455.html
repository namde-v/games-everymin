🌊 Tempest Surfer

A storm surge has stranded swimmers across a reef of rocks and sharks. Ride the waves, leap from crest to crest, and bring everyone home safely!

How to Play:
← → or A / D — Steer the surfboard and build momentum
↑ / W / Space — Jump between wave crests (hold for a higher leap)
Mouse click / tap — Jump  |  Drag / move — Steer toward the pointer
🏊 Touch a swimmer to rescue them  |  🪨🦈 Rocks and sharks cost a life
Land on a wave crest for a momentum boost and bonus points. Rescue 12 swimmers to win!

Press Enter or Space to start. Sound begins only after you press Start.

You Win!

Or press R / Enter to play again.

Keyboard: Arrows / WASD / Space  •  Mouse: click to jump, move to steer  •  Touch: tap to jump, drag to steer
