☁️ VAPOR VOYAGER ☁️

Strategy / Airship Trade Expedition — Trade wisely. Fly farther. Reach 2000 gold.
💰 Gold: 300 ⛽ Fuel: 100 📦 Cargo: 0 / 12 📍 Port: Aetherport Status: Docked Day: 1

Welcome, Captain!

How to Play:

  • Goal: Grow your gold from 300 to 2000 gold by buying low and selling high across floating islands.
  • Flying: Click / tap an island, or use keys 1–6 to select it, then press Fly (or Enter / F). Each hop burns fuel — tailwinds are cheap, headwinds are costly. Watch the wind arrow!
  • Trading: At a port, buy cargo when prices are low (green) and sell where prices are high (red). Hold at most 12 units. Prices drift every day.
  • Fuel: Buy fuel at any port (10 gold per 20 fuel). If fuel hits 0 while away from a port, you are stranded — Game Over.
  • Win / Lose: Win at 2000 gold. Lose if stranded with no fuel, or if you are broke (under 20 gold) with no cargo and no fuel to reach a market.

Keyboard: 1–6 select island, B buy, S sell, F fly, R refuel, Enter start. Mouse & touch work on all buttons and islands.

Victory!

Docked at Aetherport. Prices are fresh — check the market board below the sky before you fly.
Tip: Silk is dear at Ember Spire today, but cheap at Mistral — the wind favours the eastern route. Tap an island to see its price and fuel cost.
