Willow Whisperer

An ancient willow sings only when the wind is just right. Breezes carry glowing gusts down five hanging chimes. Your job is not to tap — it is to hold and release at the perfect moment.

How to Play

1. When a glowing gust nears a chime, press and HOLD that chime's key (or press and hold it with mouse / touch).

2. Keep holding as the gust crosses the golden line — then RELEASE exactly as it touches the line for a Perfect chime.

3. Release too early or too late and the gust slips away. Miss 10 gusts and the willow falls silent.

4. Complete all 3 melodies to win and wake the willow.

A = Chime 1 (low)S = Chime 2D = Chime 3F = Chime 4G = Chime 5 (high)

Mouse: press and hold a chime. Touch: press and hold a chime. Keys 1–5 work too.
