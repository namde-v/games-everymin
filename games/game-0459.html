XENON XPLORER

Slingshot around planets. Steal their gravity. Grab the stars.

How to Play: Your ship rests at the launch station. Press and drag away from the ship to draw an aim arrow and a dotted gravity preview, then release to launch. Gravity from the purple planets will bend your path — use it to swing around them and reach far shards.

Collect 15 star shards to win. Crashing into a planet costs 1 life. You have 3 lives. After launching, use ← → ↑ ↓ or W A S D for a small thrust, or press R to relaunch from the station. On touch screens, drag to aim and release to fly.

Watch out: drifting off-screen also returns you to the station and costs a life. Plan each slingshot!

Tip: a short drag = gentle hop, a long drag = fast slingshot. Aim so the dotted preview curves around a planet, not into it. Shards near planets are worth the risk — they respawn in new spots as you collect them.
