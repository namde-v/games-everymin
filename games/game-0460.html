⛷️ Summit Skier

Outrun the avalanche to the village!

How to Play: You are skiing down a mountain while a huge avalanche chases you. Jump over icy crevasses, dodge pine trees, and grab glowing snow crystals for bonus points.

⌨️ Jump: Space / Arrow Up / W  •  Tuck (faster): Arrow Down / S

🖱️ Mouse: Click to jump  •  📱 Touch: Tap the screen to jump

🏁 Win: Reach the village at 2000 m  •  💥 Game Over: The avalanche catches you or you lose all 3 lives

🏆 You Made It!

You reached the village!

The avalanche rumbles to a stop behind you. The villagers cheer as you ski safely into town with your crystals sparkling.

🌨️ Avalanche!

The avalanche caught you!

Shake off the snow, grab your skis, and try the slope again. Jump earlier over crevasses and watch the trees!

Tip: Tucking (Down key) makes you faster — but watch for trees! Crystals are worth 50 points.
