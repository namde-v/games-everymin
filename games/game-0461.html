EMBER ENGINEER

How to Play: Orders arrive at the top. Each order wants a metal, a mold shape, and a target temperature. Select the metal, use Heat + / − to reach the target range, select the mold, and press POUR. The casting cools in the mold — a pour inside the target range fulfils a matching order for points. A pour outside the range is a defect and costs a life. An expired order also costs a life.

Keyboard: 1/2/3 metal · ←/→ mold · ↑/↓ heat · Space pour  |  Mouse / touch: tap the buttons and the molds on the canvas.

Win by fulfilling 20 orders. Game Over if you lose all 3 lives. Good luck, engineer!
