Fjord Fisher

The fjord is frozen, but the fish are hungry. Hop between five ice holes, drop your line, and reel in 20 fish before your line snaps three times.

How to Play
  • Move: ← → arrow keys, A / D, click or tap an ice hole.
  • Cast: Space, Enter, or click / tap the CAST button when a fish shadow is near your hole.
  • Reel: HOLD Space, mouse button, or touch while the fish is calm. RELEASE instantly when it dashes (red flash) — reeling during a dash spikes line tension.
  • Danger: Drifting ice chunks add tension if they strike your line. If tension hits 100%, the line snaps and you lose a life.
  • Scoring: Arctic char, cod, halibut and the rare Golden Trout are worth more at higher levels. Chain catches without a snap for a combo bonus.

Tip: watch the shadow under the ice. A slow, circling shadow is curious — cast now. A fast, darting shadow is a fighter; save your hardest reeling for its calm moments.
