Glyph Gardener

How to Play
Pick a rune seed below, then tap or click an empty garden plot to plant it. Rotate your seed with the Rotate button or R before planting.
Line up 3 or more of the SAME glyph pointing the SAME way in a row or column and they bloom for points!

Weeds sprout every few seconds. Tap a weed to pull it. If 8 weeds fill the garden you lose a life. Reach 600 points to win the harvest. Keyboard: Arrows move, Space plants, R rotates, 1–4 picks a seed.
