IRON INFILTRATOR

You are inside a high-security vault facility. Guards sweep the halls with vision cones and laser grids pulse on and off. Collect all 3 keycards, then escape through the glowing EXIT door.

How to Play:
Move: WASD / Arrow keys • or drag with mouse / touch • or tap / click a spot to walk toward it.
Avoid yellow vision cones and red active lasers. Getting spotted or zapped costs a life.
Keycards open the exit. Finish fast and unseen for bonus points.

Stealth tip: wait for a guard to look away and for lasers to blink off before crossing a corridor. Detection fills fast in a cone, but stepping out breaks line of sight.
