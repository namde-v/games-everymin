🌴 Jungle Juggler 🍌

Swing through the canopy, juggle falling jungle fruit between your baskets, and chain perfect catches for huge combos!

How to Play:
🕹️ Move: ← → / A D keys, mouse, or drag on touch.
🍍 Catch fruit: Mango, banana, pineapple and berries score points. Catch several in a row to raise your combo multiplier.
🥥 Dodge coconuts: A coconut costs one life. Missed fruit breaks your combo.
⭐ Golden fruit: Rare, worth 50 points and restores your combo to max.
🏆 Win: Reach 1500 points or catch 60 fruit before your 3 lives run out.

Tip: Stay under the densest fruit stream, but dart away when a coconut shadow appears!

Keyboard, mouse and touch supported. Sound starts only after you press Start.
