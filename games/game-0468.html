🏛️ Lagoon Legislator

How to Play: You chair the Reef Council for 12 sessions. Each session, three factions bring you a policy. Pick ONE policy card, pay its influence cost, and live with its effects on coral, fish, and tourism.

Keep every meter above zero — if coral, fish, tourism, or influence collapses, your term ends. Balanced meters earn bonus points. Decide before the session timer runs out!

Controls: Mouse / touch: tap a policy card. Keyboard: press 1, 2 or 3 to pass a policy, V to veto, R to restart, Enter to start.

Term Complete

Tip: A policy that helps one faction usually hurts another. The best legislators protect the middle, not just one meter.
