Deep below the mountain, gems pulse to a beat. Swing your pickaxe exactly when the beat marker hits the center and the rock shatters in rhythm.

How to Play:
Press SPACE, click the canvas, or tap on the beat to swing.
Perfect timing = bigger gems and combo multipliers.
Miss the beat and a cave-in costs a life. Collect all gems in 3 levels to win!
