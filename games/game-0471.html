How to Play
🕹️ Move: Arrow Keys / WASD, or drag with mouse / touch
🦘 Jump: Space, Up Arrow, W, or tap the player
💎 Collect gems for 50 points each — collect 30 to WIN
🪨 Avoid falling rocks — a hit costs 1 life. You have 3 lives.
⚠️ Don't fall behind! The floor collapses left to right. Falling into a pit costs 1 life and respawns you on solid ground.
🏆 Win: 30 gems. Lose: 0 lives left.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
