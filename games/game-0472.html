🗺️ How to Play

  • Solve the chain: Read each riddle. Pick the one answer that fits every clue.
  • Unlock coral clues: A correct answer reveals the next coral clue and grows your reef. A wrong answer costs 1 life.
  • Chain logic: Later riddles build on earlier answers — remember words like echo, tide, pearl and current.
  • Win: Solve all 10 clues before you run out of lives to claim the Treasure Pearl.
  • Controls: Click or tap an answer tile, use Arrow Keys to move the selection, press Enter / Space to confirm. Press H for a hint (costs 10 points).

Fully offline. No downloads, no accounts. Just you, the reef, and ten clever riddles.
Tip: On touch devices, tap a tile twice — once to select, once to confirm — or tap then press Confirm.
