☀ Solar Shepherd

Strategy / Orbital Power Management

You are the shepherd of a small solar grid. Orbiting panels harvest sunlight, but no panel alone can feed every station. Route power wisely, split it fairly, and survive five shifting demand waves.

How to Play

  • Goal: Reach 1500 points to win. Lose all 3 lives and the grid collapses.
  • Panels: Three panels orbit the sun. Their output rises and falls as they circle — watch the number on each panel.
  • Stations: Four stations each demand a certain amount of power. A station is happy only when it receives at least its demand.
  • Links: A panel linked to two stations splits its power between them. A panel linked to one station gives it everything.
  • Scoring: Every happy station earns points every second. A station starved for too long costs you a life.
  • Mouse / Touch: Tap a panel to select it, then tap a station to toggle its link. Tap a linked station again to cut the link.
  • Keyboard: Left / Right choose a panel, Up / Down choose a station, Space toggles the link, Enter starts or restarts.
  • Waves: Demand shifts every 18 seconds. Re-route quickly when a new wave arrives!
