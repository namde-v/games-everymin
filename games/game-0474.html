✂️ Tempo Tailor

A rhythm tailoring challenge! Stitch markers scroll down four thread lanes toward the needle line. Press the matching key or tap the button at the perfect moment to sew a beautiful stitch.

How to Play:
• Press Start to begin the beat.
• When a stitch marker reaches the glowing needle line, press A / S / D / F or tap the matching on-screen button.
• Perfect (+100), Good (+60), Early/Late (+25). A miss unravels the seam and costs a life.
• Complete 40 stitches to finish the garment and win. Lose all 3 lives and the seam unravels — game over.
• Watch the beat pulse: markers arrive on the rhythm, so trust your ears as well as your eyes.
