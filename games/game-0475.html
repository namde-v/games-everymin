Wisp Warden

You are the keeper of the last lantern. Night wisps drift through the haunted grove — herd them into the glowing pen before the shadow hounds snatch them away.

How to Play:
• Move with Arrow Keys / WASD, or drag with mouse / touch — the warden follows your pointer.
• Wisps flee from you: approach slowly from behind and steer them toward the golden pen in the corner.
• Pen 8 wisps to win the night. Each penned wisp scores 100 points.
• Shadow hounds chase wisps and steal them. If a hound touches you, you lose a life.
• Press Space (or tap the Bell button) to ring the bell — it stuns nearby hounds for 3 seconds. Bell recharges in 6 seconds.
• Lose all 3 lives, or let 4 wisps be stolen, and the night is lost.

Tip: corner a wisp against a wall, then walk it gently into the pen. Fast lunges scatter the flock!

Night Saved!

The lantern burns bright.

Press Start, then herd the wisps into the golden pen.
