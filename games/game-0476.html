Cipher Canyon

Puzzle / Code-Breaking Trail — Every bridge plank is locked by a secret word written in canyon symbols. Crack the code and walk across!

📖 How to Play

  • Goal: Decode all 5 words — CANYON, BRIDGE, TRAIL, STONE and QUEST in random order — to finish the bridge and win.
  • Pick a symbol: Click or tap a symbol tile, or use Arrow Left / Right to select it. The selected tile glows pink.
  • Assign a letter: Click a letter button, tap it, or just type A–Z on your keyboard. Press Enter to check a finished word and Backspace to clear a tile.
  • Use the clues: Each word gives you a hint sentence and one revealed symbol-to-letter clue. The word pattern and length help too.
  • Wrong decodes cost you: A wrong letter loses points and 5 seconds. Three wrong guesses in a row cost 1 life. You have 3 lives and 240 seconds.
  • Scoring: +100 per correct letter, +250 per completed word, +10 per second left at the finish.
  • Win or lose: Finish all 5 planks to win. Lose all lives or run out of time and the trail ends.

🖱️ Mouse   ⌨️ Keyboard   👆 Touch — all supported. Sound starts only after you press Start.
