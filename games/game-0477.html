Captain’s Tips

Spices and Silk are pricey and swing wildly with the tide — big risk, big reward. Fish and Timber are steadier. Never fill your hold with one good at a high price. Watch the tide meter: a rising tide lifts Fish prices, a falling tide favors Timber and Spices. Sell before Tide 12 ends!

⚓ TIDAL TRADER

Strategy / Harbor Cargo Trading

You start with 300 gold and an empty hold of 20 units. Sail between three harbors, buy low, and sell high before the 12th tide ends. Reach 1500 gold to win!

How to Play

  • Goal: Reach 1500 gold by the end of Tide 12. Your net worth is gold plus cargo value at current prices.
  • Mouse / Touch: Tap a harbor to sail there (20 gold fee). Tap Buy, Sell, or Sell All on any good.
  • Keyboard: ← → choose harbor • ↑ ↓ choose good • Enter trade 1 unit • T toggle Buy / Sell • Space advance tide.
  • Tides: Every tide shifts prices. A new tide can make yesterday’s bargain today’s treasure — or the reverse.
  • Cargo: Your hold fits 20 units total. Sell to free space. Travel costs 20 gold, so do not sail empty-handed for no reason.
  • Win / Lose: Win instantly at 1500 gold. If Tide 12 ends below 1500 gold, or you go broke with no cargo, the voyage ends.
