❤ PULSE PILOT

You are the pulse, flying down a living artery. Obstacles appear in sync with the heartbeat. Move with the rhythm or crash out of sync.

How to Play

  • Move: Arrow Left / Right, A / D, click left or right side, or tap left / right half of the screen to switch lanes.
  • Dash: Press Space, click the DASH button, or tap the centre button when the beat ring flashes. A dash on the downbeat smashes through an obstacle for +100.
  • Mistimed dash: Dashing off the beat costs a life. Crashing into an obstacle costs a life too.
  • Collect: Golden hearts restore rhythm and give +50. Survive to 5000 points to WIN.
  • Audio: Procedural heartbeat sound starts only after you press Start.

Tip: watch the pulsing ring at the bottom. Dash only when it is full and bright.
