🪨 Boulder Bouncer

Launch a bouncing boulder, ricochet it off angled walls, and smash every crystal target in as few shots as you can!

How to Play
• Mouse / Touch: drag from the launcher to aim, release to launch.
• Arrow Keys: Left / Right adjust angle, Up / Down adjust power.
• Space: launch the boulder. R: restart instantly.
• Ricochets off walls and angled bumpers count — use them to reach hidden targets.
• You have 12 shots. Smash all targets to win. Fewer shots = bigger bonus!

Tip: a long, slow bounce often clears a corner better than a fast straight shot.
