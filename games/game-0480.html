How to Play
  • Goal: Reach 120 population before Turn 30 ends. Lose all 3 Health and the city collapses.
  • Hubs cost 10 nutrients and house spores. A hub only grows if it is connected by paths to the glowing Mother Spore in the centre.
  • Paths cost 2 nutrients and carry nutrients through the mycelium network. Build paths outward from the Mother Spore first.
  • Each Grow Turn: every connected hub gains population but consumes nutrients. Unconnected hubs gain nothing and still cost upkeep if crowded. Random blight may strike overgrown districts.
  • Balance: Too many hubs without enough nutrients drains Health. Too few hubs and you will never reach 120 in time.

Controls: Mouse / touch — tap a tile to place. Arrow Keys — move cursor. Space — place selected tile. Enter — Grow Turn. Buttons above also work.
