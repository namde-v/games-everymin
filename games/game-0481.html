✨ Aurora Alchemist ✨

How to Play: The Aurora Council orders one potion at a time. Each order gives a poetic clue naming its ingredients. Deduce the recipe, tap or press keys to drop ingredients into the cauldron, then Brew!

  • 🔥 Ember, ❄️ Frost, 🍃 Leaf, ⭐ Star, 🌊 Tide, 🪨 Stone — keys 1 to 6.
  • Correct brew: +100 points, plus a speed bonus if you use no hints.
  • Wrong brew: you lose 1 life and the cauldron empties.
  • Brew all 8 potions to win. Lose all 3 lives and the shop closes.
  • Hints reveal one correct ingredient but cost 15 points.

Example clue: “Winter fire, frozen light” hints at Ember + Frost + Star. Read the words, trust the elements!
