🐍 Basilisk Bounce

Arcade / Reflective Orb Pong

Guide the serpent orb with your paddle. Hit angled mirrors to bend its path, smash all the crystals, and build huge combos.

  • Move: Arrow keys / A D, mouse, or touch-drag. Your paddle tilts as you move — the tilt steers the orb.
  • Launch: Press Space or tap Start, then the orb flies. Keep it in play!
  • Score: Crystals are worth 100 points. Consecutive crystal hits without touching the paddle raise your combo multiplier.
  • Mirrors: Golden angled mirrors reflect the orb at sharp new angles — use them to reach tricky crystals.
  • Lives: You have 3 lives. Lose the orb past the bottom and you lose one. Clear every crystal to WIN.
  • Bonus: Green serpent crystals are worth 250 points and restore a bounce bonus.
