How to Play

You are the Cadence Courier, racing a delivery route that pulses to the beat. Parcels slide down four lanes toward the delivery line. Strike each parcel exactly as it crosses the line.

  • Keyboard: press A, S, K, L for lanes 1 to 4. Arrow keys move the courier too.
  • Mouse: click a lane (or its lane button below the canvas) when its parcel hits the line.
  • Touch: tap a lane on the screen at the right moment.
  • Timing: Perfect = 100 pts, Good = 50 pts, Early/Late = 25 pts. A miss drops a parcel.
  • Win: deliver 30 parcels. Game Over: drop 3 parcels.

Sound starts only after you press Start.
