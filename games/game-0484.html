DUNE DIPLOMAT

Strategy · Desert Alliance Negotiation

You are the envoy of the Oasis Council. Five desert tribes guard the wells, roads, and spice routes. The great storm arrives in 12 days. Secure 3 alliances before then, or every caravan is lost.

How-to-Play

  • Each turn is one day. Pick a tribe, offer one resource, pick a dialogue stance, then Negotiate.
  • Trust meters matter. A tribe joins you at 75 trust. Match their favoured resource and stance for big gains; a disliked stance can cost trust.
  • Read the hints. Each tribe card whispers what it values. Mystics love respect, traders respect firmness, raiders admire cunning.
  • Resources are limited. Every gift costs 1 unit. Run low? Use Gather Supplies to restock — but it still costs a day.
  • Standing is your lives. A badly botched negotiation costs 1 Standing. Lose all 3 and the council recalls you in disgrace.
  • Win: 3 alliances before Day 12 ends. Lose: fewer than 3 alliances on Day 12, or 0 Standing.

Controls: mouse click, touch tap, or keyboard (arrows, 1-4, Q/W/E/R, Enter, G). Sound is procedural and starts only after you press Start.

Tip: do not waste gifts on an already-allied tribe. Spread early gifts to learn each tribe, then focus on the two or three closest to 75. A tribe at 70+ needs just one well-matched gift. Gather early if you plan to negotiate every day — you start with 25 resource units for 12 days.
