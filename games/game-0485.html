🌑 Eclipse Engineer

Align the eclipse shadows. Tap a mirror to rotate it. Light all three gold targets before you run out of moves.

How to Play
  • Sunlight starts at the bright sun cell and travels in a straight line.
  • Mirrors bend light: / and \ send it in a new direction. Tap, click, or select with arrows and press Space to rotate a mirror.
  • Diamond lenses split light: one beam goes straight and one branches off.
  • Light every gold target cell on the grid to clear the level.
  • Each rotation costs one move. Clear all 3 levels to win.

You Win!
