🦅 FALCON FLYER

You are a peregrine falcon over golden grassland. Catch 20 prey to win. Ride glowing thermals to climb safely, then dive for a high-score catch — but the faster you dive, the harder you turn, and storm clouds will knock you out of the sky.

How to Play:
• Move: Arrow keys / WASD, or move the mouse, or drag on touch.
• Dive: Hold Down / S, hold mouse button, or hold touch — dive speed rises, turning gets sloppy, catch points multiply.
• Thermals: Golden rising columns lift you and restore control. Stay in one for a +25 bonus.
• Prey: Small birds and field mice. Touch one to catch it. Dive catches are worth more.
• Storms: Dark clouds drift across the sky. Hitting one costs 1 life. You have 3 lives.
• Win: Catch 20 prey. Lose: Lose all 3 lives.
⌨️ Arrows / WASD  •  🖱️ Mouse move + hold to dive  •  👆 Touch drag + hold to dive
Space = dive  •  P = pause  •  R = instant restart

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads, no network — fully offline.
