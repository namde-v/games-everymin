Glacier Gardener

How to Play: Pick a crop below, then tap or click an icy plot to plant it. Tap a growing crop to water it with meltwater. Tap a glowing golden crop to harvest it for points. Seasons change every few days: Summer melts extra water, Winter frost damages thirsty crops. Lose 3 crops to frost or drought and the glacier wins. Reach 150 points to win!

Keyboard: Arrow keys move the cursor, Space plants / waters / harvests, 1-4 picks a crop, Enter starts.

Frostberry (fast, 10 pts) • Ice Lettuce (medium, 15 pts) • Snowroot (slow, 25 pts) • Glacier Melon (slowest, 40 pts). Watered crops grow faster and survive Winter. Unwatered crops wilt in frost storms. Watch the season banner and keep meltwater topped up in Summer.
