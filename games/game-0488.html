🌾 Harmonic Harvester 🎵

Musical crops are growing in four rows. Each crop sings a note as it ripens. Harvest it at exactly the right moment to play the melody and earn a combo!

How to Play:
• Crops scroll down four lanes toward the golden harvest line.
• Press the matching lane when a crop touches the line: D = C (red), F = E (orange), J = G (green), K = A (blue).
• Or tap / click a lane button below, or tap the lane on the canvas.
• Perfect (dead centre) = 100 pts, Good = 50 pts, Early / Late = 20 pts.
• Chain hits to raise your combo multiplier up to x5. Missing a crop breaks the combo and costs a life.
• Survive all 60 crops and score 1500+ to WIN. Lose all 5 lives and the harvest fails.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Best with sound on.

Game Over

Keyboard: D F J K (or 1 2 3 4)  •  Mouse: click a lane / lane button  •  Touch: tap a lane / lane button  •  Space also restarts on the end screen.
