🔍 The Case of the Vanishing Manuscript

Last night, the priceless manuscript The Midnight Atlas vanished from the Old Library. The thief left only ink blots, a broken quill, and five nervous suspects. Every clue points to one person — but false leads point everywhere else. Examine the clues, cross-check the alibis, eliminate the innocent, and accuse the culprit before time runs out.

How to Play

  1. Examine a clue (left column): tap or click a clue to see what it reveals — ink colour, pen type, handwriting, time, location, or a torn fragment.
  2. Examine a suspect (right column): read their alibi and traits. Tap Eliminate when their story contradicts a clue.
  3. Link clues: select a clue, then select the suspect it implicates, then press Link Clue to Suspect. Correct links earn +50 points; wrong links cost 10 points.
  4. Accuse: when you are sure, press Accuse on a suspect. A wrong accusation costs one life. You have 3 lives and 180 seconds.
  5. Win by accusing the true culprit. Lose if lives or time run out.

Keyboard: ↑↓ move between suspects, 1–5 select a suspect, C cycle clues, L link, A accuse selected suspect, R restart on the end screen. Mouse and touch work everywhere.

Fully offline. No downloads, no accounts. Just you, the ink, and the truth.
