Jetstream Juggler

Shifting jetstreams toss your balloons around. Tap, click or press a key to bounce them back up before they hit the ground!

  • How to Play: Keep all balloons aloft. Each bounce scores points, and you score every second you survive.
  • Wind: Gusts push balloons sideways. Watch the wind bar and the arrows in the sky — strong gusts need early taps.
  • Lives: A balloon touching the ground costs 1 life. Lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over.
  • Win: Reach 750 points to win. New balloons and faster winds appear as you climb.
  • Controls: Mouse click / tap a balloon to bounce it. Keys 1, 2, 3, 4 bounce balloons in order. Space bounces the lowest balloon. Arrow Left / Right nudges the wind fan.

Tip: bounce a balloon a little early when a gust is coming — a high balloon has room to drift and recover. Tap the balloon itself for a bigger bounce than a tap elsewhere.
