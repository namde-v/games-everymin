🌊 Kelp Keeper

Can you earn 150 coins before the season ends?

How to Play

  • Select a kelp bed by tapping / clicking it, or with the Arrow Keys.
  • Plant a seed in an empty bed. Kelp grows through stages: sprout, young, tall, then PEAK.
  • Feed costs 5 Nutrients and speeds growth. Aerate costs 5 Oxygen and keeps kelp healthy.
  • Harvest ONLY at PEAK for full coins (20–35). Early harvest pays little; waiting too long makes kelp rot and costs a life.
  • Feed and Aerate refill slowly over time, and a small bonus arrives every 30 seconds.
  • Reach 150 coins before the 180-second season timer ends to win. Lose all 3 lives and the farm fails.

Keyboard: Arrow Keys move, P plant, F feed, A aerate, H harvest, Enter start / restart.

Game Over

Select a bed, then plant your first kelp!
Tip: Kelp at PEAK glows golden. Harvest it right away — golden kelp rots after about 10 seconds.
Mouse / touch: tap a bed, then tap an action. Keyboard: Arrow Keys to select + P / F / A / H.
