Lantern Labyrinth

You are lost in a pitch-dark labyrinth. Your lantern is your only friend — and it is fading.

How to Play
🕹️ Move: Arrow keys / WASD, or tap / click a nearby floor tile.
🔑 Keys: Collect golden keys scattered in the maze.
🔥 Braziers: Step onto a brazier while carrying a key to light it (uses 1 key).
👻 Shadow Wisps: Avoid them! Touching one drains 20% lantern light.
🚪 Win: Light all 3 braziers, then reach the glowing exit door.
💡 Lose: If your lantern hits 0%, the darkness wins.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Best played with sound on.
