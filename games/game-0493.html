METEOR MECHANIC

Pilot a repair drone. Dodge the meteor storm. Save the station.

How to Play

  • Move: Arrow keys / WASD, or mouse / touch-drag to steer the drone.
  • Collect scrap: Fly over glowing scrap (yellow bolts) to earn repair material and score.
  • Weld breaches: Fly to a red sparking breach on the station ring and hold position briefly to weld it. Each weld costs 2 scrap.
  • Dodge meteors: Meteors fall from above. A hit costs 1 drone hull. If a meteor strikes an open breach, station hull drops fast.
  • Win: Repair all 6 breaches before station hull reaches 0%.
  • Lose: Station hull hits 0% or your drone hull hits 0.
  • Tip: Grab scrap early, weld the breach nearest a falling meteor first, and use the station ring as a shield lane.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
