Nightingale Navigator

Chart a safe route across the night sky. The nightingale ship must hop from star to star in constellation order, reach the golden Home Star, and do it before the fuel runs out.

How to Play
• Goal: Visit the stars in chart order: Vega, Altair, Deneb, Rigel, Sirius, Polaris, Capella, then the Home Star.
• Mouse / Touch: Tap or click the glowing next star. A wrong star wastes 8 fuel.
• Keyboard: Arrow keys move the selector, Enter or Space confirms the selected star. Press R to restart.
• Fuel: Every jump costs fuel based on distance. Long jumps are expensive — plan a short route.
• Storms: Purple nebula storms drift across the map. A route line crossing a storm costs 1 life and 10 extra fuel.
• Win: Reach the Home Star in order. Lose: Fuel hits 0 or all 3 lives are lost.

Tip: the next correct star pulses gold. Storms move slowly — wait for a clear lane when you can.
