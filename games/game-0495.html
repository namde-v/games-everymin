Obsidian Oracle

How to Play: The Oracle has hidden a secret prophecy of four runes, drawn from eight ancient runes. Runes may repeat. You have eight guesses to reveal it.

  • Tap a rune in the palette, then tap a stone to place it. Tap a filled stone to clear it.
  • Keyboard: keys 1–8 choose a rune, Left / Right move between stones, Up / Down cycle a rune, Enter consults the oracle, Backspace clears.
  • Mouse and touch work everywhere: tap runes, stones, and buttons.
  • After each guess, the oracle reports Exact matches (right rune, right place) and Echoes (right rune, wrong place). Use elimination and position logic to narrow the prophecy.
  • Solve it early for a bigger score. A hint reveals one true stone but costs 50 points.

Scoring: 1000 points for solving, plus 120 for every unused guess, minus hints used. Losing still rewards 25 points per Exact match found along the way.

Fully offline. No outside sounds, images, or files — only runes, logic, and you.
