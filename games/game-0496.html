PRISM PILOT

Arcade / Color Refraction Shooter — split light, match color, survive the reflections.

How to Play:
• Fly your prism ship and split light beams at falling target prisms.
• Match the color! A Ruby beam destroys only Ruby targets, Cyan only Cyan, and so on.
• Mismatched beams bounce back! A wrong-color beam reflects toward you — dodge it or lose a life.
• Move: Arrow keys / WASD, or drag with mouse / touch. Shoot: Space / Click / Tap.
• Switch beam color: 1–4 or C / Q to cycle, or tap the color buttons above.
• Do not let a target reach the bottom. Clear all 3 waves to win!
