⛏️ QUARRY QUEST

Run your own quarry! Dig a grid of rock to uncover coal, iron, gold, gems and rare mithril. Sell ore, upgrade your pickaxe, rest your workers, and reach 1500 wealth before the mine collapses.

  • How to Play: Click / tap a rock tile (or move with arrow keys / WASD and press Space / Enter) to dig it. Each dig costs energy and pickaxe durability.
  • Ore values: Coal 15, Iron 30, Gold 80, Gem 150, Mithril 300. Deeper rows hide richer ore — and tougher rock.
  • Hazards: Gas pockets cost a life. Empty rock still costs energy, so dig wisely.
  • Manage: Sell ore for gold, repair your pickaxe, rest to restore energy, and buy upgrades to dig faster and deeper.
  • Win: Reach 1500 total wealth. Lose: Timer runs out, all lives lost, or energy hits zero with no gold to rest.

Keyboard: Arrows / WASD to move, Space to dig  |  Mouse & touch fully supported
