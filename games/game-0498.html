How to Play:

🎵 A beat marker travels down the wave toward the glowing hit zone. Tap SURF, press SPACE, click, or touch the wave exactly when the marker lands in the zone.

⭐ Perfect (centre) = +100 × combo  •  Good (edge) = +50  •  Miss = lose 1 balance heart.

🔥 Perfect hits build your combo multiplier (up to x5). Hold nothing — rhythm is everything: tap on the beat, not early, not late.

🏆 Survive all 60 beats and finish with 5000+ points to win. Lose all 3 hearts and you wipe out.

⌨️ Keys: SPACE / UP to surf  •  R to restart  •  Mouse and touch work too.
