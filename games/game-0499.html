Solar Shepherd

Six star-lambs are drifting through a dangerous stellar pasture. Two black holes are hungry. One glowing orbit pen is home. Lead every lamb into the pen — without losing three of them to the dark.

How to Play

  • Move your shepherd ship: Arrow keys / WASD, or drag with mouse / touch. The ship steers toward your pointer.
  • Herd: Fly close to a lamb and it will trust you and follow. Lead it slowly into the glowing golden pen.
  • Gravity nudge: Click or tap anywhere to send a gentle pulse that tugs nearby lambs toward that point. Use it to rescue a lamb sliding toward a black hole.
  • Penning: A lamb inside the pen settles into orbit and stays. Pen all 6 to win.
  • Danger: Black holes pull everything nearby. If a lamb is swallowed, you lose a life. Lose 3 lives and the flock is lost.

Tip: slow and steady wins. Fast turns scatter the flock.
