⛈ TEMPEST TACTICIAN

You are the Stormcaller. The enemy fleet has sailed into your tempest — now use wind, rain, and lightning like pieces on a chessboard to sink every last ship.

⚡ LIGHTNING🌧 RAIN💨 WIND
How to Play
1. Select one of your 4 storm units (blue) on the 8×8 sea grid.
2. Move it to a highlighted blue tile, then attack an enemy in red range — or attack without moving.
3. Each unit acts once per turn. Press End Turn when ready; then the fleet moves and fires back.
4. Exploit matchups: Lightning beats Iron, Rain beats Fire, Wind beats Sails. Wrong elements still hit, but for half damage.
5. Win by sinking all 5 ships. Lose if all your storm units fall. High ground? No — high storm wins here: position matters, so attack from range and never leave a wounded unit exposed.

Controls: Mouse click • Touch tap • Arrow keys / WASD + Enter • E = End Turn • R = Restart

Sound starts only after you press Start. Your score grows with damage and sinking bonuses.
