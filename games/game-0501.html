VAULT VOYAGER

You are deep inside a shifting treasure vault. The floor becomes the ceiling in a heartbeat. Flip gravity, slip past laser grids, grab golden relics, and reach the escape vault.

Tip: Flip at the last moment to thread between ceiling and floor lasers.

How to Play

Run automatically through endless vault corridors. Your only powers are movement and gravity itself. Collect 15 golden relics to unlock the escape vault and win. Touching a laser costs one life. Lose all 3 lives and the vault seals shut.

Move
← → or A / D keys, or on-screen left / right zones
Flip Gravity
Space, W, ↑, mouse click, or tap the vault
Dash
Shift, or double-tap, for a short burst of speed
Pause / Restart
P pauses, R restarts instantly
  • Gold diamonds are relics: +100 points each, plus a speed bonus.
  • Green crystals restore a lost life when you are below full health.
  • Red laser bars and pulsing laser orbs are deadly — flip or dodge around them.
  • Silver doors appear after 10 relics and slow the corridor for a breather.
  • The corridor gets faster and laser patterns get denser as distance grows.

Vault Notes

Vault Voyager is a gravity-flip runner, not a farming, mining, or shooting game. There are no crops, pickaxes, or guns — only momentum, timing, and the vault itself. Every run shuffles laser gaps, relic lines, and ceiling hazards, so no two escapes feel the same. Watch the glowing warning strip before a laser grid spawns: it tells you whether the safe path is high, low, or in the middle.
