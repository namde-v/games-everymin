How to deduce: Tap a soil plot to plant a seed. A willow branch grows if you guessed a hidden spot. Otherwise the plot shows a clue number — how many hidden branches share that row or column — and a leaf colour: gold means very close, green means nearby, grey means far away. Combine row, column and colour clues to narrow the pattern before you spend lives. Five wrong plants end the game.

Keyboard: Arrow keys move, Enter or Space plants, R restarts. Mouse and touch work on every plot.

🌿 Whispering Willow

Puzzle / Botanical Pattern Logic
A quiet grove, a hidden pattern, and leaves that whisper clues.

How to Play
  • Find all 8 hidden willow branches in the 6 × 6 grove.
  • Plant by clicking, tapping, or using arrow keys + Enter.
  • A wrong plant reveals a row + column clue number and a leaf colour.
  • Gold leaves are closest. Combine clues — do not guess blindly.
  • You have 5 lives. Each wrong plant costs one life and 10 points.
  • A correct branch earns 100 points plus a bonus for lives left.

No downloads. No network. Just you and the willow.
