XENON XENOLITH

You are the commander of a crystal colony on planet Xenon. Meteor storms are coming. Place harvesters on glowing crystal veins, power them with conduits and reactors, and guard them with turrets.

How to Play

  • Goal (WIN): Survive 8 sols AND bank 250 crystals. If your Colony HP reaches 0, the colony falls.
  • Harvester (Key 1, 25 Energy): Place ONLY on a pink crystal vein. Earns +6 crystals per tick, +4 more if next to a Conduit.
  • Power Reactor (Key 2, 20 Crystals): Earns +8 energy per tick. Place anywhere empty.
  • Conduit (Key 3, 5 Energy): Boosts adjacent harvesters and links your grid. Cheap and vital.
  • Turret (Key 4, 30 Crystals + 10 Energy): Auto-fires at falling meteors. Without turrets, meteors smash buildings and cost 12 HP.
  • Erase (Key 0 / E): Remove a building and refund half its cost.
  • Controls: Mouse click or tap to place. Arrow keys move the glowing cursor, Space / Enter places, 1-4 select tools, R restarts instantly.
  • Strategy tip: Build 2 reactors first, then harvesters on veins, then conduits, then turrets at the edges before Sol 3 storms intensify.
