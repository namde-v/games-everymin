YODEL YARD

High in the Alps, the mountain sings — and it expects an answer. Notes roll down the valley toward the echo post. Yodel them back at exactly the right moment!

How to Play:
▲ High notes (orange, top lane) — press Up Arrow / W, tap the orange button, or tap the top half of the valley.
▼ Low notes (blue, bottom lane) — press Down Arrow / S, tap the blue button, or tap the bottom half of the valley.
Hit a note as it crosses the glowing echo post on the left. Perfect timing scores most!
Miss a note (or hit the wrong pitch) and you lose a life. Lose all 3 lives and the song ends.
Win: Echo all 40 notes of the mountain song to reach the summit celebration!

Sound starts only after you press Start. Headphones recommended!
