Zenith Zookeeper

Your sky zoo floats above the clouds. Four amazing creatures live on floating habitats. They get hungry, bored, and messy. Visitors arrive in waves — happy creatures attract happy crowds and big tips!

How to Play

  • Watch the meters: Hunger, Fun and Cleanliness fall over time. Keep them high to keep Happiness high.
  • Select a habitat by clicking / tapping it, or with ← → arrow keys, then act.
  • Feed F costs 5 coins · Enrich E costs 6 coins · Clean C costs 4 coins. Earn coins from visitors.
  • Visitors arrive in 5 waves. Each wave lasts 30 seconds. Happy zoos earn more score and coins.
  • Win: Finish Wave 5 with reputation left. Game Over: Lose all 3 reputation stars when a creature stays miserable.
  • Tip: Fix the lowest meter first, and never let Happiness drop below 25 for long!

Keyboard + Mouse + Touch supported. Sound starts only after you press Start.

How to Play — Details

  • Sky Fox (Ember) loves food and play, hates mess. Cloud Turtle (Puff) is slow and calm, needs regular cleaning.
  • Storm Owl (Zephyr) needs enrichment toys or it gets bored fast. Baby Cloud Dragon (Nimbus) eats a lot but delights huge crowds.
  • Each wave brings more visitors. Visitor happiness = average creature happiness. Score = visitors × happiness bonus.
  • If a creature's Happiness hits 0, you lose a reputation star after a short warning. Three stars lost = Game Over.
  • Special event: every wave, one random creature gets a “Craving” — satisfy it quickly for +50 bonus points!
