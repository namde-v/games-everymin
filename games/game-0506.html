🔮 Saffron Sorcerer

A puzzle of amber, order, and proportions. A customer wants a rare potion. You know how many drops it needs and a few clues — but not the exact order. Brew, learn from the feedback, and deduce the secret recipe before the cauldron cools.

How to Play

  • Read the Recipe Scroll: it gives the potion length, the ingredient counts, and position clues.
  • Add ingredients in order by tapping, clicking, or pressing 1–6. Use ← / → to highlight and Enter to add.
  • Press Brew when your mixture is full. Feedback tells you how many drops are exactly right and how many are right ingredients in the wrong place.
  • Keep the heat up with Stoke Fire. If heat hits zero, you lose a life.
  • Wrong brews cost a life. Brew all 5 potions to win. Lose all 3 lives and the shop closes.
  • Scoring: 100 points per potion, plus a heat bonus and a bonus for fewer brews.
