🌊 Brine Breaker

You are the captain of a tiny skiff on a rising, restless sea. The tide keeps climbing, waves roll in faster, and driftwood tumbles toward you. Leap from crest to crest and scoop up shining shells before the sea wins.

How to Play

  • Jump: Press Space, Up Arrow, or W — or click / tap the sea — to hop between waves.
  • Double jump: Press jump again while airborne for a second boost. Time it over tall crests.
  • Dive / drop: Press Down Arrow or S to drop fast and land early.
  • Dodge: Driftwood costs one life. Floating barrels bounce you but do not hurt you.
  • Collect: Shells are worth 25 points. Golden shells are worth 100 points.
  • Win: Collect 30 shells before you run out of lives. Survive the storm surge at the end!

Keyboard: Space / ↑ / W to jump · ↓ / S to dive · Mouse: click to jump · Touch: tap to jump
