Cobalt Cartographer

An old map has been torn into tiles and spun every which way. Only one true river runs from the western spring to the eastern sea. Turn the tiles, restore the route, and finish before your ink runs out.

How to Play

  • Goal: Connect the glowing cobalt river from the START spring (left edge) to the GOAL sea (right edge) in one continuous line.
  • Rotate: Click or tap a tile to turn it 90 degrees clockwise. Each turn costs 1 ink.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys move the golden cursor, Space or Enter rotates the selected tile, R restarts.
  • Touch: Tap any tile to rotate it. No dragging needed.
  • Scoring: Every tile joined to the river from the spring earns 10 points. Rotating a tile that shortens the river costs a life.
  • Win: Reach the sea. Lose: Run out of ink (60 turns) or lose all 3 lives.
  • Roads are decoration: only the cobalt river counts toward the route.

Tip: tiles already glowing blue are part of your river so far. Protect them.
