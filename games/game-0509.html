MIRAGE DRUMMER

The caravan crosses the singing dunes, and only your drums keep it on the trail. Beats roll down from the sky toward the drum line. Strike each drum exactly as its beat lands on the glowing line.

How to Play
• Left drum: press A or Left Arrow, click the left drum, or tap the left pad.
• Right drum: press D or Right Arrow, click the right drum, or tap the right pad.
• Double beats (two notes at once) need both drums at the same moment: press Space, or tap both pads.
• Hit dead on the line for PERFECT (+100), close for GOOD (+50). A missed beat costs a life.
• The tempo rises as the caravan picks up speed: 100 BPM, then 120, then 145 BPM.
• Survive the whole caravan song with at least one life left to WIN.
Sound starts only after you press Start. Headphones recommended.
