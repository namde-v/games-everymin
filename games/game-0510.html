🌋 PYRO PLANT PLANNER

You are the chief engineer of a volcano power plant. Open valves to send lava to turbines, match the town's changing power demand, bleed off pressure, and cool turbines before they overheat. Survive all 3 shifts to win.

How to Play

  • Goal: Survive 3 shifts (30 seconds each) and score as much energy as you can. Meet or beat Demand to earn bonus points.
  • Mouse / Touch: Tap a valve circle on the volcano pipes, or tap the Valve buttons below, to open / close it. Tap a turbine to select it.
  • Keyboard: ← → select turbine  |  ↑ increase throttle, ↓ decrease throttle  |  Space / Enter toggle selected valve  |  1 / 2 / 3 select turbine  |  M mute sound
  • Pressure: Closed valves make pressure rise. Open valves relieve it. At 100% the plant vents violently and you lose 1 Integrity.
  • Heat: Running a turbine builds heat. Over 100° it trips offline and you lose 1 Integrity. Close its valve or lower throttle to cool it.
  • Brownouts: If output stays far below demand, the town complains and morale drops — keep at least one turbine spinning!

Tip: Turbine C is powerful but runs hottest. Turbine A is safe but weak. Balance them.
