QUARTZ QUARRY DASH

Your mine cart is rolling out of control down the old quarry line! Leap the broken rails, duck the support beams, dodge falling boulders, and scoop up quartz gems before the tunnel collapses behind you.

HOW TO PLAY
← → or A / D — switch track (left / right lane)
↑ / W / Space — JUMP over gaps and boulders
↓ / S — DUCK under low beams
Mouse click / screen tap — jump  |  Swipe left / right — switch track  |  Swipe down — duck
⭐ Collect gems for points. ⚠ Hitting an obstacle costs 1 cart (you have 3).
WIN: reach 2000 m OR collect 40 gems — before your carts run out!
Quartz gem (+100) Boulder — jump or switch Beam — duck! Gap — jump!

Tip: the cart speeds up the farther you go. Watch the track ahead, not just the cart!
