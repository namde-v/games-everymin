Welcome, Librarian! The moon-phase tiles on your shelf have been shuffled by a mischievous comet. Only you can put the lunar cycle back in order, from New Moon to Waning Crescent.

How to Play
  • Goal: Arrange all 8 moon-phase tiles in true lunar-cycle order.
  • Clue cards: Read them! Each one rules out a wrong position or confirms an order (for example, waxing phases come before the Full Moon, and the New Moon comes first).
  • Swap tiles: Click or tap a tile, then click or tap another tile to swap them.
  • Keyboard: Arrow Keys move the glowing cursor, Enter selects a tile, Enter again on a second tile swaps them.
  • Check: Press "Check Order" when you think the shelf is right. A correct shelf wins the level with a bonus for few swaps. A wrong shelf costs one star — and the game highlights the misplaced tiles.
  • Stars: You have 5 stars for the whole journey. Lose them all and the library closes (Game Over). Finish all 5 levels to Win!

Controls: Mouse click • Touch tap • Arrow Keys • Enter to select / confirm

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
