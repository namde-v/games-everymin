🌊 Tidal Turbine Tycoon

How to Play: You run a coastal energy company for 10 days. Place tidal turbines in the sea, sell their power for coins, repair storm damage, and reach 420 MWh before Day 10 ends.

  • Place: Click or tap a blue sea cell to build the selected turbine. Click a turbine again to upgrade it (if you can afford it).
  • Turbines: 1 = Small (40 coins, 6 MWh/day)   2 = Medium (80 coins, 14 MWh/day)   3 = Large (140 coins, 26 MWh/day)
  • Next Day (N): Earn energy and coins, pay upkeep, and maybe face a storm. Damaged turbines produce less and can be destroyed.
  • Repair (R): Repairs the selected / cursor turbine for 25 coins, or all damaged turbines if none is selected.
  • Move: Arrow Keys move the yellow cursor. Keys 1, 2, 3 select a turbine type. Enter or Space places it.
  • Win: Reach 420 MWh. Game Over: Go bankrupt (coins below 0) or finish Day 10 short of the target.

Start with 250 coins. Spend wisely — storms love unrepaired turbines!

Mouse, touch and keyboard all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.

📋 Controls & Strategy

  • Mouse / Touch: Click or tap a sea cell to place or upgrade. Tap a land cell does nothing — turbines belong in the water.
  • Keyboard: Arrow Keys = move cursor · 1 / 2 / 3 = select turbine · Enter / Space = place at cursor · R = repair · N = next day.
  • Upgrades: Clicking an existing turbine upgrades it one tier (Small → Medium → Large) for the price difference, if affordable.
  • Storms: From Day 3 onward, storms can damage turbines. A turbine at 0 health is destroyed. Repair before the next storm.
  • Upkeep: Each turbine costs upkeep per day. Stronger turbines earn more but cost more to maintain.
