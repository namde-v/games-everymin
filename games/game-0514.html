Welcome to the Bazaar!

The market stalls are drumming and the crowd is dancing. Beat notes tumble down four drum lanes toward the golden stall line. Strike each drum exactly when its note lands on the line!

How to Play:
  • Hit notes on the stall line for Perfect (+100), Good (+50) or Okay (+25) points.
  • Miss a note and you lose a life and crowd cheer. Lose all 5 lives and the bazaar closes.
  • Build combos: 10+ hits in a row = x2, 20+ = x3, 30+ = x4 multiplier.
  • Crowd meter: every Perfect adds cheer. At 100% you earn a +500 crowd bonus.
  • Tempo rises each round: Round 1 is warm-up, Round 2 is busy, Round 3 is a festival rush. Finish all 3 rounds to WIN.
  • Each lane has its own drum: deep bongo, snappy drum, high tom and bright shaker.

Controls: Press D / F / J / K, click a lane, or tap a lane on touch screens.
