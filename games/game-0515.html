🦅 FALCON FERRY

You are a falcon courier. Glide — do not flap — through shifting winds, grab parcels from cliff nests, and drop them on the glowing rooftops.

How to Play:
• Arrow Keys / WASD — steer and glide. Left / Right turn, Up catch lift, Down dive for speed.
• Space — pick up a parcel at a nest, or drop it on a rooftop. Dive (Down + Space) near a target for a bonus.
• Mouse / Touch drag — drag on the sky to steer the falcon toward your finger or pointer.
• Ride the pale updraft columns to gain height. Beware strong gusts that push you sideways.
• Do not hit the ground, rooftops, or cliff towers. You have 3 lives.
• Complete 5 deliveries to win. Each delivery is worth 100 points, plus dive and wind bonuses.

Wind changes every delivery — watch the HUD

Tip: glide in long smooth arcs. Sharp dives build speed but cost height fast.
