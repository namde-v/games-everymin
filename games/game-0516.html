Glyph Geyser

Rotate the glyph pipes so geyser water flows from the spring to every flower before your pressure runs out.

How to Play
• Mouse / Touch: tap a pipe tile to rotate it 90°.
• Keyboard: Arrow Keys move the glowing selector, Enter or Space rotates the selected tile.
• Each rotation costs 1 pressure. Rocks cannot move. Locked valve tiles need one tap to unlock, then they rotate.
• Win by watering all flowers. Lose if pressure reaches 0 first. Press R to restart a level.
