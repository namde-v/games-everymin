🌵 Cactus Cantina

Run a desert cantina for 10 days! Thirsty travellers arrive, sit at tables, and order drinks. Serve the right drink before their patience runs out. Happy customers pay and tip. Wrong drinks and slow service cost reputation. If reputation hits 0, the cantina closes!

  • How to Play: Click or tap a customer (or use Arrow Keys) to select them, then serve their drink with keys 1-4 or the drink buttons below.
  • Drinks: 1 = Desert Water (4 coins)   2 = Cactus Juice (7 coins)   3 = Agave Ale (10 coins)   4 = Prickly Tea (8 coins)
  • Supplies: Every drink uses 1 supply. Buy more with the green shop buttons — running out means lost customers!
  • Days: Each day lasts about 60 seconds. Press N or Next Day to end a day early. New travellers arrive all day.
  • Win: Finish Day 10 with 50+ Reputation AND 120+ Coins. Lose: Reputation reaches 0.
  • Controls: Mouse / Touch click customers, tables and drink buttons. Keys 1-4 serve drinks. Arrow Keys select a customer. N = next day. Enter = start.

Tip: expensive drinks pay more but cost more supply runs — keep all four stocked!
