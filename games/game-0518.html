Steelpan Serenade

How to Play: The band plays a short steelpan melody. Listen carefully, watch which pans light up, then play the exact same notes back in order. Pitch accuracy matters — the right rhythm with the wrong note still counts as a miss.

  • Rounds 1–5 — Melody Memory: Melodies get longer each round (3, 4, 5, 6, then 7 notes). Every correct note scores points and builds your combo. A wrong note breaks your combo and costs one life.
  • Round 6 — Freestyle Finale: No melody to copy. Play on the beat while the golden dot pulses to win over the crowd. Reach 800 freestyle points before the timer ends.
  • Lives: You have 3 lives. Lose them all and the show is over.
  • Win: Complete all 6 rounds to earn a standing ovation and the Win screen.

Controls: Keys 1–8 or A S D F G H J K play the eight pans from low to high. You can also click a pan with the mouse or tap it on a touchscreen. Press Enter to start.

Sound starts only after you press Start. This game works fully offline.

Practice mode is on — tap any pan to hear it, then press Start when you are ready.
