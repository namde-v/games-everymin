ASTEROID ANGLER

Precision star fishing from a lonely space station. Drifting asteroids float past — cast, hook, and reel them in before your line gives out.

How to Play:
• Aim upward from the station and cast your magnetic line.
• Stars = 50 pts  •  Crystals = 100 pts
• Golden asteroids are rare and worth 300 pts!
• Junk debris is worth 0 and damages your line by 20%.
• While reeling a catch, watch Line Tension. Hold Space (or hold mouse / touch) to ease tension and reel slowly. Let it hit 100% and the line snaps — you lose the catch and 25% durability.
• WIN: Catch 8 valuable asteroids OR reach 600 points.
• LOSE: Line durability reaches 0% OR you run out of casts.

Controls: Mouse / touch — drag to aim, release to cast, hold while reeling to ease tension  •  Arrow Keys — aim & power  •  Space — cast / reel / ease tension

Tip: Longer casts reach high-value targets, but heavy golden asteroids fight harder on the way back.
