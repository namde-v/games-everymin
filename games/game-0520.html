Welcome, Archivist

Six fossils were unearthed in one amber vault, but their labels washed away. A hidden true order runs from oldest (left) to youngest (right). Read the clue tablets, reason about older/younger relationships and layer hints, then place every fossil in its true position.

How to Play

  • Read clues: tap a clue tablet to reveal it. Each clue costs points, so use as few as you can.
  • Build your order: tap a fossil, then tap a slot (1–6, oldest to youngest). Tap a filled slot to clear it.
  • Check: press Check Order (or Enter) when all six slots are filled. Correct positions lock in green; wrong ones turn red and cost a life.
  • Win: place all six correctly. Game Over: lose all 3 lives with wrong guesses.
  • Scoring: start at 1000. Each clue used costs 40 points, each wrong check costs 60, and each round cleared adds a speed bonus.

Controls

  • Mouse / Touch: click or tap fossils, slots, and clue tablets.
  • Arrow Keys: move the selection between fossils and slots.
  • 1–6 keys: jump straight to a fossil.
  • Enter: confirm a fossil into a slot, or check the order when all slots are filled.
  • C: reveal the next unrevealed clue. R: clear your current order.

Fully offline. No downloads, no accounts, just you and the amber.
