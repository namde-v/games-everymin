Circuit Sage

Power the whole board, one turn at a time.

Click or tap any wire tile to rotate it 90°. Connect the glowing power source to every lamp before time runs out. A lamp only lights when a continuous wire path joins it to the source.

Mouse / Touch: tap a tile to rotate • Keyboard: Arrow keys move, Space or R rotates, Enter starts / restarts

Press Start to begin your first circuit.
How to Play
1. The yellow battery tile is the power source. Bulb tiles are lamps.
2. Tap, click, or use Space / R on a tile to rotate its wires clockwise.
3. Wires only carry power when neighbouring ends line up exactly.
4. Light all lamps to finish the level and earn a time bonus.
5. If the timer reaches zero you lose a life and the level is rewired. Lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over. Finish Level 10 to win the game.
Straight, corner, T and cross wires • Live power glows gold • Lit lamps glow bright
