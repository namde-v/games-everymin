🏠 Lighthouse Luminary

You are the keeper of Stormreach Light. Across five stormy nights, fishing boats and cargo ships will cross dark, rock-strewn waters. Your lamp is their only guide — but oil is scarce, the lens must be focused, and storms will test you.

How to Play
• Aim the beam: move mouse / drag finger, or use ↑ ↓ / W S to sweep the beam.
• Lamp: Space or Lamp button toggles the light. It burns oil while on.
• Focus: A / D or Focus buttons sharpen the beam. Sharp focus reaches farther but burns oil faster.
• Rescue flare: R or Flare button — instantly calms a ship in danger, costs 12 oil.
• Refuel: F or Refuel button — restores 35 oil, but takes 3 seconds while the lamp is dim.
• Ships caught in the beam turn toward safe water and earn points. A ship that hits the rocks is a wreck.
• Win: survive all 5 nights. Lose: 3 wrecks, or oil hits 0% while ships are still at sea.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.

Game Over

Tip: Sweep ahead of each ship, not behind it. In a storm, widen focus to catch drifting ships, then sharpen to guide the farthest ones home. Save at least one flare for the final night.
