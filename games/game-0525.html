MAGNET MARAUDER

You are a rogue magnet loose in a charged maze. Opposite poles attract, like poles repel. Flip your charge to slingshot around magnetic nodes, dodge the spike traps, grab all 8 energy cores, then reach the glowing exit gate.

Move: Arrow keys / WASD  |  Flip polarity: SPACE, X, or the FLIP button  |  Touch: drag to steer, tap FLIP
How to Play
1. Steer the glowing magnet with keys or by dragging on the maze.
2. Red nodes are North, blue nodes are South. Opposite polarity pulls you in; same polarity pushes you away.
3. Flip at the right moment to slingshot through narrow gates and around corners.
4. Collect all 8 yellow cores to open the exit gate at the far side.
5. Spike traps cost a life. Lose all 3 lives and the run ends. Reach the exit to win!

You Win!

Tip: approach a node with the opposite charge to get pulled in, then flip mid-swing to launch yourself away at speed.
