Rune Reorder

A hidden sequence of 5 runes has been carved in secret. You have 8 guesses to deduce it exactly. Every guess earns clue pegs — use logic, not luck, to reorder the runes.

How to Play

  1. Tap a rune in the palette, or tap a cell in the glowing row, to place it.
  2. Fill all 5 cells, then press Submit Guess.
  3. Read the clue pegs: green = right rune, right place; amber = right rune, wrong place; grey = not in the secret.
  4. Runes never repeat in the secret. Win by guessing the exact sequence within 8 tries.
  5. Scoring: win fast for more points — 100 points plus 25 per unused guess, plus a round bonus.

Keyboard: 1–8 select runes, arrow keys move the cursor, Backspace deletes, Enter submits. Mouse and touch work on every button and cell.
