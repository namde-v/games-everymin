🐝 HIVE HERALD

Strategy / Beehive Management — a tiny colony, a big winter ahead.

How to Play

  • Goal: Reach 180 population and 120 honey by the end of Day 15.
  • Foragers bring in honey, but leave the hive exposed.
  • Guards cut damage from mites, wasps and storms.
  • Nurses grow the population, but eat honey to feed larvae.
  • Every day your bees eat honey. Run out and bees starve. Lose all bees and the hive falls.
  • Watch the event banner: mite outbreaks and storms need guards ready.
  • Mouse, touch, or keyboard: F/G/N to assign, hold Shift to remove, Space to end the day.
