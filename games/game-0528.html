🕺 Taproot Tango 💃

How to Play: Dance arrows scroll down four lanes toward the glowing target zone. Hit the matching arrow exactly when it lands in the zone.

◀ ▲ ▼ ▶

Use Arrow Keys / WASD, click the on-screen pads, or tap them on touch screens. Perfect steps score 300, Good steps 100, misses break your combo and cost a life.

Finish all 60 steps with at least one life left to win the dance!
