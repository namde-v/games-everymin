Comet Catcher

Drag from the slingshot to aim, release to launch your catcher probe. Planetary gravity bends its path — use it! Land the probe in the glowing net zone to catch the comet.

  • How to play: Press and drag anywhere near the slingshot (bottom-left), aim with the dotted trajectory, release to fire.
  • Goal: Catch the required comets before you run out of launches.
  • Watch out: Gravity wells bend your shot, wind pushes it sideways, and crashing into a planet costs a life.
  • Controls: Mouse drag / touch drag to aim and launch. Arrow keys nudge aim, Space launches at current aim, R restarts.

Tip: a gentle pull gives a short lob; a long pull gives a fast, flat shot. Aim slightly upwind when the wind arrow is strong, and let a planet's gravity swing you into the net — a direct line is rarely the best line.
