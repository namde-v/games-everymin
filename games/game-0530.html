Kaleidoscope Keeper

The Keeper's beam has gone dark. Place mirrors on the grid, rotate them, and bounce the golden beam around walls until it touches every crystal.

Tap a cell to place a mirror, tap it again to rotate it, tap a third time to remove it. Then press Fire Beam. A wrong shot costs a life!

🏆

You Win!

Keyboard: Arrows move cursor • Space place / rotate • Enter fire beam • C clear • R restart level
Mouse & touch: tap a cell to place, rotate, or remove a mirror.

How to Play

Goal: Light every purple crystal on the board with one beam.

Mirrors: A / mirror turns a beam 90° one way; a \ mirror turns it the other way. Walls (dark blocks) stop the beam. The beam starts at the golden emitter and travels in its arrow direction.

Budget: Each level gives you a limited number of mirrors. Unused mirrors become bonus points when you win the level.

Lives: You have 3 lives. Firing a beam that misses any crystal costs one life. Lose all lives and the game is over — but you can restart instantly.

Scoring: +100 per crystal lit, +50 per unused mirror, +25 speed bonus if you solve quickly. Complete all 6 levels to win the game.
