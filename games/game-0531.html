Storm Sailor

Sail through a raging storm! Dodge lightning and wreckage, collect barrels, and survive all 3 storm levels to win.

How to Play:
• Steer: Arrow Keys / WASD (Left & Right) — or drag with Mouse / Touch
• Collect: 🛢️ Barrels = +25 points
• Avoid: ⚡ Lightning strikes & floating wreckage — each hit costs 1 life
• Win: Survive all 3 levels (20s each) OR reach 500 points
• Tip: Lightning flashes before it strikes — move away from the glow!
Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
