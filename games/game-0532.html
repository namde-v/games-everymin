🌿 Moss Maze

Grow a living path of moss so a tiny seed can travel from the sprout to the flower.

  • Goal: Connect START 🌱 to FLOWER 🌸 with moss paths, using at least the target route length.
  • Mouse / Touch: Tap a tile to rotate its moss path. Tap Send Seed when ready.
  • Keyboard: Arrow Keys select a tile. Enter or Space rotates it. Press S to send the seed.
  • Limits: Every rotation costs 1 move. Run out of moves and the maze withers.
  • Win: Finish all 4 levels. Game Over: Out of moves before the seed blooms.

Tip: corners turn the path, straights go through, crosses connect everywhere. Fixed tiles cannot rotate.
