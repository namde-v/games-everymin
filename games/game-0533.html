🌲 Paper Mill Tycoon 📄

Your goal: reach $6000 by the end of Day 20. You start with $1000, 20 timber and 4 workers.

How to Play: Buy timber, assign workers to Pulping (timber → pulp), Pressing (pulp → pressed sheets) and Drying (pressed → finished paper). Each assigned worker processes 5 units per day. New orders arrive daily with a 3-day deadline — fulfil them before they expire for cash. Watch machine wear: high wear slows production, and repairs cost money.

Win: $6000 or more before Day 20 ends. Game Over: miss 3 orders, or run out of money with no timber, pulp or paper left to sell.

Controls: Mouse / touch — click buttons and panels. Keys 1–4 assign workers to Pulping / Pressing / Drying / Sales. N advances to the next day. Arrow Left / Right select a station, Arrow Up assigns to the selected station, Enter fulfils an order.
