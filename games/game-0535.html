🧊 Glacier Grappler

Swing across frozen chasms! Aim your grappling hook, watch the wind, and land on every glowing target platform.

How to Play:
Mouse / Touch: press, drag to aim, release to launch the hook.
Keyboard: Arrow Keys aim  |  Space launches  |  R restarts
Land on the target to advance. Miss the ice and you lose an attempt!

You Win!

Drag from the climber to aim — the dotted arc shows your shot.
