🪸 Coral Current

Ocean currents have broken apart! Place coral tiles to carry the current from the orange Source to every pink Reef site. The flow must be continuous — no breaks, no dead ends in the main path.

How to Play

  • Goal: Connect ALL reefs in the level before you run out of moves.
  • Place: Pick a tile from the palette, then click / tap an empty sea cell.
  • Rotate: Click / tap a placed tile to rotate it. It costs 1 move.
  • Remove: Pick Eraser, then click a tile to remove it and get it back.
  • Tiles: Straight (— / |), Corner (⌐), Tee (⊤) and Cross (╬) join currents in the directions shown.
  • Check: Use Check Current Flow to light up every tile the water reaches.
  • Win: All reefs glow when connected. Game Over: Moves reach 0 first.

Controls

  • Mouse / Touch: Click or tap palette and grid cells.
  • Arrow Keys: Move the yellow selection box around the grid.
  • Enter: Place the selected tile on an empty cell, or rotate a placed tile.
  • Keys 1-4: Select Straight, Corner, Tee, Cross. Key 0: Eraser. C: Check flow.
