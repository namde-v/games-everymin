Banjo Barnstorm

How to Play: Notes scroll down five banjo strings toward the golden strum line. Strum the matching string exactly when a note touches the line to play the tune and earn points. Perfect timing builds your combo. A wrong strum or a missed note breaks the flow and costs stamina.

Controls:
Keys 1-5 or A / S / D / F / G strum strings 1-5
Mouse: click a string lane or a string button below
Touch: tap a string lane or a string button
Press Enter or tap Start to begin. Reach 500 points and finish the tune to win!

Stamina: 5 hearts. Misses and wrong strums cost 1 stamina. Finish all 64 notes to complete the barn tune.
