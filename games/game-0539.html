Lantern Launcher

Launch glowing sky lanterns across the night sky and land them on every floating target.

How to Play: Drag from the launcher to aim, then release to launch. Each shot has a new wind that can push your lantern left or right. You have limited lanterns per level.

Controls: Mouse / Touch — drag and release to aim and launch. Arrow Keys — Up/Down adjust power, Left/Right adjust angle. Space — launch. R — restart after Win or Game Over.

Hit all targets to win. Score bonuses for close landings and remaining lanterns.

You Win!

Tip: Strong wind calls for aiming into it. Watch the drifting clouds for a clue.
