🏂 DUNE DASHER

Sandboard down shifting dunes, jump over rocks, grind rails for bonus points, and switch lanes to grab flags. Speed increases every section — reach the oasis at 1500m to win!

HOW TO PLAY
← → or A / D — Switch dune lane  |  ↑ / W / Space — Jump
Mouse move — Steer lanes  |  Mouse click / Tap — Jump
Touch: drag left/right to steer, tap to jump
🪨 Rocks = lose a life  |  🚩 Flags = +50  |  🛤️ Rails = +10 per grind tick
Press Enter or tap Start to begin. Audio starts only after you interact.
