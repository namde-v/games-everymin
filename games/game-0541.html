Polarity Pursuit

Flip your charge. Repel the same. Dodge the opposite. Outrun the storm.

How to Play

  • Move lanes: Left / Right arrows, A / D, or tap the left / right side of the track.
  • Switch polarity: Press Space, or click / tap the centre of the screen. Your magnet flips between POSITIVE + and NEGATIVE −.
  • Same polarity = good: A matching magnet is repelled away and scores +25.
  • Opposite polarity = danger: It slams into you and costs 1 life.
  • Energy orbs: Golden orbs always score +15 and restore a little shield energy.
  • Hazards: Spiked grey blocks always damage you, whatever your polarity. Dodge them!
  • Win: Reach 1500 points. Game Over: Lose all 3 lives.
  • P pauses, R restarts instantly.

Tip: watch the colour of the next magnet before you flip. Red is positive, cyan is negative.
Speed rises the longer you survive — stay calm and flip early.
