🌊 Tide Lock

A Tide Gate Logic Puzzle

Water is waiting behind the source. The gates are scrambled. Rotate them to build one safe channel from the source through every basin — without letting a single drop reach a drain.

How to Play

  • Rotate gates: Click or tap a gate, or move the yellow cursor with Arrow Keys and press Space / Enter.
  • Straight gates go east-west or north-south. Elbow gates turn a corner. T gates split three ways. Cross gates go all four ways.
  • Release Tide: When your channel looks right, press Release Tide and watch the water flow.
  • Win a level: Every basin fills and no drain floods. Fail: A drain floods, a basin stays dry after release, or you run out of moves.
  • Score: Earn points for each level, plus a bonus for moves you did not need.
  • Keys: Arrows move cursor, Space/Enter rotate, R restart, N next level.

There are 6 levels. The grids get bigger, the channels twist more, and the drains get sneakier.

How to Play

  • Tap / click a gate to rotate it 90 degrees clockwise. Each rotation counts as one move.
  • Build a continuous pipe path: neighbouring gates must open toward each other.
  • Basins accept water from any side. Drains flood if water reaches them from any side.
  • Press Release Tide to test your layout. Do not release until every basin is connected.
  • Flooding a drain or exceeding the move limit ends the level — retry it instantly.
