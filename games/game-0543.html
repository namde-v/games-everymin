🐝 HIVE HORIZON 🐝

Manage a beehive over 12 days. Assign bees, gather nectar, repair the hive, and fight off mites — reach 200 honey before Day 12 ends, or the hive collapses.

How to Play:
  • Forage: gathers honey. Best when flowers bloom and the sun shines.
  • Build: repairs the hive, raises health, and can recruit new bees.
  • Guard: defends against mites and storms. Too few guards and mites drain health.
  • Use the + / − buttons (or tap the role cards), keys 1–3 to select a role, ↑ / ↓ to assign bees, N for next day, R to restart.
  • Unassigned bees rest — they gather nothing and guard nothing. Every bee counts!
  • Rain hurts foraging, storms damage the hive, and mites strike hardest late in the season.

Sound starts only after you press Start.
