☄️ Comet Slingshot

Bend your comet around planets and slingshot it into every star target!

How to Play:
• Drag from the comet with mouse or finger to aim — pull back like a slingshot, release to launch.
• Arrow Keys adjust aim angle and power. Space launches. R restarts instantly.
• Planets pull your comet with gravity — use the curve to reach targets hidden behind them.
• Hit ALL targets in a level before you run out of launches. Clear all 5 levels to win!

Tip: a dotted preview shows your first arc — but gravity bends it after launch!
