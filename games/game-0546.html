Signal Switchboard

Connect every matching pair of plugs on the switchboard by drawing wires across the grid.

  • How to play: Drag from a plug to its matching plug (same colour and letter). Wires must run along the grid, one cell at a time.
  • Wires may not cross other wires, pass through blockers (dark rivets), or run through other plugs.
  • Tap a finished plug to remove its wire. Press U to undo the last wire.
  • Complete every connection to clear the board. 3 illegal moves on one board blocks it and ends the run.
  • Clear all 6 boards to win. Mouse drag, touch drag, or Arrow Keys + Space / Enter.
