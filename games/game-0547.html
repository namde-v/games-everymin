🏔️ Glacier Expedition

Strategy / Turn-Based Expedition Planning

How to Play

  • Goal: Reach the golden summit flag at the top of the glacier within 30 turns, with supplies and stamina still remaining.
  • Move: Click or tap an adjacent tile, or use Arrow Keys / W A S D. Every move uses 1 turn.
  • Costs: A normal step costs 2 supplies and 4 stamina. A storm step costs 5 supplies and 12 stamina — avoid storms when you can.
  • Crevasses: Dark cracked tiles cannot be entered. Plan a route around them.
  • Camps: Orange tents restore 30 stamina when you step on them. Press Space anywhere to rest: 1 turn, 1 supply, +20 stamina.
  • Supply caches: Green crates give +15 supplies, once each.
  • Storm drift: Every 3 turns the wandering storm shifts one tile. Watch its new position before you move.
  • Lose if: Supplies reach 0, stamina reaches 0, or you use all 30 turns before the summit.
  • Restart anytime: Press R or the Restart button.

Tip: the shortest path is not always the safest. Detour to a camp and a crate, then push for the summit.
