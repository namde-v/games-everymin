Glass Harmonica

A melody of singing glass bowls. Watch them glow, hear their tones, then play the same song back — one note at a time, in rhythm.

How to Play
  • Watch & Listen: the bowls light up and sing a sequence. Do not tap yet.
  • Repeat: tap the bowls in the same order, on the beat if you can, for bonus points.
  • Controls: Click / tap a bowl, or press 1–4 / A S D F. Press R to restart.
  • Survive: a wrong note costs a life. You have 3 lives. Finish Round 8 to win.
  • Timing bonus: notes played close to the beat pulse earn +25 extra points.

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads needed — everything plays offline.
