🔱 Trident Toss

Fish are gliding through the lagoon. Read the wind, drag to aim, and land your trident dead-center for the biggest points.

How to Play
  • Drag on the water (mouse or touch) to aim from the boat, then release to throw.
  • Arrow Keys nudge your aim. Space throws. R restarts.
  • Wind pushes the trident sideways — aim into the wind to compensate.
  • Bullseye = 25 pts, inner ring = 15 pts, outer ring = 8 pts, graze = 3 pts.
  • Reach 100 points in 12 throws to win. Bonus: hitting 3 fish in a row adds +10.

Audio starts only after you press Start.
