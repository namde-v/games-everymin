SKYLINE SKATER

Skate across the city rooftops! Leap the gaps, grind the rails, dodge obstacles and grab every star on the way to the finish line.

How to Play:
• Jump: Space / ↑ / W, mouse click, or tap the screen
• Grind: Land on a glowing yellow rail — you grind automatically and earn bonus points!
• Dodge: Jump over air vents, stay low under birds. Falling into a gap costs a life.
• Win: Reach the checkered finish rooftop. You have 3 lives. Good luck!
Sound starts only after you press Start. Works with keyboard, mouse, and touch.
