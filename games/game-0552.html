Prism Pantry

How to Play: Each recipe asks for one target color. Add drops of Red, Yellow, Blue and White to your cauldron in the exact ratio shown by the recipe. Press Mix & Serve when you think the blend matches. A perfect match serves the dish and scores points. A wrong mix spills the cauldron and costs one life. You have 3 lives to finish all 10 recipes!

Tip: watch the cauldron color change as you add drops. The target swatch never lies, but the recipe card hides the exact counts — use the color and the hint to work them out.
