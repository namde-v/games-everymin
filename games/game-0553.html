⚓ HARBOR HAULER

Run the port. Beat the clock. Hit $1500 before your shift ends.

  • How to Play: Ships dock at the top. Each ship lists the cargo it still needs, with colored crates.
  • Load cargo: Pick a cargo type below (or press 1, 2, or 3), then click / tap a ship to load one matching crate. Correct crates earn coins; a completed ship pays a $200 bonus.
  • Watch deadlines: The bar under each ship is its time left. A ship that sails unloaded costs one reputation star.
  • Balance the port: Do not waste clicks on cargo a ship does not need — serve the ship closest to sailing first.
  • Win: Reach $1500. Lose: Lose all 3 reputation stars, or end the 180-second shift below $1500.
  • Keyboard: 1 / 2 / 3 select cargo, R restart. Mouse and touch work everywhere.

Fully offline. Sound starts only after you press Start.
