CANYON ECHO

Hidden echo crystals are tucked behind canyon rock. Your pulse can bounce off walls and angled rock slabs — use ricochets to reach what you cannot hit directly.

How to Play
• Aim: move the mouse, drag on touch, or press ← / → to rotate the launcher.
• Fire: click / tap the canyon, or press Space / Enter. Each shot costs one pulse.
• Win a level: hit every crystal before your pulses run out.
• Lives: running out of pulses on a level costs one life. Lose all 3 lives and the run ends.
• Win the game: clear all 5 levels. Press R at any time to restart.
