Solar Orchard

How to Play: Choose a tool, then tap or click a square on the 8×8 farm grid. Panels make energy; trees keep water in the soil. A tree next to a panel shades it and cuts that panel's energy — so plan sunny rows for panels and shady groves for trees.

Each season you may place up to 8 tiles. Press End Season to harvest energy and water. Reach 120 energy before the end of Season 6 to win. If water falls below zero you lose a life; lose all 3 lives and the farm fails.

☀️ Panel: +5 Summer, +4 Spring/Autumn, +2 Winter (minus 2 if shaded)  |  🌳 Tree: +3 water per season  |  Keyboard: 1/2/3 tools, N next season, R restart
