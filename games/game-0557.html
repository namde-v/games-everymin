☀️ Sundial Sorting

How to Play: Each puzzle lights up golden target hours around the sundial. Rotate the sun and place shadow-casting stones on the dial so that every shadow tip lands exactly on a target hour.

Tap / click a ring slot to place or remove a stone. Use Sun ◀ ▶ buttons or ← → keys to rotate the sun — shadows always fall opposite the sun. Press Check Shadows when you think all targets are covered.

Wrong checks cost 1 life. You may place only the stones allowed for that puzzle. Solve all 8 puzzles to win! Keyboard: ← → rotate sun • ↑ ↓ choose slot • Enter / Space place stone • C check • R restart.

Touch: tap a slot to place a stone, tap the sun buttons to rotate. Sound starts only after you press Start.
