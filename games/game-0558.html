SONAR SALVAGE

How to Play: Five treasures lie hidden on the sea floor. You cannot see them — you must find them with sonar.

1) Move the crosshair and press Ping (or P) to send a sonar pulse. The colour tells you how close the nearest treasure is: red = very close, orange = near, yellow = far, blue = cold.

2) When a ping glows hot, press Drop Claw (or Space) right on that spot. A precise drop recovers the treasure. A miss costs a life.

3) Recover all 5 treasures before you run out of lives, pings and claws.

Mouse: move + click to aim · Touch: tap to aim · Keyboard: Arrow keys / WASD to aim, P to ping, Space to drop claw.
