Emberline Express

How to Play

  • Your fire train races along three parallel tracks toward the Final Station.
  • Switch tracks to dodge burning bridges (flames) and blocked rails (barriers).
  • Collect water drops to douse fires and score points. Reach a station checkpoint for a big delivery bonus.
  • You have 3 lives. Hitting an obstacle costs one life and briefly shields you.
  • Win by completing 100% of the route to the Final Station. Game Over if you lose all lives.
  • Controls: Up / Down arrows or W / S to switch tracks, Left / Right to speed up or slow down. Click the upper or lower track on screen, or use the on-screen buttons. On touch devices, tap the upper or lower half to switch, or swipe up / down.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
