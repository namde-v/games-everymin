STARDUST STEPPER

Dance across the stars!

How to Play: Glowing star notes fall down four lanes. When a note reaches the bright hit line, step on its matching pad at exactly the right moment. Perfect timing scores more and builds your combo.

Keyboard: Arrow Keys or A / S / W / D
Mouse: Click a glowing pad
Touch: Tap a pad on the screen
Goal: Finish all 60 steps. Miss a step and you lose a life. Lose all 3 lives and the dance ends.

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads needed — everything runs offline.
