Lantern Leap

Hop up the floating platforms of the old lantern tower. Grab glowing lanterns, bounce higher, and reach the golden crown at the very top before the bats knock you down.

How to Play
🕹️ Move: Arrow keys / A & D, or drag with mouse / touch.
🦘 Jump: You bounce automatically when you land. Press Space / Up / W or tap to jump higher.
🏮 Lanterns: +50 points each. Golden lanterns give +100 and a bonus bounce.
🦇 Bats: Avoid them! A hit costs 1 life and drops you to a safe platform.
🏆 Win: Reach the tower top (height 3000) or score 2000 points.
💔 Lose: Fall below the screen or lose all 3 lives.

Tip: Moving platforms are gold — ride them to secret lantern rows.
