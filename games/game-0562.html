QUARRY QUILT

Stitch a quilt of quarry gems before your moves run out!

How to Play

  • Study the Pattern to Copy on the right: it shows the exact colour and notch direction for all 25 squares.
  • Pick a gem colour from the tool row, then tap an empty square on your quilt to sew it in. A correct colour scores 100 points.
  • Pick the Rotate tool and tap a placed gem to turn its white notch 90 degrees. A correct rotation scores 50 points.
  • Pick the Clear tool to remove a gem you placed (clue squares with green borders cannot be changed).
  • A wrong colour costs one life and the gem bounces back. You have 3 lives and 45 moves.
  • Win by matching all 25 squares, colour and direction. Run out of lives or moves and the quilt unravels.
  • Keyboard, mouse and touch are all supported. Use the Hint button to flash one unfinished square.
