🪸 Reef Restoration

Strategy / Coral Reef Management

You have 30 days and limited supplies each day to restore a damaged reef to 85 health or more. Lose all 3 lives and the reef is lost.

How to Play

  • Select a zone: Click / tap a reef zone, or use arrow keys to move the selection.
  • Plant Coral (key 1, cost 2): Raises coral cover. Healthy coral attracts fish.
  • Clean Zone (key 2, cost 2): Removes pollution. High pollution damages coral and fish.
  • Release Fish (key 3, cost 3): Adds fish, but only works well where coral is already growing.
  • End Day (key N): Nature takes its course — coral grows, fish move, pollution drifts, and a random event may strike. Supplies refill to 10.
  • Balance is everything: Clean polluted zones first, plant where pollution is low, and keep fish fed by coral.
  • Keys: Arrows = select zone, 1 / 2 / 3 = actions, N = next day, Enter = start / restart.

Mouse, touch and keyboard all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.

You Win!

Press Enter for an instant restart.
