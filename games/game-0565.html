Paper Plane Pilot

Launch your paper plane across a breezy valley, thread every glowing ring, and land dead-centre on the target pad.

How to Play

  • Start: Press Start Flight. Your plane launches automatically from the left cliff.
  • Steer: Hold Up / Down arrows or W / S to climb and dive. Move the mouse up and down, or drag on touch, to steer smoothly.
  • Wind gusts: Blue gust zones push you up or down — watch the arrows and ride them, do not fight every gust.
  • Rings: Fly through a ring for +20 points. Each ring only counts once per flight.
  • Landing: Touch down on the striped target pad for +50 plus an accuracy bonus (up to +50 for a bullseye). Crash into the ground or a mountain and you lose a plane.
  • Win: Reach 150 points before you run out of planes. Game Over: No planes left below 150 points.
  • Extra controls: Press Space for a quick dive boost, R to restart instantly at any time.

Tip: gentle steering wins. Small corrections beat wild swoops in strong wind.
