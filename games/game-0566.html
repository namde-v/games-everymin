Welcome to the Bubble Bazaar!

The market is floating away! Bubbles, mangoes, oranges and golden stars are raining from the stalls. Slide your basket underneath and catch them before they hit the ground.

📖 How to Play

  • Move: ← → or A / D keys, move your mouse, or drag with your finger on touch screens.
  • Catch good stuff: Bubbles = 10 pts, Fruit = 25 pts, Golden Star = 100 pts and +1 combo boost.
  • Avoid hazards: Prickly Poppers and rotten fruit cost 1 life and break your combo. Missing fruit does not cost a life, but missing a bubble does not either — only hazards hurt you.
  • Combos: Catch 3 good items in a row without a hazard to raise your combo multiplier up to x5. Every catch after that scores multiplied points.
  • WIN: Reach 2000 points to win the Grand Bazaar Trophy. GAME OVER: Lose all 3 lives and the stall closes.
  • Speed: The bazaar gets busier every 250 points — items fall faster and more hazards appear. Stay sharp!

Tip: Golden Stars are rare and fast — chase them! A perfect run keeps your combo alive.
