Welcome, Apprentice Locksmith

A great vault door stands before you. Its lock has four dials, each numbered 0 to 9, and no digit repeats. You have 10 attempts to guess the code. After every guess you earn feedback pegs: a green peg means a digit is correct and in the right place, a gold peg means a digit is in the code but in the wrong place.

How to Play
  • Study the clue rungs on the right before you guess. Each rung is a true fact about the secret code.
  • Turn a dial by tapping its arrows, clicking it, or selecting it and pressing Up / Down.
  • Move between dials with Left / Right, Tab, or by tapping a dial.
  • Press Try the Code or the Enter key to submit a guess. A duplicate digit is not allowed.
  • Use the pegs plus the clues to narrow the code down. Every wrong guess costs 40 points; a win keeps the rest of your score, plus a speed bonus.
  • On Win or Game Over, press Restart instantly to climb a brand-new ladder.

Keyboard: ← → to choose a dial, ↑ ↓ to change it, Enter to guess, R to restart.
Mouse and touch work everywhere — tap dials, arrows and buttons.
