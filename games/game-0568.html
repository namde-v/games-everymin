🌬️ Windmill Village

You are the village planner! Place windmills, houses and farms on the grid and keep energy supply ahead of demand through Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter.

How to Play
• Pick a tool above (or keys 1–4), then tap / click a grass square to build.
• Windmill (50 coins): generates power. Wind is weak in Summer, strong in Autumn and Winter.
• House (30 coins): adds people and energy demand, especially in cold Winter.
• Farm (20 coins): earns 6 coins every second to fund more building.
• Upkeep: each windmill costs 2 coins/sec, each farm 1 coin/sec.
• Blackout: if demand beats supply for a whole second, you lose a life. Lose all 3 lives and the village goes dark — Game Over.
• Win: survive all 4 seasons with at least 1 life left. Balanced villages score more!
• Keyboard: 1–4 tools, Arrow keys move the cursor, Enter / Space builds, R restarts, E is demolish.

Mouse, touch and keyboard all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.
