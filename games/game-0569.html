Welcome to the Rainforest!

The animals are drumming a secret rhythm. Listen carefully, then play it back exactly — beat for beat — to keep the jungle party going.

How to Play

  1. Listen: Watch and listen while an animal plays a drum pattern. Each drum lights up and makes its own sound.
  2. Repeat: When it says Your turn!, tap the drums in the exact same order.
  3. Grow: Every round the pattern gets one beat longer. Clear all 10 rounds to win.
  4. Careful: A wrong drum costs one life. You have 3 lives. If you lose all 3, the game is over — but you can restart instantly.
  5. Scoring: Earn 10 points for every correct beat, plus a 50-point bonus for finishing a round. Longer patterns mean bigger scores!
