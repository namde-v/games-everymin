🧲 Magnet Marbles

Precision flick aim. Flick your silver marble, bend its path around magnets, and knock all 5 glowing target marbles into the corner pockets.

How to Play:
• Drag back from your silver marble (mouse or touch) to aim, then release to flick.
• Red magnets pull marbles toward them; blue magnets push marbles away. Use the curve, do not fight it.
• Knock a target marble into a corner pocket for 100 points. Pocketing your own marble costs 50 points.
• You have 12 shots. Pocket all 5 targets to win. Run out of shots and the game ends.
• Keyboard: Left / Right rotate aim, Up / Down change power, Space flick, R restart.

Sound starts only after you press Start or flick — no autoplay.
