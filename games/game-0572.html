Tidepool Tiles

Connect the spring on the left to the coral reef on the right. Every tile turn costs one move.

How to Play
• Click, tap, or use arrow keys + Space / R to select and rotate a tile.
• Pipes must join end-to-end. Water flows only where both tiles point at each other.
• Connect the 💧 spring to the 🪸 reef before your moves run out.
• Out of moves? You lose a life and get a fresh tidepool. Lose all 3 lives and the tide goes out.

You Win!

Goal: spring (left, middle row) to reef (right, middle row). Water only flows where two neighbouring pipes face each other.

Keyboard: Arrows move • Space / R rotates • H hints • Enter starts / restarts. Mouse and touch work on the tiles too.
