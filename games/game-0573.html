🌴 Desert Oasis Manager

Can you turn a dusty spring into a thriving oasis? Allocate scarce water and shade across 60 days and four seasons. Grow to 60 people to win — before thirst, hunger or despair empties your settlement.

  • Water is life: crops need it for food, people need it for happiness, wells refill it.
  • Shade (palms + tents) slows evaporation and protects happiness in Summer heat.
  • Each day people drink and eat. No food or water = happiness falls, then people leave.
  • Feasts attract newcomers, but only if happiness is 50 or more.
  • Summer is brutal, Winter is kind. Tap the canvas once per day for a small rain blessing.
  • Win at 60 people. Lose if population hits 0 or happiness hits 0.
  • Surviving to day 60 with 35 or more people is also a victory.
  • Use Rest when you are ahead — it slowly restores water and happiness.
  • Do not let both water and food hit zero on the same day.
