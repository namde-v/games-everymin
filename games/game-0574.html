Volcano Beat Forge🔥 FORGE TO THE BEAT 🔥

Strike falling molten ores on the beat and forge legendary gear in the heart of the volcano!

How to Play:
• Molten ores fall down 4 forge lanes.
• Strike each ore exactly as it lands on the anvil line.
• Keys: D F J K — or tap / click the lane buttons.
• Perfect (centre hit) = 100 pts, Good = 50 pts.
• Miss an ore and you lose a life. Lose 3 lives and the forge cools!
• Forge 40 items to win and claim the Volcano Crown.

Tip: watch the pulse of the lava glow — ores spawn on the beat. Chain Perfect hits to build a combo multiplier. Every 10 combo adds a bonus!
