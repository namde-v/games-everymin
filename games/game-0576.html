Midnight Market Maze

How to Play: Guide the lantern thief through the night-market maze. Collect all the glowing lanterns to win. Avoid the patrolling guards — if one touches you, you lose a life.

Hide in a striped market stall (press H, Space, or tap Hide) and guards will walk right past you. Moving breaks your cover. Stalls only protect you while you stay still inside one.

Move: Arrow keys / WASD • Mouse: click the maze to steer toward that spot • Touch: swipe or use the D-pad • Hide: H / Space / Hide button

Game Over

Collect all lanterns to win. Watch the guards!

Tip: guards speed up as you collect more lanterns. Lure them away, then double back.
