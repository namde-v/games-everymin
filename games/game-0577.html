STAR CHART CIPHER

Decode the constellation, one star at a time.

Each chart hides a secret sequence. The cipher chips above the sky show the exact colour and size of each star, in order. Find the star that matches the glowing chip — decoys share a colour or a size, but never both.

How to Play: Click or tap stars in cipher order. Arrow keys move the glowing cursor, Enter / Space selects it. A correct link earns 100 points. A wrong link costs a life and breaks your current trail. Finish all 5 charts to win.

Mouse, touch and keyboard supported. Sound starts only after you press Start.
