Fog Lantern Relay

Ships are lost in the fog. Build a network of light to guide them home.

  • Goal: Light every ship (⛵) before the fog timer runs out.
  • Place: Pick a tool, then tap / click a grid cell. Mirrors bend the beam 90°.
  • Lens: Splits one beam into three: straight ahead plus both sides.
  • Rotate: Tap a placed mirror to flip it between / and \. Use Eraser to remove a part and get it back.
  • Keyboard: 1-4 choose tools, arrows move the yellow cursor, Enter places / rotates, Backspace erases.
  • Strategy: Lenses are scarce — use one junction to reach two ships at once. Blockers (🪨) stop beams.

You Win!

Tip: place a lens at a junction, then mirrors to steer each branch to a ship.

Level 1 hint: one lens in the middle row can reach both ships.

Mouse, touch and keyboard supported. No network needed.

Scoring: 200 points per cleared level, plus 2 points for every second left on the fog timer.

Lives: losing all time on a level costs one life and resets that level's board.

Parts: mirrors and lenses are limited each level. Erasing a part returns it to your inventory.

Beams: a beam stops at a rock, lights a ship, bends at a mirror, and splits at a lens.

Win: light every ship on all 3 levels. Lose: run out of lives before the last ship is lit.

Controls recap: click or tap to place, tap a mirror to flip it, Eraser to reclaim parts.

Keyboard recap: 1 mirror /, 2 mirror \, 3 lens, 4 eraser, arrows move, Enter place, Backspace erase.

Touch recap: tap a cell to place or flip, use the toolbar buttons to switch tools.
