Welcome to the Choir

How to Play:

  • Press Start. The glacier plays a short chime melody.
  • Watch and listen — each glowing chime has its own pitch.
  • When it says Your turn, tap the chimes in the exact same order.
  • Each correct note scores points. A wrong note costs one life.
  • Melodies grow longer every round. Finish Round 8 to win!

Controls: tap / click a chime, or press keys 1–6. Touch works too.

Tip: Hum the melody as it plays. Group notes in pairs. The rhythm matters for memory, not speed — there is no timer on your turn, so take a breath and trust your ear.

Rhythm bonus: Repeating a whole round without a mistake earns a +50 harmony bonus.
