Bamboo Bounce

How to Play: Aim from the launcher at the bottom, then fire. Your ball ricochets off bamboo stalks. Use the angles to reach lanterns hidden behind bamboo — a straight shot will not reach them all.

Mouse: move to aim, click to fire  |  Touch: drag to aim, tap Fire  |  Keyboard: Left / Right to aim, Space to fire, R to restart.

You have 10 shots to knock down all 8 lanterns. Each bounce that leads to a hit earns bonus points. Good planning beats fast firing.

You Win!

Press Restart or R to play again with a fresh set of lanterns.
