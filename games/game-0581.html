Harbor Hoist

How to Play: Slide the harbor crane left and right along the top rail. Drop a crate when it is lined up over the dock stack. Land it squarely to keep the tower standing!

A crate that lands too far off-centre slides off the stack and costs a life. You have 3 lives. Stack 25 crates to win the shift and fill the freighter.

Controls: Arrow keys / A D to move, Space to drop. Mouse: move to aim, click to drop. Touch: drag to aim, tap to drop.

Tip: watch the swinging hook and the ghost guide line. A perfect centre drop earns +25 bonus points. A near miss earns fewer points but still counts. Three dropped crates off the dock ends the game.

Game Over

Press Restart to run another shift at the harbor.
