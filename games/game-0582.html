Glyph Grid

How to Play:

  • Swap two adjacent glyphs to line up 3 or more of the same symbol in a row or column.
  • Matched glyphs vanish, new ones fall from above, and cascades multiply your score.
  • Each swap costs 1 move. You have 30 moves to reach 1500 points.
  • Reach the target and you win. Run out of moves, or get stuck with no possible match, and the game ends.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move the glowing cursor, Enter or Space selects and swaps. Mouse and touch work too.
