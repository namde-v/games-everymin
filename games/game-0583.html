🐝 Alpine Apiary

Goal: Earn 500 honey in 12 seasons without losing all 6 hives.

  • Flowers (1): Foragers gather nectar. Best in Spring and Summer, weak in Winter.
  • Honey (2): House bees turn nectar into honey. Needs nectar in store.
  • Wax (3): Builders make wax, used for mite treatment and planting flowers.
  • Guard (4): Guards slow mites and protect the hive. Use it when mite levels rise.
  • Weather matters: Rain cuts foraging, Frost hurts bees, Bloom boosts nectar, Mite Surge raises mites fast.
  • Mites: If a hive hits 100% mites it collapses. Treat mites with wax before that happens.
  • Winter warning: Foraging nearly stops in Winter — stockpile nectar and honey in Autumn.

Tip: Balance tasks! A hive on Honey with no nectar makes nothing. Check mite levels every season.
