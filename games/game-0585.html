☄️ Comet Catcher

Precision / Gravity Steering Aim

Steer your catcher ship with short thrust pulses. Comets rain down through drifting gravity wells that bend their fall. No slingshots — just careful aiming, timing, and one clean intercept at a time.

  • How to play: Move the mouse or drag on touch to aim. A dotted prediction line shows where your next thrust pulse will carry you.
  • Thrust: Click / tap to pulse toward your aim point. Arrow keys or WASD fire pulses up, down, left, and right. Pulses cost nothing, but momentum carries — plan ahead!
  • Catch: Fly so a falling comet lands inside your glowing net zone above the ship.
  • Win: Catch 20 comets. Game Over: Let 3 comets burn up in the atmosphere at the bottom.
  • Watch out: Purple gravity wells pull comets sideways. Golden comets are fast and worth double points. Red comets are wild — catch them for bonus points if you dare.
