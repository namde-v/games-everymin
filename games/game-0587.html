RUNE VAULT

ᚠ ᚢ ᚦ ᚨ ᚱ ᚲ ᚷ ᚹ

The vault is sealed with a secret sequence of 5 different runes. You have 10 guesses to crack it. After every guess, the vault answers in code: exact matches, runes present but misplaced, and runes that are not in the code at all. Use logic, not luck.

How to Play

  • Tap a rune below, or press keys 1–8, to fill the 5 slots from left to right.
  • Press Submit Guess (or Enter) when all 5 slots are filled. Each guess must use 5 different runes.
  • Read the feedback dots: green means that rune is exactly right; gold means the rune is in the code but in another position; grey means it is not in the code.
  • Eliminate grey runes, lock in green runes, and shuffle gold runes to new positions.
  • Solve within 10 guesses to open the vault and win. Score starts at 1000 and drops with each extra guess; hints cost 50 points.
  • Mouse, touch, and keyboard all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.
