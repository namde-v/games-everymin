🌾 Harvest Haven 🌻

Strategy / Farm Season Planning • Reach 1000 gold by the end of Year 1

Welcome to Harvest Haven!

Your goal: End Year 1 with 1000 gold or more. Start with 220 gold and 60 water per season.

How to Play: Each season, pick a crop from the palette, then tap a plot to plant it. Match crops to their best season and soil for a bonus. Watch your water budget — every planting costs water. When your fields are ready, press Grow & Harvest. Random pests or drought may strike! Good crops earn gold; bad matches earn little. If your gold falls below 0, you go bankrupt.

Controls: Mouse / touch: tap crops and plots. Keyboard: 1–5 choose a crop, Arrow keys move the cursor, Enter plants, Space grows & harvests, R restarts. Sound starts after your first click or key press.

🌱 Wheat loves Spring and fertile soil. 🌽 Corn loves Summer. 🎃 Pumpkin loves Autumn. 🥕 Roots survive Winter. 🫐 Berries love Summer but cost more water. Planting in the wrong season still grows, but pays only half. Dry soil cuts yield unless the crop is hardy. Save some water for late-season planting — you cannot plant without it!
