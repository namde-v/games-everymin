🌸 How to Play 🌸

  • Watch & listen: A target tempo (BPM) is shown. The big meadow flower pulses and a soft beat sounds on every beat.
  • Tap in time: Press Space, click the meadow, tap the big TAP button, or touch the screen exactly on the beat.
  • Precision pays: Within 70 ms = Perfect (+100). Within 150 ms = Good (+50). Within 220 ms = Okay (+20). Further away = a miss.
  • Drift costs a life: A miss, or a tap far from any beat, loses one life. You have 3 lives. Lose them all and the meadow wilts.
  • 5 tempo rounds: Each round needs 8 on-beat taps. Then the tempo shifts — slower, faster, or a surprise jump. Hold your accuracy across all 5 rounds to win!
  • Tip: Do not rush. Count with the pulse, breathe, and tap when the flower is at its fullest bloom.
