❄️ POLAR PLUNGE ❄️

Precision / Ice Dive Aim

How to Play: You stand on an ice cliff above freezing water. Aim your dive angle, set your power, and watch the wind! Hit the floating target rings to score — bullseyes are worth the most. Dodge the drifting ice floes, or your dive is ruined. You get 10 dives. Reach 150 points to win!

Controls: ← / → aim  •  ↑ / ↓ power  •  Space / Enter dive  •  Mouse drag to aim, click Dive  •  Touch drag + Dive button
