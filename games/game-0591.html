🛶 Canyon River Run

Deliver 20 crates down the canyon river!

How to Play: Steer your raft left and right to dodge rocks and spinning logs. Pick up floating cargo crates to score. Each crate is worth 50 points. Hitting an obstacle costs 1 life. You have 3 lives.

⌨️ Arrow Keys or A / D to steer
🖱️ Mouse move / drag to steer
👆 Touch and drag to steer
⏸️ P pauses  •  R restarts

Win by delivering all 20 crates. Good luck, river runner!
