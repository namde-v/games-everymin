🪞 Mirror Maze

How to Play: A light beam leaves the yellow source. Rotate the mirrors so the beam reaches the green target. Light all 3 crystals for a big bonus. You have limited moves — every rotation costs one move.

Arrow Keys move the selection cursor • Space or R rotates the selected mirror • Click or tap a mirror to select it, tap again to rotate it.

Complete all 3 levels to win. Running out of moves costs a life and resets the level.

Mirrors: / and \ reflect the beam 90°. Walls block it. Crystals glow when lit. Watch the beam path — it updates live as you rotate!
