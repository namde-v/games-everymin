⚓ Lighthouse Logistics

How to Play

  • Ships sail down from the open sea toward the harbor. Each ship shows its type, speed, and a countdown bar.
  • Select a ship with Arrow Keys or by clicking / tapping it, then press 1–4 (or tap a berth button) to assign it a dock berth.
  • Each berth has a signal color. A ship must reach the harbor while its berth is free — two ships in one berth collide!
  • Big freighters are slow but worth more; fast ferries are worth less but arrive quickly. Cargo ships are in the middle.
  • Score for every on-time docking. A ship that reaches the harbor unassigned, or collides, costs a life.
  • Win at 1,500 points or 12 successful dockings. Game Over after 3 misses.

Keyboard: ← → select ship · 1–4 assign berth · Enter confirm / restart · Mouse & touch fully supported.

Game Over

Select a ship, then assign it a berth before it reaches the harbor.

Tip: assign slow freighters early and keep one berth free for fast ferries. Berths refill a few seconds after a ship docks.
