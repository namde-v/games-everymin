☄️ Meteor Miner

Aim the mining cannon. Crack asteroids. Grab 8 glowing ore cores.

How to Play
← → aim angle   ↑ ↓ power   Space fire
Or drag with mouse / touch from the cannon and release to fire.
Shots bounce off the side and top walls. Hit an asteroid twice to crack it open, then fly through a core to collect it. You have 18 shots — make them count!

Angle & Power

Tip: a gentle low-power shot can bounce into a far asteroid. Watch drifting rocks — they move! A core is worth 100 points, cracking a rock is worth 25, and a bounce shot bonus adds 15. Collect 8 cores to win before your shots run out.

Arrow keys aim & set power • Space fires • Drag from cannon & release to fire • Touch supported
