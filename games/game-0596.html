🪁 Sky Kite Surfer

How to Play: You are flying a kite high above the hills. Hold to climb, release to dive — and let the wind do the rest!

Hold SPACE / ↑ / Mouse Hold / Touch Hold = Climb  •  Release = Dive
⭐ Collect stars (+50)  •  🌬️ Ride green gust currents (+10/sec)  •  ☁️🐦 Avoid storm clouds & birds
Win at 1200 points. You have 3 lives. One hit loses a life and briefly shields you.

Tip: gusts push you forward and lift you — surf along them for big bonus points. Watch the wind meter!

Keyboard: Space / Arrow Up to climb. Mouse: hold left button to climb. Touch: hold anywhere on the sky to climb. P pauses. M mutes sound. Works fully offline — no downloads needed.
