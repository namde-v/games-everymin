🏔️ Glacier Expedition

Lead a small expedition team from Base Camp to the Summit. Every step costs supplies — choose cheap snow over costly rock and crevasses, grab supply caches, and pitch camps to recover.

How to Play
• Arrow Keys / WASD — move the expedition one tile (only to a neighbouring tile).
• Enter or Space — pitch a camp on your tile: costs 5 Fuel, restores 15 Food (max 3 camps).
• Mouse / Touch — tap a neighbouring tile to move there; tap your own tile to pitch a camp.
• Terrain costs: Snow = 1 Food, Ice = 1 Food, Rock = 3 Food, Crevasse = 4 Food, Storm = 3 Food. Every move also uses 1 Fuel.
• Supply caches (crates) give +12 Food and +8 Fuel. Reach the flag on the Summit to win!
• Game Over if Food or Fuel reaches zero before the summit.

You Win!

Press Start, then use Arrow Keys or tap a neighbouring tile to begin.
