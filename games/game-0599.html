Welcome to the Bakery!

Ingredients are falling to the beat. Tap the right lane exactly when an ingredient reaches the oven line to bake a perfect treat.

How to Play

  • D = Flour lane   F = Sugar lane   J = Egg lane   K = Berry lane
  • Tap, click, or touch a lane button when its ingredient is inside the golden oven zone.
  • Perfect timing = more points. Keep the combo going for bonus points!
  • Miss an ingredient or tap the wrong lane and it burns. 5 burnt treats = Game Over.
  • Bake 30 treats to win and become Head Baker!
