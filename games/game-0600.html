AURORA ARCHERYPRECISION • WIND • NORTHERN LIGHTS

Shoot glowing aurora targets drifting across the night sky. Read the wind, charge your shot, and chase the bullseye.

How to Play
  • Arrow Keys: Up / Down aim, Left / Right adjust power. Space: release the arrow.
  • Mouse / Touch: drag from the bow to aim, release to shoot. Longer drag = more power.
  • Wind pushes arrows sideways in flight. Aim into the wind on strong gusts.
  • Bullseye = 150 points, inner ring = 100, outer ring = 50, edge graze = 25.
  • Win at 1000 points. Game over if your 12 arrows run out below 1000.

Tip: moving targets are worth more when they are fast. Watch the wind flag!
