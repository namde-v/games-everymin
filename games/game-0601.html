EMBER FORGE

How to Play: Glowing embers fall from the furnace. Move the blacksmith's bucket with Arrow keys / A & D, the mouse, or touch-drag to catch them. Caught embers land on the anvil — press Space or tap HAMMER when the hammer gauge is in the gold zone for a perfect strike. Three good strikes forge a blade. Dodge cold water splashes: they cool the forge and cost a life.

Forge 12 blades to win. Lose all 3 lives and the forge goes cold.

Keyboard: ← → / A D to move • Space to hammer • Mouse & touch supported
