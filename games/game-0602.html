Coral Cipher

How to Play: A secret coral sequence appears at the top of the reef. Tap the glowing glyph tiles at the bottom, in the exact order, to rebuild the code. Finish a sequence to score points and earn bonus tide time. A wrong glyph costs a life. Decode all 8 rounds to win. Lose all 3 lives, or let the tide timer hit zero, and the reef floods.

Mouse / Touch: tap a glyph tile • Keyboard: press 1–6 to pick a glyph, Backspace to undo, R to restart • Complete a sequence to advance.
