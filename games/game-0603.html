Vineyard Ventures

How to Play: You run a hillside vineyard through four seasons. Pick a tool below, then tap a field plot to use it.

Plant (10 coins) seeds a row · Water (free) speeds growth · Worker assigns a helper that doubles growth · Harvest turns ripe purple rows into wine bottles.

Wine orders arrive each season. Press Deliver Order when you have enough bottles. Fill 6 orders before the Harvest Festival ends to WIN. Let 3 orders expire and the vineyard goes bust — Game Over.

Keys: 1 Plant, 2 Water, 3 Worker, 4 Harvest, D Deliver, Arrow keys move cursor, Enter applies tool, R restarts. Mouse and touch work too.
