Flamenco Footwork

Welcome to the tablao! Steps fall down four rhythm lanes. Strike each step exactly as it crosses the glowing beat line — perfect timing builds your combo, misses break the spell.

  • How to Play: Press A / S / K / L, tap a lane, or click a lane button when a step hits the beat line.
  • Timing: Perfect = centre of the line. Good = close. Miss = step passes unhit.
  • Combos: Chain Perfect hits for bonus points. Three missed steps cost a life.
  • Goal: Survive 60 beats and reach 2500 points to win the night!

Headphones recommended. Sound starts only after you press Start.
