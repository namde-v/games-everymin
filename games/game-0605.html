Zephyr Balloon Race

How to Play: You are racing your hot-air balloon across the sky! Wind changes with altitude: climb or dive to find the fastest tailwind band, shown by drifting arrows. Grab golden stars for points, dodge dark storm clouds and flapping birds, and reach the finish line before you run out of lives.

Controls: Up / Down arrows or W / S to change altitude • Mouse: move pointer up/down • Touch: drag up/down • Space: burst of hot air (brief speed boost)

Reach 6000 m to WIN. Three hits = Game Over. Good luck, pilot!
