⚙ CLOCKWORK CONTRAPTION ⚙

How to Play: Each machine has a hand crank (left) and a clock tower (right). Between them sit gear sockets. Click or tap a socket to cycle its gear type: straight gears pass power left-to-right, elbow gears turn it up or down. The tower lights up only when a complete chain of meshing gears connects crank to tower.

Watch the gear direction arrows: neighbouring gears must spin opposite ways, and the chain must actually reach the tower. Finish before you run out of moves or you lose a life. Beat all 10 machines to win the game!

Controls: Mouse / touch — tap a socket to rotate its gear. Keyboard — arrow keys move the selector, Space or Enter rotates the selected gear, R restarts the level.
