🐪 Desert Caravan Co.

How to Play: You start at Home Oasis with 100 gold, full water and camel stamina. Travel oasis to oasis toward the Far City in the east. Every leg costs water and stamina.

At each oasis, buy goods cheap (dates, spice, silk) and sell them where prices are high. Refill water at wells and rest your camels when stamina is low — but resting costs a day and water still matters.

WIN: Reach the Far City with 180 gold or more (a real profit!). GAME OVER: Run out of water or stamina in the open desert.

Use the buttons, click/tap an oasis on the map, or use keys 1–4, B, S, W, R.
