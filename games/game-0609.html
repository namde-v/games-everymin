🏮 Lantern Lake Fishing

How to Play: Press SPACE or tap Cast to throw your line. When the bobber dips and flashes, press SPACE / tap to hook the fish! While reeling, hold SPACE or hold your pointer to reel in — but watch the tension meter. If tension stays in the red, the line snaps and you lose a life.

Land 8 fish to win, including the rare Golden Moon Koi. Lose all 3 lives and the night ends.

Keyboard: SPACE to cast / hook / reel  |  Mouse & touch supported
