AVALANCHE ARCHITECT

A wall of snow is coming down the mountain. The village below is in its path.

  • BUILD: Click or tap the snowy slope to place a snow barrier. You have 14 barriers.
  • ANGLE: Press Q / E, or the Angle buttons, to tilt the next barrier. A good angle deflects snow sideways, past the village.
  • ADJUST: Drag a barrier to move it before you release the avalanche. Click a barrier while holding Shift to remove it.
  • RELEASE: Press Space or the Release button. Snow that rolls safely past the village scores points. Snow that hits the village costs health.
  • WIN: Survive all three snow waves with village health remaining. LOSE: Village health reaches zero.
  • Controls: mouse, touch, arrow keys to nudge the selected barrier, R to restart.

How to play is above — build smart, angle carefully, then release!
