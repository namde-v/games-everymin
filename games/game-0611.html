COMET COURIER

Deliver the packages. Dodge the rocks. Beat the belt.

How to Play: Pilot your courier ship through the asteroid field. Collect the glowing packages in numbered order, then fly to the moving station dock to deliver each one.

Each delivery scores 100 points. Collecting out of order does nothing. Hitting an asteroid costs a life. Complete all 6 deliveries to win.

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to fly   Mouse move to steer, click to boost   Touch drag to steer

Boost with Space or a mouse click for a short burst of speed. Careful: boosting makes tight turns harder.
