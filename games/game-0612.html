How to Play

Goal: Rotate mirrors and the prism so one beam of light travels from the glowing source on the left edge to all three target crystals before you run out of moves or time.

  • Click or tap a mirror or prism tile to rotate it. Each rotation costs 1 move.
  • Arrow keys move the yellow cursor. Press Enter, Space, or R to rotate the tile under the cursor.
  • Mirrors flip between two diagonal angles. The prism cycles through straight, split-left, split-right, and split-both modes.
  • A crystal glows brightly when the beam reaches it. Light all three to win.
  • Watch out for dark wall blocks — they stop the beam cold.
  • You have 20 rotations and 120 seconds. Plan before you spin.
● Source — beam start ◆ Crystal — target / or \ — mirror ▲ Prism — beam splitter ■ Wall — blocks beam

Tip: Follow the beam step by step. If a crystal stays dark, trace backwards from it to the last mirror that should have sent light its way.
