⚓ HARBOR MASTER

You are in charge of a busy harbor. Ships arrive with different cargo. Send each one to the right dock before it loses patience and sails away angry.

How to Play:
• Mouse / Touch: Tap a waiting ship to select it, then tap its matching dock.
• Keyboard: ← → to choose a ship, 1 = Container dock, 2 = Tanker dock, 3 = Ferry dock.
• Container   Tanker   Ferry
• Right dock = points + cargo unloaded. Wrong dock = −50 points and −1 reputation.
• A ship that runs out of patience = −1 reputation.
• WIN: Deliver 15 ships. LOSE: Reputation hits zero, or the 180-second shift ends first.

Tip: serve ships with the shortest patience bar first. Green bars are calm, red bars are urgent.
