Neon Beat Bounce

How to Play: A glowing ball bounces down a track of neon pads. A beat ring shrinks toward the landing pad on every beat. Press the matching key (or tap the pad) at the exact moment the ball lands to bounce it higher and score. Perfect timing builds your combo multiplier. Press too early, too late, or not at all and the ball drops — lose all 3 lives and the song ends.

A / ← : Left Pad S / ↓ : Mid Pad D / → : Right Pad SPACE : Any Pad

Complete all 48 beats to finish the track and WIN. Chain combos for bonus points!

Tip: watch the shrinking beat ring — hit when it touches the pad rim.

Keyboard: A / S / D or Arrow Keys or Space  •  Mouse: click a pad  •  Touch: tap a pad
