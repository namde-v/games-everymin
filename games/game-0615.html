🪁 Kite Storm Surfer

Ride the storm. Catch the wind. Reach the lighthouse!

How to Play:
• Steer: Arrow Keys / A-D to move, or drag with mouse / touch.
• Jump: Press Space, Up Arrow, W, or tap JUMP to launch off ramps.
• Collect: Grab glowing wind orbs (+50) and starfish (+25) for points.
• Avoid: Rocks and lightning bolts cost a life. You have 3 lives.
• Wind Meter: Strong wind boosts speed and jump height. Gusts push you — ride them, do not fight them.
• WIN: Travel 1200m OR collect 18 wind orbs. LOSE: Lose all 3 lives.

Keyboard + mouse + touch supported. Sound starts only after you press Start.
