Circuit Gardener

How to Play: A power source on the left must reach all three plants through wire tiles. Click or tap a wire tile to rotate it 90 degrees. Use arrow keys to move the glowing cursor and Space or Enter to rotate the selected tile. Each rotation costs one move. Beware the thorny weeds: powering a weed causes a short circuit and costs a life! Win when all 3 plants bloom. You lose if you run out of moves or lives.

Wire pieces: straight lines, corners and junctions. A wire only carries power when its metal ends line up with its neighbours.
