Ovenlight Bakery

Welcome to your little bakery! Customers are hungry and impatient.

  • How to play: Pick a recipe (buttons below or keys 1–4), then tap an empty oven to start baking it.
  • When an item finishes, it goes to your tray. Tap a waiting customer whose order matches your tray stock to serve them and earn coins.
  • Watch patience bars! If a customer leaves angry, you lose reputation. Lose 3 customers and the day ends.
  • Reach 120 coins to win the day. Spend 40 coins on a Faster Oven to bake 35% quicker.
  • Recipes: Croissant 12 coins / 6s, Muffin 10 coins / 5s, Bread Loaf 15 coins / 8s, Berry Cake 22 coins / 10s.

Mouse, touch, or keyboard — all work. Sound starts after you press Start.

Pick a recipe, bake it, then serve the matching customer!
Keyboard: 1–4 select recipe · B bake · ← → select customer · Enter / Space serve · U upgrade · R restart after Win / Game Over · Tap or click the canvas to bake in an oven or serve a customer.
