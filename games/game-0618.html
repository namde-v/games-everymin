🎨 Pendulum Painter

How to Play: A paint pendulum swings above a blank picture made of target shapes. Watch the swing, pick the colour each shape needs, and release a drop at the exact moment it passes over the shape. A perfect landing fills that piece. Misses and wrong colours cost paint.

Controls: SPACE / Click / Tap = release a drop  •  1–5 or tap a colour dot = change paint  •  ← → = nudge the swing  •  R = restart

Fill the whole picture before your paint runs out to win. You have 24 drops — make every swing count.
