Subzero Skater

Skate across a frozen lake and collect 30 snowflakes before your luck runs out. Ice is slippery — you glide, you drift, and you turn slowly. Plan your slides!

How to Play
• Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD to push and steer. Hold a direction to build speed, release to glide.
• Mouse: Hold the mouse button and point — your skater slides toward the pointer.
• Touch: Touch and drag on the ice to steer toward your finger.
• Collect: Snowflakes sparkle on the lake. Grab them quickly in a row to build a combo multiplier.
• Avoid: Dark cracks in the ice and snowmen. Hitting one costs a life and breaks your combo.
• Win: Collect all 30 snowflakes. Lose: Lose all 3 lives.

Tip: long smooth slides chain better than sharp turns. Momentum is your friend.
