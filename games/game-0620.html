ORBIT DOCKING

Precision Spacecraft Docking

  • Goal: Fly your capsule to the glowing docking port on the rotating station and dock softly.
  • Rotate: Left / Right arrows or A / D keys, or the on-screen turn buttons.
  • Thrust: Up arrow or W key, the THRUST button, or hold the mouse / touch on space.
  • Brake gently: Down arrow or S key fires a retro burst. Docking needs LOW speed and good alignment.
  • Crash warning: Touching the station hull away from the port costs a life. Running out of fuel ends the run.
  • Win: Dock cleanly to score. Each successful docking makes the station spin a little faster.

How to Play: Watch the port marker, line up your nose with it, and creep in. Speed over the safe limit bounces you off — or worse.
