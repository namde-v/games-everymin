Tidepool Treasure Dive

Swim the tidepool, collect 20 treasures, dodge jellyfish, and surface before your oxygen runs out!

How to Play
• Move: Arrow keys / WASD, mouse drag, or touch and drag
• Collect pearls (10 pts), shells (20 pts) and golden chests (50 pts)
• Jellyfish sting: lose 1 life and drop back toward the surface
• Oxygen drains underwater and refills at the surface — reaching 0% costs a life
• Win by collecting 20 treasures. You have 3 lives. Good luck, diver!

Keyboard: Arrows / WASD  |  Mouse: click-drag  |  Touch: drag
