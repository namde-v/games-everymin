A sealed vault hums behind six carved runes. Only the true sequence opens it. Every wrong guess costs an attempt — but every clue brings you closer.

How to Play

  • Goal: Crack 5 vaults to win. You have 3 lives; failing a vault costs one life.
  • Guess: Tap runes (or press 1–6) to fill the slots, then press Submit Guess (or Enter). Backspace clears the last rune.
  • Clues after each guess: ● gold = right rune in the right place. ● silver = right rune, wrong place. No dot = that rune is not in the sequence.
  • Scoring: Each cracked vault earns points, with a bonus for cracking it in fewer attempts. Later vaults use longer sequences.
  • Tip: Use early guesses to test many different runes — information is worth more than a lucky first guess.
