✈️ Skyport Air Traffic

You are the tower controller. Guide every incoming plane to the runway that matches its color before its fuel runs out — and keep them from colliding!

How to Play
• Mouse / Touch: press on a plane and drag to draw its route, release to send it.
• Click: click a plane to select it, then click anywhere to add a waypoint.
• Keyboard: 1–9 select a plane, Arrow keys nudge its next waypoint, Space pauses, R restarts.
• Land a plane on its matching runway: Red Blue Green Yellow
• Wrong runway = the plane keeps flying. Crash or fuel-out costs a life. Land 15 planes to win!
Tip: low-fuel planes flash red and beep — route them first!

Game Over

Press R or tap Restart to play again.
