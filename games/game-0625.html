EMBER CANYON RUNNER

Dash through a blazing canyon! The ground crumbles into lava, rocks tumble from the cliffs, and ember gems glitter in the heat. Reach 1500 metres to win — you have 3 lives.

How to Play
⬆️ / Space / Tap upper screen / JUMP button — Jump over lava gaps and rolling boulders
⬇️ / S / Hold lower screen / DUCK button — Duck under falling rocks and cliff overhangs
💎 Collect ember gems for +50 points each
⚠️ Watch the red warning marker: a rock is about to fall there!
🏆 Survive to 1500m to WIN. Three hits and the run ends.

Keyboard: Space / ArrowUp to jump, ArrowDown to duck. Mouse and touch work too.

GAME OVER

Press R, Space, or tap Restart to play again.

Tip: jump early for wide lava pools. Duck the moment a falling rock locks into place overhead. Speed rises as you go!
