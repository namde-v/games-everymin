WORDFALL ANAGRAM

Catch falling letters. Spell the word. Beat the clock.

WORDFALL

Catch the falling letter tiles in the right order to spell each target word before it hits the ground!

How to Play
• Move the catcher with Arrow keys / A & D, the mouse, or touch-drag.
• Catch the next letter of the target word, shown at the top.
• Wrong letters just bounce away — no penalty.
• Lose a life if a needed letter hits the ground or the word timer runs out.
• Spell 6 words to win. You have 3 lives.

You Win!

Move: ← → or A / D  •  Mouse  •  Touch-drag  •  Press Enter to start / restart
