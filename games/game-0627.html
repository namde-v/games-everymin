Ant Colony Architect

The queen needs you! Direct her workers, stockpile food, and build a colony of 50 ants before the beetles destroy the nest.

How to Play

  • Mouse / Touch: Tap or click anywhere to set the rally point — nearby workers rush there. Tap a beetle to squash it and defend the nest.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys / WASD move the rally point. Press B to build a chamber (20 food, +10 ant capacity), S to hatch a worker (10 food), R to restart.
  • Goal: Workers automatically collect golden crumbs and carry them home. Each crumb is +5 food. Reach 50 ants to WIN. If colony health hits 0, the colony falls.
  • Balance: Spending food on chambers and workers grows the colony, but beetles get stronger over time — keep some ants near the nest!
