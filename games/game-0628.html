⚙ Clockwork Catapult

Load a brass gear, read the wind, and knock every clockwork target off its perch.

How to Play:
• Aim with Up / Down arrows, W / S, mouse drag, or touch drag.
• Watch the moving power gauge — press Space, Launch, or tap Fire at the right moment.
• Wind pushes your gear left or right. Strong wind needs a counter-aim.
• Hit all targets before you run out of shots to win the level.

Keyboard: ↑ / ↓ aim  •  Space launch  •  R restart  •  Mouse & touch supported

Tip: higher angle = higher arc, shorter range. Low angles fly fast and flat. Aim into the wind when it is strong.
