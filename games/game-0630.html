Marble Magnet Maze

Guide a steel marble through three magnetic mazes. Magnets pull the marble — choose the right one at the right moment!

How to Play
• Click / tap a magnet, or press 1–4, to make it the active magnet.
• Press ← / → to cycle magnets. Arrow keys / WASD also give a gentle nudge.
• Collect stars, avoid pits, bounce off walls, and reach the glowing goal.
• Falling into a pit costs a life. Complete all 3 mazes to win!

You Win!

Choose a magnet and watch the marble follow its pull.
