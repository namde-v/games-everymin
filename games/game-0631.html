Harbor Crane Stacker

How to Play: A crane swings a crate back and forth above the pier. Press SPACE, click, or tap to drop it at just the right moment.

Land squarely for a perfect stack and bonus points. A misaligned drop shrinks the next crate and shifts the tower. If a crate becomes too narrow, or the tower leans too far, it collapses!

Stack 12 crates to win the harbor contract.

Keyboard: Space / Arrow keys to drop & nudge. Mouse: click to drop. Touch: tap to drop.
