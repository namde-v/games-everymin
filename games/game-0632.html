Mosaic Mender

🟦 🟨 🟥 🟪

How to Play:
1. A mosaic pattern appears — memorise it! You have a few seconds.
2. Some tiles are then scrambled to the wrong colours.
3. Click or tap a tile to cycle its colour. Use arrow keys to move the yellow frame and Space to cycle the selected tile.
4. Restore the exact pattern before your moves run out.
5. Finish all 5 mosaics to win. Running out of moves costs 1 life. You have 3 lives.

Tip: each colour also has its own shape, so you can memorise by shape as well as colour.

You Win!

Press Enter or Space to restart.
