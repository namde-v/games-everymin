🌊 Tide Mill Operator

How to Play: You run a mill powered by the sea. The tide rises and falls on its own cycle. Open sluice gates to turn the waterwheel — more open gates and a higher tide mean more milling power.

  • Toggle gates with buttons A / S / D, or by tapping the gates on the canvas.
  • Grind: hold Space, the Grind button, or press on the millstone. Grinding turns 1 grain into 1 flour, faster with more power. It needs at least 20% power.
  • Buy grain with B when you run low (5 coins for 15 grain).
  • Deliver orders: click an order card (or press 1, 2, 3) when you have enough flour. Earn its coin reward before its timer runs out.
  • Missed order: lose 15 reputation. WIN at 120 coins. GAME OVER if reputation reaches 0.

Tip: close gates at low tide to save nothing — gates cost nothing, but grinding without power wastes precious time. Stockpile flour just before high tide ends.
