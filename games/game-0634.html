🎭 Samba Street Parade

How to Play: Drum cues scroll down four lanes toward the glowing hit zone at the bottom. When a drum reaches the zone, hit its matching pad — or press its arrow key — right on the beat!

← Red Drum  •  ↓ Orange Drum  •  ↑ Green Drum  •  → Blue Drum
On-screen pads work with mouse and touch too.

Perfect hits score 100 and build your combo. Good hits score 50. Misses break your combo and drain crowd energy. Fill the parade meter to WIN — but if crowd energy hits zero, the parade stops!

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads, fully offline.
