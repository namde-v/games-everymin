🌋 Geyser Hopper

How to Play: Leap across erupting geysers to reach the far ridge, 600 metres away. Each geyser runs on a visible timer: it bubbles, then ERUPTS in a tall jet of steam. Jump while your geyser is erupting and the blast launches you safely to the next rock — a perfectly timed jump near the peak scores a bonus. Jump too early or too late and you splash into the water and lose a life. Grab the floating steam crystals for extra points.

Controls: Space / Arrow Up or Click / Tap = Jump  |  R = Restart  |  You have 3 lives.

Tip: watch the gauge above your geyser — jump when it flashes ERUPTING!
