Herbalist's Potion Lab

Welcome, herbalist! Customers arrive with mysterious ailments. Each one gives you clues about the herbs their remedy needs. Deduce the exact combination and brew it!

How to Play:
• Read the clues in the panel above the lab bench.
• Tap / click up to 3 herbs on the grid to select them (selected herbs glow).
• Keyboard: Arrow keys move the highlight, Space or Enter selects, B brews, C clears.
• Press Brew Potion when you have exactly 3 herbs selected.
• A correct brew scores points and the next customer arrives.
• A wrong brew wastes 1 unit of ingredient stock. Run out of stock and the shop closes!
• Brew all 6 remedies correctly to WIN.

Mouse, touch, and keyboard all work. Sound starts after you press Start.

You Win!

Tip: clues describe categories, colours and traits. An exclusion clue rules a herb out entirely — use it to narrow the grid!
