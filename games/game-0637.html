🚂 Railway Junction Tycoon

How to Play: Trains roll in from the west, each painted in one colour. Before a train reaches the junction diamond, set the track switch to its colour: tap a switch button below, click a switch on the tracks, or press keys 1–4. A correctly routed train earns 100 points. A wrong station or a collision costs a life. You have 3 lives. Route 15 trains correctly to win and become Tycoon!

Switches — 1: Red North  ·  2: Blue East  ·  3: Green South  ·  4: Yellow Depot

Mouse, touch and keyboard supported. Sound starts after you press Start.
