✈ Paper Plane Postman

Deliver 10 letters to WIN!

How to Play: Steer your paper plane with Arrow Keys / WASD, or drag with mouse or touch. Ride the glowing updrafts to climb without losing speed. Dodge trees and kites — a crash costs a life. You have 3 lives.

When a red mailbox slides by below, press Space or Click / Tap to drop a letter. Land it in the mailbox to deliver it and score 100 points. Near misses still earn 10 points. Watch the wind — it pushes your plane sideways!

Controls: Arrows / WASD = steer  |  Mouse / Touch drag = steer  |  Space / Click / Tap = drop letter  |  P = pause
