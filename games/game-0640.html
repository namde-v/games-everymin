Glassblower's Breath

Welcome to the furnace, apprentice. A glowing blob of molten glass waits on your pipe. Shape it to the exact size the master ordered — no more, no less.

How to Play
HOLD Space, the mouse button, the Hold Breath button, or your finger on the canvas to blow air into the glass and make it grow.
RELEASE when the blob matches the dashed target ring. Land inside the glowing tolerance band for a perfect piece.
Over-blow or under-blow and the piece cracks. Blow past the red burst line and it shatters instantly.
Shape 6 perfect pieces to win. 3 cracks and the shift is over.

Tip: watch the size meter and release gently — the glass grows faster the longer you hold.
