Glacier Kayak Rapids

How to Play: Steer your kayak down the glacier river. Dodge grey rocks and floating logs, collect sparkling river gems, and survive 1500 metres to the calm lake — or collect 25 gems for an early win!

Steer left / right to change lanes, and press Up (or tap the top of the river) for a quick paddle burst. Hitting an obstacle costs one life. You have 3 lives.

Controls: ← → Arrow Keys / A D to steer • ↑ / W / Space for paddle burst
Mouse: move to steer, click for burst • Touch: drag to steer, tap for burst
