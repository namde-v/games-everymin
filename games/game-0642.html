Constellation Weaver

Weave the night sky back together, one star at a time.

How to Play: Each level gives you a constellation clue and a sky full of stars. Click or tap the stars in the correct order to draw the constellation. The first star glows gold. Wrong stars cost a life — you have 3 lives for the whole journey. Use Hint to briefly reveal the next star. Complete all 6 constellations to win!

Mouse: click a star  •  Touch: tap a star  •  Keyboard: Arrow keys move the cursor, Enter / Space selects, H = Hint, R = Restart

Clues describe the shape and star count. Watch for decoy stars that are not part of the pattern!
