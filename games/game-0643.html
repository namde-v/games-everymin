🍯 Beehive Honey Manager

You are the queen bee's chief manager. Lead your colony through four seasons and fill every honey quota!

How to Play

  • Foragers gather nectar. Producers turn nectar into honey. Guards stop raids.
  • You have 30 bees total. Every bee must be assigned or resting — reassign anytime during a season.
  • Each season lasts 45 seconds and has a higher honey quota: Spring 40, Summer 70, Autumn 110, Winter 150.
  • Wrong assignments lose honey: wasp raids steal honey if guards are low; unguarded nectar spoils if producers are low; over-foraging without guards is risky.
  • Random events appear: flower blooms (bonus nectar), wasp raids, and cold snaps. React fast!
  • Lose all 3 hive hearts, or miss a season quota, and it is Game Over. Finish Winter to win!

Keyboard: 1/2/3 select role, arrows adjust, Enter/Space to start. Mouse and touch fully supported.
