🏹 Archery Wind Trial

Hit 350 points before you run out of arrows or lives!

How to Play: You have 3 rounds of 5 arrows (15 total). Aim the bow, watch the wind, and hit the target. Bullseye = 100, inner ring = 50, middle = 30, outer = 10. A complete miss costs one life. Lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over.

Controls: Mouse — move to aim, click to shoot. Touch — drag to aim, lift your finger to shoot. Keyboard — Up / Down to change angle, Left / Right to change power, Space to shoot, R to restart. You can also use the sliders and the Shoot button below.

Wind pushes your arrow sideways while it flies, and farther targets in later rounds are smaller and harder. Aim slightly into the wind and allow for the arrow dropping over distance.

Tip: strong right wind means aim left of the target. Strong left wind means aim right. Longer shots need more power and a higher angle.
