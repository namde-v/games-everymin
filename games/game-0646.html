VOLCANO ASH GLIDER

Ride the hot winds above a erupting volcano. Dodge embers and rock pillars, grab crystals, and reach the safe landing zone!

How to Play:
• Arrow keys / W S or mouse move or touch drag — steer the glider up and down.
• Hold Space or press and hold to catch lift — updrafts (glowing orange columns) push you up and give bonus points.
• Collect cyan crystals (+50 points). Avoid falling embers and rock pillars — each hit costs a life.
• Survive 3000m to reach the green landing zone and WIN. You have 3 lives.

Tip: stay near updrafts when embers rain down — lift is your best escape.
