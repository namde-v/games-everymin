How to Play

  • You face 5 safes, each with a secret numeric code. Later safes use longer codes.
  • Set a combination with the dials, keypad, or keyboard, then press Try Combination.
  • Each digit gets feedback: green = exact, blue ↑ = your digit is too low, orange ↓ = your digit is too high. The summary also counts exact positions.
  • Use the clues to narrow every digit down. You have a limited number of attempts per safe.
  • Crack a safe to score points — faster cracks score more. Fail a safe and you lose a life.
  • Win by cracking all 5 safes. Game Over if you lose all 3 lives.

Sound is procedural Web Audio and starts only after you press Start.
