🐪 Desert Oasis Caravan

How to Play: Lead your caravan across the desert to the distant city of Zafaran (top right). Each leg of the journey costs water and food based on distance. Stop at oases to rest, refill supplies, and trade goods — buy cheap, sell dear.

Trading: Dates are cheap at Palm Spring, spice is cheap at Mirage Wells, silk is cheap at Salt Flats. Sell where prices are high to earn gold. Gold becomes score, and leftover water and food add bonus points at the city.

Survival: If water or food hits zero, or you lose all camels to sandstorms and bandits, the caravan is lost. Travel wisely: short hops are safer, long jumps save days but cost more supplies.

Controls: Mouse click an oasis then Travel • Arrow keys / W A S D to select, Enter to travel • Touch: tap a node, tap Travel • Keys 1-3 to buy, 4-6 to sell, R to restart.
