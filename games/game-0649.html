How to Play

Watch the glowing dancers and listen to the pattern. Patterns use single, double and triple claps. When it is your turn, tap the big CLAP button, press Space, or tap the circle in time with each glowing beat. Hit the beat for points; miss and lose a heart. Finish all 8 rounds to win!

Controls: Space / Enter = clap  •  Mouse click = clap  •  Touch = clap  •  R = restart
