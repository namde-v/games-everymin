Ice Sculptor Chisel

How to Play: A rough ice block hides a sculpture. A glowing weak point glides along the block. Move your chisel to it, watch the power meter swing, and strike when the power is in the green sweet zone.

Perfect strikes carve big chunks and score big points. Strikes that are too powerful crack the ice — 3 cracks and the sculpture shatters. Weak strikes barely chip anything. Reveal all 5 sculptures to complete the gallery and win.

Mouse: move to aim, click to strike  |  Touch: drag to aim, tap Strike  |  Keyboard: Left / Right or A / D to aim, Space to strike
