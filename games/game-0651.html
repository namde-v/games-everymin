Monsoon Rooftop Runner

Arcade / Rooftop Sprint — Sprint across rain-slick rooftops while the monsoon rolls in. Leap the gaps, slide under storm debris, and grab every coin.

How to Play

  • Jump: Space, Arrow Up, W, click, or tap the top half of the screen.
  • Slide: Arrow Down, S, or tap the bottom half of the screen.
  • Double jump: Press jump again while airborne for one extra boost.
  • Avoid gaps, water towers, antennas, and flying debris. You have 3 lives.
  • Collect coins for bonus points. Survive 1,200 metres to win!
