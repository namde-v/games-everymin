☀ Sundial Shadow Puzzle

Rotate the gnomons. Bend the light of time onto every golden marker.

Score: 0 Lives: 3 Level: 1 / 5 Moves left: 12 Status: Title

How to Play

  • Each level shows a stone sundial court with 3 gnomons and golden target markers.
  • Every gnomon casts a long shadow in the direction it points. Rotate a gnomon until its shadow touches a target.
  • Light ALL targets in the level before you run out of moves to advance.
  • Tap / click a gnomon to select it, then use Rotate buttons. Clicking a gnomon directly turns it 15° clockwise.
  • Keyboard: 1 / 2 / 3 select a gnomon, Left / Right rotate, Space rotates the selected gnomon, Enter starts or restarts.
  • Touch: tap a gnomon to select it, tap Rotate buttons to turn it. Every rotation costs 1 move.
  • You have 3 lives. Running out of moves on a level costs a life and resets that level.

Clear all 5 levels to win. Good luck, keeper of shadows!
