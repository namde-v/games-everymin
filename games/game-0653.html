Lighthouse Keeper Shifts

How to Play: Each night you have only 4 actions. Spend them wisely: refuel the lamp, clean the lens, send a rescue signal, or sweep the sea to spot ships. A dim or dirty light lets ships wreck on the rocks — and wrecks cost beacon strength. Survive all 5 nights and rescue at least 12 ships to win.

Keys: 1 = Refuel   2 = Clean Lens   3 = Rescue Signal   4 = Sweep Sea   Enter = Next Night / Start
Mouse / touch: tap the action buttons, or click a ship in the sea to signal it.
