Steelpan Island Beats

How to Play: Island rhythms scroll down four steelpan lanes. When a note reaches the glowing strike line, tap its pan in time! Perfect hits score 300, Good hits 100. Miss a note and you lose a life. Survive the whole song to win!

Keys: A S K L  or tap / click the pans below the canvas. Press Space for the centre pan.

Tip: watch the beat markers and keep your combo alive for bonus points every 10 combo!

Four pans: Bass (A), Tenor (S), Alto (K), Soprano (L). Notes fall from the top. Hit them exactly on the gold line for PERFECT. You have 3 lives. Hit 60 notes and finish the song to win. Good luck, island star!
