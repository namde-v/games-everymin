Falconry Flight Trial

You are the falconer. Your falcon circles high above the meadow. Wind pushes every dive sideways — judge the drift, release at the right moment, and strike the moving prey.

  • Move: Arrow keys / A & D, mouse, or drag on touch to steer the circling falcon.
  • Dive: Press Space, click Dive, or tap the falcon to begin the dive.
  • Release / Strike: While diving, press Space, click, or tap again when the falcon is over the prey.
  • Score by accuracy: a dead-centre strike is 100 points. You have 6 dives. Reach 300 points to win.

Watch the wind arrow. Strong wind means your dive will curve — aim upwind of the prey. Prey animals dart left and right; a late release is better than an early miss. A miss costs a dive, but a near miss still earns points.
