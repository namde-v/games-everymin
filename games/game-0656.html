Avalanche Snowboarder

Carve down the mountain and outrun the avalanche!

How to Play: Steer with Arrow Keys / A & D, move the mouse, or drag on touch. Dodge pine trees and rocks. Grab red flags for 50 points and golden flags for 150. Your speed grows as you descend. Beware: an avalanche wave sometimes surges up from behind — stay ahead of it!

3 crashes end the run. Reach 3000m to reach the lodge and WIN. Press Space to start, R to restart.
