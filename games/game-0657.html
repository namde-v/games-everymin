Cartographer's Map Match

Four cities wait on an old parchment map. Rotate each map tile, lay it on the grid, and stitch their trade routes together.

  • Goal: Connect all four cities into one route network to win.
  • Good placement: A tile touching a city or another route scores +50.
  • Wrong placement: A tile touching nothing costs 1 move.
  • Limit: You have 25 moves. Fill the map wisely.
  • Controls: Tap / click a tile, then tap a grid cell. Press R to rotate, Arrow keys to move the cursor, Enter to place.

How to Play: Select the tile at the bottom, rotate it until its roads point where you need them, then place it. Roads must line up with neighbours to count as connected.
