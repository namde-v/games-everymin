Welcome, parade leader! Drum and dance cues roll down the street toward the golden hit line. Strike the matching instrument at just the right moment to keep the crowd roaring.

How to Play:
🥁 Drum = D   🌴 Shaker = F   💃 Dance = J   🎷 Trumpet = K

Win: finish the full parade route. Lose: crowd meter empties or all 3 lives are lost.
