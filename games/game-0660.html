Clockmaker Gear Calibration

How to Play: A gear spins on the left. A golden notch marks the target angle. Press STOP (or Space / tap) when the red tooth lines up with the golden mark. Within 6° is a PERFECT mesh (+250), within 15° is GOOD (+120), within 30° is LOOSE (+50). Miss by more than 30° and you lose an attempt. Mesh all 8 gears before your attempts run out to wind the clock and win.

Keyboard: Space / Enter = Stop & Start  •  Mouse: click STOP  •  Touch: tap the canvas or STOP button. Later gears spin faster and reverse direction.
