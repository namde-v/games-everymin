🚢 Tidal Harbor Ferry Dash

Pilot your ferry across a busy harbor. Collect waiting passengers and deliver them to their destination dock before the clock runs out!

How to Play
🕹️ Steer: Arrow keys / WASD to turn and thrust, or hold mouse / touch and drag — the ferry steers toward your pointer.
👥 Pick up: Sail close to a dock with a waiting passenger (yellow dot) to board them.
🏁 Deliver: Sail to the flashing destination dock to drop them off (+100 points).
⚠️ Avoid: Rocks, drifting buoys and rolling waves — each crash costs a life. 3 crashes = Game Over.
🏆 Win: Deliver 10 passengers within 120 seconds. Run out of time or lives and the harbor closes!
