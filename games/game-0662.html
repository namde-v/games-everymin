How to Play

Goal: Light all 25 lanterns before you run out of moves.

Rule: Tapping a lantern toggles it AND its up, down, left and right neighbours. A dark lantern lights up; a lit one goes dark.

Think ahead: Every tap changes five lanterns at once, so plan chains of moves instead of tapping at random.

Controls: Click or tap a lantern. Use arrow keys to move the golden frame, then Enter or Space to toggle. Press H for a hint, R to restart.

Levels: Each new level starts a fresh riddle with fewer spare moves. Win to advance; run out of moves and the night wins.

Sound starts only after you press Start.
