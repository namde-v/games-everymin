Welcome to the Alpine Apiary!

You manage beehives through Spring, Summer and Autumn. Gather enough honey before Winter arrives, keep your colonies healthy, and do not miss a seasonal quota.

How to Play
  • Place hives: Click an empty wooden plot on the meadow, or press P then click. Each hive costs 15 honey and adds bees.
  • Assign bees: Use Forage / Pollinate / Defend buttons, or keys 1 2 3. Forage makes honey, Pollinate grows flowers, Defend fights mites.
  • Balance: Too much foraging exhausts bees and hurts health. Too little defence lets mites spread. Low flowers means low forage.
  • Seasons: Spring days 1–20, Summer 21–40, Autumn 41–60. Each season has a honey quota. Winter is day 61.
  • Win: Reach Winter with all quotas met and health above 0. Lose: Health hits 0, or any seasonal quota is missed.
  • Mouse, touch and keyboard all work. Press Space to speed time, N for next day.

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads, fully offline.
