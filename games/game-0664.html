🥁 TAIKO THUNDER DRUMS

Thunder rolls across the festival stage. Drum notes race toward the strike zone — hit the right drum exactly on the beat and build a blazing combo!

How to Play:
🔴 Red notes (DON) — hit the CENTER drum. Keys: Space / K, tap the gold drum, or click it.
🔵 Blue notes (KA) — hit a SIDE drum. Keys: F / J (left / right), or tap a side drum.
⭐ Gold notes — hit ANY drum for bonus points.
Hit as close to the strike line as possible: PERFECT (+300), GOOD (+150), OK (+50). Misses break your combo and drain the spirit meter. Fill the song to WIN — empty the spirit meter and the show ends!

72 notes • about 90 seconds • keyboard, mouse & touch supported
