A harbor crane swings over moving cargo ships. Drop the hook at the perfect moment to snatch a crate and stack it neatly on the dock.

How to Play
• Watch the hook swing left and right over the ship.
• Press DROP, Space, or tap the crane area to release the hook.
• Land on a crate for points. A perfect center grab is worth 100!
• Miss the ship or splash into the water and you lose a life.
• Stack 10 crates to win. Lose all 3 lives and the shift ends.
