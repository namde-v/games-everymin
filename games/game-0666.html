🛶 Mangrove Kayak Escape

Night falls over the mangroves. Your kayak must slip downstream before the tide turns. Steer through the winding channel, ride the glowing currents, and gather fireflies for light.

How to Play
← → or A / D — steer the kayak
Mouse move / drag — steer toward pointer
Touch & drag — steer with your finger
🌿 Mangrove roots & 🪵 logs cost 1 hull point
✨ Fireflies: +50 points   🌊 Current: speed boost
Win by paddling 1500 m. Lose all 3 hull points and you sink!
