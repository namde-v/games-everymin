✦ Constellation Connect Logic

Connect the stars in the correct numbered sequence to reveal each hidden constellation. One wrong link costs a life. Three mistakes and the night fades.

How to Play:
  • Each star shows a clue number. Connect them in order: 1 → 2 → 3, and so on.
  • Click / tap a star to select it, then click / tap the next numbered star to draw a line.
  • Lines must not cross an existing line. A crossing or wrong-number link is a mistake.
  • Complete all 4 constellations to WIN. You have 3 lives.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys move the glowing cursor, Enter / Space selects a star, R restarts.
  • Mouse and touch both work. Correct lines score 100 points, plus a 250-point constellation bonus.

Sound starts only after you press Start.
