Welcome to the Tea House

Seat guests, brew their teas, and serve before patience runs out!

How to Play
• Guests arrive and sit at tables. Click a table to take its order.
• Click the matching tea button to brew it (brewing takes a few seconds).
• Click a table again while holding a brewed tea to serve it.
• Serve quickly for tips and reputation. Slow service loses reputation.
• Buy upgrades with earnings. Reach the earnings goal before Day 3 ends to win.
• Keyboard: 1–4 brew teas, Space serves the selected table, arrows move selection.
