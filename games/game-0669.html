How to Play

  • Listen: the reed flute plays a melody, one note at a time. Watch the glowing reeds.
  • Repeat: tap the five coloured notes in the same order, or press keys 1–5 (also A S D F G).
  • Grow: every round adds one more note. A wrong note costs one life. You have 3 lives.
  • Win: complete all 8 rounds to become River Melody Master. Lose all lives and the song ends.
  • Each correct note scores 10 points; finishing a round adds a 50-point bonus.
