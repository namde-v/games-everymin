Blacksmith Hammer Strike

Heat the metal, watch the glowing strike zone slide along the bar, and hammer at the perfect moment.

How to Play
• The glowing orange zone moves back and forth along the metal bar.
• Gold target marks show where to strike. Press SPACE, click, or tap STRIKE when the zone overlaps a mark.
• Dead centre = PERFECT (+100), inside the mark = GOOD (+50), outside = a miss and you lose a life.
• Land 3 good strikes to forge one item. Forge 6 items to win. Lose all 5 lives and the metal cracks — game over.

Keyboard: SPACE / ENTER to strike • Mouse: click • Touch: tap

Forged!

Tip: do not rush every pass. Wait for the zone to centre on the gold mark, then strike once. Speed rises with every item.
