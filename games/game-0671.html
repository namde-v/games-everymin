Desert Dune Buggy Jump

Drive your buggy across endless desert dunes! Jump over rocks and gaps, collect fuel cans to keep going, and reach the oasis 3000 m away to win.

How to Play
🕹️ Space / ↑ / Click / Tap — Jump (hold for a higher jump, double-tap for a double jump!)
⛽ Collect fuel cans for +50 points and +1 fuel
🪨 Avoid rocks and gaps — each crash costs 1 life
❤️ You have 3 lives. Reach 3000 m to win! Speed and hazards increase as you go.
