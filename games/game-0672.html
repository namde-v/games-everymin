How to Play:

  • A master weaving pattern is shown on the right. Your loom on the left starts scrambled.
  • Click or tap one tile, then another, to swap them. On keyboard, move the dashed cursor with Arrow keys and press Enter or Space to select.
  • Each swap costs 1 move. Tiles that already match the target glow with a golden sparkle.
  • Complete the pattern before moves run out to advance. Finish all 5 levels to win the game!
  • Levels get bigger and trickier — plan your swaps carefully. Score bonus for moves left over!
