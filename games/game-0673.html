Observatory Night Scheduler

How to Play: You run a mountaintop observatory for 5 nights. Observation requests arrive with a target, a visibility window, a cost, and a reward. Select a request, then assign it to a free telescope before its window closes. While a telescope observes, it cannot take another job. Completed jobs earn research points and money. Expired jobs hurt satisfaction and count as misses. Watch clouds, budget, and the night clock.

Win: Finish Night 5 with budget at or above $0 and fewer than 8 misses.
Game Over: 8 missed requests, budget below $0, or satisfaction falls to 0%.

Controls: Mouse, touch, or keyboard. Click / tap requests and telescopes. Keys 1-3 select requests, Q/W/E assign, Space pauses, R restarts after the game ends.
