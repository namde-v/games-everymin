SYNTHWAVE BEAT RUNNER

How to Play:

  • Neon synth notes race down 4 highway lanes on the beat.
  • Hit each note exactly when it reaches the glowing HIT ZONE near the bottom.
  • Use D F J K on keyboard, or tap / click the matching lane.
  • Perfect hits score 300, Good hits score 100. Hits build your combo multiplier.
  • Missing a note breaks your combo and costs 1 life. Lose all 5 lives and it is Game Over.
  • Survive the full 60-second track and reach 6000 points to WIN.
