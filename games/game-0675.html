🪁 Sky Kite Collector

ARCADE / KITE WIND COLLECTION

How to Play: Steer your kite through the sky. Collect 30 stars to win! Grab balloons for bonus points. Dodge storm clouds and birds — each hit costs a life.

Controls: Arrow keys or WASD to fly • Mouse: move to steer, click to boost • Touch: drag to steer. Watch the wind! Gusts will push your kite sideways and gusts get stronger each level.

Tip: Stay away from the bottom edge — storm clouds drift there. A golden star is worth 50 points, a balloon is worth 25.
