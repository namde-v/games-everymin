Lighthouse Keeper Roster

How to Play: You command three keepers through five stormy nights. Assign each keeper to Lamp, Foghorn, Rescue Watch, or Supply Run. Keep lamp power and oil high, watch ships approach, and answer distress calls before they wreck.

Controls: Click or tap a duty button, use keys 1–4 to choose a duty, or press L / F / R / S. Space pauses. Every assignment costs energy; rested keepers work better.

Win by surviving all 5 nights with ships safe. Lose if 3 ships wreck or oil reaches zero.
