🏹 Archery Wind Shot

Hit moving targets. Beat 100 points with 12 arrows!

How to Play: Aim with your mouse, touch drag, or Arrow Keys (Up/Down to raise or lower aim, Left/Right to fine-tune). The wind pushes your arrow sideways — aim into the wind when it is strong. Targets drift left and right and change speed. Wind changes every round. Reach 100 points to win!
