🦪 Deep Sea Pearl Diver

Collect 12 pearls, then return to the surface to WIN!

How to Play: Swim with Arrow Keys / WASD, or steer with your mouse / finger by pressing and dragging on the water. Grab glowing pearls and air bubbles. Avoid sharks and jellyfish — each hit costs a life.

⚠️ Air drains faster the deeper you go. Air bubbles refill your tank. If your air runs out or you lose all lives, the dive is over.

🏆 Win: collect all 12 pearls AND swim back up to the surface (top of the screen).

Controls: Arrow Keys / WASD to swim  |  Mouse: hold + move to steer  |  Touch: drag on the water  |  P = Pause
