🪞 Mirror Labyrinth Reflector

How to Play: A laser fires from the red emitter. Place mirrors on empty grid squares to bend the beam around walls and into every glowing crystal target.

Click or tap a square to place a mirror, click it again to rotate it ( / then \ ), click a third time to remove it. Use Arrow Keys to move the cursor and Enter to place / rotate. R rotates, C clears the last mirror.

Each placement, rotation, or removal costs 1 move. Every level has a limited mirror supply and move budget. Light all crystals to advance. Run out of moves and it is Game Over!
