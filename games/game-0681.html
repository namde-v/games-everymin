Aurora Ice Skater Glide

Skate across a moonlit frozen lake beneath the aurora. Collect glowing shards, glide with real momentum, and dodge cracks and snowmen.

How to Play
• Arrow keys or WASD to push and steer — you keep sliding, so plan your turns.
• Mouse: click and hold in a direction to glide that way. Touch: drag on the ice to steer.
• Collect 25 aurora shards to WIN. Hitting a crack or snowman costs a life.
• Speed rises every 15 seconds. You have 3 lives. Survive, sparkle, and win!

Tip: gentle taps beat hard pushes on slippery ice.
