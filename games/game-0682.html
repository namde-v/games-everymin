⚙️ Clockwork Gear Lock

How to Play:

  • Tap / click a gear to rotate it 90° clockwise.
  • Arrow keys move the golden selector; Space or Enter rotates the selected gear.
  • Press R to rotate counter-clockwise, H for a hint.
  • Line up the gear teeth so power flows from the ⚡ Power Source (top-left) through every link to the 🔒 Lock (bottom-right).
  • Solve it within 30 moves. Run out and you lose a life and the gears reset — lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over!

Tip: gears with glowing blue paths are already carrying power. Follow the glow!
