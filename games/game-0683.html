Harbor Crane Stacker

Welcome to the docks! A cargo ship is waiting, and the manifest must be loaded in the exact order shown.

How to Play:
• Move the crane: Left / Right arrows or A / D, mouse move, or drag on touch.
• Drop a container: Space, click on the harbor, tap DROP, or the on-screen arrows.
• Each column of the ship needs 3 containers. The target color for the next slot in every column is shown on the ship.
• Drop the matching color into the right column to score. A wrong color or a full column costs a life.
• Fill all 12 slots to WIN. Lose all 3 lives and the shift ends.

Tip: Watch the crane cargo — plan your column before you drop!
