🌴 How to Play 🌴

  • Watch and listen: the drums light up and play one by one. Memorise the whole pattern.
  • Repeat it: tap the drums in exactly the same order. Each correct round adds one more beat.
  • Score points: +10 per correct beat, +50 bonus for finishing a round, +25 speed bonus if you answer quickly.
  • Lives: a wrong drum costs one life. You have 3 lives. Lose them all and the circle breaks!
  • Win: complete all 10 rounds to become the Jungle Drum Master and unlock the victory rhythm.
  • Controls: click or tap a drum, or press 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 (or A / S / D / F). Press Space to start or restart.

Tip: say the colours out loud as they play — Red, Yellow, Blue, Green — it really helps on long patterns!
