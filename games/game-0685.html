Falcon Dive Hunter

How to Play: You are a falcon soaring high above a meadow. Prey scurries along the ground below. Move your mouse, finger, or arrow keys to aim the falcon. Hold mouse button, touch, or SPACE to charge your dive — watch the power bar! Release to stoop down and snatch the prey. A perfect hit on a fast rabbit scores more. Beware: sudden wind gusts push your dive sideways, so lead your target! Catch 12 prey to win. Miss 3 times and the hunt is over.

Controls: Mouse / Touch / Arrow Keys + SPACE. Hold to charge, release to dive.
