🐜 Ant Trail Architect

How to Play: You are the colony architect. Draw a pheromone trail from the Nest to each Food source.

  • Drag from the Nest (brown mound, left side) through empty grass cells to a Food (apple / leaf / crumb).
  • Trails must be continuous, step to a neighbouring cell, and must NEVER cross an existing trail, a rock, or another route.
  • Rival red ants patrol the field. If your drawing touches a rival ant, you lose a life and the trail is cancelled.
  • Shorter trails score more. Complete all 3 food routes to WIN. Lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over.
  • Mouse: drag to draw. Touch: drag with your finger. Keyboard: Arrow keys move the cursor, Space starts / extends trail, Enter starts, R restarts, C clears current trail.

Tip: Plan around rocks first — a blocked middle cell can ruin a long route.
