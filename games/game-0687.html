💎 Gemstone Mine Match

How to Play: You are deep in a glittering mine. Swap two adjacent gems to line up 3 or more of the same kind in a row or column. Matched gems burst, score points, and new gems cascade down from above. Chain cascades for combo bonuses!

  • Goal: Reach 1500 points before you run out of moves.
  • Moves: You have 25 swaps. Only a successful match uses a move.
  • Scoring: 10 points per gem, plus bigger bonuses for 4- and 5-gem lines and cascades.
  • Controls: Mouse click, touch tap, or keyboard arrows + Enter / Space.

Tip: look for L-shapes and 4-in-a-row setups — they clear extra gems!
