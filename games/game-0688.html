Airport Runway Controller

How to Play: Planes approach from every direction. Tap or click a plane to select it, then assign it to a free runway (buttons below, or keys 1 / 2 / 3) or send it to a holding pattern (H). Assigned planes steer to their runway and land for points. Unassigned planes that fly off screen are missed. If two planes get too close they collide!

Land 12 planes to win. You have 3 lives. Collisions and missed planes cost a life. Watch the fuel ring: planes flashing red are urgent — land them first!

Mouse / touch: click a plane, then a runway. Keyboard: 1, 2, 3 = runway, H = hold, Esc = deselect.
