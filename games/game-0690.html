🐢 Coral Reef Turtle Race

Race to the finish before time runs out!

How to Play: Steer your sea turtle through glowing coral gates for points. Dodge jellyfish and rocks — each hit costs a life. Collect bubbles for a speed boost and bonus points. Reach 2400 m before the 90-second timer ends to win.

⌨️ Arrow Keys / WASD to steer  •  🖱️ Move mouse / click to steer  •  👆 Touch and drag to steer

Tip: Gates are worth 100 points, bubbles 25 points, and boosts help you outrun the current.

Race Over

Press Restart to race again through the reef.
