🌋 VOLCANO LAVA RUNNER

You are sprinting down a volcano slope as it erupts! Lava pools block the path and burning rocks rain from the sky.

How to Play:
⬆️ / SPACE / TAP — Jump over lava gaps & low rocks
⬇️ / S / SWIPE DOWN — Slide under high falling rocks
← → / A D — Move left / right slightly to dodge
CLICK the Start button or press ENTER to begin
Reach 3000m to WIN. You have 3 lives — one hit = one life lost.

Speed increases as you run further. Collect ember crystals for +50 points!

Tip: Jump early for wide lava pools. Slide only when a rock is high — sliding into lava still hurts! Grab crystals, but never trade a life for one. Good luck, runner!
