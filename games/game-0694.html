🦀 Tide Pool Crab Catcher

Crabs are scuttling across the tide pools! Time your swinging net perfectly to scoop them up — but watch out for jellyfish.

How to Play:
• Move the net: Mouse, Touch drag, or ← → / A D keys
• Swing the net: SPACE, Click, Tap, or the big SWING button
• Catch a crab: +10 points (combo multiplies consecutive catches!)
• Catch a jellyfish or miss badly: lose a life and break your combo
• WIN by catching 15 crabs before the 90-second timer runs out
• Lose all 3 lives, or run out of time short of the goal, and it is Game Over

Tip: wait until the crab is right under the swinging net, then strike!
