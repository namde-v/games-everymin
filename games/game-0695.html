Windmill Power Grid

How to Play: Five windmills must power nine houses. Click or tap a windmill to rotate it 90° clockwise — power flows in a straight line from its blades. Each rotation costs 1 turn of daylight. Wind strength (3–6) limits how far power travels: distant houses only light when the wind is strong. Use Wait for Wind to reroll the wind for 1 turn. Beware: pointing a windmill straight at a neighbouring rock causes a short circuit and costs a life!

Keyboard: Arrows move the cursor, Space / Enter rotates, W waits for wind, R restarts. Mouse and touch work too.

Click Start, then rotate windmills to aim their power at the houses.

💡 Yellow houses are lit, grey houses are dark. Green lines are live power. ⛰️ Rocks block power and cause shorts at point-blank range. You win only when ALL 9 houses glow at the same moment.
