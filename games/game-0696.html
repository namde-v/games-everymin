Echo Cave Explorer

How to Play: You are lost in a pitch-dark cave. You can only see a tiny glow around you — unless you send out a sonar ping!

Move with WASD / Arrow Keys, or tap / click a direction pad below. Press SPACE, click the Ping button, or tap the cave to send a sonar pulse that briefly reveals walls, gems, pits and the exit. Pings are limited — use them wisely!

Collect all 8 glowing gems and reach the golden EXIT. Falling into a pit or triggering a cave-in costs a life. You have 3 lives. Avoid the red danger tiles revealed by your pings!

Sound is procedural Web Audio and starts only after you press Start. No downloads, no network — fully offline.

You Win!
