Snowy Yeti Snowball Toss

How to Play: Drag from the yeti (or use arrow keys) to aim. The longer you drag, the more power you charge. Release to throw! Watch the wind — it pushes snowballs sideways. Snowballs bounce off ice and snow, so bank a shot around a ledge if you need to.

Knock every target off its ledge to clear a level. You get a limited number of snowballs per level. Score points for each target, with bonus points for long shots and for snowballs left over.

Mouse / touch: drag and release  •  Keyboard: ← → aim, ↑ ↓ power, Space throw, R restart  •  Sound starts only after you press Start.
