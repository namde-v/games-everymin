Mushroom Forest Forager

Morning light filters through the pines. Forage the forest floor, fill your basket, and bring your harvest home before the day ends.

How to Play:
• Move with WASD / Arrow keys, drag with mouse, or drag with touch.
• Collect edible mushrooms (brown caps) for 10 pts and berries (blue clusters) for 15 pts.
• Your basket holds 8 items. Carry them to the golden basket by the cottage (top-left) to bank the points.
• Avoid poisonous red mushrooms — they cost a life and spill your basket!
• Beware the roaming boar: if it charges into you, you lose a life. It calms down briefly after a hit.
• Win by banking 200 points before the 120-second day ends. Lose all 3 lives and the day is over.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
