Sky Lantern Festival

How to Play: Press Start Festival, then press SPACE, click, or tap Launch to release a lantern. Steer it with Arrow Keys / WASD, by dragging with the mouse, or by dragging with your finger on touch screens.

Guide each lantern through a glowing golden gate at the top. Watch the flame fuel — if it runs out, the lantern falls! Avoid night birds, kites and storm clouds. Deliver 8 lanterns to win the festival. You have 3 lives.

Tip: ride gusts to the right when the wind arrow points right, and slow down near birds. A perfect gate (dead centre) scores bonus points!
