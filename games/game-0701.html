Bamboo Panda Bounce

How to Play: Your panda bounces automatically! Steer left and right to land on bamboo trampolines, grab bamboo shoots for bonus points, and climb as high as you can. Miss a platform and fall off the bottom and you lose a life. Reach 2500m to win!

Move: ← → or A / D  |  Mouse: move to steer  |  Touch: drag to steer
Green platforms = safe  |  Gold platforms = super bounce  |  Red platforms = break after one bounce

Tip: Gold trampolines launch you much higher. Chain bamboo pickups for combo points!
