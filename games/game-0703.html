🍉 Highland Vineyard Scheduler

Assign workers to ripening vine rows before the grapes spoil!

Mouse / Touch: tap a row to select it, then tap Assign (or tap a ripe row twice to harvest). Keyboard: 1-6 select row, Enter or Space assigns, R restarts, A rests workers.

Highland Vineyard Scheduler

How to Play

  • Six vine rows ripen over time. A row is ripe when its bar turns gold (60-100%). Harvest it before it hits 100% and spoils.
  • Click a row (or press 1-6, then Enter) to assign an idle worker. The worker waits on an unripe row and harvests automatically when it ripens.
  • Each harvest earns coins and costs the worker 20 energy. Energy slowly recovers; use Rest to recover faster (but resting workers cannot harvest).
  • Win: harvest 12 rows before the 120-second season ends. Lose: if 5 rows spoil, or time runs out.
  • Ripe rows pay more coins the closer they are to perfect ripeness — but waiting too long risks spoilage!

Tip: keep one worker free for rows about to turn gold.
