Comet Tail Sketcher

How to Play

  • Drag from the comet at the launch pad to pull back the slingshot — the arrow shows power and direction.
  • Release to launch. Gravity from the blue planets will bend your path — use it to slingshot around them!
  • Fly through all 6 target stars (gold rings) to win. Each star is worth 100 points, plus a bonus for launches left.
  • Avoid crashing into a planet — a crash ends that flight. You have 6 launches total.
  • Keyboard: ←→ aim angle, ↑↓ power, Space launch, R restart, Enter start.

Touch works too: drag on the canvas to aim and lift your finger to launch.
