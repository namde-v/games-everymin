🛶 River Rapids Raft Rescue

You are the river rescue crew! Ride the rapids to the finish line, dodge rocks and whirlpools, and rescue stranded swimmers before they drift away.

  • Steer: Arrow keys / WASD, or drag with mouse / touch.
  • Dodge: Grey rocks and spinning whirlpools cost 1 hull hit.
  • Rescue: Touch a swimmer (orange ring) for +100 points.
  • Win: Reach 2400m with at least 8 rescues.
  • Lose: 3 hull hits sinks the raft. Rocks also slow you down.
  • Bonus: Near-misses and fast current stretches add points.
How to Play: Hold LEFT / RIGHT to steer. Avoid the banks — the river narrows and widens as you go! Grab life rings for a bonus.
