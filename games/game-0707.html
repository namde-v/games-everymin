The Stone Tablets Awaken

Eight ancient runes guard a lost treasure. Watch the tablets glow, remember the order, then carve the same sequence back.

How to Play:
  • Watch: Runes light up one by one. Memorise the full order.
  • Decode: Tap / click the rune stones in the same order, or press keys 1–8.
  • Advance: Each level adds one more rune. Complete all 8 levels to win.
  • Lives: A wrong rune costs 1 life. Lose all 3 lives and the tablets seal shut.
  • Scoring: +100 per correct rune, +250 level bonus, +50 speed bonus if you finish a level quickly.
  • Clues: Read the Elder Clue each level — it tells you the length and a pattern hint.

Mouse, touch, or keyboard 1–8. Press Enter to start.
