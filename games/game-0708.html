Wildflower Hive Keeper

How to Play: You manage 12 bees. Send bees to the three flower fields to gather nectar and make honey. Rest tired bees so their energy recovers. Assign guards before wasps attack, or the hive loses HP.

Win by reaching 120 honey before the 150-second season ends. Lose if hive HP reaches 0 or time runs out.

Controls: Click a flower field on the canvas (or use buttons below) to send one bee there. Click the hive to recall a bee to rest. Keys: 1/2/3 = fields, G = guard, R = rest one bee, Space = pause.

Fields bloom and fade: a full field yields the most honey. Working bees lose energy; exhausted bees stop working until rested. Wasps arrive in waves — each guard can block one wasp. Guarded hive takes no damage. Balance gathering, resting and defence!

Welcome, Keeper! Press Start to begin the season.
Tap a field on the canvas to assign bees • Tap the hive to rest a bee • Keyboard, mouse and touch all work.
