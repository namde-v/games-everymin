Paper Plane Wind Tunnel

How to Play: Launch your paper plane and fly it through 20 floating rings. Wind gusts will push you up and down — steer gently and stay off the ground and ceiling.

  • Keyboard: Arrow Up / W / Space = climb, Arrow Down / S = dive. Hold to steer smoothly.
  • Mouse: Hold and drag up or down — the plane follows your pointer.
  • Touch: Drag your finger up or down on the screen to steer.
  • Fly through a ring centre for +100 points. Hitting a storm cloud, the ground, or the ceiling costs one plane.
  • Collect all 20 rings to win. You have 3 planes — good luck, pilot!

Tip: small corrections beat big swoops. Watch the wind arrow.

You Win!

Wind gusts change every few seconds. A strong gust can help you reach a high ring — or push you into trouble.
