Firefly Meadow Catcher

How to Play

  • Fireflies blink across the night meadow. Catch one only while it glows bright yellow-green.
  • Watch for fake glowbugs — they glow dull red-orange. Never tap them!
  • Chain catches quickly to build your combo multiplier (up to x5).
  • Win: catch 20 fireflies before the 90-second night ends.
  • Game Over: 3 strikes — tapping a fake glowbug, tapping empty grass, or letting a lit firefly vanish uncaught.

Controls: Mouse click or tap to catch • Arrow keys / WASD move the net, Space or Enter to catch • P pauses.

Fully offline. Sound starts only after you press Start.
