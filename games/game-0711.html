Aurora Ice Slide Dash

Slide across a frozen aurora lake! Ice is slippery: once you push, you keep gliding until you hit a wall, a snowbank, or brake.

How to Play
Arrow keys / WASD — push in a direction
Space — brake (stop sliding)
Mouse / Touch — tap a direction arrow, or drag to aim and release
Collect all aurora crystals. Avoid the black cracks — they cost a life!

Tip: plan your slide! You cannot turn mid-glide except by braking.
