⚙ Clockwork Gear Lock

How to Play

  • Every gear carries one golden tooth. The lock opens only when all 9 golden teeth point UP.
  • Tap, click, or select a gear and press Space / Enter to turn it one click clockwise.
  • Gears are interlocked! Turning one gear turns its neighbours one click counter-clockwise.
  • Think ahead: a turn that fixes one gear may knock its neighbours out of line.
  • You have 30 moves. Finish inside the limit to unlock the vault and score a bonus.
  • Arrow keys move the brass selector. R shuffles a fresh lock. M toggles sound.

Best played with sound on. Every puzzle is generated from a solved lock, so every lock can be opened.
