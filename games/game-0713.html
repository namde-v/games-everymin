🐪 Desert Oasis Caravan

Lead a caravan across a living desert. Plan routes between six oases, manage water and food, and trade goods for profit. Reach 500 gold to win — but if water or food hits zero, your caravan is lost.

  • Travel: Click / tap an oasis on the map, or use ← → to pick one, then press Enter or the Travel button. Longer routes cost more water and food.
  • Trade: At an oasis, buy goods cheap and sell them where they are dear. Prices differ at every oasis and shift when you arrive.
  • Supplies: Buy water and food at any oasis. Carry at most 20 units of cargo. Watch the gold price hints: green means a good deal, red means pricey.
  • Keyboard: ← → select oasis • Enter travel • ↑ ↓ select good • B buy • V sell • W buy water • E buy food • R restart after game ends.
  • Mouse / Touch: Tap an oasis, tap Buy / Sell buttons, tap Travel. All controls work on touch screens.
