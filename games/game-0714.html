🌊 Tide Drum Circle

How to Play: Ocean-wave beats roll in from the top of the lagoon toward the glowing drum line near the bottom. Each beat is coloured for one drum: blue Wave, orange Shell, or purple Storm.

  • Hit on time: Press A for Wave, S for Shell, D for Storm — or click / tap the matching drum pad below the lagoon.
  • Perfect (right on the line) = 150 points, Good (near the line) = 80 points. Every hit builds your combo multiplier.
  • Miss: Letting a beat pass the line, or hitting the wrong drum, breaks your combo and costs 1 life. You have 3 lives.
  • Win: Survive all 60 beats of the tide song. Game Over: Lose all 3 lives before the song ends.
  • Watch the tide bar: it fills as you progress through the song. Faster waves arrive in the final stretch!

Sound is made live in your browser with procedural drums — no downloads needed. Audio starts only after you press Start.
