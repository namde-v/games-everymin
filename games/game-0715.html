Harbor Lighthouse Beam Aim

How to Play: You are the lighthouse keeper. Ships and buoys sail through the dark harbor, each marked with a number. Rotate your golden beam and hold it on the next numbered target until it is fully lit. Light every target in order to clear the level — before your beam energy runs out!

← / → or A / D — rotate beam  |  Mouse move / drag — aim beam
Touch drag — aim beam  |  Hold the beam steady on a target to charge it

Lighting a target out of order does nothing, so watch the gold marker. Running out of energy costs a life. Clear all 3 levels to win!

You Win!

Press Start, then aim the beam at target 1.
