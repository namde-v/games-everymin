🌋 Volcano Ember Dodger

How to Play: The volcano is erupting! Embers rain down and lava bursts explode from below. Survive the waves and collect 20 cooling orbs to win.

Move: Arrow Keys / WASD  •  Mouse: move to pointer  •  Touch: drag to move
Dash: Space / Shift / Dash button — brief invulnerability, 2.5s cooldown
Avoid: 🔥 Embers (falling) and lava burst zones (orange warning, then eruption)
Collect: 💧 Cooling Orbs (+50)  •  Surviving a wave: +100  •  You have 3 lives

Tip: Dash through a dense ember cluster or out of a lava zone just before it erupts. Grab orbs quickly — they melt away!

Game Over
