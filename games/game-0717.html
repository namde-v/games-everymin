🧵 Mosaic Tile Weaver

Can you re-weave the master mosaic?

How to Play:

  • The Target Mosaic on the right is the pattern you must recreate on your loom.
  • Tap / click a tile to select it, then tap / click a neighbouring tile to swap the two threads.
  • Keyboard: move the glowing cursor with arrows, press Space to select, then press an arrow to swap in that direction.
  • Every swap uses 1 move. You have 25 moves and 3 lives. A swap that makes the mosaic worse costs a life.
  • Matching tiles glow softly. Score points for every tile you place correctly.
  • Win by matching all 36 tiles before moves or lives run out. Use Hint if you get stuck.

Tip: work from the corners inward — the border pattern is the easiest to lock in first.
