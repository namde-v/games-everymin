🔭 Mountain Observatory Scheduler

Run one night on a mountaintop observatory. Assign telescopes to celestial targets before dawn!

How to Play

  • Targets drift across the sky. Each shows its type, priority (★ to ★★★), and the time it stays visible.
  • Assign a telescope by selecting it (buttons or keys 1–3), then clicking / tapping a target — or use arrow keys to highlight a target and press Enter.
  • Weather matters: clouds roll in and block a telescope's view. Radio targets ignore clouds; optical targets pause under cloud cover.
  • Instrument conflicts: each telescope can watch only one target at a time, and each target needs its matching instrument (optical or radio).
  • Score points for completed observations — higher priority targets pay more. Finish an observation before its window closes!
  • Win by reaching 1000 discovery points before dawn (180 seconds). Lose if 3 priority targets (★★★) slip away unobserved.
