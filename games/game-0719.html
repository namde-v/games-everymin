⭐ Starlight Archery Range ⭐

How to Play: Moving star targets drift across the night sky. Drag from the bow (bottom-left) to set your angle and power, then release to loose an arrow. Wind gusts push your arrow sideways — watch the wind gauge and aim into the wind to compensate.

Small fast stars are worth 30 points, medium stars 20, and large slow stars 10. Golden bonus stars are worth 50 but vanish quickly!

You have 12 arrows. Reach 180 points to win. If you run out of arrows first, the range closes.

Mouse / Touch: drag & release  •  Keyboard: ↑ ↓ aim, ← → power, Space shoot, R restart

Game Over

Press Restart to try a new night at the range — wind and stars will be different.
