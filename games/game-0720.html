🪁 Moonlit Kite Runner

Fly 3000 metres to the Moon Festival!

How to Play: Steer your kite with Arrow Keys / WASD, mouse, or touch-drag. Ride glowing wind gusts upward for a speed boost. Collect moon lanterns for points. Dodge storm clouds and tree tops — each hit costs a life. You have 3 lives.

💛 Lantern = +50 points   🌬️ Gust = boost   ⛈️ Storm = -1 life   🌲 Tree = -1 life

Tip: Stay in the middle of the sky when storms stack up, and dive low only to grab a lantern.

Keyboard: Arrows / WASD  |  Mouse: move to steer, click to dash  |  Touch: drag to steer
