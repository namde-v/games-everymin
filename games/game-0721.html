Coral Reef Diver Quest

Dive the reef. Pearls glitter in the deep — but jellyfish drift everywhere, and your air is limited.

How to Play
• Move: Arrow keys / WASD, or drag with mouse / finger.
• Collect: Touch glowing pearls to score 100 points each.
• Avoid: Jellyfish sting! 3 hits and the dive is over.
• Breathe: Oxygen drains steadily. Touch bubbles to refill +25%.
• Win: Collect 25 pearls to complete the quest.
• Lose: Oxygen runs out, or you take 3 jellyfish hits.

Tip: stay shallow early to learn the currents, then dive deep where pearls are plentiful.
