How to Play

  • Each scene shows a glowing target outline on the stage. Fill it exactly with the shadow pieces below.
  • Tap or click a piece in the tray to select it, then tap or click a stage cell to place it.
  • Use Rotate and Flip to turn the selected piece until it matches the shadow. Keyboard: R rotates, F flips.
  • Tap or click a placed piece to lift it back to the tray. Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Enter places, Backspace lifts.
  • Keys 1 to 5 select tray pieces. Every placement, lift, or failed fit costs 1 move.
  • Complete all 5 shadow scenes to win. If moves run out, you lose a life and retry the scene.

Mouse, touch, and keyboard are all supported. Sound starts only after you press Start.
