How to Play

  • Planes enter from the edges, each labelled with a colour. Land each plane on the runway of the SAME colour.
  • Click or tap a plane to select it, then drag on the sky to draw its flight path. The plane follows your path, then heads for its runway.
  • Draw wide, separated routes: two planes that touch will collide. Close calls count as near misses.
  • Keyboard: 1-9 selects a plane in the air, arrow keys nudge the selected plane's next waypoint, Space pauses, R restarts, Enter starts.
  • Win by landing 20 planes. The game ends after 2 collisions, so keep the skies separated!

Mouse, touch, and keyboard all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.
