Echo Cave Clapper

The cave remembers every beat. A friendly bat taps a pattern on the stalactite drums — listen, then tap it back exactly.

How to Play

  • Listen: Watch and hear the glowing drums play a pattern. Do not tap yet.
  • Repeat: Tap the same drums in the same order when the cave says “Your turn!”
  • Grow: Each round the pattern gets one beat longer. Complete all 8 rounds to win.
  • Careful: A wrong sequence costs one life. You have 3 lives — lose all 3 and the cave goes silent.
  • Controls: Tap / click a drum, or press keys 1–4 (also Q W E R or arrow keys).

Tip: say the colours out loud as they flash — it really helps on longer patterns!
