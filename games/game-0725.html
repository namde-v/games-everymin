Blacksmith Forge Hammer

The metal glows, the gauge swings, and one perfect strike makes the blade. Strike too early or too late and the steel weakens — miss badly and the blade is ruined.

How to Play
1. Press Start, then watch the heat needle sweep back and forth.
2. Strike when the needle is inside the golden sweet spot — dead centre is Perfect.
3. Strike with Space, a mouse click, or a tap on the forge.
4. Forge 8 perfect blades to win. Ruin 3 blades and the forge goes cold.
5. Each blade gets faster and its sweet spot gets narrower. Good luck, smith!

Keyboard: Space / Enter to strike • Mouse: click • Touch: tap
