🍄 Mushroom Grove Forager

Collect 30 good mushrooms to win. Dodge the wandering badger and never eat a poisonous mushroom!

How to Play

  • Move: Arrow keys / WASD, on-screen arrows, mouse click to steer, or swipe / drag on touch.
  • Good mushrooms: golden-brown caps with cream spots — worth 10 points each.
  • Poisonous mushrooms: bright red caps with white spots — eating one ends the game!
  • Badger: a striped wanderer. If it catches you 3 times, the game is over. It moves faster at night.
  • Day / Night: the grove cycles every 20 seconds. At night your lamp only lights a small circle — stay close to the paths you know.
  • Tip: mushrooms regrow in clearings. Poisonous ones sparkle red — learn the colours before you rush.

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.

Press Start and fill that basket!
