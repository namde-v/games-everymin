How to Play

  • A target word appears at the top. Letters rain down from the sky.
  • Move the basket with arrow keys / A & D, the mouse, or by dragging on touch.
  • Catch letters in the correct order to spell the word. Wrong letters cost nothing, but a needed letter that hits the ground costs a life.
  • Golden stars give bonus points. Catch several correct letters in a row to raise your combo.
  • Spell 6 words to win. Lose 3 lives and the game is over.

Tip: press Space to pause, R to restart after the game ends.
