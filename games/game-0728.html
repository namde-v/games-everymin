🚂 Railway Switch Yard

Trains roll in from the west. Each train carries a colour. Flip the junction switches so each train diverts down the spur to its matching depot — before it runs off the end of the line!

How to Play
• Mouse / Touch: tap or click a junction circle to flip it between STRAIGHT (→) and DIVERT (↓).
• Keyboard: press 1, 2, 3, 4 to flip junctions 1–4. Press P to pause.
• Junction 1 = Red depot, 2 = Blue, 3 = Green, 4 = Yellow.
• A train diverted to the wrong depot, or reaching the end of the line undelivered, counts as a crash.
• Win: deliver 18 trains. Game Over: 2 crashes. Later waves are faster!

Sound starts only after you press Start. Fully offline — no downloads needed.
