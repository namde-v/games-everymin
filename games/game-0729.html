Rain Tap Symphony

A rhythm storm is rolling in. Raindrops fall in time with the beat — tap them the moment they splash onto a drum pad.

How to Play:
• Drops fall down 4 lanes onto glowing drum pads.
• Tap / click / press a pad exactly when a drop lands on it.
• Keys: A S K L for lanes 1–4, or tap the pads below.
• Perfect timing = 300 pts, Good = 150, Early/Late = 50.
• Build combos for bonus points. Miss 10 drops and the storm wins.
• Win by scoring 5000 points or finishing the whole song!

Sound starts only after you press Start. Headphones recommended.
