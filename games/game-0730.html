Glassblower Breath Control

At the furnace bench, a glowing blob of molten glass waits on your pipe. Breathe life into it — gently! — and sculpt it to match each customer's silhouette.

How to Play:
• Hold SPACE, the mouse button, the big BLOW button, or your finger on the canvas to blow. Release to stop.
• Watch the pressure gauge: blowing raises pressure. If it hits the red zone, the piece POPS and you lose a life.
• Each piece has a target silhouette (dashed outline). Grow the glass to match it, then press Finish Piece (F) or tap Finish when the match is close.
• Match quality scores points. Shape 6 pieces to win. 3 pops ends the game.
• Controls: SPACE / mouse hold / touch hold to blow · F or Finish button to finish · R to restart.

Game Over

Press Start, then hold to blow gently.
