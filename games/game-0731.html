Canyon River Raft Rush

Steer your raft down a wild canyon river! Dodge rocks and logs, grab floating supply crates, and survive 3000 metres to reach calm water.

  • How to Play: Move left and right to steer. Avoid grey rocks and brown logs.
  • Crates: Collect supply crates for +50 points and a bonus life every 10 crates.
  • Controls: Arrow keys / A and D, mouse move, or drag with touch.
  • Lives: You have 3 lives. A crash costs one life and briefly makes you invulnerable.
  • Win: Reach 3000 m. The river gets faster and busier as you go!

Sound starts only after you press Start.
