Harbor Lighthouse Keeper

How to Play: Ships arrive from the open sea on the left and must reach the harbor mouth on the right. Your lighthouse beam is their guide. A ship inside the golden beam steers safely toward harbor; a ship left in darkness drifts with the current and can strike a reef.

Controls: Move the mouse or drag a finger to aim the beam. Click or tap the sea to drop a signal buoy (you have 4) — ships near a buoy also steer safely. Arrow keys aim the beam, Up / Down change beam range, Space toggles the beam, B drops a buoy at the beam tip. Beam fuel drains while the beam is on and slowly refills when it is off.

Goal: Save 12 ships to win. Lose 3 ships to reefs or storms and the harbor closes. Watch for storms: they shrink your beam and push ships off course!

Keyboard, mouse and touch all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.
