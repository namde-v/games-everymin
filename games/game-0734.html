🥁 Drumline Parade March

How to Play: You are the drumline leading the parade down Main Street. Drum notes march down four lanes toward the gold hit line. Strike the matching drum pad exactly when a note reaches the line.

  • SNARE (A), BASS (S), TENOR (D), CYMBAL (F) — use keys, mouse clicks, or touch taps.
  • Perfect hits (right on the line) score 100, good hits score 50. Hits build your combo multiplier.
  • Missing a note or hitting an empty lane costs Parade Spirit. If Spirit hits 0%, the parade falls apart — Game Over.
  • March the full route (60 seconds) without losing the crowd to WIN the parade!
  • Watch for marching-step cues: golden notes are worth double points. Keep the combo alive!
