How to Play:

  • A white needle sweeps back and forth across the strike gauge.
  • Press Space, click, or tap CHISEL STRIKE when the needle is inside the coloured target zone.
  • Green (Perfect): +100 points and a clean carve. Yellow (Good): +50 points.
  • Miss: the ice cracks! You lose a life. Lose all 3 lives and the sculpture shatters.
  • Perfect strikes build your combo multiplier. Each sculpture needs 10 clean carves.
  • Finish all 3 sculptures — Swan, Diamond, and Castle — to WIN.

The gauge gets faster and the target gets smaller as you progress. Stay frosty!
