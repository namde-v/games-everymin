⛵ Storm Cloud Sailor

Sail your little boat through a raging storm. Dodge lightning and whirlpools, ride the waves, and rescue 25 floating cargo crates before the storm claims you.

How to Play

  • Move: Arrow keys / WASD, or drag with mouse / finger. The boat follows your pointer while you hold it.
  • Sail trim: Hold SPACE (or hold the Reef Sail button) to reef the sail. Reefed = slower but steady. Full sail = faster, but dangerous in strong gusts.
  • Balance: Watch the Heel meter. High wind + full sail = heavy heel. If heel hits 100%, you capsize and lose a life.
  • Collect: Touch a golden cargo crate for +100 points. Chain pickups quickly for combo bonuses.
  • Avoid: Yellow warning circles mean lightning is about to strike — sail away fast. Swirling whirlpools pull you in and cost a life.
  • Win: Collect 25 crates. Lose: Lose all 3 lives.

Tip: In a red gust warning, reef immediately and steer cross-wind. In calm wind, keep full sail and chase the golden crates. Waves push you up and down — use them, do not fight them.

Game Over

Press Start, then sail. Hold SPACE to reef in gusts.
