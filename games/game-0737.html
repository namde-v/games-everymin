🐚 Tidepool Shell Sorter

How to Play

  • A mixed shell appears in the tidepool. Look at its color and pattern.
  • Sort it into the matching basket below: tap a basket, click it, or press 1, 2, 3 or 4.
  • Correct sorts score points. Streaks build a combo bonus: 2x, 3x, 4x and more!
  • Wrong sorts cost a life and make the tide rise faster. Sort 30 shells to win.
  • Watch the tide! If it reaches the top of the pool, or you lose all 3 lives, the game ends.
  • Arrow keys / A and D also cycle the selected basket; Enter or Space confirms it.

Tip: striped shells go to the striped basket, spotted to spotted, swirled to swirled, and plain to plain — color must match too. Fast correct streaks are the key to a high score!
