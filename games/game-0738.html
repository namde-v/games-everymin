ARCTIC RESEARCH STATION

You are the Station Chief. Assign your 6 crew members, balance power, heat and supplies, survive blizzards and breakdowns, and reach 200 Research Points before Day 30.

How to Play:
• Click / tap a crew member, then click a task to assign them. Or use arrow keys to pick a crew member and keys 1–5 to assign a task.
• Research earns points. Power restores power. Heating restores heat. Supplies gathers food and fuel. Repair fixes integrity.
• Blizzards drain heat and power fast — move crew to Heating and Power. Equipment failures drain integrity until repaired.
• If integrity, heat or supplies hit zero, the station fails. Reach 200 RP by Day 30 to win!
• Keys: ← → select crew, 1–5 assign, Space pause, R restart, M sound on/off. Mouse and touch both work.

Tip: Never leave Research empty for long, but never ignore a blizzard warning.
