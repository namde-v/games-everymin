Firefly Lantern Beat

A rhythm firefly-catching night! Fireflies drift through the garden and pulse in time with the beat. Trap them in your lantern exactly on the beat to build combos.

How to Play
  • Mouse / Touch: tap a glowing firefly directly when it shines brightest on the beat.
  • Keyboard: move the net with Arrow keys or WASD, press Space or Enter to catch.
  • Perfect (right on the beat) = 100 pts · Good = 50 pts · off-beat tap = 20 pts.
  • If a firefly escapes, your lantern dims — lose all 3 glows and the night ends.
  • Win by catching 20 fireflies. Chain catches without a miss to raise your combo multiplier.
Tip: watch the beat bar — the flash marks the perfect moment.
