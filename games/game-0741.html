🌋 VOLCANO
ASH RUNNER

Outrun the eruption! Dash across the burning ridge, leap over rivers of lava, duck under falling ash rocks, and grab glowing ember gems before the volcano claims you.

How to Play:
⬆ / Space / Tap top half = Jump  •  ⬇ / S / Tap bottom half = Slide
Click or tap to jump too. Collect ◆ ember gems (+50).
Avoid ash rocks and lava gaps. You have 3 lives. Survive to 3000 points to WIN!

Best played with sound on — all sounds are generated live in your browser.
