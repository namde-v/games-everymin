Mirror Maze
Light Bender

How to Play: A beam leaves the glowing emitter. Click, tap, or select a mirror and press Space to rotate it between / and \. Guide the beam through every mirror to the sparkling target crystal before you run out of moves.

Each rotation uses 1 move. Finish fast for a big bonus. Running out of moves costs a life. Lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over. Clear all 5 levels to win!

Keyboard: Arrow keys move the cursor • Space / Enter rotates • R restarts
Mouse & touch: tap a mirror to rotate it.

You Win!

Tip: trace the beam in your head first. A / mirror turns Right→Up and Down→Left. A \ mirror turns Right→Down and Up→Left. Mirrors on the wrong path are decoys — rotating them wastes moves!
