🌳 Orchard Harvest Planner

How to Play: You run a small orchard on a 10 × 7 field. Plant trees, water them, and harvest ripe apples for coins. Workers placed next to trees auto-harvest ripe fruit. Sun and water refill over time — water also helps trees grow faster. Storms arrive regularly: harvest ripe fruit before a storm, or you lose the fruit and a life!

Win: reach 300 coins. Lose: lose all 3 lives.
Controls: Mouse / touch: tap a plot. Keys: 1-4 tools, Arrows move cursor, Enter/Space place, R restart.

🌱 Sapling grows with sun + water → 🌿 Growing → 🌳 Mature → 🍎 Ripe (harvest now!)  |  Worker 👨‍🌾 harvests neighbouring ripe trees automatically.  |  Storm ⛈️ destroys unharvested ripe fruit.
