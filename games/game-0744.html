🎸 Samba Street Drummer

The carnival parade is marching down the street and you are the drummer! Beats roll down four lanes toward the glowing hit zone. Strike the matching drum exactly on the beat.

  • How to Play: Watch a note fall into the yellow hit zone, then hit its drum pad at the right moment.
  • Controls: D Surdo • F Tamborim • J Agogô • K Shaker. Or click / tap a pad or its lane.
  • Scoring: Perfect = 100, Good = 50, plus combo bonus. A missed note or a wrong-drum hit costs a life.
  • Win: Survive the full parade (about 75 seconds) with at least one life left. Game Over: lose all 5 lives.
  • Tip: Samba swings! Notes often arrive in pairs and syncopated bursts — listen to the drum sounds and stay in the groove.
