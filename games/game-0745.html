Deep Sea Pearl Diver

How to Play: You are a pearl diver exploring a coral reef. Swim with arrow keys / WASD, or steer with your mouse or touch — the diver follows your pointer. Collect glowing pearls from clams, dodge jellyfish and patrolling sharks, and watch your oxygen meter (the bar at the bottom). Surface to the top of the water to bank your pearls and refill your air. Grab air bubbles to top up oxygen mid-dive.

Collect 30 pearls to win. You have 3 lives. A jellyfish sting or shark bite costs a life. Running out of air costs a life too. Rare golden pearls are worth 5 points!

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD · Mouse move · Touch drag · Space = dash
