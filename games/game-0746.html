How to Play
1. Watch the rune stones glow in order and memorize the sequence.
2. Tap the stones in the same order to reproduce it. A wrong rune costs a life.
3. Then turn the rotating rune dials until they match the target pattern shown above the dials.
4. Clear all 5 levels to open the vault and win. You have 3 lives — guard them well.

Mouse / Touch: tap stones and dials  •  Keyboard: keys 1–6 pick a rune, ← → choose a dial, ↑ / Space turn it, Enter starts, R restarts

Tip: Listen to the tones — every rune has its own sound. Sequences get longer each level.

You Win!

Press Start and watch the stones closely.

Dial puzzle: click a dial to rotate it forward. Hold Shift while clicking, or use ↓, to rotate it backward. Match every dial to the golden target row.
