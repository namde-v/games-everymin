🌬️ Windmill Blade Balancer

How to Play: Gusts of wind push your windmill rotor off balance. Steer it back into the GREEN ZONE and hold it there to score. Stay in the green to gain points; drift too far for too long and you lose stability.

🕹️ Controls: ← → or A / D keys to tilt • Hold on-screen buttons • Tap / drag on the canvas: left side tilts left, right side tilts right • Space = brake (slows the rotor swing) • P = pause

🏆 Win: reach 1500 points. 💔 Lose: all 3 stability hearts run out.
