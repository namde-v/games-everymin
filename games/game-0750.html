Meteor Shower Defender

How to Play: A meteor storm is falling on your base! Move the turret, aim, and blast meteors before they strike. Big meteors split into dangerous fragments. Protect all three shield generators — if a meteor hits an unshielded generator, your base loses a heart.

Destroy meteors to score. Clear every wave to advance. Survive 6 waves to win!

Move: ← → or A / D  |  Aim & Shoot: Mouse / Tap / Spacebar
Tap a meteor to shoot at it directly. Press P to pause.
