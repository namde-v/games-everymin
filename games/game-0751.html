Glacier Penguin Slide

Collect all 18 fish before your lives run out!

How to Play
Your penguin slides on slippery ice: it keeps gliding until it hits a snowbank or you steer it.
Steer gently — momentum is real! Grab fish for points, avoid dark crevasses, and dodge sliding ice blocks.
Falling into a crevasse or being hit by a block costs a life. Collect every fish to win.

Arrow keys / WASD to steer  •  Mouse or touch: drag toward a direction to steer  •  Press Enter to start
