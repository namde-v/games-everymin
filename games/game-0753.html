🏜️ Desert Oasis Trader

Buy cheap. Cross the desert. Sell high. Beat the season.

How to Play

  • You start in Siwa with 100 gold, 20 water and room for 20 cargo.
  • Each oasis pays different prices. Dates are cheap in one town, spice in another — prices shift every day.
  • Buy goods, travel to a town that pays more, and sell for profit.
  • Travel costs days and water (longer routes cost more). Buy water or rest if you run low.
  • WIN: reach 600 gold at any time before the season ends.
  • LOSE: Day 30 ends and you are still below 600 gold, or you are stranded with no water and no gold to buy any.
  • Controls: mouse click, touch tap, or keyboard (1–5 travel, B/S trade, R rest).

No arcade dodging or shooting — pure trading strategy. Sound starts only after you press Start.
