🔔 Bell Tower Chime Master

The old tower bells are out of tune and only you can ring them true. Bells slide down the tower — ring each one the instant it crosses the golden chime line.

How to Play
Keys: A Small bell   S Medium bell   K Large bell   L Great bell
Or tap / click the lane buttons below, or tap directly on a lane.
Perfect timing = 100 pts · Good = 50 pts · Miss = lose a heart.
Ring all 64 bells to win. Lose all 5 hearts and the tower falls silent.

Tip: big bells sing low, small bells sing high. Listen for the pitch!
