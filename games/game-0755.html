Paper Plane Glider

How to Play: Your paper plane launches into a breezy sky. Steer it through 15 floating rings before you run out of flights. Climbing costs speed and diving gains it — fly too slowly and you stall! Wind gusts will push you up or down, so correct gently.

↑ / W or hold mouse / touch & drag — steer & lift  •  ↓ / S — dive for speed  •  Space — gentle lift
Pass a ring dead-centre for a bonus +25. Miss a ring and you lose a flight.

Keyboard, mouse and touch all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.

Tip: stay near the middle of each ring, dive before a long climb, and watch for gust warnings.
