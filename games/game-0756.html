🐝 Meadow Bee Pollinator

How to Play: Flowers bloom in numbered order — visit them in that order to collect pollen. Carry pollen back to the golden hive before the flower wilts! Each hive delivery scores points. Wasps patrol the meadow: touching one costs a life. Deliver 5 loads to win before your 3 lives run out.

Move: Arrow keys / WASD  |  Mouse: steer toward cursor  |  Touch: drag to fly  |  Space: dash
