Tile Bridge Builder

How to Play

  • Click or tap a dotted cell over the water to place the selected tile. Click a placed tile to remove it.
  • Wood tiles are plentiful but weak: they collapse if they sit more than 2 steps from an island or rock pillar.
  • Steel tiles are strong: they hold up to 4 steps from support, but you only have a few. Use them on long spans.
  • Fill every gap column, then press Walk to send the walker across. A missing tile or a collapsed tile costs a life.
  • Finish all 3 levels to win. Keys: 1 = Wood, 2 = Steel, W or Space = Walk, C = Clear, R = Restart.

Mouse, touch, and keyboard all work. Sound starts only after you press Start.

You Win!

Choose Wood or Steel, then build across the gap.

Tip: rock pillars count as supports. The red glow marks a tile that is too far from support for its material.
