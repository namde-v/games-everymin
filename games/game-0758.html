Bakery Supply Chain

Run a bakery for one busy day! Orders arrive with deadlines. Buy flour, sugar and butter, bake the right recipe in an oven, and deliver before the order expires or the baked good spoils.

Bread
2 Flour • 4s bake
Reward 28 gold
Cake
2 Flour + 2 Sugar • 7s
Reward 48 gold
Croissant
1 Flour + 2 Butter • 6s
Reward 42 gold

HOW TO PLAY: Click an order, then click a free oven to start baking. When an oven flashes READY, click it to deliver. Buy supplies before you run out. Missed orders and spoiled goods cost reputation.

Keys: 1/2/3 select oven • B bread • C cake • R croissant • F buy flour • S sugar • U butter • Space start/restart

Day Complete!
