Ukulele Island Strum

How to Play: Chord shapes drift down the island track toward the golden strum line.

Tap a chord button (C, G, Am, F) or press 1–4 to match the chord nearest the line. Then press Space, click, or tap STRUM! exactly as it crosses the line.

Perfect strums score 100, good strums 60, early/late strums 25. Miss a chord or strum the wrong chord and you lose a life. Reach 1500 points before the song ends to win. You have 3 lives.
