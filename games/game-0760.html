🏹 Archery Wind Shot

How to Play

  • Aim with your mouse, finger, or arrow keys. The dotted line previews your shot.
  • Click, tap Shoot, or press Space to loose an arrow. Hold nothing back — power and angle matter.
  • Wind pushes every arrow sideways. A strong headwind or tailwind changes the arc, so aim off-target when the wind is strong.
  • Moving targets are worth 25, 50, or 100 points by ring. Bullseyes are worth the most.
  • You have 12 arrows. Hit 8 targets to win. Run out of arrows first and the match is over.

Keyboard: Up / Down = angle, Left / Right = power, Space = shoot, R = restart.

Sound starts only after you press Start. No downloads needed — everything runs offline.
