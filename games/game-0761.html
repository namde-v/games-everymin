Moonlight Firefly Catcher

How to Play: Move your glowing jar around the moonlit meadow and catch fireflies for points. Bright fireflies are worth more! Watch out — bats swoop through the night and drain a life if they touch your jar.

Reach 500 points in 90 seconds to win.
You have 3 lives. Fireflies get faster as the night goes on!

Move: Arrow Keys / WASD • Mouse: move jar to pointer • Touch: drag to move
Firefly = +10 to +30 points  •  Bat = −1 life

Tip: Fireflies blink — catch them when they glow brightest for bonus points. Stay away from bats, especially near the top of the meadow where they dive. Your jar catches anything inside its rim.

You Win!

Tip: on touch devices, drag anywhere on the meadow to move the jar.
