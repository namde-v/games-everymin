⚙️ Gearworks Cog Puzzle

How to Play

  • Every cog has power ports (bright spokes). Power can only pass where two neighbouring cogs face each other.
  • Click or tap a cog: it turns 90° clockwise and its meshed neighbours turn 90° the other way.
  • Light the whole path from the green SOURCE cog to the red TARGET cog to clear the level.
  • There are 5 levels, each larger than the last. Every rotation costs 1 move.
  • Run out of moves on a level and the machine stops — Game Over. Finish all 5 levels to Win!
