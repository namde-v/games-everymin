Harbor Ferry Scheduler

Your shift: Passengers queue at 4 docks, each wanting a different dock. Dispatch a ferry to collect them, then send it on to their destination. Every delivery earns fare. Every passenger who gives up waiting costs reputation.

How to play
• Mouse / touch: tap a ferry, then tap a dock to dispatch it.
• Keys 1–4: select ferry  • Arrow keys: choose dock  • Space: dispatch
• Ferries carry up to 4 passengers and drop off anyone whose destination matches.
• Win: earn $1000 fare  • Game over: reputation reaches 0.

Patience bars shrink as passengers wait — red bars are about to leave!

Shift Complete

Tip: send the nearest empty ferry to the longest queue, and chain deliveries — a full ferry heading to a popular dock earns the most.
