🦅 Canyon Condor Soarer

Ride the desert thermals! Hold to climb inside a golden thermal, release to glide. Snatch feathers and fish, but steer clear of cliff walls and storm clouds.

How to Play:
Hold Space / mouse / touch to soar in thermals • Arrows / WASD to steer
Drag with mouse or touch to guide the condor • Collect 40 items to win
3 hits from cliffs or storms = game over. Fish = 25 pts, Feather = 10 pts.

Tip: golden columns are thermals — stay inside them to gain height without losing speed.
