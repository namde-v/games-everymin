💎 Crystal Prism Sorter

Coloured crystals are stacked in glass tubes. Sort them so every tube holds only one colour.

  • Click / tap a tube to lift its top crystal.
  • Click another tube to drop it there — only onto the same colour or into an empty tube.
  • A tube holds at most 4 crystals. A tube is complete when all its crystals match.
  • Finish the level before you run out of moves, then clear all 6 levels to win!
  • Keyboard: Left / Right arrows select a tube, Space picks up or drops, U undoes, R restarts.
