⛷️ Mountain Ski Resort Manager

How to Play: Run a ski resort for a 20-day season. Happy skiers pay you; long queues, closed lifts and unhappy guests drain satisfaction.

  • ← → select a building   1-3 select action   Space confirm   N next day
  • Action 1 — Toggle Lift: open or close the selected lift.
  • Action 2 — Ticket Price: cycles price 15 → 25 → 35 → 45. Low price brings more skiers, high price earns more per skier.
  • Action 3 — Assign Staff: move a staff member to the selected lodge/lift (max 3 each, 6 staff total).
  • Click or tap a building to select it, and click an action button to select it. Clicking a building also applies the selected action.
  • Serve skiers before their patience runs out. Queues grow when lifts are closed or understaffed.

Win: reach 1500 coins with satisfaction above 0 by the end of Day 20.
Game Over: satisfaction hits 0.

Season Complete!
