🏔 Alpine Yodel Echo

How to Play:

  • Press Start. The yodeller sings a short melody.
  • Watch the mountain glow and listen to the pitch order.
  • Repeat it with keys A S D F, by clicking, or by tapping the four note buttons.
  • Each round adds one more note. Finish all 8 rounds to win!
  • A wrong note costs a life. You have 3 lives — lose them all and it is game over.
  • Bonus points for a perfect round and for quick, confident echoes.

Keyboard: A S D F  |  Mouse: click a note  |  Touch: tap a note

You Win!

Tip: hum along while the melody plays — rhythm matters as much as pitch. A perfect round earns +50 bonus.
