🌋 VOLCANO
LAVA RUNNER

Escape down the erupting volcano! Sprint across the burning slope, leap the lava pits, and dodge falling rocks before the mountain catches you.

How to Play

🡅 / Space / Tap / Click — Jump (double-jump in mid-air!)
🡇 / S — Duck under high rocks  •  Avoid lava pits and rocks
Reach 2000m to escape and WIN. You have 3 lives.

Coins boost your score. Landing in lava or getting hit by a rock costs a life. Brief invincibility follows each hit.

Keyboard, mouse, and touch supported. Sound starts after you press Start.
