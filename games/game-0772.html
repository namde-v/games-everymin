Lighthouse Lens Puzzle

How to Play: A beam leaves the lighthouse lamp. Rotate mirrors and lenses so it reaches the target beacon.

  • Click / Tap a mirror or lens to rotate it 45° (mirrors) or cycle its lens mode.
  • Arrow keys move the glowing selector, Space / R rotates the selected tile, Enter starts.
  • Mirrors bounce the beam. Lenses split or bend it. Walls block it. The beam path lights up live.
  • Solve each level before you run out of moves. Finish all 6 levels to win!

Tip: watch the beam — a dim tip means it hit a wall or missed the beacon.

🏆 Beacon Lit — You Win!

All beacons are shining. Every ship is safe tonight.

🌊 Out of Moves — Game Over

The beam faded before it reached the beacon. The night is still dark.

Rotate mirrors to guide the beam to the beacon.

Controls: Mouse / Touch — tap a tile to rotate  |  Keyboard — ←↑↓→ select, Space rotate, Enter start / restart. Lenses: clear lens passes straight, amber lens bends 90° clockwise.
