Desert Oasis Trader

Turn 100 gold into 500 gold in 30 days.

How to Play

  • Trade: Buy goods cheap in desert towns and sell them dear at the Oasis Market. Prices change every day.
  • Travel: Moving to another town costs water and camel stamina, and advances the day. Rest to recover stamina.
  • Survive: Water costs gold at most towns. Running out of water on the trail ends your run. Sandstorms can damage cargo — keep a little gold in reserve.
  • Win: Reach 500 gold. Lose: Go bankrupt, run out of water, or finish Day 30 below target.
  • Controls: Click or tap the buttons, or use keys 1–4 to travel, B to buy, S to sell, R to rest.
