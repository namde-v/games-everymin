🌊 Deep Sea Pearl Diver 🦪

Precision / Breath-Hold Diving — dive deep, grab pearls, and surface alive.

Deep Sea Pearl Diver

How to Play: You start at the surface with a full air tank. Dive down, collect 12 pearls to win, then return to the surface — or surface early to refill your air and dive again!

Deeper pearls are worth more points, but your air drains faster the deeper you go. Watch out for jellyfish and sharks — touching one costs air and knocks you back. Bubbles refill a little air.

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to swim • Mouse: hold and move to steer toward the pointer • Touch: drag on the screen to swim. Press Space to dash (costs a little air).

Win: collect 12 pearls. Game Over: your air reaches 0 while underwater.
