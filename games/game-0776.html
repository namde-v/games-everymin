🌪️ STORM CHASER

Your mission: Drive the storm-chasing van into the tornado's outer data ring to collect 12 readings — without letting the funnel itself smash your van!

HOW TO PLAY
🚐 Move: Arrow Keys / WASD — or drag / tap on the plains
🟡 Outer ring (yellow): COLLECTING ZONE — stay here to fill the probe meter
🔴 Inner funnel (red): DANGER ZONE — health drains fast, escape now!
💨 Watch out: Flying debris hurts you, and storm gusts push your van
🏆 Win: 12 readings  |  💥 Lose: Van health hits 0%

Tip: Dart in, fill the probe, dart out. Do not park inside the funnel!

YOU WIN!

Keyboard: WASD / Arrows  •  Mouse: click / hold to steer toward pointer  •  Touch: drag to drive  •  Space: horn / probe boost
