🌱 Space Station Hydroponics

Strategy / Station Farming Management. You run the grow-bays on Station Meridian. Plant crops, keep them watered, harvest before they wilt, and fill crew food orders to reach the supply target.

  • Goal: Deliver 25 food to crew orders before the 180-second shift ends. Win = target reached. Lose = time runs out or power hits zero.
  • Plant: Click an empty bay (or press 1–6 then P). Costs 5 credits. Different crops give different yields.
  • Water: Click a growing bay or press W. Watering costs water — dry crops wilt!
  • Harvest: Click a glowing ready bay or press H. Harvested food goes to stock.
  • Deliver: Press D or the Deliver button when stock covers the current order.
  • Manage: Use Solar Recharge and Water Refill buttons (cost credits) before resources run out. Arrow keys move selection.

Mouse, touch, and keyboard all work. Sound starts after you press Start.
