🐟 Arctic Ice Fisher 🧊

Drill a hole in the frozen lake, drop your line, and strike at exactly the right moment!

How to Play:
1. Click / tap / press SPACE to drill a hole, then drop your line.
2. Watch the bobber. When a fish bites, the bobber plunges and flashes!
3. Strike while the bobber is under water to hook the fish.
4. Rare fish (golden & glowing) are worth more but bite for a shorter time.
5. Missed strikes scare fish away and cost a strike. Catch 12 fish to win!

Controls: Mouse click / Tap / SPACE = drill, drop & strike. R = restart anytime.
